Audacy — JPMorgan sees U.S. humanoid robot output reaching 400,000 by 2030

J. P. Morgan estimates that U. S. humanoid robot production could reach 300,000 to 400,000 units by 2030 and about 1.5 million units by 2035. Labor shortages, improving costs, and rising production capacity are driving adoption in manufacturing, logistics, and warehousing. Robot costs are nearing parity with labor costs, potentially saving customers roughly $100,000 annually per robot when run across multiple shifts.
