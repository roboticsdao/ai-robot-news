Fierce Sensors — Huang’s exit gives physical AI firms reason to break the Nvidia habit

Robotics makers face cost, power, and single-vendor dependence issues with Nvidia's full physical AI stack, while Jensen Huang's resignation adds leadership and strategic continuity risk. Principal analyst Jack Gold noted that companies may not want to be overly dependent on Nvidia's full stack longer term or on proprietary platforms like CUDA. A heterogeneous architecture can process camera, tactile sensing, sensor fusion, and motor controls across different accelerators rather than relying on one Nvidia GPU stack. Waymo uses a custom 5nm ASIC for its sixth-gen robotaxi alongside other suppliers, Mobileye utilizes its proprietary EyeQ processors, SAP and Intel demonstrated an open physical AI warehouse project using Intel edge-AI hardware, and Kuka Robotics and partners are adopting Qualcomm's Dragonwing robotics architecture.

总结：机器人制造商对英伟达全面的物理人工智能技术栈在成本、功耗和单一供应商依赖方面感到不适，而詹森·黄于周四的辞职增加了领导层和战略连续性风险。 首席分析师杰克·戈德指出，从长远来看，公司可能不希望过度依赖英伟达的全套技术栈或像CUDA这样的专有平台。 异构架构可以在不同的加速器上处理摄像头、触觉传感、传感器融合和电机控制工作负载，而不是将一切汇聚到单一的英伟达GPU技术栈中。 Waymo在其第六代机器人出租车系统中使用了定制的5nm ASIC芯片，同时保留了包括英伟达在内的其他供应商；Mobileye构建了围绕EyeQ处理器的替代物理AI技术栈；SAP和Intel展示了一个使用英伟达Isaac之外的英伟达替代品的开源物理AI仓库项目；库卡机器人及工业机器人合作伙伴则正在采用高通Dragonwing机器人架构。

TechTarget — Agility Robotics goes public as first U.S. specialist humanoid maker

Agility Robotics became the first publicly listed U. S. humanoid robotics company, trading on Nasdaq under AGLT after completing its combination with Churchill Capital Corp XI. The offering generated about $465 million in pre-expense capital to accelerate commercialization of its Digit robot. Gartner analyst Bill Ray and ABI Research analyst George Chowdhury noted the strategic significance of an American company manufacturing robots given Chinese leadership in the space, as well as Agility's focus on structured industrial and warehouse environments over consumer applications. While Agility has about 100 Digit robots at work today and active distribution networks, investors currently tend to prefer companies promising a fully automated future rather than those commercializing technology today.

总结：Agility Robotics在完成与前Churchill Capital Corp XI的业务合并后，成为美国首家上市的人形机器人公司，其普通股上周开始在纳斯达克交易所交易，股票代码为AGLT。 该公司表示，此次发行筹集了约$465 million的税前资金，计划用于加速其Digit人形机器人的商业化。 Gartner分析师比尔·雷和ABI Research分析师乔治·乔杜里指出，鉴于中国公司在机器人制造领域的领先地位，美国本土公司上市具有重要的政治和市场意义。 虽然Agility专注于制造和物流等更为扎实的工业应用，目前已有约100台Digit机器人在实际工作中运行并建立起分销网络，但乔杜里指出，投资者目前往往更偏爱承诺全面自动化未来的公司，而不是那些今天就在将技术商业化的企业。

AI Insider — Nvidia Introduces GR00T 2 Robotics Model, New Simulation Tools and Benchmarks at CoRL

Nvidia introduced GR00T 2, an open AI model for complex robotic tasks, at the Conference on Robot Learning in Austin, Texas. GR00T 2 achieved a 71% average success rate across 50 tasks with one demonstration, rising to 95% with five demonstrations. Built on Nvidia Cosmos, it uses 100,000 hours of point-of-view video data. Nvidia also launched Isaac Lab 3.0, offering up to 2.5 times faster training throughput using the Newton physics engine, alongside industrial benchmarks DexBench and VABAR. Several companies, including Foxconn, Siemens, and Nexuni, are testing these tools.

总结：英伟达在得克萨斯州奥斯汀举行的机器人学习会议上推出了用于复杂机器人任务的开放式人工智能模型GR00T 2。 GR00T 2在50个任务中，通过单次示范取得了71%的平均成功率，进行五次示范时成功率上升至95%。 该模型基于英伟达Cosmos构建，使用了100,000小时的第一人称视角视频数据。 英伟达还推出了Isaac Lab 3.0，在使用Newton物理引擎时可提供最高2.5倍的训练吞吐量，并发布了工业基准DexBench和VABAR。 富士康、西门子和Nexuni等多家公司正在测试这些新工具。

Supply Chain Digital Magazine — Hyundai's Boston Dynamics Launches Stretch Gen 2 robot

Boston Dynamics, a wholly-owned subsidiary of Hyundai Motor Group, unveiled Stretch Gen 2, an autonomous mobile robot designed for warehouse activities such as trailer unloading.
