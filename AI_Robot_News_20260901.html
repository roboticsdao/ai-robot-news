TechSpot — ICE seeks to purchase up to $2 million in Boston Dynamics Spot robots

Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) plans to spend between $1 million and $2 million on Boston Dynamics Spot robot dogs, according to a Department of Homeland Security procurement notice. The robots are intended for inspection, situational awareness, and hazard assessment in risky environments to improve officer safety and operational decision-making. A senior DHS official confirmed the robots will not be used to apprehend people or make arrests. The potential purchase has drawn controversy due to past law enforcement deployments, such as the NYPD's previous use of a modified Spot known as Digidog, which faced public backlash over surveillance concerns and the appearance of policing. Boston Dynamics explicitly prohibits using Spot to harm or intimidate people, and while it warns it will take measures against misuse, the company faces scrutiny over whether the sale is worth the negative publicity.

总结：根据美国国土安全部的采购通知，移民与海关执法局（ICE）计划斥资100万至200万美元购买波士顿动力公司的Spot机器人狗。 这些机器人将用于危险环境下的检查、态势感知和隐患评估，以提高警员安全性并支持运营决策。 一位国土安全部高级官员确认，这些机器人不会用于抓捕人员或实施逮捕。 由于纽约警察局等执法机构过去部署同类机器人的经历曾引发关于监控和警务外观的公众争议，此次潜在采购也备受关注。 波士顿动力公司明令禁止将Spot用于伤害或恐吓他人，并警告将对滥用行为采取应对措施，该公司目前面临这笔销售是否值得承受负面舆论的权衡。

Rescana — Critical Active Exploitation Alert: Unitree G1 EDU Humanoid Robot Vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-76639, CVE-2026-76640) Enable Root RCE via Bluetooth and Chatbot Flaws

Two critical vulnerabilities, CVE-2026-76639 and CVE-2026-76640, have been identified in the Unitree G1 EDU Humanoid Robot running firmware versions up to and including v1. 5. 2, enabling unauthenticated attackers to achieve root remote code execution. CVE-2026-76639 is a path traversal flaw in the chat_go AI chatbot service allowing arbitrary file writing via the knowledge upload API, while CVE-2026-76640 involves a BLE-based wormable root RCE chain in the btgatt-server featuring an insecure Wi-Fi configuration script and a 1050-byte buffer overflow in the SSID handler. Security researchers have demonstrated exploitation in the wild, posing risks of rapid propagation among multiple G1 robots within Bluetooth range, though there are no reports of exploitation by APT groups or criminal organizations.

总结：运行v1. 5. 2及更低版本固件的宇树G1 EDU人形机器人被发现存在CVE-2026-76639和CVE-2026-76640两个严重漏洞，未经身份验证的攻击者可借此实现root远程代码执行。 CVE-2026-76639是chat_go人工智能聊天机器人服务中的路径遍历漏洞，允许通过知识上传API写入任意文件；CVE-2026-76640则是btgatt-server中基于蓝牙低功耗（BLE）的可蠕虫化root远程代码执行链，包含不安全的Wi-Fi配置脚本及SSID处理程序中的1050字节缓冲区溢出。 安全研究人员已在野外演示了漏洞利用，这给蓝牙范围内的多个G1机器人带来了快速传播的风险，但目前尚无高级持续性威胁（APT）组织或犯罪团伙利用该漏洞的报告。
