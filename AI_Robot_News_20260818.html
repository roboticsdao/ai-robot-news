AOL.com — Better Robotics Stock: Tesla vs. Nvidia

Nvidia and Tesla are advancing in robotics with different strategies. Nvidia provides AI architecture, supercomputers, and foundation models like Isaac GR00T, and its physical AI revenue reached an annual run rate of $10 billion, with $48. 5 billion in free cash flow at the end of the first quarter. Jensen Huang states that physical AI will become a $100 billion business in less than 10 years, while Morgan Stanley estimates humanoid robotics could be worth $5 trillion by 2050. Conversely, Tesla focuses on building its Optimus humanoid robot, retooling lines in Fremont and building Giga Texas to reach high production capacities. However, Tesla's capital expenditures in Q2 2026 increased 142% to nearly $5. 8 billion, dropping free cash flow to negative-$1. 1 billion as it scales manufacturing and misses production guidance.

总结：NVIDIA与特斯拉在机器人领域采取了不同的发展策略。 NVIDIA开发了AI架构、超级计算机以及Isaac GR00T等基础模型，其物理AI业务的年营收运行率已达到$10 billion，第一季度末自由现金流为$48. 5 billion。 NVIDIA首席执行官黄仁勋表示，该业务在不到10年内将成为一个$100 billion级别的业务，而摩根士丹利估计到2050年人形机器人市场价值可能高达$5 trillion。 相比之下，特斯拉专注于制造其Optimus人形机器人，在得克萨斯州弗里蒙特和Giga Texas重新规划并建造工厂以实现大规模生产。 然而，由于加速机器人生产，特斯拉2026年第二季度资本支出大增142%至近$5. 8 billion，自由现金流降至负$1. 1 billion，且未能达成生产目标。

株式会社インプレス — 脳理論に基づくAIモデルでヒューマノイドロボットが介護動作を学習 NCNPと早稲田大学が実証
