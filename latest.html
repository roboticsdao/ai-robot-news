Interesting Engineering — Robots power US AI foundry lab's push for faster alloy discovery

The Department of Materials Science and Engineering at Texas A&M University received a $2 million grant from the WoodNext Foundation to build the Aggie AI Foundry, a first-of-its-kind self-driven research lab. Conceived by professors Raymundo Arróyave and Ibrahim Karaman, the fully automated facility will combine artificial intelligence and robotic workers to accelerate the discovery and development of new metal alloys. Computer-controlled robotic arms will function in an assembly line, taking samples from a wall cabinet of elements from the entire periodic table to mix, melt, and test materials based on directions from a trained AI brain. The automated processes are expected to reduce alloy development time from months to a week or less. Arróyave noted that while computers can predict some behaviors, no computational tool can predict alloy behavior without physical testing on actual materials. The lab aims to perform trillions of mixing combinations across dozens of elements, helping scientists explore possibilities at an unprecedented scale, while freeing students from laborious tasks to focus on creative science.

总结：得克萨斯农工大学材料科学与工程系获得了WoodNext Foundation提供的$2 million资助，用于建造名为Aggie AI Foundry的首个同类全自主研究实验室。 该全自动化设施由该系教授Raymundo Arróyave和Ibrahim Karaman构想，将结合人工智能与机器人工人，以加速新金属合金的发现与开发。 受过训练的人工智能大脑将指导计算机控制的机械臂在流水线上运作，从包含整个元素周期表元素的壁柜中取样，对材料进行混合、熔炼和测试。 这些自动化过程预计将合金开发时间从数月缩短至一周甚至更短。 Arróyave指出，虽然计算机可以预测某些行为，但在不对实际材料进行物理测试的情况下，没有任何计算工具能够预测合金的行为。 该实验室旨在对数十种元素进行数万亿种混合组合测试，帮助科学家以空前规模探索各种可能，同时将学生从繁重的手工任务中解放出来，专注于更具创造性的科学研究。

Startup Fortune — Boston Dynamics workers petition against ICE deal for Spot robots

In early September, nearly 300 Boston Dynamics employees signed an internal petition asking company leadership to cancel a $1.3 million robot sale tied to Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE). Boston Dynamics builds quadruped Spot robots capable of climbing stairs, opening doors, and performing backflips. ICE finalized the $1.3 million contract in September through Connecticut-based reseller Radeco to acquire four Spot units equipped with a robotic arm, thermal camera, and sensors to detect radiation and explosives for searching border tunnels, crawl spaces, and closets. Employees argued the sale links the company directly to an agency sowing fear across immigrant communities, though nearly half of the signatures were anonymous among the company's 1,500 employees. Boston Dynamics defended the contract by stating it runs through ICE's Homeland Security Investigations division, which targets transnational crime rather than detention and deportation, and asserted that the robot helps keep people safe. The contract complies with a 2022 joint pledge by robotics makers not to weaponize general-purpose robots while permitting surveillance, inspection, and reconnaissance use. In May, employee pressure led the company to cancel a separate sale to a police equipment supplier wanting to attach flash-bang grenades to a Spot unit.

总结：9月初，近300名波士顿动力公司员工签署了一份内部请愿书，要求公司领导层取消一笔与美国移民和海关执法局（ICE）有关的$1.3 million机器人销售订单。 波士顿动力公司制造能够爬楼梯、开门和后空翻的四足Spot机器人。 ICE于9月通过总部位于康涅狄格州的转售商Radeco敲定了这笔$1.3 million的合同，采购了4台配备机械臂、热成像仪以及用于检测辐射和爆炸物的传感器的Spot机器人，供国土安全调查局特工在边境搜查隧道、狭窄空间和壁橱。 员工在请愿中辩称，此项销售使公司与在移民社区制造恐惧的机构直接产生联系，尽管在公司1,500多名员工中，近半数的签名是匿名的。 波士顿动力公司为该合同进行辩护，称该合同是通过ICE负责打击跨国犯罪的国土安全调查局而非拘留和驱逐部门进行的，并表示该机器人有助于保障人们的安全。 该合同符合机器人制造商在2022年签署的联合承诺，即不将通用机器人武器化，同时允许将其用于监视、检查和侦察。 今年5月，员工的压力曾促使该公司取消了向一家希望将震爆弹装到Spot机器人上的警察设备供应商出售机器人的计划。

Mashable — Robot companies, like Boston Dynamics, sign pledge to not have their products turned into weapons

Several robotics companies, including Boston Dynamics, Agility Robotics, ANYbotics, Clearpath Robotics, Open Robotics, and Unitree Robotics, signed an open letter pledging not to weaponize their advanced-mobility general-purpose robots or the software enabling them. The companies stated that adding weapons to remotely or autonomously operated robots accessible to the public creates serious ethical issues and risks of harm, and they committed to reviewing customer applications to prevent weaponization. However, the pledge specifies general-purpose robots, leaving room for military-specific machines, and notable companies producing armed robot dogs did not sign.

总结：包括Boston Dynamics、Agility Robotics、ANYbotics、Clearpath Robotics、Open Robotics和Unitree Robotics在内的多家机器人公司共同签署了一封公开信，承诺不将其先进移动通用机器人或相关软件武器化。 这些公司在信中表示，为公众可接触的远程或自主操作机器人添加武器会带来严重的道德问题和伤害风险，并承诺审查客户应用以避免潜在的武器化。 不过，该承诺明确针对通用机器人，为专门制造军用机器人留出了空间，且生产武装机器狗的公司并未签署该承诺。
