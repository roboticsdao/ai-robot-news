Marine Insight — U.S. Navy Quadruples AI Robotics Deal To $300 Million To Boost Fleet Readiness

The U. S. Navy has expanded its contract with Gecko Robotics, increasing the agreement ceiling from $71 million to $300 million to deploy robotics and AI systems for countering threats in the Indo-Pacific and speeding up ship maintenance. The expanded deal aims to address shipyard delays, labor shortages, and maintenance schedules to reach an 80 percent fleet readiness target. Gecko Robotics utilizes wall-climbing robots to inspect destroyers, amphibious warships, and littoral combat ships for metal loss and corrosion 50 times faster than manual inspections. The data is processed through the company's Cantilever software platform, which helps maintenance teams identify issues early and can save up to 8 million dollars in repair costs per ship. Additionally, Gecko revealed a specialized robot named Komodo designed to scan steel decks beneath non-skid coatings for hidden corrosion without stripping surfaces. CEO Jake Loosararian stated that AI-driven maintenance systems enable the military to outsmart operational bottlenecks and keep vessels combat-ready.

总结：美国海军已将与Gecko Robotics的合同上限从7100万美元扩大至3.03亿美元，以部署机器人和人工智能系统来应对印太地区的威胁并加快舰艇维护。 此项扩大的协议旨在应对造船厂延误、劳动力短缺和维护进度问题，以实现80%的舰队战备目标。 Gecko Robotics利用爬壁机器人检查驱逐舰、两栖战舰和濒海战斗舰的金属流失与腐蚀情况，速度比人工检查快50倍。 相关数据输入至公司的Cantilever软件平台，帮助维修团队及早发现问题，每艘舰艇可节省高达800万美元的维修成本。 此外，Gecko推出了一款名为Komodo的专用机器人，用于扫描防滑涂层下的钢制甲板以寻找隐藏腐蚀，且无需剥离表面。 Gecko Robotics首席执行官Jake Loosararian强调，人工智能驱动的维护系统使军方能够克服运营瓶颈并保持舰艇战备状态。

Supply Chain Digital — Audi and NVIDIA Use Agile Robots for Parts Sequencing Trial

Audi is collaborating with Agile Robots, Dassault Systèmes, and NVIDIA in a proof-of-concept project at its Ingolstadt plant to automate parts sequencing using mobile humanoid robots. Audi's material supply operations manage approximately one million picks daily, and the trial focuses on picking mixed components and placing them in correct assembly sequences. The project utilizes Audi's production expertise, Agile Robots' mobile humanoid robotics, Dassault Systèmes' DELMIA virtual-twin technology, and NVIDIA's physical-AI libraries. Gerd Walker, Member of the Board of Management for Production and Logistics at Audi, stated the project explores how AI-powered robotics can solve tough picking challenges and unlock productivity. Before deployment, a virtual twin created through Dassault Systèmes' 3DEXPERIENCE platform and NVIDIA Omniverse libraries helps engineers test robot feasibility, cycle times, and ergonomics. Agile Robots, based in Munich and having deployed over 20,000 robotic solutions, provides mobile humanoids featuring dual-arm manipulation and a mobile base to operate within existing factory environments. Dr. Zhaopeng Chen, Founder and CEO of Agile Robots, stated that the partnership demonstrates how mobile humanoids perform critical tasks that were previously impossible to automate.

Interesting Engineering — Two-letter trigger makes US robot turn knife toward human hand in security test

Researchers at Binghamton University in New York demonstrated how a two-letter trigger can compromise large language models used in robot task planning, forcing a Segway-based mobile robot to direct a knife toward a model human hand instead of an apple. The attack, named MuTRAP, inserts hidden malicious behavior activated by specific triggers. The team also developed a defense system called PROTEA to identify unsafe actions step by step. The findings were presented at the 2026 IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems.

总结：纽约宾汉姆顿大学的研究人员展示了双字母触发器如何破坏用于机器人任务规划的大型语言模型，促使基于赛格威的移动机器人将刀指向模型人手而非苹果。 这种名为MuTRAP的攻击会植入由特定触发器激活的隐藏恶意行为。 研究团队还开发了名为PROTEA的防御系统，用于逐步识别不安全动作。 相关研究成果在2026年IEEE/RSJ智能机器人与系统国际会议上进行了展示。
