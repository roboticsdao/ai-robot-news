The Conversation — China is exploring humanoid robots for war – but what role could they play?

Shortly after, the official newspaper of China's People's Liberation Army called for accelerating the transition of these machines from laboratories to military training grounds, referring to them as 'combatants'. While tracked or wheeled drones are superior in open-field combat, humanoid robots offer advantages in human-built urban environments where they can navigate stairs, doors, and standard infrastructure without custom modifications. The technology benefits from solved mechanical hurdles, carbon fiber and aluminum bodies, high-torque actuators up to 400Nm, and reinforcement learning in virtual simulations using tools like ROS 2 and Gazebo. While offensive warfare is controversial, proponents note their utility in defensive scenarios, hostage rescue, and neutralizing threats to save human lives.
