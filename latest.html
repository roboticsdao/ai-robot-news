Fortune — How Boston Dynamics is turning robots into a real business

Boston Dynamics is focusing on scaling operations, sales, and cost control for its robots, as outlined by former CFO and interim CEO Amanda McMaster before Rohit Prasad took over as CEO on Oct. 7. The quadruped robot Spot costs about $100,000 for the base platform and up to $300,000 with payloads, performing inspections in semiconductor, food and beverage, and public safety sectors, with Intel as a major customer. Meanwhile, the Atlas humanoid represents a test in scaling manufacturing and reducing costs, with Hyundai Mobis producing actuators—which account for roughly 70% of an Atlas bill of materials—at cost, and Hyundai committing to about 50,000 robot deployments across its facilities to provide planning and cost certainty. During her interim tenure, McMaster reorganized the company and worked with the board on leadership transitions.

总结：波士顿动力公司正专注于为其机器人业务推进规模化运营、销售及成本控制。 前首席财务官兼临时首席执行官阿曼达·麦克马斯特在罗希特·普拉萨德于10月7日接任首席执行官之前阐述了这一战略。 其四足机器人Spot的基础平台售价约为$100,000，根据负载最高可达$300,000，主要用于半导体制造、食品饮料生产及公共安全领域的巡检，英特尔是其主要客户之一。 同时，人形机器人Atlas则是对规模化制造和降本的雄心勃勃的测试，其物料清单中约有70%为执行器，将由现代摩比斯按成本价生产，而现代汽车已承诺在其设施中部署约50,000台机器人，以提供规划和成本确信度。 在担任临时首席执行官期间，麦克马斯特对公司进行了重组并参与了领导层交接工作。

UA.NEWS — US Army names five winners of humanoid robot competition — Defense News

The US Army announced five winners of the xTechHumanoid competition to assess humanoid robots in military operations: ACES Group, IHMC Robotics, Hydroplane Limited, Ras Labs, and Velocity Explorations. US Army Chief Technology Officer Alex Miller stated the program identifies tasks and capabilities suited for humanoids. Participants tested checkpoint guarding, obstacle clearing, and fire extinguishing. 总结：美国陆军宣布了评估人形机器人未来军事应用潜力的xTechHumanoid竞赛的五家获胜者，包括ACES Group、IHMC Robotics、Hydroplane Limited、Ras Labs和Velocity Explorations。 美国陆军首席技术官亚历克斯·米勒表示，该计划旨在确定最适合人形机器人的任务和功能。 在103份申请中选出了10名决赛选手，每人获得$25,000，经过实地测试后根据军事效用和技术成熟度选出了五家获胜者。 参与者测试了守卫检查站、跨越障碍和灭火等任务。 IHMC高级研究员罗伯特·格里芬指出，某些要求（例如以每小时28英里的速度移动、携带50磅负载并连续运行24小时）超出了现代机器人技术的极限。
