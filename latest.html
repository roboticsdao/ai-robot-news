note — ヒューマノイドロボティクスの到達点と産業実装の現実：日本のFA・ロボット産業が採るべき競争戦略

Global robotics is shifting from generative AI to physical AI, with US and Chinese companies racing to develop humanoid robots. Technological progress is driven by VLA models and world foundation models, enabling robots to handle natural language instructions and adapt to external disturbances. US firms like Figure AI, Agility Robotics, Tesla, and Apptronik focus on rigorous KPI metrics, while Chinese firms like UBTECH, AgiBot, and Unitree benefit from state policy and mass production. However, severe physical bottlenecks remain, including contact dynamics, thermal design, battery limitations, tactile sensor durability, and the Sim-to-Real gap, which exponentially increase development workloads. Industrial deployments in factories are largely restricted to static, local tasks rather than 24-hour continuous production.

总结：全球机器人产业正从生成式AI向具身AI转型，中美企业在人形机器人领域展开激烈竞争。 VLA模型与世界基盘模型的发展赋予机器人自主应对复杂环境的能力。 美国企业注重实施工厂实测与关键绩效指标，中国企业则在国家政策支持下推进量产与低价格化。 然而，物理世界的接触力学、热设计、电池密度、触控传感器耐久性及Sim-to-Real差距等硬件瓶颈显著增加了开发工数。 目前在工厂现场的实际应用多局限于局部的静止任务，距离全天候连续作业仍有较大差距。
