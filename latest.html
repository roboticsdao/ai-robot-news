The author views Nvidia as the simplest way to invest in physical AI primarily due to its massive data center business.

The Robot Report — Boston Dynamics gives more insight into its redesigned humanoid hand

Boston Dynamics revealed details about its new redesigned humanoid robot hand, featuring four fingers and 13 degrees of freedom, and designed directly for mass manufacturing and high-fidelity simulation to enable sim-to-real reinforcement learning. Alberto Rodriguez, director of robot behavior for Atlas, explained that the design balances dexterity, strength, ruggedness, cost, reparability, and sensing. The company dropped the pinky finger and reduced gripper size based on lessons from the Atlas body, emphasizing simplicity, modularity, and actuator maturity. The hand is built from 13 identical, completely encapsulated actuators with no fragile cables crossing joints. While previous hands could lift over 100 pounds, strength was slightly decreased to improve actuation transparency. Boston Dynamics develops hands in-house to integrate with its product roadmap. Durability is ensured through experienced hardware design, early collaboration between behavior and hardware teams, mockups, early prototypes, and rigorous validation and verification testing. Testing has involved common industrial tasks like using handheld power tools, handling cables, and fishing for screws, alongside bring-up and limit-exploration tasks.

总结：波士顿动力公司公布了其重新设计的全新人形机器人手部的更多细节，该手部拥有四个手指和13个自由度，直接面向批量生产以及高保真模拟而设计，以支持仿真到现实的强化学习。 Atlas机器人行为总监阿尔韦托·罗德里格斯解释称，该设计在灵巧性、强度、坚固性、成本、可修复性和传感之间取得了平衡。 基于Atlas躯干设计吸取的教训，该公司去掉了小指并缩小了抓手尺寸，强调了简洁性、模块化和执行器的成熟度。 该手部由13个完全封装且相同的执行器构成，没有易损电缆跨越关节。 尽管先前的双手能够举起超过100磅的重物，但其强度略有降低，以进一步提高驱动透明度。 波士顿动力公司在内部开发手部组件，以使其契合产品路线图。 耐用性通过经验丰富的硬件设计、行为与硬件团队在早期的紧密合作、模型制作、早期原型机以及严格的验证和测试来确保。 测试内容涵盖使用手持电动工具、处理电缆、抓取螺钉等常见的工业任务，以及启动任务和极限探索任务。

WRIF Rocks Detroit — Detroit Debuts Boston Dynamics’ Robot Dogs for Urban Innovation Testing

Bedrock Detroit and Boston Dynamics are testing Spot robot dogs in downtown Detroit to turn the city into a technology proving ground. Kevin Mull, Bedrock Detroit's senior director for the Office of Urban Strategy, showcased a robot named Astro during an interview. Testing begins as early as the following week, focusing on tasks such as picking up litter, including objects as small as a cigarette butt, using a robotic arm. The robot dogs function as autonomous mobile sensing platforms to inspect buildings, map tricky areas, and access unsafe locations for humans. Additional proposed uses include building inspections, security patrols in parking garages, and package handling, such as transferring packages dropped by drones on roofs into buildings. The project has been in development for six to eight months, requiring careful navigation in human-designed environments. Litter pickup is an emerging solution not yet widely on the market, making Detroit one of the first testing locations. Officials will monitor the test mode for a few months before potentially moving to a live setting. Quadruped robots have previously guarded construction sites in Cleveland and were deployed by the Taylor Police Department in Wayne County in March 2024.

总结：Bedrock底特律公司与波士顿动力公司正在底特律市中心测试Spot机器人狗，旨在将该市打造成未来技术的试验场。 Bedrock底特律城市战略办公室高级总监凯文·马尔在接受采访时展示了一台名为Astro的机器人。 测试最早将于下周开始，重点关注使用机械臂捡拾垃圾等任务，甚至能捡起小至烟头大小的物体。 这些机器人狗可作为自主移动传感平台，用于检查建筑物、测绘复杂区域以及进入人类不安全的地点。 其他提议的用途包括建筑检查、停车场等场所的安全巡逻以及包裹处理，例如将无人机投递在屋顶上的包裹运送到建筑内部。 该项目已经过静默推进了六到八个月，需要在专为人类设计环境中进行精细的导航。 捡拾垃圾是一项尚未上市的新兴解决方案，这使底特律成为波士顿动力公司测试该功能的首批地点之一。 官方将对测试模式进行数月的密切关注，如果一切顺利，机器人将进入实际运营环境。 此前，四足机器人曾在克利夫兰看守过建筑工地，韦恩县的泰勒警察局也在2024年3月部署了四足机器人用于风险评估。

NVIDIA Developer — 5 Steps to Create SimReady Assets for Robotics with Frontier AI Models | NVIDIA Technical Blog

Preparing CAD assets for robotics simulation requires configuring and validating materials, collision geometry, joints, and other physics properties using tools like NVIDIA Omniverse libraries, SimReady Foundation specifications, and agentic NVIDIA skills. Frontier AI models assist developers by interpreting reference materials and calling Omniverse tools. The workflow uses an ABB Robotics YuMi robot and a frontier AI model like GPT-6 Astra across five steps: STEP-file conversion, appearance validation, physics configuration, SimReady validation, and a final pick-and-place task using both arms and grippers in NVIDIA Isaac Sim. SimReady defines OpenUSD asset requirements for specific use cases. Missing simulation properties like collision geometry or mass and friction can cause simulation failure. Key supporting technologies include OpenUSD, SimReady Foundation, NVIDIA Omniverse libraries, NVIDIA Isaac Sim, and NVIDIA Omniverse AI Agent skills. Prerequisites involve installing Isaac Sim 6.1, creating a reference folder with specifications, STEP files, and media, installing a frontier AI model, and launching Codex CLI in the Isaac Sim directory. The workflow uses a remote Python server to connect the agent to Isaac Sim for USD conversion and import.

总结：为机器人仿真准备CAD资产需要配置和验证材料、碰撞几何体、关节以及其他物理属性，所使用的工具包括NVIDIA Omniverse库、SimReady Foundation规范以及智能NVIDIA技能。 前沿人工智能模型通过解释参考资料并代表开发者调用Omniverse工具来协助开发。 该工作流使用ABB机器人YuMi机器人和诸如GPT-6 Astra之类的前沿AI模型，通过五个步骤完成：STEP文件转换、外观验证、物理配置、SimReady验证，以及最后在NVIDIA Isaac Sim中使用双臂和夹爪完成抓取放置任务。 SimReady为特定的仿真用例定义了OpenUSD资产要求。 缺少碰撞几何体、质量和摩擦力等仿真属性可能会导致仿真失败。 支持该工作流的关键技术包括OpenUSD、SimReady Foundation、NVIDIA Omniverse库、NVIDIA Isaac Sim以及NVIDIA Omniverse AI Agent技能。 前期准备工作包括安装Isaac Sim 6.1，在Isaac Sim目录中创建一个包含规范、STEP文件及媒体的参考文件夹，安装前沿AI模型，并在Isaac Sim目录中启动Codex CLI。 该工作流利用远程Python服务器使智能体与运行中的Isaac Sim应用进行Python通信，以引导转换为OpenUSD并导入到Isaac Sim中。
