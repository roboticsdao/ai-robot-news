FOX 2 Detroit — US humanoid robot production could hit 400,000 units by 2030, JPMorgan says

A report by JPMorgan states that U. S. humanoid robot production is expected to reach between 300,000 and 400,000 units by 2030 and could grow to 1.5 million by 2035. The investment bank attributes this adoption in manufacturing, logistics, and warehousing to labor shortages, improving economics, and rising production capacity as the industry transitions from demonstrations to broader commercial deployment. JPMorgan also noted that Trump administration determinations regarding national security risks from imported robotics could lead to tariffs or other policy measures to boost domestic production. Deployment could be constrained by hardware availability, AI training data, computing capacity, and manufacturing output, with most U. S. developers targeting annual volumes of 10,000 to 30,000 units, excluding Tesla. Key leading U. S. platforms identified include Tesla's Optimus, Agility Robotics' Digit, Boston Dynamics' Atlas, Figure AI's humanoid robots, and Apptronik's Apollo.

总结：根据摩根大通发布的一份报告，预计到2030年，美国人形机器人产量将达到300,000至400,000台，并可能在2035年增长至150万台。 该投资银行表示，随着行业从展示阶段转向更广泛的商业部署，劳动力短缺、经济效益的改善以及产能的提升正在推动其在制造、物流和仓储领域的应用。 摩根大通还指出，特朗普政府认定进口机器人和工业机械构成国家安全风险，这可能会引发关税或其他旨在促进本土生产的政策措施。 该报告称，部署可能会受到硬件可用性、AI训练数据、计算能力和制造产出的限制，除特斯拉外，大多数美国人形机器人开发商的目标年产量在10,000至30,000台之间。 报告指出的美国领先人形机器人平台包括特斯拉的Optimus、Agility Robotics的Digit、波士顿动力的Atlas、Figure AI的人形机器人以及Apptronik的Apollo。

Fierce Sensors — Physical AI depends on sensor redundancy. Does it need Nvidia redundancy too?

Physical AI relies on sensor redundancy and computing infrastructure to process data from cameras, lidar, IMUs, and tactile sensors. Nvidia provides computing backbones, simulation environments, and software frameworks like Isaac Lab, Isaac Sim, and Omniverse, extending to Boston Dynamics' electric Atlas humanoid, which uses the Jetson Thor computing platform. Boston Dynamics and Nvidia collaborate on functional safety, security architectures, learning, and computer-vision pipelines, though Boston Dynamics retains its own whole-body and manipulation controllers. Other semiconductor companies such as Qualcomm, Intel, and NXP are also maneuvering for the physical-AI market across humanoids, autonomous mobile robots, and industrial automation.

总结：物理AI依赖于传感器冗余以及用于处理来自激光雷达、摄像头、IMU和触觉传感器数据的计算基础设施。 Nvidia为许多此类机器提供计算骨干、仿真环境以及软件框架（如Isaac Lab、Isaac Sim和Omniverse），其技术已延伸至波士顿动力的电驱动Atlas人形机器人，该机器人采用了Jetson Thor计算平台。 波士顿动力与Nvidia在功能安全、安全架构、学习和计算机视觉管道方面展开合作，不过波士顿动力仍保留其自身的全身和抓取控制器。 与此同时，高通、英特尔和恩智浦等其他半导体公司也在争夺涵盖人形机器人、自主移动机器人和工业自动化等领域的物理AI市场。

Supply Chain Digital Magazine — Hyundai's Boston Dynamics Launches Stretch Gen 2 robot

Boston Dynamics, a wholly-owned subsidiary of Hyundai Motor Group, unveiled Stretch Gen 2, an autonomous mobile robot designed for warehouse activities such as trailer unloading. Built from data across more than 100,000 industrial operating hours and tens of millions of handled cases, the platform features hardware upgrades including heavy-duty automotive-grade connectors, modular maintenance design, real-time diagnostics, and an upgraded vacuum-based gripper head. Key specifications include 1,000 cases per hour throughput, an 81% internal cabling reduction, 1-hour swerve drive replacement, dual-shift battery endurance with a 75-minute fast-charging cycle, and zero-programming vision deployment. Hyundai acquired full ownership of Boston Dynamics in August 2026, and has advanced other robotics initiatives including the Atlas humanoid robot production at its Boston hub and plans for a Robotics Innovation Hub in Georgia.

总结：现代汽车集团的全资子公司波士顿动力公司推出了Stretch Gen 2，这是一款专为仓库活动（包括拖车卸货）设计的下一代自动移动机器人。 该平台基于超过10万小时的工业运行时间和数千万件处理货物的运营数据构建，引入了硬件、电气和结构升级，以最大化设备可用性并提高卸货门吞吐量。 其核心特性包括每小时1,000箱的吞吐量、内部布线减少81%、一小时内完成转向驱动装置更换、支持双班次续航及75分钟快充至90%容量，以及零编程视觉部署。 现代汽车于2026年8月收购了波士顿动力的全部所有权，并推进了波士顿制造枢纽的Atlas人形机器人生产以及佐治亚州机器人创新枢纽的建设计划。

Agility Robotics became the first publicly listed U. S. humanoid robotics company after completing its business combination with Churchill Capital Corp XI, with its common stock beginning to trade on the Nasdaq exchange under the ticker AGLT. The offering generated about $465 million in capital before expenses, which Agility plans to use to accelerate the commercialization of its Digit humanoid robot. Gartner analyst Bill Ray noted that the IPO is politically significant for the U. S. in the robotics market given Chinese manufacturing leadership. ABI Research analyst George Chowdhury stated that the offering reflects confidence in the commercial potential of robotics and helps differentiate humanoids from the broader tech AI-data center market. Ray highlighted Agility's industrial manufacturing and logistics focus as more commercially practical than developers targeting domestic applications, noting that Tesla's Optimus cannot currently be bought. Chowdhury described Agility as the most commercially mature humanoid company targeting enterprise markets, supported by distribution networks, progressive pricing strategies, safety and regulatory cooperation, and about 100 Digit robots at work today. However, Ray pointed out challenges in integrating humanoids into existing automated warehouse workflows without costly infrastructure changes, while Chowdhury noted that Agility's valuation remains below rivals because investors often prefer companies promising a fully automated future over those commercializing technology today.

总结：Agility Robotics在与前Churchill Capital Corp XI完成业务合并后，成为美国首家上市的人形机器人公司，其普通股开始在纳斯达克交易所交易，股票代码为AGLT. 该公司表示，此次发行在扣除费用前筹集了约46500万美元资金，Agility计划将其用于加速其Digit人形机器人的商业化。 Gartner分析师Bill Ray指出，鉴于中国在机器人制造领域的领先地位，此次IPO对美国在机器人市场的地位具有重要的政治意义。 ABI Research分析师George Chowdhury同样表示，此次公开募股反映出对机器人技术商业潜力的日益信心，并有助于将人形机器人与面临市场动荡的整体科技AI数据中心市场区分开来。 Ray强调，Agility专注于制造和物流，比针对家庭应用的开发商更具商业实用性，并指出目前无法买到特斯拉的Optimus等机器人。 Chowdhury称Agility是进军企业市场的商业化程度最高的人形机器人公司，拥有分销网络、渐进式定价策略、安全与监管机构合作，且目前约有100台Digit机器人在工作中。 不过，Ray指出将人形机器人融入现有自动化仓库工作流而不改变基础设施面临挑战，而Chowdhury指出Agility的估值仍低于一些知名竞争对手，因为投资者更青睐承诺全自动未来的公司，而不是今天就在进行技术商业化的公司。
