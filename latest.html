The Verge — Tesla’s Optimus robot is going through growing pains

Tesla is encountering manufacturing difficulties in scaling production of its Optimus humanoid robot to 20,000 units per week. According to The Information, Tesla produced several hundred robots a week last month after repurposing its Model S and Model X production lines. The strategy has created snags, such as trouble lining up parts precisely, because Optimus parts are much smaller and require greater precision than car parts. Additionally, the V3 Optimus robots still rely on humans to manually assemble their hands and forearms, which contain over 100 screws and small components. The new robots are lighter and feature more cameras than previous versions, but they currently lack a sensing glove in development that will improve durability by allowing touch sensors to be replaced without swapping the entire hand. Although CEO Elon Musk has promised Optimus will be Tesla's biggest product of all time, the robots are currently used only internally for specific tasks without remote teleoperation. Tesla reportedly plans to initially lease the robots and use collected training data to improve them.

总结：特斯拉在将其Optimus人形机器人的产量提升至每周20,000台的目标时遇到了制造难题。 据The Information报道，在今年早些时候将Model S和Model X的生产线重新用于Optimus之后，特斯拉上个月生产了“每周数百台机器人”。 然而，这一策略引发了制造上的阻碍，例如难以精确对齐零件，因为Optimus的零件比汽车零件更小，且需要更高的装配精度。 此外，现阶段生产的V3版本Optimus机器人仍然需要人工手动组装其手部和前臂，其中包含100多个螺丝和其他小部件。 新版本的机器人比以往更轻且配备了更多摄像头，但目前仍缺少正在开发中的“感应手套”，该手套原本可以通过单独更换触控传感器而不是整个手掌来提升耐用性。 尽管特斯拉CEO埃隆·马斯克曾豪言Optimus将成为特斯拉有史以来最大的产品，但这些机器人目前仅在内部特定区域用于特定任务，并未实现通用或远程遥操作。 据报道，特斯拉还计划最初采取租赁而非出售的方式，并利用收集到的训练数据来改进机器人。

The Motley Fool — Tesla vs. Nvidia: Which Physical AI Stock Has the Bigger Robotics Payoff by 2030?

Humanoid robots are increasingly viewed as future co-workers, and investors are evaluating whether Tesla or Nvidia will achieve a greater robotics payoff by 2030. While Tesla pursues a bold vision with its Optimus humanoid robot to perform unsafe, repetitive, or boring tasks, its success depends on multiple complex factors, including vehicle demand, self-driving regulations, energy projects, and factory execution while juggling automotive and robotaxi priorities. In contrast, Nvidia is pursuing a simpler approach by supplying the foundational technology, chips, and tools for the broader robotics industry. At its 2026 GTC conference, Nvidia highlighted partners building production-scale physical AI on its full-stack platform, which includes computing hardware, open models like Cosmos world models, simulation frameworks like Isaac Lab, and compact robot computer modules such as Jetson Thor and Jetson T4000. If Nvidia's strategy succeeds, it stands to generate consistent revenue streams from chips, software, and AI models as the robotics industry grows through 2030.

总结：人形机器人正逐渐被视为未来的同事，投资者正在评估特斯拉与英伟达谁能在2030年前获得更大的机器人业务回报。 特斯拉围绕其Optimus人形机器人制定了宏伟的愿景，旨在执行不安全、重复或枯燥的任务，但其成功取决于多个复杂因素，包括汽车需求、自动驾驶监管、能源项目以及在兼顾汽车和无人驾驶出租车业务时的工厂执行力。 相比之下，英伟达采取了更简单的战略，即为整个机器人行业提供基础技术、芯片和工具。 在2026年的GTC大会上，英伟达强调了合作伙伴正在其全栈平台上构建生产规模的物理人工智能，该平台涵盖计算硬件、Cosmos世界模型等开放模型、Isaac Lab等仿真框架，以及Jetson Thor和Jetson T4000等紧凑型机器人计算机模块。 如果英伟达的战略成功，随着机器人行业在2030年前不断增长，它将从芯片、软件和AI模型中获得持续的收入来源。

AIsmiley — ファナック、産業用ロボットのフィジカルAIシステム「AI溶接エージェント」開発。溶接条件とロボット動作を自動生成

Fanuc has developed the 'AI Welding Agent,' a physical AI system for industrial robots that automatically generates welding conditions and robot movements by reading component blueprints, thereby enabling actual welding without manual setup or teaching. Developed in collaboration with Google using the enterprise generative AI platform 'Gemini Enterprise,' the system aims to address the global shortage of skilled welding workers. Scheduled to start shipping at the end of December 2026, the product ensures high security as data is not reused for training other users' AI. It utilizes the tablet teaching pendant built-in camera of the CRX robot for blueprint reading without requiring additional devices, and will be available via a subscription model.

总结：发那科（FANUC）开发了面向工业机器人的物理AI系统“AI溶接エージェント”（AI焊接代理），可通过读取图纸自动生成溶接条件和机器人动作，从而在无需设定与示教作业的情况下实现实际焊接。 该产品旨在利用最新AI技术弥补全球溶接作业人员的不足，在与谷歌的协作下基于企业级生成式AI“Gemini Enterprise”开发。 它能够读取零件设计图并自动生成电流电压等溶接条件及机器人动作，操作员还可进行微调。 在安全性方面，“Gemini Enterprise”可保护图纸等数据不被用于其他用户的AI训练。 该系统利用CRX平板内置相机读取图纸，无需额外设备，并将以订阅形式于2026年12月末开始出货。

projectdesign.jp — ものづくり日本大賞優秀賞 製造現場で独自AIとロボットによるダイカスト外観検査の自動化を実現

HACARUS won the Excellence Award at the 10th Monodzukuri Nippon Grand Award for automating appearance inspections of die-cast products using robots and proprietary AI. Die-cast products are traditionally difficult to automate due to shape and surface variations. HACARUS's AI utilizes a 'good-product learning' approach, training on standard high-quality products to detect anomalies rather than relying on collecting all possible defect patterns, allowing it to adapt to changing environmental conditions through lightweight re-learning. The solution integrates AI software with third-party cameras and robots. Following its initial implementation, the company is expanding this application to other difficult-to-automate areas such as rubber products and food packaging, thereby helping manufacturers alleviate labor shortages and stabilize quality control.

总结：株式会社HACARUS凭借“利用机器人和独自AI实现适应ダイカスト（压铸）的外观检查自动化”项目，在第10届日本制造大赏中荣获优秀奖。 由于压铸产品形状和表面状态容易产生差异，其外观检查长期被认为难以实现自动化。 HACARUS的AI采用了“良品学习”的方法，即通过学习合格产品的标准状态来检测异常，而非收集所有不良品模式，并能通过轻量化和高速再学习适应现场环境的变化。 该方案将AI软件与外部相机和机器人等设备结合。 继压铸领域之后，其应用范围正在扩展至橡胶制品和食品包装等同样难以通过简单规则进行判定的人工检查领域，从而帮助制造企业应对人手不足并稳定质量管理。
