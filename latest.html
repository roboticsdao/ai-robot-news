The Robot Report — Boston Dynamics adds wheels to Atlas humanoid to test whole-body control

Boston Dynamics released a video showing its Atlas humanoid robot using wheels attached to its legs to move around, combining classical model-based motor control with whole-body reinforcement learning. Controls engineer Andy Wu explained that the wheeled Atlas is a research testbed rather than a product, designed to test actuation limits and unstructured environment adaptability. The system was trained entirely in simulation and executed in real life by mounting an off-the-shelf scooter rim onto Atlas' existing ankle actuator using a custom adapter. The experiment demonstrates that Atlas can smoothly switch between fast rolling on flat terrain and articulated legged motion over irregular surfaces. The company is preparing to ramp up Atlas testing at Hyundai facilities as it continues developing the humanoid's capabilities.

总结：波士顿动力发布了一段视频，展示其Atlas人形机器人利用安装在腿部的轮子进行移动，该实验将经典的基于模型的电机控制与全身强化学习结合起来。 控制工程师安迪·吴解释说，带轮子的Atlas是一个研究测试平台而非产品，旨在测试驱动极限和非结构化环境适应性。 该系统在模拟中训练并在现实中执行，仅通过定制适配器将现成的滑板车轮辋安装到Atlas现有的脚踝执行器上。 该实验表明，Atlas能够在平坦地形上的快速滚动与不规则表面上的关节式腿部运动之间平稳切换。 该公司正准备在现代的设施中加强Atlas的测试，同时继续开发该人形机器人的各项功能。

High Plains Journal — AGCO, Agility Robotics plan humanoid robot pilot at Kansas plant

AGCO and Agility Robotics have signed a commercial agreement to deploy artificial intelligence-powered humanoid robots in manufacturing operations. The United States pilot, scheduled to begin in 2027, will take place at AGCO's Hesston, Kansas, production facility. Agility Robotics' Digit robot will perform assembly and production tasks, including sorting, stacking parts, and placing totes in storage, to automate repetitive and physically demanding work. Digit utilizes artificial intelligence to adapt to changing workflows, while Agility Robotics' cloud-based Agility Arc platform manages robot fleets for integration without extensive infrastructure changes.

总结：AGCO与Agility Robotics签署了一项商业协议，将在制造业务中部署人工智能驱动的人工智能人形机器人。 这项美国试点计划定于2027年开始，将在AGCO位于堪萨斯州黑斯顿的生产设施进行。 Agility Robotics的Digit机器人将执行装配和生产任务，包括分拣、堆叠零件以及将周转箱放入存储区，以实现重复性和体力消耗大的工作的自动化。 Digit利用人工智能来适应不断变化的工作流程，而Agility Robotics基于云的Agility Arc平台则负责管理机器人机群，以便在无需进行大规模基础设施更改的情况下进行集成。
