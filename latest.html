Robotics & Automation News — Boston Dynamics launches next-generation Stretch robot with major reliability upgrades

Boston Dynamics introduced Stretch Gen 2, the next iteration of its autonomous mobile trailer unloading robot, designed with major hardware and structural enhancements to maximize uptime and reduce maintenance in high-volume logistics. Drawing on operational data from more than 100,000 operating hours and tens of millions of moved boxes, the updated model features automotive-grade connectors, an improved gripper, continuous rotation joints, and an 81 percent reduction in internal cabling. Stretch Gen 2 unloads up to 1,000 cases per hour, supports a multipick feature, operates for two shifts on a single battery charge, and includes pre-trained vision capabilities.

总结：波士顿动力推出了新一代自主移动拖车卸货机器人Stretch Gen 2，该机器人在硬件和结构上进行了重大升级，旨在最大限度地提高高吞吐量物流环境中的正常运行时间并简化维护。 基于超过100,000个运行小时和数千万个纸箱的数据，该型号采用了汽车级连接器、升级的抓手、连续旋转关节，并将内部电缆减少了81%。 Stretch Gen 2每小时可卸载多达1,000个箱子，支持多重抓取功能，单次充电电池可续航两个班次，并配备了预训练的视觉系统。

사이언스모니터 — Nvidia Isaac and GR00T: The Race for Robot AI Platform Dominance - Science Monitor

Nvidia provides a full-stack robotics ecosystem encompassing simulation tools, foundation models, and chips, positioning itself against competitors like Google DeepMind. Its robotics strategy operates across training, simulation, and deployment. The brain component is Isaac GR00T N1.7, a 3-billion-parameter vision-language-action model for humanoids that integrates Cosmos, a world model predicting physical changes and generating synthetic data. Virtual training occurs through Isaac Sim, Isaac Lab 3.0, and the Newton physics engine. Isaac OS serves as the open-source runtime, Jetson Thor acts as the real-time inference chip, and Halos provides the safety layer. Unveiled on April 17, Isaac GR00T N1.7 combines the Cosmos-Reason2-2B model with a diffusion transformer. Unlike predecessor N1.6, which relied on remote teleoperation data, N1.7 was trained on 20,854 hours of first-person human video, which Nvidia stated more than doubled the average task completion rate. Validated on robots such as Unitree's G1 and AgiBot's Genie 1, the model is released under a commercial-use license. Additionally, Isaac Lab 3.0, released on October 21, supports the Newton physics engine, which was jointly developed by Nvidia, Disney Research, and Google DeepMind and contributed to the Linux Foundation in September 2025.

总结：Nvidia提供涵盖仿真工具、基础模型和芯片的全栈机器人生态系统，与Google DeepMind等竞争对手展开角力。 其机器人战略贯穿训练、仿真和部署三个互锁阶段。 大脑核心为Isaac GR00T N1.7，这是一个拥有30亿参数的人形视觉-语言-动作模型，并辅以预测物理环境变化的宇宙世界模型Cosmos以生成合成数据。 虚拟训练通过Isaac Sim、Isaac Lab 3.0和Newton物理引擎进行。 Isaac OS作为开源运行时，Jetson Thor是处理实时推理的芯片，Halos则提供安全层。 于4月17日亮相的Isaac GR00T N1.7结合了Cosmos-Reason2-2B视觉语言模型和生成电机指令的扩散Transformer。 与依赖远程操作数据的先前版本N1.6不同，N1.7接受了20,854小时人类第一人称视频的训练，Nvidia称这使机器人的平均任务完成率提高了一倍以上。 该模型在宇树科技的G1和智元机器人的Genie 1等机器人上得到验证，并以允许商业使用的许可证发布。 此外，10月21日正式发布的Isaac Lab 3.0支持由Nvidia、Disney Research和Google DeepMind于2025年9月共同向Linux Foundation捐赠的开源物理引擎Newton。

36Kr — MIIT Unveils 5 Humanoid Robot Industry Standards | Intelligent Capability Classification & Training Site Construction Standards Explained

On November 16, 2026, the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology released Announcement No. 30 of 2026, officially issuing and implementing five standards under the Humanoid Robot and Embodied Intelligence Standardization Technical Committee. These standards cover intelligent capability evaluation, key components, and training of humanoid robots. Specifically, the five newly enforced standards include SJ/T 12353—2026 for training site construction technical specifications, SJ/T 12354—2026 for training data management requirements, SJ/T 12355—2026 for intelligent capability classification, SJ/T 12356—2026 for integrated electric drive joint technical specifications, and SJ/T 12357—2026 for dexterous hand technical specifications. Among them, the intelligent capability classification and training site construction standards address critical industry challenges by establishing a six-level evaluation path from L0 to L5 across four core dimensions—perception and cognition, decision-making and learning, execution performance, and collaboration and interaction—to provide a unified measuring ruler and standardized infrastructure.

总结：2026年11月16日，工业和信息化部发布2026年第30号公告，正式发布并实施人形机器人与具身智能标准化技术委员会集中管理的人形机器人领域五项标准。 这批标准涵盖智能能力评估、关键零部件及训练等三个主要方向。 具体五项标准包括《人形机器人 训练场建设技术规范》（SJ/T 12353—2026）、《人形机器人 训练数据管理要求》（SJ/T 12354—2026）、《人形机器人 智能能力分级》（SJ/T 12355—2026）、《人形机器人 一体化电动关节技术规范》（SJ/T 12356—2026）以及《人形机器人与具身智能 灵巧手技术规范》（SJ/T 12357—2026），均自发布之日起施行。 其中智能能力分级和训练场建设标准尤为关键，前者通过感知与认知、决策与学习、执行表现、协作与交互四个核心维度将智能能力划分为从L0到L5的六个等级，解决了评估难题，后者则规范了训练场基础设施建设。

Tech Times — GlobalFoundries to Test Boston Dynamics Atlas Humanoid Robot in Chip Fabs, With No Task Named

GlobalFoundries will begin on-site trials of Boston Dynamics' Atlas humanoid robot at its chip fabs in early 2027, making it the first chipmaker to test Atlas, as announced by the two companies on November 17. The trial involves no named tasks, fabs, or robot counts. Meanwhile, GlobalFoundries has utilized Boston Dynamics' four-legged inspection robot, Spot, across its sites including Burlington, Vermont, Malta, New York, Dresden, Germany, and Singapore. At the Singapore site, two Spot robots have walked nearly 900 kilometers and completed over 90,000 inspections since April with a 99% mission success rate. GlobalFoundries' Chief Manufacturing Officer Pradip Singh noted that Spot collects data across thousands of inspection points for predictive maintenance. Spot integrates sensors, computer-vision models from Levatas, and Google DeepMind's Gemini Robotics-ER 1.6 via Boston Dynamics' AIVI-Learning software. Deploying robots in cleanrooms, which often run at ISO Class 7 or 5 levels requiring strict particle limits, could present new testing conditions for Atlas.

总结：GlobalFoundries（GF）与波士顿动力于11月17日宣布，将于2027年初在其芯片工厂开始对波士顿动力的Atlas人形机器人进行现场测试，使其成为首家测试Atlas的芯片制造商。 该测试目前未指定具体任务、工厂或机器人数量。 与此同时，GlobalFoundries已在其位于佛蒙特州伯灵顿、纽约州马耳他、德国德累斯顿和新加坡的工厂部署了波士顿动力的Spot四足巡检机器人。 根据公司公布的数据，自4月以来，新加坡站点的两台Spot机器人已在亚fab区域步行近900公里（约559英里），完成了90,000多次检查，任务成功率为99%。 GlobalFoundries首席制造官Pradip Singh表示，Spot通过收集数千个检查点的数据，将维护模式从被动转变为预测性维护。 Spot结合了传感器、Levatas的计算机视觉模型以及波士顿动力AIVI-Learning软件中的Google DeepMind Gemini Robotics-ER 1.6。 洁净室通常运行在ISO 7级或5级标准下并对微粒有限制，这可能会对Atlas的洁净室适应性进行测试。
