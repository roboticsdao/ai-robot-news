Engineering.com — Boston Dynamics expands robotics partnership with GlobalFoundries

Boston Dynamics and GlobalFoundries (GF) have expanded their robotics partnership, with GF deploying Spot robots for autonomous enterprise asset management across global semiconductor fabs in Malta, New York; Burlington, Vermont; Dresden, Germany; and Singapore. In addition, GF will become the first customer outside the automotive industry to test Boston Dynamics' Atlas humanoid robot as part of its early adopter program and skills development workshop beginning in early 2027. GF has utilized Spot at its Burlington site for more than four years and recently expanded the program to Singapore, where two Spot robots have traveled nearly 900 kilometers and completed more than 90,000 inspections in sub-fab areas since April, achieving a 99% mission success rate.
