C. is set to open the "At Home in Space" exhibit later this week, exploring the 50-year history of humans living and working in space. Robotics Tomorrow — The Humanoid Robotics Funding Race: What the Money Tells Us About Commercial Readiness

The humanoid robotics sector has attracted nearly $11.7 billion in disclosed capital, yet the most heavily funded companies remain at the pilot or prototype stage, while commercially available humanoids originate from firms that raised the least. Data from The Robotic Life's funding tracker as of September 1, 2026, indicates that six companies raised over $500 million each, holding $7.6 billion (about 65% of the sector total), while four companies with humanoids in general commercial availability raised a combined $1.03 billion (under 9%). Major fundraising includes Figure AI at $2.34 billion cumulative capital with a $39 billion valuation following a Series C exceeding $1 billion in September 2025, Apptronik holding $1.1 billion at a $5.5 billion valuation, NEURA Robotics at $1.6 billion total funding with a $7 billion valuation backed by Tether, NVIDIA, Amazon, Qualcomm, Bosch, and Schaeffler, and UBTECH raising over $1 billion before listing in December 2023 with Walker S units active at BYD, Foxconn, Zeekr, and Audi FAW plants. Meanwhile, Unitree achieved commercial shipping and a public listing on the Shanghai Stock Exchange on roughly $155 million in pre-IPO private capital. Tesla and Xiaomi's internally funded corporate programs of over $20 billion and $10 billion respectively are excluded from the external capital figures.

总结：人形机器人行业已吸引了近117亿美元的公开融资金额，然而获得最多资金支持的公司仍处于试验或原型阶段，实现全面商业化的人机反而是融资最少的企业。 根据《机器人生活》截至2026年9月1日的融资追踪数据，有6家公司各自筹集了超过5亿美元，共计持有76亿美元（约占行业总资金的65%），而拥有实现全面商业化人形机器人的4家公司总共筹集了$1.03 billion（不足9%）。 主要的融资案例包括：Figure AI在2025年9月完成超过$10 billion的C轮融资后，累计资本达到23.4亿美元，估值390亿美元；Apptronik拥有11亿美元资金，估值55亿美元；NEURA Robotics总融资额达到16亿美元，估值70亿美元，投资方包括Tether、英伟达、亚马逊、高通、博世和舍弗勒；优必选在2023年12月上市前私下筹集了超过10亿美元，其Walker S机器人在比亚迪、富士康、极氪和奥迪一汽工厂投入运行。 与此同时，宇树科技凭借约1.55亿美元的IPO前私人资本实现了商业出货并在上海证券交易所上市。 特斯拉和小米内部资助的企业项目分别超过200亿美元和100亿美元，未计入外部资本数据中。

Tesla plans to use its Optimus humanoid robots to plug in Robotaxis to charge while developing a more automated wireless charging system. Tesla has already secured FCC approval for wireless Cybercab charging and plans an Austin Robotaxi hub with 80 wireless chargers, but wireless charging will take time to implement. Tesla recently began early Optimus production at its Fremont factory with first corporate customer deliveries planned for 2027. In addition to charging, Tesla engineers indicated Optimus might eventually service the fleet and assist in the delivery space, forming part of a physical AI trifecta alongside Cybercab and Robovan.
