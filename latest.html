Yahoo!ニュース — 4足歩行ロボット 栗拾いを自動化 10秒に1個のペースで農作業軽減 高知大など2年後の実用化を目指す（日本農業新聞）

高知工科大学和高知大学在高知県四万十町进行了使用4足歩行ロボットSpot収穫栗的实验。 研究由高知工科大学の栗原徹教授らの研究グループが栗収穫の作業負担を軽減するため進めている。 Spotは本体のカメラでイガ栗を発見し近づき、アームに付けたカメラで検出してつかみ、上部のカゴに入れるという流れで、10秒以内に1個のペースで収穫する。 当日は傾斜5度ほどの園地で実証し20分で20個ほど収穫した。 研究グループは2年後の実用化を目指しており、野生動物を誘引する落下果実の回収への転用も視野に入れている。

总结：高知工科大学和高知大学在高知县四万十町使用四足步行机器人Spot进行了收栗实验。 该项研究由高知工科大学栗原彻教授等研究小组为减轻农忙体力负担而推进。 Spot通过本体相机发现栗子并靠近，利用手臂所附相机检测并抓取，放入上方筐中，整个过程保持10秒以内收集一个栗子的速度。 当天在倾斜约5度的果园进行了实证测试，20分钟内收获了约20个栗子。 研究小组目标在两年后实现实用化，并指出该技术未来也可用于回收易引诱野生动物的落地果实。

supercarblondie.com — World's first large-scale robot theme park debuts in China with hundreds of humanoid machines

China has opened the world's first large-scale embodied intelligence theme park at the Hengqin Chimelong resort in Guangdong, featuring more than 300 humanoid robots working alongside human visitors. Officially opened on September 24 at Chimelong Spaceship, the park offers over 100 interactive experiences. The robots, made primarily by AGIBOT, serve as tour guides, shopping assistants, entertainers, science educators, and hotel receptors, engaging visitors in games, dances, and science education. AGIBOT and Chimelong established the park as a real-world testing ground for embodied AI. The park currently requires roughly one human operator for every three robots, with plans to reduce that to one for every ten, and AGIBOT plans to increase the robot count to more than 1,000 by the end of 2026.

总结：中国在广东横琴长隆度假区开设了全球首个大规模具身智能主题公园，拥有300多台人形机器人与人类游客共同工作。 该公园于9月24日在长隆飞船乐园正式开业，提供100多种互动体验。 这些机器人主要由中国机器人公司智元机器人（AGIBOT）制造，担任导游、购物助手、艺人、科普教育员和酒店接待员等职务，与游客进行游戏、舞蹈和科学教育等互动。 智元机器人与长隆将该公园打造成具身智能的现实测试场。 该公园目前大约每3台机器人配备1个人类操作员，目标是最终减少到每10台机器人配备1名操作员，且智元机器人计划在2026年底前将园内的机器人数量增加到1000台以上。

The Star — AgiBot pledges potential RM1bil investment after PM Anwar meeting

Chinese humanoid robotics firm AgiBot Innovation has pledged a potential investment of up to RM1bil in Malaysia by 2035, aiming to create 5,000 job opportunities. This commitment followed a meeting between AgiBot founder, chairman and chief executive officer Deng Taihua and Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim in Hangzhou, China. The investment builds on existing collaborations, including the AI Living @ i-City project, a 500-home development scheduled for completion in 2030 that integrates domestic robots. Earlier this year, over 100 AgiBot robots were deployed at the AI World Experience Centre in Shah Alam. Deng and i-City founder Tan Sri Lim Kim Hong have collaborated to explore how embodied intelligence can serve changing household needs, and FIRDAUS & Associates has appointed three top property agencies to market the residential concept.

总结：中国人形机器人公司智元机器人（AgiBot Innovation）承诺到2035年在马来西亚进行高达10亿令吉的潜在投资，并创造5000个就业机会。 这一承诺是在智元机器人创始人、董事长兼首席执行官邓泰华与马来西亚总理安瓦尔在中国杭州会晤后达成的。 该投资建立在现有合作基础之上，包括定于2030年完工、旨在整合家用机器人的500户住宅开发项目AI Living @ i-City。 今年早些时候，100多台智元机器人已被部署在莎阿南的AI世界体验中心。 邓泰华与i-City创始人林金鸿合作探讨具身智能如何服务于不断变化的家庭需求，而FIRDAUS & Associates已指定三家马来西亚顶级房地产机构来营销这一住宅概念。

Mashable — First humanoid robot surgeon performs live surgery

A video from Mashable visits UCSD's Center for the Future of Surgery to explore the first ever live surgery using humanoid robots. The video covers chapters including the introduction of humanoid robots in surgery, the need for remote and robotic surgery, challenges of adapting humanoid robots, testing the teleoperation setup, why live animal surgeries are necessary, performance and thermal management issues, and the potential for AI autonomy in surgery. Host and scriptwriter Jesse Orrall, along with producers Jon Gomez and Owen Poole, document the setup involving camera work by Owen Poole and video editing by Jon Gomez and Owen Poole, with thumbnail design by Zooey Liao.

总结：Mashable团队访问了加州大学圣迭戈分校（UCSD）的外科未来中心，了解首次使用人形机器人的现场外科手术。 视频涵盖了外科手术中引入人形机器人的介绍、远程和机器人手术的需求、适应人形机器人的挑战、测试远程操作设置、为什么需要活体动物手术、性能与热管理问题，以及手术中AI自主性的潜力等章节。 主持人兼编剧杰西·奥拉尔（Jesse Orrall）与制片人乔恩·戈麦斯（Jon Gomez）和欧文·普尔（Owen Poole）共同记录了这一设置，其中欧文·普尔负责摄影，乔恩·戈麦斯和欧文·普尔负责视频剪辑，缩略图设计则由祖伊·廖（Zooey Liao）完成。

Engineering.com — RoboParty unveils RP1 open-source humanoid robot

RoboParty unveiled its RP1 open-source humanoid robot at IROS 2026 for its global debut. RP1 features up to 160 N·m peak joint torque and supports dynamic motion, disturbance recovery, and embodied AI development. The robot relies on PartyOS and the UFO unsupervised reinforcement learning framework, enabling it to rapidly adjust posture and recover balance when pushed or kicked, as demonstrated in the 'Kick Me' interactive exhibition. RoboParty previously open-sourced RPO in January 2026, which received over 2,500 GitHub stars. RP1 is targeted at researchers, educators, developers, and embodied AI teams to provide a full-stack open-source bipedal robotics system that allows hardware, motion control, simulation environments, SDKs, training tools, and PartyOS to be modified, trained, and extended. RoboParty plans to announce further details of the open-source roadmap in October 2026, with software and hardware releases and a mass-production program following in Q4.

总结：RoboParty在IROS 2026上全球首发了RP1开源人形机器人。 RP1具备最高160 N·m的关节峰值扭矩，支持动态运动、抗扰动恢复和具身智能开发。 该机器人依托RoboParty的开源人形机器人研发基础PartyOS以及UFO无监督强化学习框架，在展会的“Kick Me”互动演示中，参观者可以直接施加外部干扰，测试机器人在被推或踢时的姿态调整和平衡恢复能力。 在此之前，RoboParty于2026年1月完全开源了首个项目RPO，获得了超过2,500个GitHub星标。 RP1旨在为研究人员、教育工作者、开发者和具身智能团队提供一个全栈开源的双足人形机器人系统，使其能够在实际开发工作流程中使用、修改、训练和扩展。 RoboParty计划在2026年10月公布RP1开源路线图的更多细节，并在第四季度晚些时候推出更广泛的软硬件发布和量产计划。
