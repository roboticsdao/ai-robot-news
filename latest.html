Interesting Engineering — Boston Dynamics' Stretch Gen 2 cuts robot repair time to just one hour

Boston Dynamics unveiled Stretch Gen 2, an autonomous robot designed to unload trailers and reduce maintenance downtime in high-volume logistics operations. The upgraded robot features 81 percent less internal cabling, a redesigned gripper, improved sensing, and modular components to prevent mechanical and electrical faults. According to Vice President Brian Donnelly, the Gen 2 platform is engineered for easier servicing, reducing a complete swerve drive replacement from an entire day to about one hour. Running on a redesigned computing platform that supports continuous software improvements, the robot can unload up to 1,000 cases per hour, features a battery lasting two work shifts, and is equipped with onboard lighting and a pre-trained vision system.

总结：Boston Dynamics推出了Stretch Gen 2，这是一款旨在卸载挂车并减少高吞吐量物流运营中维护停机时间的新一代自主机器人。 升级后的机器人内部电缆减少了81%，并改进了抓取器、传感和模块化组件，以防止机械和电气故障。 物流机器人副总裁Brian Donnelly表示，Gen 2平台旨在更易于维护，将完整的转向驱动更换时间从整整一天缩短至大约一小时。 该机器人运行在支持持续软件改进的重新设计计算平台上，每小时最多可卸载1,000箱货物，电池可持续使用两个工作班次，并配备了机载照明和预训练视觉系统。

AI Insider — Boston Dynamics Unveils Stretch Gen 2 Autonomous Trailer-Unloading Robot

Boston Dynamics unveiled Stretch Gen 2, an upgraded autonomous trailer-unloading robot designed to reduce downtime, simplify maintenance, and improve reliability in warehouses and distribution centers. Drawing on over 100,000 operating hours from its predecessor, the redesigned robot features modular components, heavy-duty automotive-grade connectors, an 81% reduction in internal cabling, continuous rotation joints, and an improved gripper with precise sensors. 总结：波士顿动力推出了Stretch Gen 2，这是一款升级版的自主拖车卸货机器人，旨在减少停机时间、简化维护并提高仓库和分销中心的可靠性。 该重新设计的机器人借鉴了上一代产品超过100,000小时的运行时间，采用了模块化组件、重型汽车级连接器、减少了81%内部布线、连续旋转关节以及带有精密传感器的改进型夹持器。 Stretch Gen 2每小时可卸载多达1,000个箱子，支持多重抓取功能，电池可支持两班倒作业并通过快充在约75分钟内恢复至90%的电量，同时配备内置照明和支持常规软件更新的车载计算平台。
