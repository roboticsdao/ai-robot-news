The Korea Herald — Why Hyundai’s Atlas has four fingers — and Tesla’s Optimus has five

Hyundai Motor Group’s Boston Dynamics chose a four-finger design for its electric Atlas humanoid, concluding a fifth finger adds cost and complexity without due benefit for industrial work. In contrast, Tesla’s Optimus retains five fingers and 22 degrees of freedom for humanlike dexterity across both factories and homes. However, Tesla faces manufacturing bottlenecks due to the complex cable and motor system required for five fingers, with Samsung Securities analyst Im Eun-young noting hand assembly challenges.
