TechTarget — Nvidia-backed Skild AI teaches robots new tasks from a single video

Skild AI, a Pittsburgh-based physical AI startup, is using Nvidia technology including the Cosmos, Isaac Lab, Isaac Sim, and Omniverse platforms to develop a robot foundation model named S1 that can learn new multistep tasks from a single video demonstration. The S1 model is currently deployed at an Nvidia factory in Houston, where Skild, Nvidia, and Foxconn are using dual-arm robots to assemble Nvidia Blackwell GPU systems. According to Skild, the company reached a $100 million annual revenue run rate earlier this month, just 10 months after its first commercial deployment. Amit Goel, director of product management for autonomous machines at Nvidia, noted that S1 demonstrates how quickly operators can teach robots new work and make automation more adaptable. However, Goel stated that data and evaluation remain the biggest bottlenecks for scaling physical AI due to the high cost and slowness of collecting real-world robot experience, though simulation and synthetic data can help developers expand experience more quickly.

总结：总部位于匹兹堡的物理人工智能初创公司Skild AI正在使用英伟达的技术（包括Cosmos、Isaac Lab、Isaac Sim和Omniverse平台）开发名为S1的机器人基础模型，该模型能够从单个视频演示中学习新的多步骤任务。 目前，S1模型已部署在英伟达位于休斯顿的工厂中，Skild、英伟达和富士康正利用双臂机器人组装英伟达Blackwell GPU系统。 据Skild称，该公司本月早些时候实现了1亿美元的年度收入运行率，距离其首次商业部署仅过去10个月。 英伟达自主机器产品管理总监Amit Goel指出，S1展示了操作员向机器人传授新工作以及使自动化更具适应性的速度之快。 不过，Goel表示，由于收集真实机器人经验缓慢且成本高昂，数据和评估仍然是扩展物理人工智能的最大瓶颈，尽管模拟和合成数据可以帮助开发人员更快地扩展经验。

Quantum Zeitgeist — NVIDIA Pushes Safety Checks Across All Layers Of Physical AI

Nvidia is introducing Halos, a full-stack safety system designed to engineer safety across design, validation, and deployment layers for physical AI systems such as autonomous vehicles and industrial robots. With 49 million level 3-5 autonomous vehicles projected on roads by 2035 and an estimated 60 million industrial robots deployed between 2026 and 2035, Nvidia asserts that continuous, lifecycle-integrated safety models are a present imperative. The company's Halos AI Systems Inspection Lab has achieved accreditation as an ISO/IEC 17020 inspection body through the American National Standards Board, enabling it to transform safety requirements into repeatable inspections and prepare integrations for independent agency certification. Nvidia's framework incorporates third-party validation, such as TÜV Rheinland's independent assessment of NVIDIA DRIVE AV and ongoing inspections of IGX Thor, Halos OS, and Holoscan Sensor Bridge. The platform combines real-world testing with simulation and synthetic data generation to address the complex challenges of dynamic real-world environments and AI behavior.

总结：英伟达正在推出Halos，这是一个全栈安全系统，旨在为自动驾驶汽车和工业机器人等物理人工智能系统在设计、验证和部署各个层面进行安全性工程设计。 预计到2035年路上将有4900万辆3-5级自动驾驶汽车，且在2026年至2035年期间预计将部署6000万台工业机器人，英伟达断言，持续的、生命周期集成的安全模型已成为当前的当务之急。 该公司旗下的Halos AI系统检查实验室已通过美国国家标准局获得ISO/IEC 17020检查机构的认证，使其能够将安全要求转化为可重复的检查，并为独立机构认证准备集成工作。 英伟达的框架结合了第三方验证，例如TÜV Rheinland对NVIDIA DRIVE AV的独立评估以及对IGX Thor、Halos OS和Holoscan Sensor Bridge的持续检查。 该平台将真实世界测试与模拟及合成数据生成相结合，以应对动态真实环境和人工智能行为的复杂挑战。

Tomorrow's World Today — Agility Unveils 5th Generation Warehouse Humanoid Robot

Agility Robotics unveiled Digit 5, its fifth-generation warehouse humanoid robot designed to operate safely alongside human workers in open factory and warehouse environments without protective barriers. According to CEO Peggy Johnson, the robot was built based on customer requirements gathered after three years of operating Digit 4 on production floors. Digit 5 features sensors and AI algorithms that track nearby workers, utilizing visual and audio cues and automatic avoidance or stopping behaviors. Developed in partnership with Nvidia using the IGX Thor and Halos framework, Digit 5 builds on over 65,000 hours of real-world operation logged by Digit 4 units at companies like Amazon, Toyota, and GXO. The robot stands 5 feet 11 inches tall, weighs 284 pounds, reaches up to 7. 2 feet high, can repeatedly lift 50-pound loads, and includes a battery that charges in 9 minutes to provide 90 minutes of operation. With over $300 million in customer orders, Digit 5 is entering full-scale deployment.

总结：Agility Robotics发布了其第五代仓库人形机器人Digit 5，该机器人旨在无需防护栏的情况下，在开放的工厂和仓库环境中与人类员工安全地协同工作。 据首席执行官Peggy Johnson称，该机器人是根据Digit 4在生产车间运行三年后收集的客户需求建造的。 Digit 5配备了跟踪附近工人的传感器和人工智能算法，利用视觉与音频提示以及自动避让或停止行为。 在与英伟达合作并使用IGX Thor和Halos框架开发的基础上，Digit 5汲取了Digit 4在亚马逊、丰田和GXO等公司积累的超过65,000小时的真实运行经验。 该机器人身高5英尺11英寸，重284磅，最高可达7. 2英尺，能够重复举起50磅的负载，并配有一块充电仅需9分钟即可提供90分钟运行时间的电池。 凭借超过$300 million的客户订单，Digit 5正在进入全面部署阶段。
