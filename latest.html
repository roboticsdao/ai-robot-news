The Robot Report — AGIBOT introduces Worken to measure robot productivity at AGIBOT Innovation Day 2026

AGIBOT introduced Worken, a framework for measuring robot productivity in real-world environments, at AGIBOT Innovation Day 2026 in Shanghai. The concept combines "Work" and "Token" to focus on autonomous and consistent useful work rather than just manufacturing scale. Additionally, AGIBOT unveiled AHA3, an integrated three intelligences in one hardware platform, and highlighted advances in its AGILE motion intelligence framework. The company also debuted GO-3 Preview, showcased its WITA-Omni 1.0 omni-modal interaction model, and launched the G2 Max industrial robot and Link-U OS 1.0. Founded in 2023 as Zhiyuan Xinchuang Technology Co. , AGIBOT focuses on embodied AI, with a portfolio spanning humanoid robots, quadrupeds, dexterous systems, and commercial cleaning systems. Last month, AGIBOT announced its 20,000th robot rolled off the production line, while IDC reported the company led global humanoid robot shipments in the first half of 2026. According to AGIBOT co-founder, president, and CTO Peng Zhihui, Worken is calculated as Worken equals Number of Active Robots multiplied by Autonomous Working Hours multiplied by Output Efficiency Ratio, measuring active tasks, autonomous time, and productive output against a human benchmark.

总结：智元机器人在上海举行的2026智元创新日上推出了Worken，这是一个用于衡量真实环境中机器人生产力的框架。 该概念结合了“工作”与“Token”，旨在将注意力从制造或部署的机器人数量转移到它们能够自主且持续执行多少有用工作上。 此外，智元发布了AHA3集成“三智合一”硬件平台，并重点介绍了AGILE运动智能框架的进展，包括一项新的自进化智能体功能。 该公司还首次亮相了GO-3 Preview次世代具身智能基础模型，展示了WITA-Omni 1.0全模态交互模型，并发布了G2 Max工业机器人和Link-U OS 1.0。 智元机器人成立于2023年，原名为上海智元新创技术有限公司，专注于具身智能，其产品组合涵盖人形机器人、四足机器人、灵巧系统和商业清洁系统。 上个月，智元表示其第20,000台机器人已下线，IDC报告显示该公司在2026年上半年引领全球人形机器人出货量。 根据智元联合创始人、总裁兼首席技术官彭 Zhihui的介绍，Worken的计算公式为：Worken等于活跃机器人数量乘以自主工作时间乘以产出效率比，通过将活动任务、自主时间和参照人类标准确定的生产输出进行关联来衡量机器人生产力。
