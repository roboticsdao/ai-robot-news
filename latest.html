projectdesign.jp — ものづくり日本大賞優秀賞 製造現場で独自AIとロボットによるダイカスト外観検査の自動化を実現

HACARUS received an Excellence Award at the 10th Monodzukuri Nippon Grand Award for automating visual inspections of die-cast products using proprietary AI. Die-cast products are created by pouring molten metal into molds, but their surface variations and shape inconsistencies have historically made automated visual inspection difficult. HACARUS utilizes a 'good-product learning' approach, where the AI learns the characteristics of normal, defect-free items and identifies deviations as anomalies, thereby avoiding the need to collect all possible defect patterns in advance. The system combines software with third-party cameras and robots to reduce labor and stabilize quality control, and the company is now expanding this inspection method to rubber products and food packaging.

总结：HACARUS凭借利用专有AI实现压铸产品外观检查自动化的成果，荣获了第10届“制造日本大赏”优秀奖。 压铸产品是通过将熔融金属倒入模具中制成的，但其表面差异和形状不一致性历史上使得自动化外观检查变得困难。 HACARUS采用了“良品学习”方法，即AI学习正常且无缺陷物品的特征并将偏离视作异常，从而避免了预先收集所有可能缺陷模式的需求。 该系统将软件与第三方相机和机器人相结合，以减少人工并稳定质量控制，目前该公司正将这种检查方法扩展到橡胶制品和食品包装领域。
