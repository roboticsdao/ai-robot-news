Interesting Engineering — Three robotic arms 3D print nuclear vessel for America’s expanding reactor fleet

The United States is exploring large-scale metal 3D printing to overcome manufacturing bottlenecks in nuclear reactor expansion, as domestic forging capacity remains limited. Oak Ridge National Laboratory and Idaho National Laboratory collaborated to develop wire arc additive manufacturing using the MedUSA platform, which coordinates three robotic arms to melt steel wire with electric arcs. Researchers successfully printed a nuclear-relevant pressure vessel measuring approximately three feet by five feet using a relevant steel alloy. To ensure these components meet strict nuclear requirements, the laboratories plan to combine additive manufacturing with digital engineering and real-time monitoring to create "born-qualified" pressure vessels, reducing the need for extensive post-manufacturing testing. INL will provide expertise in nuclear materials and digital engineering, while ORNL leverages its Manufacturing Demonstration Facility. This technology could also benefit aerospace, defense, chemical processing, and oil and gas industries by providing an alternative to limited forging capacity.

总结：美国正转向大规模金属3D打印技术，以解决可能影响其核能扩张计划的制造瓶颈，因为国内锻造能力仍然有限。 橡树岭国家实验室和爱达荷国家实验室合作开发了电弧增材制造技术，利用由三个机械臂协调的MedUSA平台通过电弧熔化钢丝来制造工业压力容器。 研究人员成功使用核相关钢合金打印出了一个尺寸约为三英尺乘五英尺的核相关压力容器。 为了确保这些部件符合严苛的核能要求，实验室计划将增材制造与数字工程和实时监测相结合，创造出“天生合格”的压力容器，从而减少对漫长制造后测试的依赖。 INL将提供核材料和数字工程方面的专业知识，而ORNL则利用其制造示范设施。 这项技术最终还可以为航空航天、国防、化学加工以及石油和天然气行业提供支持，为受限的锻造能力提供替代方案。

CDO Magazine — LG, NVIDIA Accelerate Robotics Collaboration

LG Electronics is expanding its robotics collaboration with NVIDIA by developing a Data Factory in Seoul's Yangjae R&D Campus to support physical AI, robot learning, and commercialization. Following an August 13 memorandum of understanding signed in Santa Clara, LG and NVIDIA officials reviewed the facility, which is scheduled to be fully operational by the end of the year. LG has deployed its self-developed LG CLOiD home robots at scale across replicated home environments, simulated manufacturing spaces, logistics automation solutions, and robot hand training areas. 总结：LG电子正在扩大与英伟达的机器人合作，在首尔良재研发园区建设数据工厂，以支持具身智能、机器人学习和机器人业务的商业化。 在8月13日于圣克拉拉签署谅解备忘录之后，LG与英伟达官员视察了该数据工厂，该设施计划于年底前全面投入运营。 LG已大规模部署自主研发的LG CLOiD家庭机器人，覆盖复制的家庭环境、模拟制造空间、物流自动化解决方案以及机器人手部训练区域。 收集到的数据将与英伟达的机器人技术栈相连接，利用Omniverse库、Cosmos世界基础模型和Isaac平台等技术构建强大的数据飞轮。 到今年年底，LG预计将积累100,000小时的训练数据，用以推进支撑人形机器人性能的机器人基础模型。
