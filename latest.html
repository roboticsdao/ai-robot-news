Robotics & Automation News — Boston Dynamics launches next-generation Stretch robot with major reliability upgrades

Boston Dynamics has introduced Stretch Gen 2, the next generation of its autonomous mobile trailer unloading robot, featuring major hardware and structural upgrades derived from insights gathered across more than 100,000 operating hours and tens of millions of unloaded boxes. Designed for high-volume logistics environments, the new model incorporates heavy-duty automotive-grade connectors, an upgraded gripper with precise sensing, continuous rotation joints that eliminate hard stops, and an 81 percent reduction in internal cabling to minimize electrical faults. Furthermore, Stretch Gen 2 features a recessed emergency stop, durable exterior panels, a swerve drive swap time reduced to about one hour, onboard diagnostics, and a redesigned computing platform that supports continuous software updates for enhanced case detection and motion planning. The robot unloads up to 1,000 cases per hour, operates for two shifts on a single battery charge, and includes a fast charger that reaches 90 percent capacity in approximately 75 minutes alongside onboard lighting and a pre-trained vision system.

总结：波士顿动力推出了新一代自主移动拖车卸货机器人Stretch Gen 2，该产品基于从超过100,000小时的运行时间和数千万箱货物的卸货数据中获得的洞察，进行了重大的硬件和结构升级。 专为高吞吐量物流环境设计的新型号采用了重型汽车级连接器、配备精确传感的升级版抓取器、消除了硬限位的连续旋转关节，并将内部电缆减少了81%以尽量减少电气故障。 此外，Stretch Gen 2还具有凹陷式紧急停止按钮、耐用的外壳面板、缩短至约一小时的转向驱动更换时间、车载诊断功能以及支持持续软件更新以增强箱子检测和运动规划的重新设计计算平台。 该机器人每小时可卸货多达1,000箱，单次充电可运行两个班次，其快速充电器可在约75分钟内充至90%的电量，并配备了车载照明系统和预训练视觉系统。
