Ars Technica — Agility’s new humanoid robot will stop, squat to avoid harming human coworkers

Agility Robotics has debuted its Digit 5 humanoid robot, designed to work safely near humans without physical separation barriers. When the robot detects a person nearby, it can autonomously move away, stand still, or squat into a seated position. Pras Velagapudi, Agility's chief technology officer, stated that the robot features a complex safe motion system for various human-presence mitigations. Customers will gain early access to Digit 5 in the first half of 2027, with general availability by the end of that year, produced at Agility's RoboFab facility in Salem, Oregon. Agility's earlier Digit robots have accumulated over 65,000 hours in North American warehouses and factories since deploying at a GXO warehouse in Atlanta in 2024, with clients including GXO, Schaeffler, Amazon, and Toyota Motor Manufacturing Canada.

总结：Agility Robotics 推出了其首款人形机器人 Digit 5，旨在无需物理隔离栏的情况下在人类附近安全工作。 当检测到附近有人时，该机器人能够自主避让、原地站立或蹲下。 Agility 首席技术官 Pras Velagapudi 表示，该机器人设计有复杂的安全运动系统，可根据检测到的人类presence采取多种缓解措施。 客户预计将在 2027 年上半年获得 Digit 5 的早期使用权限，并于该年底实现全面上市，生产将在俄勒冈州塞勒姆的 RoboFab 工厂进行。 自 2024 年在亚特兰大 GXO 仓库部署以来，Agility 先前的 Digit 机器人已在北美仓库和工厂累计工作超过 65,000 小时，客户包括 GXO、施耐费尔、亚马逊和加拿大丰田汽车制造公司。

Stock Titan — NVIDIA simulation now models touch for robots

Synaptics introduced its Capacitive Tactile Sensing (CTS) module powered by the SN6012T touch controller, which is now supported by the NVIDIA Isaac Sim robotics simulation framework and works with NVIDIA Holoscan. The CTS module features a 2. 5mm taxel pitch in a 5x12 array, measures forces up to 100N with 0. 1N resolution, and offers a rebound response 10x faster than traditional foam sensors. It is built for NVIDIA's Newton physics engine and integrates with Synaptics Astra Edge AI processors to aggregate touch, vision, and motion data into unified streams delivered over standard Ethernet. Rahul Patel, President and CEO of Synaptics, noted that tactile sensing is essential for fast, accurate, and robust robotics and humanoid experiences across all markets.

总结：Synaptics 推出了搭载 SN6012T 触控控制器的首款电容式触觉传感（CTS）模块，现已获得 NVIDIA Isaac Sim 机器人仿真框架的支持并可与 NVIDIA Holoscan 配合使用。 该 CTS 模块采用 5x12 阵列的 2. 5mm 触点间距，可测量高达 100N 的力且分辨率达 0. 1N，回弹响应比传统泡沫传感器快 10 倍。 它专为 NVIDIA 的牛顿物理引擎构建，并与 Synaptics Astra 边缘 AI 处理器集成，可将触觉、视觉和运动数据汇聚成统一的数据流，通过标准以太网传输。 Synaptics 总裁兼首席执行官 Rahul Patel 指出，触觉传感对于所有市场中快速、准确且稳健的机器人和人形机器人体验至关重要。
