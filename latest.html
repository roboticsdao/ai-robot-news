The Business Standard — IPO for humanoid robot maker Boston Dynamics unlikely in 2027: Executive

Boston Dynamics, Hyundai Motor Group's humanoid robot unit, is unlikely to pursue an initial public offering in 2027 because it has yet to deploy its flagship Atlas robots at scale and remains unprofitable, according to a senior executive at Hyundai Motor Group. Hyundai bought a controlling stake in Boston Dynamics in 2021 and plans to make it a wholly owned subsidiary by acquiring SoftBank's roughly 10% stake in a deal valued at about 500 billion won ($371. 51 million). Boston Dynamics recorded a 2025 loss of 528. 4 billion won, with total losses from 2021 through 2025 reaching nearly 1. 7 trillion won. Analysts estimate the company's valuation ranges from 50 trillion won to 100 trillion won, with some suggesting an IPO may occur around 2029 or 2030 as Hyundai works to gather operational data and improve robot capabilities.

总结：现代汽车集团的一位高管表示，现代汽车集团旗下的人形机器人部门波士顿动力公司不太可能在2027年进行首次公开募股，因为其旗舰产品Atlas机器人尚未实现大规模部署且仍处于亏损状态。 现代汽车于2021年收购了波士顿动力的控股权，并计划通过收购软银持有的约10%股份将其变为全资子公司，交易价值约为500 billion won（$371. 51 million）。 波士顿动力在2025年录得528. 4 billion won的亏损，2021年至2025年的总亏损接近1. 7 trillion won。 分析师估计该公司的估值在50 trillion won到100 trillion won之间，一些人认为IPO可能会在2029年或2030年左右进行，因为现代需要收集运营数据并提升机器人性能。

Interesting Engineering — Figure introduces new humanoid robot neural network

Figure has released Helix 2. 5, a neural network deployed into 30 Bay Area homes without prior data collection, fine-tuning, or exposure to the handled objects. The network completed 56 percent of tasks such as putting toys in baskets, folding towels, and making beds. When trained from scratch without Figure's human behavior dataset, known as Index—which captures roughly 35 minutes of new human experience every second—the same network managed a 9 percent success rate across the 30 strange homes.

总结：Figure发布了Helix 2. 5神经网络，该网络被直接部署到30个湾区家庭中，且未进行任何预先的数据收集、微调或接触被操作的物体。 该网络完成了56%的任务，例如将玩具放入篮子、折叠毛巾和铺床。 如果在没有Figure人类行为数据集（被称为Index，每秒收集约35分钟的新人类经验）支撑的情况下从头开始训练，同一个网络在30个陌生家庭中仅取得了9%的成功率。

mikekalil.com — The Era of Humanoid Robot Mega Factories

UBTech has officially commenced operations at its new humanoid robot mega factory in Liuzhou, developed in partnership with Siemens. Covering approximately 14,000 square meters, the facility scales to an annual capacity of 10,000 units, bringing the company's combined annual production capacity to nearly 20,000 units. Founded by Zhou Jian in Shenzhen in 2012, UBTech initially built consumer robots before pivoting to industrial automation as government policies prioritized humanoid mass production. The company went public on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in December 2023 at a nearly $5 billion valuation. In its 2026 semiannual report, UBTech reported $189 million in revenue—a 104 percent year-over-year increase—alongside a net loss of about $50 million, while navigating recent production and shipment delays caused by flooding in Guangxi.
