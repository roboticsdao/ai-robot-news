Interesting Engineering — Underwater US robot pulls itself from the ocean without human help

Impossible Metals has unveiled footage of its Eureka II autonomous underwater vehicle successfully locating a surface vessel and recovering itself from the open ocean without human intervention during an offshore demonstration in Halifax, Nova Scotia. The 10. 5-foot long, 8,710-pound AUV used acoustic positioning beneath the wave zone followed by computer vision with AprilTag visual markers for precise navigation during its final approach, before connecting via a mechanical SmartHook system to return to the deck. Operating at depths up to 19,685 feet (6,000 meters) with a 14. 4-kWh battery and three sets of robotic arms for collecting seafloor mineral nodules, the robot aims to significantly reduce the cost and personnel risks of subsea operations.

总结：Impossible Metals公布了一段视频，显示其Eureka II自主式水下航行器在新斯科舍省哈利法克斯的近海示范中，在没有人工干预的情况下成功定位水面舰艇并从开阔海域实现自主回收。 这台长10. 5英尺、重8,710磅的AUV在波浪区下方使用声学定位，随后在最后接近阶段借助带有AprilTag视觉标志的计算机视觉进行精确导航，最后通过机械SmartHook系统连接并返回甲板。 该机器人在高达19,685英尺（6,000米）的深度运行，配备14. 4-kWh电池和三套用于收集海底矿产结核的机械臂，旨在显著降低水下作业的成本和人员风险。
