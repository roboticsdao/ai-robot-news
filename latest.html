Aviation Wire — JAL、成田で機体洗浄ロボット 国内初の協働型、夜間作業を支援

JAL Group announced on August 28 that it will introduce the remote-controlled aircraft exterior washing robot 'AW3' at Narita Airport, planning full-scale operation within the year after necessary operational training. Developed by Sweden's Aerowash, AW3 is the first collaborative aircraft washing robot with programmatic auto-approach functions to be introduced by a Japanese airline, aimed at reducing worker burden and improving safety during night shifts. The robot features 4-wheel steering for omnidirectional movement, measures 8 meters long, 2. 3 meters wide, and 2. 5 meters high, and covers narrow-body to wide-body aircraft including Boeing 737, 767, 787, and Airbus A350. According to Aerowash, water consumption can be reduced by up to 50% per aircraft. Currently, Narita night washes are performed by eight staff members over six hours using long mops and detergents. The AW3 robot will cover areas previously done by two workers with one unit, helping reduce physical strain from high-altitude and heavy labor and minimizing direct contact with organic solvents.

总结：日本航空（JAL）集团于8月28日宣布，将在成田空港引入远程遥控式机体外装清洗机器人“AW3”，在实施必要的财操作训练后，计划于年内投入正式运营。 据JAL介绍，这是国内航司首次引入具备机型程序指定等自动接近功能的协働型机体清洗机器人，旨在夜间作业中减轻作业员负担并提升安全性。 AW3由瑞典Aerowash公司开发，采用四轮独立驱动和操舵的“4WS”系统以实现全方向移动，尺寸为全長8米、全幅2. 3米、全高2. 5米，单台设备可覆盖从波音737、767、787到空客A350等窄体机至宽体机。 根据Aerowash的目录数据，AW3可将单架飞机的用水量最多削减50%。 目前成田机场的夜间清洗通常由8名工作人员耗时6小时用长柄拖把和专用洗剂完成，而AW3能够单台覆盖以往需要2人清洗的区域，从而减轻高空作业和长时间体力劳动的身体负担，并降低直接接触有机溶剂的风险。
