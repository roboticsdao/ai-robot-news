Vietnam.vn — US and China compete in a $5 trillion race for humanoid robots.

China is pulling ahead of the US in the humanoid robot race, accounting for 97% of global deliveries in the first half of the year, aided by government support, a vast component supply network, and an engineering workforce. Morgan Stanley forecasts the industry could reach over $5 trillion by 2050. While Chinese companies hold advantages in hardware and manufacturing, US rivals focus on AI brains, supported by advanced chips and leading models. LinkerBot supplies over 80% of global advanced robotic hands with clients like Samsung and Meta, and startups like PsiBot are developing cognitive abilities while nearing a $1.5 billion valuation.

总结：在政府支持、庞大的零部件供应网络和工程劳动力的协助下，中国在人形机器人竞赛中正超越美国，今年上半年占全球交付量的97%。 摩根士丹利预测，到2050年该行业价值可能超过$5 trillion。 虽然中国公司在硬件和制造方面占据优势，但美国竞争对手在先进芯片和领先模型的支持下专注于人工智能大脑。 LinkerBot供应了全球80%以上的先进机器人手，客户包括三星和Meta，而PsiBot等初创公司正在开发认知能力，估值接近15亿美元。

TechRadar — UCLA researchers built a $400 humanoid robot around cheap phone technology

Researchers at the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA), have developed PhoneBot, a compact humanoid robot that costs approximately $400 in hardware, excluding the phone, and reuses cheap Android smartphones for sensing and computing. PhoneBot stands 483 mm tall, weighs 1.8 kg, and uses 13 servo motors to control its legs and rotating torso. Instead of dedicated cameras and motion sensors, it uses four Android phones—including an older Honor 9 and three handsets costing $50 to $80—for vision, computing, and walking control via TensorFlow Lite. The team trained the walking controller using reinforcement learning in simulation, applying mirrored training samples to improve gait symmetry and reduce joint-angle symmetry error from 0.254 radians to 0.003 radians across five independent training runs. The robot is limited by low-powered servos and lacks arms, leaving uneven-terrain mobility unresolved.

总结：加利福尼亚大学洛杉矶分校（UCLA）的研究人员开发了名为PhoneBot的小型人形机器人，其不含手机的硬件成本约为400美元，并重复利用廉价的Android智能手机进行传感与计算。 PhoneBot高483毫米，重1.8公斤，使用13个伺服电机来控制其腿部及旋转躯干。 它不依赖专用摄像头和运动传感器，而是使用4款Android手机（包括一台2017年发布的旧款荣耀9以及三款售价约50至80美元的低端手机）通过TensorFlow Lite来处理视觉、计算和行走控制。 研究团队在仿真环境中使用强化学习训练行走的控制器，应用镜像训练样本来改善步态对称性，在五次独立训练中将关节角度对称误差从0.254弧度降至0.003弧度。 该机器人受限于低功率伺服电机且缺乏手臂，尚未解决不平坦地面的移动问题。
