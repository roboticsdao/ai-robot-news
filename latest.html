supercarblondie.com — UCSD Center for the Future of Surgery hosts live demonstration of the first ever humanoid robot surgeon

The UCSD Center for the Future of Surgery hosted a live demonstration of the humanoid robot surgeon 'Surgie', which was used in a preclinical trial to perform gallbladder removal procedures on live pigs using modified Unitree G1 humanoid robots. Controlled remotely by human specialists via a teleoperation system, the robots served as surgeons and assistants in operations aimed at helping specialists reach patients in areas lacking specialist care. Researchers noted challenges including shoulder joints overheating after about 20 minutes of operation, battery life limitations, and the inability to sterilize the machines to surgical standards. The team is focused on addressing safety concerns and developing the technology rather than pursuing autonomous surgery.

总结：加州大学圣迭戈分校外科未来中心（UCSD Center for the Future of Surgery）对人形机器人外科医生 Surgie 进行了现场演示。 在一项临床前试验中，研究人员使用改装的宇树科技 G1 人形机器人对活猪实施了两例胆囊切除手术。 手术由人类专家通过远程操作系统进行控制，机器人分别担任主刀和助手，旨在帮助专家将专业医疗服务延伸至缺乏专业设备的医院和社区。 研究人员指出该技术面临的挑战，包括肩关节在运行约 20 分钟后过热、电池寿命有限以及目前无法达到外科手术所需的灭菌标准。 该团队目前专注于解决安全问题和技术开发，而非追求自主手术。
