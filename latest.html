Hackster.io — Teaching Humanoid Robots to Move Like Us

Researchers from UC Berkeley and Stanford University have developed BeyondMimic, an AI framework that uses a single training setup to teach humanoid robots a diverse collection of human-like movements. Tested on a Unitree G1 robot, the framework starts with about 2. 5 hours of human motion data covering walking, running, dancing, and martial arts, which are adapted to the G1's proportions. BeyondMimic applies reinforcement learning, a variational autoencoder, and a diffusion model to generate and transition between movements. In tests, 30 motions were transferred to physical hardware without task-specific training, and 70. 8% of 77 participants judged the generated movements to look more human-like than those produced by Unitree's standard controller.

总结：加州大学伯克利分校和斯坦福大学的研究人员开发了BeyondMimic，这是一个利用单一训练设置来教导人形机器人各种人类般动作的AI框架。 该框架在宇树G1机器人上进行了测试，首先使用了约2. 5小时涵盖步行、跑步、跳舞和武术的人类动作数据，并将其调整适应G1的比例。 BeyondMimic应用了强化学习、变分自编码器和扩散模型来生成动作并在其间进行过渡。 在测试中，30种动作无需针对特定任务的训练便被迁移到了实体硬件上，且在77名参与者的研究中，70. 8%的人认为该框架生成的动作比宇树标准控制器产生的动作更具人类特征。

The production robot stands about 5 feet 7 inches tall, weighs 143 pounds, has 76 degrees of freedom, and travels at around 6. 5 feet per second. IRON features a self-charging feature, obstacle avoidance, and a "world model" powered by three Turing AI chips running a Llama-like model onboard. Xpeng plans to deploy IRON internally in showrooms and tech campuses, with commercial shipments in China expected to start in 2027.

interestingengineering.com — Why robot hands are one of the hardest problems in humanoid robotics

Performing household chores like folding a shirt or picking up a wet sponge remains significantly harder for humanoid robots than executing athletic movements like backflips, because household environments involve constant uncertainty and irregular physical interactions. While a backflip is a predictable sequence of controlled movements, everyday items bend, slip, and deform. Building a dexterous robotic hand requires integrating mechanics, sensing, and control, as vision alone cannot measure grip force or slippage. Researchers are increasingly focusing on tactile sensing and data collection to help robots master bimanual coordination and whole-body manipulation in unstructured environments.

总结：对人形机器人而言，折衬衫或捡起湿海绵等家务活，比执行后空翻等运动要困难得多，因为家居环境充满了不确定性和不规则的物理交互。 后空翻是一系列可预测的受控动作序列，而日常物品会弯曲、滑动和变形。 构建灵巧的机器人机械手需要整合机械、传感与控制，因为单纯的视觉无法测量抓握力或打滑情况。 研究人员正日益关注触觉传感和数据采集，以帮助机器人在非结构化环境中掌握双手协同和全身操作能力。
