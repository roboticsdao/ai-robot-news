Yahoo Finance — YYForce Launches Humanoid Robot for Hotel Concierge Services Using NVIDIA Technology

YYForce Inc. announced the launch of its humanoid robot named Six Seven (67), developed for hotel concierge operations using NVIDIA Jetson edge AI computing and NVIDIA Isaac Sim simulation technology. The robot will be deployed at Novotel Singapore on Stevens to provide lobby concierge services, guest inquiry responses, local attraction information, and interactive storytelling. Integrated into the Workforce OS platform, the deployment supports YYForce's strategy to expand commercial robotics in hospitality and retail, though financial terms and timetables were not disclosed.
