THE ELEC, Korea Electronics Industry Media — Altera Targets Physical AI Market With FPGAs for Nvidia Sensor Bridge

Altera is targeting the physical artificial intelligence market, including robotics and autonomous vehicles, with field-programmable gate arrays designed for Nvidia’s Holoscan Sensor Bridge. Lee Nam-hoon, a director at Altera, discussed the technology at the Semiconductors That Power Humanoids conference held alongside the Semiconductor Exhibition 2026 at COEX in Seoul on Oct. 16. The Holoscan Sensor Bridge uses an architecture connecting sensors, FPGAs, Ethernet, and GPUs. Altera’s FPGAs aggregate sensor data and handle latency-sensitive operations in real time, while Nvidia’s GPUs focus on large-scale parallel computing and AI inference. This architecture bypasses the central processing unit to reduce latency and simplifies wiring with a single high-speed Ethernet connection. Altera offers four FPGA families for the Holoscan Sensor Bridge, ranging from the entry-level Cyclone to Agilex 3, Agilex 5, and the higher-end Agilex 7, along with modular development kits and ecosystem partnerships.

总结：Altera正凭借专为英伟达Holoscan Sensor Bridge设计的现场可编程门阵列，进军包括机器人和自动驾驶汽车在内的物理人工智能市场。 Altera总监李南勋于10月16日在首尔COEX举行的2026年半导体展览会同期会议“驱动人形机器人的半导体”上讨论了该技术。 Holoscan Sensor Bridge采用了将传感器、FPGA、以太网和GPU依次连接的架构。 Altera的FPGA汇集传感器数据并实时处理对延迟敏感的操作，而英伟达的GPU则专注于大规模并行计算和AI推理。 该架构允许传感器数据绕过中央处理器以降低延迟，并利用单一的高速以太网连接简化布线。 Altera为Holoscan Sensor Bridge提供四个FPGA系列，涵盖入门级的Cyclone、适用于紧凑型机器人的Agilex 3、适用于包括人形机器人在内的新一代机器人的Agilex 5以及高端的Agilex 7，同时还提供模块化开发套件与生态系统合作伙伴支持。

Automation World — Q&A: How U.S. Trade Policies Are Reshaping Chinese Robotics Imports

United States trade policies and federal actions are significantly altering how companies evaluate robotics imports, moving procurement beyond purchase price and performance to factor in tariffs, supply chain exposure, component sourcing, cybersecurity, and geopolitical risk. Many American robot makers continue to rely on Chinese actuators, motors, and magnets, creating near-term cost pressures. These policy changes create strategic opportunities for domestic robotics companies capable of competitive local sourcing, while increasing long-term supply chain risk awareness for deploying organizations. Industry experts advise companies to examine the entire technology and supply chain stack, verify Federal Communications Commission authorizations, assess component duty structures, and conduct scenario planning for potential regulatory or tariff adjustments. Furthermore, geopolitical and supply chain risks have become integrated into investment theses when evaluating robotics companies, alongside underlying technology, founding teams, and manufacturing scale.

总结：美国的贸易政策和联邦行动正在显著改变企业评估机器人进口的方式，使采购决策超越了购买价格和性能，转而将关税、供应链暴露、组件采购、网络安全和地缘政治风险纳入考量。 许多美国机器人制造商依然依赖中国的执行器、电机和磁铁，这带来了近期的成本压力。 这些政策变化为能够在国内进行具成本竞争力采购的本土机器人企业创造了战略机遇，同时也提高了部署机构对长期供应链风险的意识。 行业专家建议企业审视整个技术与供应链堆栈、核实联邦通信委员会授权、评估组件关税结构，并针对潜在的法规或关税调整进行情景规划。 此外，地缘政治和供应链风险在评估机器人企业时，已与底层技术、创始团队和制造规模一同被整合进投资论点中。

Fortune — How Boston Dynamics is turning robots into a real business

McMaster served as interim CEO until Rohit Prasad took the helm on Oct. 7. Additionally, the article notes leadership changes including Union Pacific CFO Jennifer Hamann retiring in early 2027 and Frank Vargo being appointed interim CFO of Emergent BioSolutions effective Jan. 1, 2027. 总结：根据前首席财务官兼临时首席执行官阿曼达·麦克马斯特的说法，波士顿动力公司正将工作重心转向通过积极的销售运营、成本控制和可靠部署，使机器人在规模化应用上具有经济可行性。 麦克马斯特在《财富》最具影响力女性峰会上指出，该公司四足机器人Spot的基础平台售价约为10万美元，根据负载不同最高可达30万美元，并被用于半导体制造、食品饮料生产和公共安全的检查工作。 英特尔是利用Orbit软件平台的最大Spot客户之一。 对于Atlas人形机器人，其物料清单中约有70%是执行器，由现代摩比斯按成本价生产，而母公司现代已承诺在现代汽车和起亚的设施中部署约5万台机器人。 麦克马斯特担任临时首席执行官至罗希特·普拉萨德于10月7日接任。 此外，文章还记录了其他领导层变动，包括联合太平洋公司首席财务官詹妮弗·哈曼将于2027年初退休，以及弗兰克·瓦尔戈被任命为Emergent BioSolutions临时首席财务官，自2027年1月1日起生效。 Anthropic还报告称，截至8月，由员工指导的AI agents记录的工作时长达到了员工自身的六倍。

Chosunbiz — Battery swap and fast charge race to boost humanoid uptime, McKinsey says - CHOSUNBIZ

Humanoid robot battery operations are split between rapid swapping and fast charging to extend operating times beyond the current 2 to 4 hours. Hyundai Motor Group's Boston Dynamics equipped the Atlas humanoid with an automatic battery swap method taking 3 minutes, targeting 24-hour operation and deployment at the HMGMA plant in Georgia in 2028. UBTECH also applied a 3-minute swap method to its Walker S2 industrial humanoid. Conversely, Agility Robotics partnered with Nyobolt for ultra-fast charging on the Digit 5, enabling a 9-minute charge after 90 minutes of work, while Figure AI integrated wireless fast charging into the Figure 03.

总结：人形机器人电池技术正在快速换电和快速充电两条路径间分化，以克服目前单次充电仅能运行2至4小时的限制。 现代汽车集团旗下的波士顿动力公司为其新一代人形机器人 Atlas 采用了3分钟自动换电技术，目标实现24小时运行，并计划于2028年在美国佐治亚州的 HMGMA 工厂部署。 优必选也在其工业人形机器人 Walker S2 上采用了约3分钟的换电方法。 另一方面，Agility Robotics 与电池技术公司 Nyobolt 合作，为 Digit 5 引入超快充技术，实现工作90分钟后充电9分钟；Figure AI 则在 Figure 03 中整合了无线快充技术。

AI Insider — Humanoid Robot Pilot by AGCO and Agility Robotics Planned for Kansas Manufacturing Plant

AGCO and Agility Robotics signed a commercial agreement to pilot Agility's Digit humanoid robot at AGCO's Hesston, Kansas, manufacturing plant starting in 2027. Digit will initially perform repetitive physical tasks such as sorting and stacking parts and placing totes into storage. AGCO aims to evaluate how humanoid robots improve ergonomics and operational efficiency in existing workflows. Digit adapts to workflows using AI and is managed via the Agility Arc cloud platform, joining other deployment sites at Toyota, GXO, and Schaeffler.

总结：AGCO 与 Agility Robotics 签署了一项商业协议，计划从2027年起在 AGCO 位于堪萨斯州黑斯顿的制造厂试点部署 Agility 的 Digit 人形机器人。 Digit 最初将负责零件分拣、堆叠以及将周转箱放入存储区等重复且体力消耗大的生产任务。 AGCO 旨在评估人形机器人如何在现有工作流程中改善人机工程学并提高运营效率。 Digit 利用人工智能适应变化的工作流程，并可通过 Agility Arc 云平台进行管理，这使其加入到了包含丰田、GXO 和舍弗勒在内的客户部署群体中。

State Council Information Office — China expects humanoid robot production to exceed 100,000 units this year

China expects humanoid robot production to exceed 100,000 units in 2026, as stated by Wei Wei, director general of the Department of Science and Technology of the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, at an October 19 press conference. By the end of June, China had developed over 400 humanoid models with production surpassing 40,000 units. Embodied AI products are being tested across more than 300 priority scenarios including warehousing, logistics, catering, retail, and household services. The ministry plans to release the 2026 national guidelines for industry standards, establish an open-source community, and enhance cybersecurity and data security monitoring.

总结：中国工业和信息化部科技司司长魏巍在10月19日的新闻发布会上表示，中国预计今年人形机器人产量将超过10万台，正加速推动具身智能从实验室走向工厂、零售店和日常生活。 截至6月底，中国已开发出400多款人形机器人模型，产量超过4万台。 具身智能产品正在仓储、物流、餐饮、零售和家政服务等300多个优先场景中进行测试和部署。 工信部计划发布2026年版国家人形机器人产业标准体系建设指南，建立高水平开源社区，并加强网络安全与数据安全监管。
