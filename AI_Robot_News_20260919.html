Interesting Engineering — Figure introduces new humanoid robot neural network

Figure has released Helix 2. 5, a neural network tested across 30 Bay Area homes with zero data collected in any of them, no fine-tuning, and no prior exposure to the objects handled. The robots were tasked with making beds, folding towels, and putting toys into baskets in unfamiliar homes, successfully completing 56 percent of their attempts. When the same network was trained from scratch without Figure's human behavior dataset, called Index, the success rate dropped to 9 percent. The Index dataset ingests approximately 35 minutes of new human experience every second.

总结：Figure发布了Helix 2. 5神经网络，并在海湾地区的30个家庭中进行了测试，这些家庭未收集任何数据，没有进行微调，机器人此前也没有接触过被处理的物品。 机器人在陌生的环境中执行铺床、叠毛巾和将玩具放入篮子等任务，成功完成了56%的尝试。 当同一网络在没有Figure名为Index的人类行为数据集的支持下从头训练时，成功率下降到了9%。 Index数据集每秒接收约35分钟的新人类经验。
