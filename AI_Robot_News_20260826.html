Built In — What Is Boston Dynamics? A Guide to the World’s Most Advanced Robot Company

Boston Dynamics, founded in 1992 by Marc Raibert, is a robotics company developing mobile robots to automate difficult or dangerous industrial tasks. According to a 2025 report by the International Federation of Robotics, roughly 542,000 robots were installed in 2024, more than double the amount in 2014. The company's lineup includes Spot, a four-legged mobile robot with laser-scanning technology to build digital twins of worksites; Atlas, an upright humanoid robot capable of lifting 110 pounds and carrying 66 pounds; and Stretch, a robotic arm with suction cups designed to lift 50 pounds and move hundreds of boxes per hour in warehouses. Boston Dynamics' robots run on an interconnected ecosystem combining hardware such as visual cameras, LiDAR sensors, thermal cameras, and acoustic imagers with software and large behavior models.

总结：波士顿动力由Marc Raibert于1992年创立，是一家专注于研发移动机器人以实现工业环境中危险或重度任务自动化的机器人公司。 根据国际机器人联合会2025年的报告，2024年约安装了542,000台机器人，是2014年的两倍多。 该公司产品线包括采用激光扫描技术构建工位数字孪生的四足移动机器人Spot、能够举起110磅并承载66磅的双足人形机器人Atlas，以及配有吸盘、每小时可在仓库搬运数百个箱子的机械臂Stretch。 波士顿动力的机器人运行在由视觉相机、激光雷达、热像仪和声学成像仪等硬件与软件及大型行为模型相结合的互联生态系统中。

Interesting Engineering — Underwater US robot pulls itself from the ocean without human help

Impossible Metals unveiled footage on Tuesday, August 25, showing its Eureka II autonomous underwater vehicle recovering itself from the open ocean without human help during an offshore demonstration in Halifax, Nova Scotia. The 10. 5 feet long, 6. 4 feet wide, 6. 3 feet tall robot weighing approximately 8,710 pounds on land and equipped with a 14. 4-kilowatt-hour battery and three sets of robotic arms operated at depths up to 19,685 feet. Eureka II utilized acoustic positioning to locate its surface vessel beneath wave zones, then switched to computer vision using AprilTag visual markers on its top plate for precise navigation before a mechanical SmartHook connected and secured the vehicle. CTO Jason Gilham noted that autonomous recovery is a gating function for commercial subsea scaling, while Executive Chairman Steve Curnutte emphasized that future ocean technology depends on autonomy and precision to reduce environmental impact.

总结：Impossible Metals于8月25日星期二公布了一段录像，显示其Eureka II自主水下航行器在新斯科舍省哈利法克斯的近海演示中，无需人工干预即可从开阔海洋中自行回收。 这台长10. 5英尺、宽6. 4英尺、高6. 3英尺，陆地重约8,710磅，配备14. 4千瓦时电池和三组机械臂的机器人可在高达19,685英尺的深度作业。 Eureka II利用声学定位在波浪区下方定位水面舰艇，随后切换至使用顶板上AprilTag视觉标记的计算机视觉进行精确导航，最后通过机械SmartHook连接并固定航行器。 首席技术官Jason Gilham指出自主回收是商业海底规模化的关键功能，执行主席Steve Curnutte则强调未来的海洋技术取决于自主性和精确度以减少环境影响。

Forcesteed Robotics announced a joint research paper with the National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology (AIST) titled 'Extraction of Risks and Risk Reduction Measures in Indoor Delivery Robots Equipped with Physical AI Using Generative AI', scheduled for presentation at the Robotics Society of Japan on September 3, 2026, by Takaaki Namba.
