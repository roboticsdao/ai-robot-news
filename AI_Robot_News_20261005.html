Robotics & Automation News — Agility Robotics partners with FORT to strengthen safety systems for Digit 5

Agility Robotics and FORT Robotics have partnered to enhance the safety architecture of Agility's Digit 5 humanoid robot. The collaboration builds on a multi-year engagement and focuses on an integrated, three-part safety architecture consisting of a safety pendant, on-robot communications, and off-robot interfaces connecting Digit with external safety systems. The jointly developed off-robot interface extends cooperative safety beyond the robot itself, complementing onboard safety systems like Safe Human Detection. The partnership establishes a broader framework for joint hardware development, solutions engineering, regulatory compliance, and deployment support as Digit 5 scales into commercial environments such as warehouses, manufacturing, and logistics. Previous versions of Digit have logged over 65,000 hours of operation at customer sites including Schaeffler, GXO, and Toyota Motor Manufacturing Canada. CTO Pras Velagapudi noted that the additions make Digit's safety system more resilient and redundant, while CEO Samuel Reeves stated that the partnership supports Agility's ongoing deployments with enterprise customers.

总结：人形机器人公司Agility Robotics与机械安全平台FORT Robotics建立合作伙伴关系，以进一步推进Agility下一代人形机器人Digit 5的安全基础设施。 此次合作深化了双方多年的合作，形成了一个三部分集成的安全架构，包括安全挂件、机载通信以及将Digit与外部安全系统连接的机外接口。 由FORT和Agility联合开发的机外接口旨在将协作安全方法扩展到机器人本身之外，补充Digit 5计划采用的机载安全架构。 随着Digit 5部署并扩展到仓库、制造和物流等更复杂的商业环境，该伙伴关系还将为未来的合作建立更广泛的运营框架，涵盖联合硬件开发、解决方案工程、法规合规和部署支持。 Digit的前代产品已累计运行超过65000小时，并在Schaeffler、GXO和加拿大丰田汽车制造厂等客户现场进行过部署。 Agility首席技术官Pras Velagapudi表示，FORT增加的每一层都使Digit的安全系统更具弹性和冗余；FORT创始人兼首席执行官Samuel Reeves则表示，正式确立战略伙伴关系反映了该架构对于让人形机器人在人类紧邻环境中安全工作的重要性。

Interesting Engineering — Boston Dynamics unveils 13-DOF robotic hand for its Atlas humanoid

Boston Dynamics has unveiled a new 13-DOF robotic hand for its Atlas humanoid, featuring four fingers and an opposable thumb instead of a conventional five-fingered hand. Designed to balance dexterity, strength, durability, sensing, manufacturability, and simulation compatibility, the hand increases articulation from the previous seven-DOF design. Each finger has three degrees of freedom while the thumb has four. The hand uses direct actuation, a single actuator type, and encapsulated joints rather than cables. It is roughly the size of a large human hand and can carry a loaded mini-fridge weighing over 100 pounds, matching the Atlas platform's instantaneous payload of 110 pounds and sustained payload of 66 pounds. The design omits a pinky finger because testing showed four fingers provide sufficient in-house object reorientation, slip recovery, and tool manipulation. The hand is optimized for high-fidelity simulation and sim-to-real reinforcement learning, utilizing backdrivable transmissions, proprioception, and dense pressure-sensitive tactile sensors on the fingertips and palm.

总结：波士顿动力公司为其Atlas人形机器人发布了一款全新的13自由度机械手，该机械手采用四根手指和一个可对握的拇指，而不是传统的五指设计。 该机械手旨在平衡灵巧性、强度、耐用性、传感、可制造性和仿真兼容性，将关节活动度从先前的7自由度设计提升至13自由度。 其中除拇指有4个自由度外，其他每根手指有3个自由度。 该手部采用直接驱动、单一执行器类型以及封装关节而非电缆。 其大小与人类的大手相仿，能够搬运重达100多磅的装满物品的小型冰箱，与Atlas平台110磅的瞬时负载和66磅的持续负载能力相匹配。 设计中省略小指是因为测试表明四根手指足以进行手内物体重定向、防滑恢复和工具操作。 该机械手针对高保真仿真和“仿真到真实”的强化学习进行了优化，利用了可反向驱动的传动装置、本体感觉以及指尖和手掌上密集的压敏触觉传感器。
