The Robot Report — Boston Dynamics gives more insight into its redesigned humanoid hand

Boston Dynamics recently showcased its redesigned humanoid robot hand featuring four fingers, 13 degrees of freedom, and direct actuation. Alberto Rodriguez, director of robot behavior for Atlas, explained that the company designed the hand for mass manufacturing and high-fidelity simulation to support sim-to-real reinforcement learning. Dropping the pinky finger and using 13 identical encapsulated actuators helped achieve simplicity and modularity without fragile cables crossing joints. The company builds its robot hands in-house to integrate smoothly with its product roadmap and design trade-offs. Boston Dynamics validates the durability of its hardware through early prototypes, collaboration between behavior and hardware teams, and rigorous testing against common industrial tasks such as handling power tools, managing cables, and fishing for screws.

总结：波士顿动力公司近日展示了其重新设计的人形机器人手部，该手部拥有四个手指、13个自由度并采用直接驱动。 Atlas机器人行为总监阿尔贝托·罗德里格斯解释称，该公司设计该手部是为了适应大规模制造以及支持从模拟到现实强化学习的高保真仿真。 通过去掉小指并使用13个完全封装且相同的执行器，设计实现了简单性和模块化，避免了跨关节的脆弱电缆。 该公司坚持自主研发机器人手部，以便与产品路线图和设计权衡保持平稳契合。 波士顿动力通过早期原型机、行为与硬件团队之间的协作，以及针对使用电动工具、处理电缆和螺丝等常见工业任务的严格测试，来验证其硬件的耐用性。

WRIF Rocks Detroit — Detroit Debuts Boston Dynamics’ Robot Dogs for Urban Innovation Testing

Bedrock Detroit and Boston Dynamics are testing Spot robot dogs in downtown Detroit as part of an initiative to turn the city into a technology proving ground. Kevin Mull, Bedrock Detroit's senior director for the Office of Urban Strategy, presented a robot named Astro and explained that the trials will explore tasks such as picking up litter as small as a cigarette butt, inspecting buildings, mapping difficult areas, and handling packages. The robotic dogs serve as autonomous mobile sensing platforms to handle tedious, dangerous, or dirty jobs in environments designed for humans. The project has been developed over the past six to eight months, and officials will monitor the test mode for a few more months before considering a live setting. Similar quadruped robots have previously been deployed for security at construction sites in Cleveland and by the Taylor Police Department in Wayne County.

总结：贝德罗克底特律公司与波士顿动力公司正在底特律市中心测试Spot机器人狗，旨在将该市打造成未来技术的试验场。 贝德罗克底特律城市战略办公室高级总监凯文·马尔展示了一名为Astro的机器人，并解释称此次试验将探索拾取小至烟头大小的垃圾、检查建筑物、测绘复杂区域以及处理包裹等任务。 这些机器人狗作为自主移动传感平台，能够在为人类设计的环境中处理沉闷、危险或肮脏的工作。 该项目在过去六到八个月中一直在低调推进，官员们将在试运行几个月后再考虑将其投入实际运营。 此前，类似的四足机器人已在克利夫兰的建筑工地以及韦恩县泰勒警察局被部署用于安全保障任务。

NVIDIA Developer — 5 Steps to Create SimReady Assets for Robotics with Frontier AI Models | NVIDIA Technical Blog

Preparing CAD assets for robotics simulation requires configuring and validating materials, collision geometry, joints, and other physics properties using tools like NVIDIA Omniverse libraries and SimReady specifications. NVIDIA released a technical guide outlining a five-step workflow for converting and validating simulation-ready assets for an ABB Robotics YuMi robot, assisted by frontier AI models such as GPT-6 Astra. The five steps consist of STEP-file conversion, appearance validation, physics configuration, SimReady validation, and a final pick-and-place task using both arms and grippers in NVIDIA Isaac Sim 6.1. Developers use OpenUSD to represent geometry and simulation properties, while NVIDIA Omniverse AI Agent skills guide the conversion and validation process through Codex CLI connected to a live Isaac Sim application.

总结：为机器人仿真准备CAD资产需要使用NVIDIA Omniverse库和SimReady规范等工具，配置和验证材质、碰撞几何体、关节以及其他物理属性。 英伟达发布了一份技术指南，概述了在GPT-6 Astra等前沿AI模型的协助下，为ABB机器人YuMi转换和验证仿真就绪资产的五步工作流程。 这五个步骤包括STEP文件转换、外观验证、物理配置、SimReady验证，以及在NVIDIA Isaac Sim 6.1中使用双臂和夹爪进行的最终抓取放置任务。 开发人员使用OpenUSD来表示几何体和仿真属性，而NVIDIA Omniverse AI Agent技能则通过连接到实时Isaac Sim应用程序的Codex CLI来指导转换和验证过程。
