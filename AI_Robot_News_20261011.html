The Motley Fool — What Is Physical AI? Nvidia Is the Stock I'd Buy to Own It.

Nvidia calls physical AI the software and hardware enabling artificial intelligence to operate in the real world, such as in warehouse robots and self-driving cars. Although rivals like AMD are investing heavily through acquisitions—such as AMD's planned purchase of World Labs for approximately $8.2 billion—physical AI remains a small portion of Nvidia's overall business. In fiscal 2026, Nvidia's automotive revenue was about $2.35 billion, and its broader physical AI sales reached around $6 billion, representing under 3% of its $215.9 billion total revenue. Nvidia sells data center chips for training, simulation platforms like Cosmos, and on-board computers such as Jetson Thor and DRIVE Hyperion. Nvidia's CFO Colette Kress noted that Amazon will adopt its full physical AI stack including Omniverse, Cosmos, Isaac, and Jetson. Meanwhile, AMD's stock valuation trades at approximately 41 times expected 2027 earnings per share, whereas Nvidia trades at about 15 times expected earnings per share for fiscal 2028.

The Robot Report — Boston Dynamics gives more insight into its redesigned humanoid hand

Boston Dynamics recently unveiled its redesigned humanoid robot hand featuring four fingers and 13 degrees of freedom with direct actuation, built specifically for mass manufacturing and high-fidelity simulation. In an interview with The Robot Report, Alberto Rodriguez, director of robot behavior for Atlas, explained that the company dropped the pinky finger, reduced the gripper size, and utilized 13 identical encapsulated actuators without fragile cables crossing joints. Drawing from previous trials where earlier hands could lift over 100 pounds, Boston Dynamics optimized the design for simplicity, modularity, and actuation transparency rather than increasing strength. The company builds hands in-house to integrate seamlessly with its product roadmap and tests them across representative industrial tasks such as using handheld power tools, handling cables, and fishing for screws.

总结：波士顿动力公司近日展示了其重新设计的仿人机器人手，该手拥有四根手指和13个自由度并采用直接驱动，专为大规模制造和高保真模拟而构建。 在接受《机器人报告》采访时，阿特拉斯机器人行为总监阿尔贝托·罗德里格斯解释称，该公司去掉了小指，缩小了抓取器尺寸，并使用了13个完全封装且没有脆弱电缆跨越关节的相同执行器。 借鉴早期手部可举起超过100磅重物的以往试验经验，波士顿动力公司将设计优化为注重简单性、模块化和驱动透明度，而不是盲目增加强度。 该公司坚持自主研发手部以无缝契合其产品路线图，并在使用手持电动工具、处理电缆和搜寻螺丝等具有代表性的工业任务中对这些手部进行了测试。

WRIF Rocks Detroit — Detroit Debuts Boston Dynamics’ Robot Dogs for Urban Innovation Testing

Bedrock Detroit and Boston Dynamics are testing Spot robot dogs in downtown Detroit to turn the city into a proving ground for future technology. Kevin Mull, Bedrock Detroit's senior director for the Office of Urban Strategy, stated that the robots will test picking up litter as small as a cigarette butt starting as early as the following week. Operating as an autonomous mobile sensing platform, Spot is also being evaluated for building inspections, mapping tricky areas, security patrols in parking garages, and package handling, such as retrieving packages delivered by drones to roofs. The project has been in development for six to eight months, and officials will monitor the testing for a few more months before potentially moving to a live setting. Quadruped robots have previously been deployed in other locations, such as guarding construction sites in Cleveland and by the Taylor Police Department in Wayne County in March 2024.

总结：Bedrock底特律公司与波士顿动力公司正在底特律市中心测试Spot机器狗，旨在将该市打造成未来技术的试验场。 Bedrock底特律城市战略办公室高级总监凯文·马尔表示，这些机器人最早将于下周开始测试捡起小至烟头大小的垃圾。 作为自主移动传感平台，Spot还被评估用于建筑检查、复杂区域测绘、停车场安全巡逻以及包裹处理（例如取走无人机投递到屋顶的包裹）。 该项目已经开发了六到八个月，官员们将在测试几个月后评估是否将其投入实际运行。 四足机器人此前已在其他地方部署过，例如克利夫兰的建筑工地安保，以及韦恩县泰勒警察局于2024年3月部署的机器人。

NVIDIA Developer — 5 Steps to Create SimReady Assets for Robotics with Frontier AI Models | NVIDIA Technical Blog

Preparing Computer-Aided Design assets for robotics simulation requires configuring and validating materials, collision geometry, joints, and other physics properties before testing robot behavior. NVIDIA Omniverse libraries, guided by SimReady Foundation specifications and agentic NVIDIA skills, provide a structured workflow to convert and validate simulation-ready assets. Frontier AI models, such as GPT-6 Astra, can assist developers by interpreting reference materials and calling Omniverse tools, reducing manual preparation. The workflow for preparing an ABB Robotics YuMi robot involves five steps: STEP-file conversion, appearance validation, physics configuration, SimReady validation, and a final pick-and-place task using both arms and grippers in NVIDIA Isaac Sim 6.1. Technologies supporting this process include OpenUSD, SimReady specifications, NVIDIA Omniverse libraries, Isaac Sim, and Codex CLI.

总结：为机器人模拟准备计算机辅助设计资产，需要在测试机器人行为之前配置和验证材料、碰撞几何体、关节以及其他物理属性。 在SimReady基金会规范和智能NVIDIA技能的指导下，NVIDIA Omniverse库提供了一个结构化的工作流程，用于转换和验证模拟就绪资产。 GPT-6 Astra等前沿人工智能模型可以通过解释参考材料和调用Omniverse工具来协助开发人员，从而减少手动准备工作。 准备ABB机器人YuMi的工作流程包括五个步骤：STEP文件转换、外观验证、物理配置、SimReady验证，以及在NVIDIA Isaac Sim 6.1中使用双臂和夹爪的最终抓取和放置任务。 支持该过程的技术包括OpenUSD、SimReady规范、NVIDIA Omniverse库、Isaac Sim和Codex CLI。
