The Robot Report — Boston Dynamics gives more insight into its redesigned humanoid hand

Boston Dynamics recently revealed its redesigned humanoid robot hand featuring four fingers, 13 degrees of freedom, and direct actuation, built specifically for mass manufacturing and high-fidelity simulation to enable sim-to-real reinforcement learning. Alberto Rodriguez, director of robot behavior for Atlas at Boston Dynamics, explained that having a product roadmap helped determine the right level of complexity, balancing dexterity, strength, ruggedness, cost, reparability, and sensing. The new hand drops the pinky finger, reduces the gripper size, and uses 13 identical, completely encapsulated actuators with no fragile cables crossing joints. Drawing from earlier trials where hands could lift over 100 lb. , the company decided to decrease strength slightly to improve actuation transparency while tackling past design challenges. Boston Dynamics designs its robot hands entirely in-house because they are integral to the humanoid's value proposition. Durability and reliability are ensured through a hardware design team, early collaboration between behavior and hardware teams using simulation and mockups, early prototypes for testing, and rigorous validation and verification tests. The new hand has been tested on various representative industrial tasks such as using handheld power tools, handling cables, and fishing for screws, alongside bring-up tasks to explore capability limits.

总结：波士顿动力公司近日展示了其重新设计的仿人机器人手，该四指手拥有13个自由度并采用直接驱动，专为大规模制造以及实现仿真到真实强化学习的高保真模拟而设计。 波士顿动力公司Atlas机器人行为总监阿尔伯托·罗德里格斯解释称，产品路线图有助于确定合适的复杂度，平衡灵巧性、力量、坚固性、成本、可修复性和感知。 新设计取消了小指，减小了夹持器尺寸，并使用了13个完全封装且跨关节无脆弱电缆的相同执行器。 吸取了早期手部能举起100磅以上重物的经验，该公司决定略微降低力量以改善执行器透明度。 波士顿动力公司选择内部自主制造机器人手，因为手部是仿人机器人价值主张的核心。 耐用性与可靠性通过硬件设计团队、行为与硬件团队在早期利用模拟和模型进行协作、早期原型测试以及严格的验证和确认测试来确保。 新手的测试涵盖了各种代表性工业任务，例如使用手持电动工具、处理电缆、搜寻螺丝，以及用于探索能力极限的启动任务。

WRIF Rocks Detroit — Detroit Debuts Boston Dynamics’ Robot Dogs for Urban Innovation Testing

Bedrock Detroit and Boston Dynamics are testing Spot robot dogs in downtown Detroit to turn the city into a proving ground for future technology. Kevin Mull, Bedrock Detroit's senior director for the Office of Urban Strategy, presented a robot named Astro, which closely matches the machines deploying to the streets. The testing includes evaluating an autonomous mobile sensing platform equipped with a robotic arm to pick up litter as small as a cigarette butt starting as early as the following week. Additional potential applications include building inspections, mapping tricky areas, reaching unsafe locations, security patrols in areas like parking garages, and package handling, such as delivering a package dropped by a drone on a roof into a building. The project has been developed quietly for six to eight months, navigating the challenges of operating in human-occupied environments that require advanced navigation and agility to climb stairs and pass through doors. While some uses are already functional, litter pickup is an emerging solution being tested by Boston Dynamics in one of its first live environments. Officials will monitor the test mode for a few more months before considering a transition to a live setting, adding to previous deployments of quadruped robots guarding construction sites in Cleveland and the Taylor Police Department's deployment in March 2024.

总结：贝德罗克底特律公司与波士顿动力公司正在底特律市中心测试Spot机器狗，旨在将该市打造成未来技术的试验场。 贝德罗克底特律城市战略办公室高级总监凯文·穆尔在采访中展示了一台名为Astro的机器人，该机器与即将走上街头的机器高度一致。 测试内容包括评估配备机械臂的自主移动传感平台，最早于下周开始捡拾小至烟头大小的垃圾。 其他潜在应用还包括建筑检查、测绘复杂区域、到达不安全地点、在停车场等区域进行安全巡逻，以及处理包裹（例如将无人机投递在屋顶上的包裹送入建筑内部）。 该项目已默默推进了六到八个月，克服了在人类居住环境中运作、需要具备惊人导航能力以及上下楼梯和穿过门的灵敏度等挑战。 虽然部分用途已经运行良好，但捡拾垃圾是一项新兴解决方案，底特律成为了波士顿动力公司测试该功能的首批地点之一。 官方将在未来几个月密切监控测试模式，随后再考虑进入实际运营环境，此前克利夫兰的建筑工地以及韦恩县泰勒警察局于2024年3月也曾部署过四足机器人。

NVIDIA Developer — 5 Steps to Create SimReady Assets for Robotics with Frontier AI Models | NVIDIA Technical Blog

Preparing CAD assets for robotics simulation requires converting geometry to OpenUSD and configuring materials, collision geometry, joints, and other physics properties using tools like NVIDIA Omniverse libraries, SimReady Foundation specifications, and agentic NVIDIA skills. Frontier AI models assist developers by interpreting reference materials and calling Omniverse tools to reduce manual preparation, demonstrated here by preparing an ABB Robotics YuMi robot with GPT-6 Astra. The five-step workflow covers STEP-file conversion, appearance validation, physics configuration, SimReady validation, and a final pick-and-place task using both arms and grippers in NVIDIA Isaac Sim 6.1. SimReady establishes asset requirements for specific simulation use cases, helping identify missing collision geometry or incorrect joints that can cause simulation failures like objects passing through grippers. Key supporting technologies include OpenUSD for geometric and simulation properties representation, SimReady Foundation for specifications and validation guidance, NVIDIA Omniverse libraries for content conversion and preparation, NVIDIA Isaac Sim for physics configuration and behavior testing, and NVIDIA Omniverse AI Agent skills like the CAD-to-SimReady skill. The workflow setup requires installing Isaac Sim 6.1, creating a reference folder with specifications, STEP files, and reference images, installing a frontier AI model such as GPT-6 Astra, and launching Codex CLI in the Isaac Sim directory to connect a remote Python server to the live application.

总结：为机器人仿真准备CAD资产不仅需要将几何体转换为OpenUSD，还必须使用NVIDIA Omniverse库、SimReady Foundation规范以及智能NVIDIA技能来配置和验证材质、碰撞几何体、关节和其他物理属性。 前沿人工智能模型可通过解释参考资料并调用Omniverse工具来协助开发人员减少手动准备工作，本文以GPT-6 Astra协助准备ABB机器人YuMi为例进行说明。 该五步工作流涵盖STEP文件转换、外观验证、物理配置、SimReady验证，以及最后在NVIDIA Isaac Sim 6.1中使用双臂和夹持器进行的抓放任务。 SimReady为特定仿真用例定义了资产要求，有助于发现缺少碰撞几何体或关节错误等会导致仿真失败的问题。 核心支持技术包括用于表示几何与仿真属性的OpenUSD、提供规范与验证指导的SimReady Foundation、用于内容转换与准备的NVIDIA Omniverse库、用于配置物理与测试机器人行为的NVIDIA Isaac Sim，以及诸如CAD-to-SimReady等NVIDIA Omniverse人工智能代理技能。 工作流设置要求安装Isaac Sim 6.1，在目录中创建包含规范、STEP文件和参考图像的参考文件夹，安装如GPT-6 Astra等前沿人工智能模型，并在Isaac Sim目录中启动Codex CLI以将远程Python服务器连接到实时应用程序。

Robotics & Automation News — Interview: Humanoid robots are advancing fast. Manufacturing them at scale is the next challenge

As humanoid robot developers move machines from pilot projects to factories, scaling production presents a major engineering challenge. Building a small number of units allows manual adjustments, but producing hundreds or thousands requires consistent quality, predictable costs, and reliable supply chains. In a Q&A, Steve Ricketts of Misumi Americas and Fictiv discusses that humanoids contain dozens of actuators, motors, precision gears, bearings, sensors, and machined components. Scaling up strains components like high-precision gearing, harmonic drives, and rare-earth magnets, while five-axis machining and precision inspection will face capacity constraints.

总结：随着人形机器人开发商将机器从试点项目推向工厂，规模化生产带来了一项重大的工程挑战。 制造少量机器允许手动调整，但生产数百或数千台则需要稳定的质量、可预测的成本和可靠的供应链。 在一次问答中，来自Misumi Americas和Fictiv的史蒂夫·里基茨探讨了人形机器人包含数十个执行器、电机、精密齿轮、轴承、传感器和机加工部件。 随着产量增加，高精密齿轮、谐波减速器和稀土永磁体等部件将面临压力，同时五轴加工和精密检测也将面临产能限制。
