Amazon Web Services (AWS) — NVIDIA Cosmos 3 on AWS: Omnimodal World Models for Physical AI

Amazon Web Services introduced NVIDIA Cosmos 3, an omnimodal world model released in May 2026 designed to address data scarcity in physical AI by unifying vision reasoning, world generation, and action prediction into a single forward pass. Cosmos 3 replaces traditional multi-model pipelines that chain vision-language models, video generation, and policy networks, eliminating contextual loss and intermediate text bottlenecks. Utilizing a Mixture-of-Transformers architecture with a Reasoner tower and a Generator tower, the model processes multimodal inputs to generate physically coherent video predictions, trajectories, and gripper commands simultaneously for applications such as warehouse robot picking.

总结：亚马逊云科技介绍了于2026年5月发布的NVIDIA Cosmos 3全模态世界模型，该模型旨在通过将视觉推理、世界生成和动作预测统一到单一前向传播中，来解决物理AI中的数据稀缺问题。 Cosmos 3取代了将视觉语言模型、视频生成和策略网络串联起来的传统多模型流水线，消除了上下文丢失和中间文本瓶颈。 该模型利用包含推理塔和生成塔的Transformer混合架构，处理多模态输入，从而同时生成物理一致的视频预测、末端执行器轨迹和夹爪执行指令，适用于仓库机器人抓取等应用。

Internationally, Ubtech showcased its Walker C1 at Slovenia's MOS fair, signing an exclusive distribution agreement with Hiproject for the Balkans, and held talks with Kazakh President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev regarding a potential factory in Almaty. These developments follow the commissioning of its 14,000-square-meter Super Smart Factory in Liuzhou capable of producing one robot every ten minutes, even as the company's stock experienced a 40% decline since the start of the year.

Infomance — This Country Just Opened a Humanoid Robot Factory Where Robots Help Build Other Robots

UBTECH Robotics, in partnership with Siemens Digital Industries Software, launched the world's first humanoid robot intelligent manufacturing facility in Liuzhou, Guangxi, spanning 14,000 square meters with an annual output capacity of 10,000 units. The plant utilizes a 'robots building robots' framework where automated systems like Cruzr Y1 and S2 handle material loading and logistics, managed by an integrated digital smart brain. The facility primarily assembles Walker S and Cruzr platforms, with finished units undergoing rigorous electromechanical testing and optical examination before entering an automated high-density warehouse.

总结：优必选科技与西门子数字化工业软件合作，在广西南宁柳州启动了全球首座人形机器人智能制造基地，占地14000平方米，年产能达10000台。 该工厂采用“机器人制造机器人”的框架，由Cruzr Y1和S2等自动化系统处理物料搬运和物流，并由集成的数字智能大脑进行管理。 该设施主要组装Walker S和Cruzr平台，成品在进入自动化高密度仓库之前需经过严格的机电测试和光学检查。
