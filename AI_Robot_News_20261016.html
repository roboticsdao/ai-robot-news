Interesting Engineering — Inside Boston Dynamics' push to build Atlas humanoids in just one hour

Boston Dynamics released a video showcasing its prototype operations team that builds and maintains the Atlas humanoid robot through precision machining, assembly, and repair. The team currently builds a robot in about a day, with a target of reducing the process to roughly an hour next year, and longer-term ambitions of bringing build times down to minutes. The prototype facility combines engineering and manufacturing in-house, performing precision machining and gear cutting to shorten the feedback loop between design and testing. This setup allows engineers to discuss requirements directly with machinists, modify parts, and evaluate revised components on physical hardware the same day or the following day. The facility also develops manufacturing methods, such as selecting cutting tools and incorporating robotic arms into assembly tasks. The approach coincides with the introduction of four-finger hands for Atlas as the company emphasizes manufacturing efficiency and prototype testing for wider industrial use.

总结：波士顿动力公司发布了一段视频，展示了其原型运营团队如何通过精密机加工、组装和修理来建造和维护Atlas人形机器人。 该团队目前大约需要一天时间建造一个机器人，目标是将明年的流程缩短至大约一小时，更长远的宏伟目标是将建造时间缩短至几分钟。 该原型工厂将工程和制造内部结合，进行精密机加工和齿轮切削，以缩短设计与测试之间的反馈回路。 这种设置允许工程师直接与机械师讨论需求、修改零件，并在当天或第二天在物理硬件上评估修订后的组件。 该 facility 还开发制造方法，例如选择切削工具并将机械臂纳入装配任务。 这种方法与为Atlas引入四指手相吻合，凸显了公司在更广泛工业应用中对制造效率和原型测试的重视。

TechGig — NVIDIA unveils Jetson Orin Nano 2 for edge AI robotics

NVIDIA announced the Jetson Orin Nano 2 on August 25, 2026, as a successor to the Jetson Orin Nano Super for edge AI robotics applications such as home robots and drones. The board features Arm Cortex-A78 cores, 1,536 CUDA cores, an Ampere GPU, and 8GB of LPDDR5X-7500 memory delivering 120 GB/s bandwidth. It provides 78 TOPS of AI performance, offering double the inference capabilities and 40% better power efficiency than its predecessor. Both the module and developer kit are expected to arrive in the first half of 2027, with pricing yet to be disclosed.

总结：英伟达于2026年8月25日宣布推出Jetson Orin Nano 2，作为Jetson Orin Nano Super的后继产品，用于家庭机器人和无人机等边缘AI机器人应用。 该板卡采用Arm Cortex-A78内核、1536个CUDA内核、Ampere GPU以及8GB的LPDDR5X-7500内存，提供120 GB/s的内存带宽。 它可提供78 TOPS的AI性能，其推理能力是上一代的两倍，能效提升40%。 模块和开发者套件预计于2027年上半年推出，定价尚未公布。

总结：弘前大学基于日本外科学会的实证研究成果，在日本国内首次开展了使用手术支援机器人的“远程手术”临床应用。 该项目通过NTT东公司的商用光纤线路连接弘前大学医学部附属医院与津轻综合医院，使用“hinotoriTM外科手术机器人系统”支援消化系统癌症患者的手术。 包括2026年8实施的第一例在内，目前已安全进行了5例远程手术，旨在解决专科医生地域分布不均以及外科医生短缺等问题。
