marketscreener.com — Boston Dynamics and GlobalFoundries Expand Robotics Collaboration

Boston Dynamics and GlobalFoundries announced an expanded collaboration to deploy Spot for autonomous enterprise asset management across GlobalFoundries' manufacturing sites in Malta, New York; Burlington, Vermont; Dresden, Germany; and Singapore.
