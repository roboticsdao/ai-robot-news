Robotics & Automation News — Boston Dynamics launches next-generation Stretch robot with major reliability upgrades

Boston Dynamics unveiled Stretch Gen 2, the next generation of its autonomous mobile trailer unloading robot, featuring major hardware and structural upgrades based on data from over 100,000 operating hours and tens of millions of unloaded boxes. Brian Donnelly, vice president and general manager of logistics robotics at Boston Dynamics, stated the updates target downtime causes and improve serviceability. Stretch Gen 2 incorporates heavy-duty automotive-grade connectors, an upgraded gripper with new valve systems and precise sensing, and continuous rotation joints moving beyond 360 degrees without hard stops. It cuts 81 percent of internal cabling, relocates and recesses the emergency stop, and uses new exterior panel materials. Maintenance time is reduced, with a full swerve drive swap taking about one hour. The robot features standardized fasteners, onboard diagnostics, a redesigned computing platform supporting regular software updates, and a multipick feature. It unloads up to 1000 cases per hour, operates for two shifts on a battery that charges to 90 percent in about 75 minutes, includes onboard lighting, and utilizes a pre-trained vision system.

总结：波士顿动力发布了新一代自主移动挂车卸货机器人Stretch Gen 2。 该机器人在超过10万小时的运营数据和数千万箱货物卸载经验的基础上，进行了重大的硬件和结构升级。 波士顿动力物流机器人副总裁兼总经理布莱恩·唐纳利表示，此次升级旨在针对停机原因并提升可维修性。 Stretch Gen 2采用了重型汽车级连接器、带有新阀门系统和更精确传感的升级版抓取器、以及可超越360度且无硬限位的连续旋转关节。 该机器人削减了81%的内部电缆，重新设计并嵌入了急停按钮，并采用了新型外壳面板材料。 维护时间大幅缩短，例如更换全转向驱动装置约需一小时。 该机器人配备了标准化的坚固紧固件、车载诊断工具、支持定期软件更新的重新设计车载计算平台以及多件抓取功能。 其卸货速度最高可达每小时1,000箱，电池续航两班倒且快充可在约75分钟内充至90%，并配有车载照明和免编程预训练视觉系统。

NVIDIA Developer — Build Training-Ready Physical AI Datasets with Two Prompts in NVIDIA Cosmos | NVIDIA Technical Blog

Nvidia has introduced NVIDIA Cosmos, an open platform designed to help developers build training-ready physical AI datasets at scale using cloud-native blueprints and composable skills. As part of this platform, NVIDIA Cosmos Data Factory provides five reference blueprints and six composable skills for tasks such as data curation, processing, and synthetic data generation. Using coding agents and prompts, developers can run preflight checks, set up their environments, and execute workflows without manual steering. The platform includes five specific use cases: generating driving scenarios for autonomous vehicles, augmenting image attributes for search and re-identification, transforming videos across different weather and lighting conditions, generating rare event videos, and creating defect images for visual inspection. The system utilizes tools like Cosmos Curator, Dataset Search, Cosmos3-Super-Image2Video, Cosmos3-Nano, and Cosmos Evaluator to ensure physical plausibility, check motion artifacts, and automatically label generated datasets.

总结：Nvidia推出了NVIDIA Cosmos，这是一个开放平台，旨在帮助开发者使用云原生蓝图和可组合技能大规模构建训练就绪的物理AI数据集。 作为该平台的一部分，NVIDIA Cosmos Data Factory提供了五个参考蓝图和六个可组合技能，用于数据策划、处理和合成数据生成等任务。 开发者可以使用编码代理和提示词运行预检、设置环境并执行工作流，而无需人工干预。 该平台包含五个具体的用例：为自动驾驶汽车生成驾驶场景、扩充用于搜索和重新识别的图像属性、跨不同天气和光照条件转换视频、生成罕见事件视频以及创建用于视觉检查的缺陷图像。 该系统利用Cosmos Curator、Dataset Search、Cosmos3-Super-Image2Video、Cosmos3-Nano和Cosmos Evaluator等工具来确保物理合理性、检查运动伪影并自动标记生成的数据集。

Chosunbiz — Korean team wins innovation award, places 2nd in Nvidia robotics AI contest

A South Korean pre-startup team named DDD won an industrial innovation award and placed second overall in Nvidia's inaugural Video to Data Challenge. Supported by the physical AI venture studio Carbon Black, DDD developed technology that converts videos of working people into 3D data used to train robots, achieving a reconstruction error of about 1 cm or less. The competition evaluated participants on data generation cost, automation level, and implementation complexity for industrial settings. The winners were announced on December 13 at an Nvidia session during the Conference on Robot Learning 2026. Team lead Choi Jae-gu stated that obtaining robot training data from ordinary videos without expensive equipment can reduce data collection costs and time.

总结：韩国物理AI领域的初创前团队DDD在Nvidia举办的首届Video to Data Challenge中获得工业创新奖并获得总成绩第二名。 在物理AI风险工作室Carbon Black的支持下，DDD开发了将人类工作视频转换为训练机器人所需的3D数据的技术，实现了约1厘米或更小的重建误差。 该大赛评估了参与者在工业环境下的数据生成成本、自动化水平和实现复杂度。 获奖名单于12月13日在机器人学习会议2026的一个Nvidia专场上公布。 团队技术负责人崔宰求表示，无需昂贵专用设备就能从普通视频中获取机器人训练数据的技术，有助于降低数据收集的成本和时间。
