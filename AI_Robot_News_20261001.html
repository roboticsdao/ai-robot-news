Boston.com — ICE settles contract to buy Boston Dynamics robots for $1.3 million

Immigration and Customs Enforcement has finalized a $1. 3 million contract to buy four remotely operated, quadruped Spot robots from Boston Dynamics. According to the contract publicly listed on Monday, the robots will be used to search tunnels at border crossings. The Department of Homeland Security will use the Quadruped Unmanned Ground Vehicle and accessories to combat transnational human trafficking, drug smuggling, and the presence of cartels and foreign gangs in areas unsafe for humans. A Boston Dynamics spokesperson stated that Homeland Security Investigations is handling the deployment, rather than Enforcement & Removal Operations. The robots are outfitted with a CBRNe kit made by RADeCO, a partner of Boston Dynamics, to detect chemical, biological, radiological, nuclear, and explosive hazards. Boston Dynamics emphasized that the robots are legally prohibited from being weaponized or used for harm, intimidation, immigrant detentions, or deportations. If misused, Boston Dynamics stated it would take immediate action, including voiding the warranty, refusing software updates, terminating licenses, taking legal action, and halting future sales. DHS did not respond to requests for comment.

总结：移民和海关执法局已敲定一项130万美元的合同，向波士顿动力公司购买四台远程操作的Spot四足机器人。 根据周一公开的合同，这些机器人将被用于搜查边境口岸的隧道。 国土安全部将利用该四足无人地面车辆及配件，在对人类不安全的区域打击跨国贩运人口、贩毒以及贩毒集团和外国帮派活动。 波士顿动力公司发言人表示，该部署由国土安全调查局而非执法和遣返行动局负责。 机器人配备了由波士顿动力合作伙伴RADeCO制造的CBRNe套件，可探测化学、生物、放射性、核及爆炸危险。 波士顿动力强调，法律禁止将机器人武器化或用于伤害、恐吓、移民拘留或驱逐。 若遭滥用，该公司将采取立即行动，包括作废保修、拒绝所有未来软件更新、终止许可证、采取法律行动以及可能永不再向该客户销售。 国土安全部未回应置评请求。

manufacturingdigital.com — IFR: Food & Logistics Pushed US Robot Automation Past Japan

The US installed 38,400 industrial robots in 2025, a 12% increase that allowed it to overtake Japan and become the world's second-largest market after China, according to the International Federation of Robotics World Robotics 2026 report. Global installations rose 11% to 603,000 units, with total operational stock reaching 5. 079 million units. IFR President Jane Heffner stated that US demand rebounded strongly, driven by the food industry, warehousing, logistics, and medical sectors, while the automotive industry remained the largest customer. In 2025, the US automotive sector installed 13,500 robots, down 1%, while food and beverage installations rose 30% to 2,900 units. China installed 354,000 robots, representing 59% of global demand. Global sales of professional service robots for transport and logistics rose 21% to 117,500 units. The IFR forecasts global installations of 655,000 units in 2026 and 806,000 in 2029, driven long-term by reshoring policies and labour scarcity, despite short-term trade tensions and uncertainty. Conversely, installations fell in Germany, down to fewer than 25,000 units amid Volkswagen's cost-cutting plant closures, and in Japan, where installations dropped 19% to 36,219 units following restructuring plans by companies like Nissan.

总结：根据国际机器人联合会发布的《2026世界机器人》报告，美国在2025年安装了38,400台工业机器人，同比增长12%，超越日本成为仅次于中国的全球第二大市场。 全球安装量上升11%至603,000台，全球运营库存达到507. 9万台。 国际机器人联合会主席简·赫夫纳表示，美国机器人需求强劲反弹，增长来自食品行业以及仓储、物流或医疗等领域，而汽车行业依然是最大的客户。 2025年，美国汽车行业安装了13,500台机器人，下降1%，而食品和饮料行业的安装量上升30%至2,900台。 中国安装了354,000台机器人，占全球需求的59%。 全球运输和物流专业服务机器人销量上升21%至117,500台。 国际机器人联合会预测2026年全球安装量为655,000台，2029年为806,000台，长远来看，回流政策和劳动力短缺将创造机遇，尽管短期内贸易紧张局势和不确定性将对需求造成压力。 相反，由于大众汽车因削减成本而关闭工厂，德国的安装量下降至不到25,000台；而随着日产等公司的重组计划，日本的安装量下降19%至36,219台。

Citigroup is the administrative agent for the term loan, while Wells Fargo serves as the administrative agent for the credit lines. In 2026, Tesla plans to spend more than $25 billion to expand factory operations and its autonomous Cybercab robotaxi fleet, with capital expenditure expected to increase further. Musk stated on a July earnings call that the company should spend on capital expenditures as fast as possible without being too wasteful.
