동아사이언스 — Boston Dynamics humanoid Atlas masters backflips and breakdance with ZEST

Researchers from the Robotics and Artificial Intelligence Lab (RAI) in the United States and Boston Dynamics have developed a reinforcement learning framework called "ZEST" that utilizes diverse motion data, including motion capture, video, and animation, to enable robots to learn various movements without separate training for specific motions. Published in Science Robotics on August 12, the framework automatically estimates motion difficulty and has been applied to Boston Dynamics' humanoid robot Atlas, Unitree's small humanoid G1, and Boston Dynamics' quadrupedal robot Spot. During training, the robots successfully executed complex full-body movements such as crawling, breakdancing, backflips, cartwheels, and handstands, demonstrating enhanced generalization across different robotic body forms.

总结：美国机器人与人工智能实验室（RAI）与波士顿动力公司的联合研究团队开发了一种名为“ZEST”的强化学习框架。该框架利用运动捕捉、视频和动画等多样化运动数据，使机器人能够学习广泛的动作，而无需针对每个特定动作进行单独训练。该成果于8月12日发表在国际期刊《Science Robotics》上，系统会自动估计每个动作的难度，并已被应用到波士顿动力的双足人形机器人Atlas、宇树科技的小型人形机器人G1以及波士顿动力的四足机器人Spot上。经过训练，这些机器人成功执行了诸如爬行、霹雳舞、后空翻、侧手翻和倒立等复杂的全身动作，展现出在不同机器人机身形态之下的广泛泛化能力。

interestingengineering.com — Samsung advances in-house humanoid robot development with cost edge

Samsung Electronics has been internally developing a humanoid robot for years—separate from its RX division and Rainbow Robotics subsidiary—that reportedly outperforms domestic competitors. Samsung is leveraging its home-appliance business motor technology, such as mass-produced brushless DC (BLDC) motors, to build cost-effective robotic actuators, which can comprise up to 60% of humanoid production costs. Software development includes the Gauss large language model, the Shallow-π Vision-Language-Action (VLA) model developed by Samsung Research that reduces computational requirements and achieves 17 decisions per second, a World Model predicting physical consequences, and a Robot Data Factory planned at its Gumi campus using operational data from semiconductor and appliance production lines.

总结：三星电子多年来一直在内部秘密开发人形机器人，该项目独立于其公开宣布的机器人体验（RX）部门和彩虹机器人（Rainbow Robotics）子公司，且据报道其性能已超越多个本土竞争对手。三星正在利用其家电业务的电机技术（如大规模生产的无刷直流电机）来开发机器人的执行器，而执行器成本最高可占人形机器人生产成本的60%，这为其带来了显著的成本优势。在软件方面，三星开发了大语言模型Gauss、由Samsung Research开发的视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型Shallow-π（该模型将计算需求降至常规的三分之一并实现每秒17次决策），以及能够预测物理后果的世界模型，并计划在其龟尾园区建立一个利用半导体和家电生产线真实数据的机器人数据工厂。

Japan Wire by Kyodo News — EXPLAINER: What is Unitree and why are China’s humanoid robot makers racing to list?

Chinese robot maker Unitree has priced its Shanghai initial public offering at 150.8 yuan per share, aiming to raise 6.1 billion yuan ($904 million) to become the first mainland-listed humanoid robot manufacturer. Founded in 2016 by Wang Xingxing, Hangzhou-based Unitree gained initial fame for affordable quadruped robots and subsequently developed G1, H1, and R1 humanoids known for viral demonstration videos. Unitree's 2025 revenue rose over fourfold to nearly 1.7 billion yuan with an adjusted net profit of about 600 million yuan, and overseas sales accounted for over 40%. The company represents China's push into embodied intelligence, though commercial success remains driven largely by university and government-backed educational and research demand. To fund heavy research, training data, and manufacturing capacity investments, other Chinese robot makers are also seeking public listings, including Leju Robotics filing for a ChiNext IPO in May and AgiBot preparing for a Hong Kong IPO in July.

总结：中国机器人制造商宇树科技将其上海首次公开募股定价为每股150.8元人民币，旨在筹集61亿元人民币（9.04亿美元），成为首家在内地上市的人形机器人制造商。宇树由王兴兴于2016年创立，总部位于杭州，最初以价格实惠的四足机器人闻名，随后开发了因病毒式传播的演示视频而备受瞩目的G1、H1和R1人形机器人。宇树2025年的营收增长了四倍多，达到近17亿元人民币，调整后净利润约为6亿元人民币，海外销售额占比超过40%。该公司代表了中国进军具身智能领域的努力，尽管其商业成功目前主要仍受高校和政府支持的教育及研究需求的驱动。为了资助庞大的研发、训练数据和制造能力投资，其他中国机器人制造商也在寻求上市，其中包括乐聚机器人在5月申请在创业板上市，以及智元机器人在7月筹备香港IPO。
