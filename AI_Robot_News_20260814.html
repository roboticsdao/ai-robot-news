동아사이언스 — Boston Dynamics humanoid Atlas masters backflips and breakdance with ZEST

Researchers from the Robotics and Artificial Intelligence Lab and Boston Dynamics developed a reinforcement learning framework named ZEST, which jointly trains on diverse motion data such as motion capture, video, and animation. Published in Science Robotics, the framework automatically estimates motion difficulty and allows robots to generalize behaviors without separate training for each movement. The research team applied ZEST to Boston Dynamics' Atlas and Spot robots as well as Unitree's G1, successfully enabling them to perform complex full-body movements such as backflips, breakdancing, and cartwheels. The team expects ZEST to help robots acquire a broader range of movements and reduce the need for motion-specific control techniques.

总结：美国机器人与人工智能实验室和波士顿动力公司的研究人员开发了一个名为ZEST的强化学习框架，能够通过对动作捕捉、视频和动画等多样化动作数据进行联合训练。该框架发表于《科学·机器人学》杂志，可自动评估动作难度，使机器人无需为每个动作进行单独训练即可泛化行为。研究团队将ZEST应用于波士顿动力公司的Atlas和Spot机器人以及宇树科技的G1机器人，成功使其执行后空翻、霹雳舞和侧手翻等复杂的全身动作，有望减少开发特定动作控制技术的负担。
