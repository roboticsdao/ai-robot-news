Technology Magazine — NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano 2 Unleashes Entry Edge AI Robotics

NVIDIA introduced the Jetson Orin Nano 2, an entry-level edge AI robotics computer designed for physical AI applications such as vision AI platforms, autonomous delivery drones, and monitoring devices. According to Deepu Talla, Vice President of Robotics and Edge AI at NVIDIA, small and medium frontier models have reached the accuracy of last year's largest frontier models, enabling real-time intelligence for edge devices. The system features an 8-core Arm processor, 8GB of system memory, and 78 TOPS of total AI computing power, doubling the inference throughput of the Jetson Orin Nano Super while operating at 15 watts and using 40% less energy than the prior generation. The platform supports lightweight open architectures including Gemma 4, Qwen 3, NVIDIA Nemotron, and NVIDIA Cosmos. Hardware and software partners such as AAEON, Advantech, Aptiv, Connect Tech, and Seeed Studio are developing compatible carrier boards, specialized systems, and custom AI software to support commercial deployment.

总结：英伟达推出了全新的Jetson Orin Nano 2，这是一款面向视觉AI平台、自动配送无人机和监控设备等物理AI应用而设计的入门级边缘AI机器人计算机。 英伟达机器人与边缘AI副总裁Deepu Talla在吹风会上表示，当今的中小型前沿模型已经达到了去年最大前沿模型的准确率，为边缘设备释放了实时智能。 该系统配备了8核Arm处理器、8GB系统内存以及78 TOPS的总AI计算能力，在15瓦功耗下运行时的推理吞吐量是Jetson Orin Nano Super的两倍，且能耗比上一代减少了40%。 该平台支持包括Gemma 4、Qwen 3、NVIDIA Nemotron和NVIDIA Cosmos在内的轻量级开放架构。 研华、AAEON、Aptiv、Connect Tech和Seeed Studio等软硬件合作伙伴正在开发兼容的载板、专用计算系统和定制AI软件，以支持商业部署。

美国之音 — 众议院一小组委员会通过旨在禁止与中共有关的危险机器人的跨党法案

总结：美国众议院能源与商务委员会通信与技术小组委员会于星期二(9月1日)通过了《保卫美国不受敌对机器人主导法案》(GUARD法案)。 该法案由众议院美中战略竞争特设委员会主席穆勒纳尔、共和党众议员杰伊·奥伯诺特和民主党众议员珍妮佛·麦克莱伦于今年6月跨党推出，并获得了美国国际无人驾驶飞行器系统协会(AUVSI)、美国人形机器人公司Agility Robotics、智库保卫民主基金会(FDD)及驼鹿项目(The Bull Moose Project)的支持。 法案要求国家安全机构对中国或其他外国敌手制造的人形及四足机器人进行国家安全风险评估，将构成不可接受风险的产品列入联邦通信委员会(FCC)受管制清单以禁止进口至美国，并在一年内未完成审查的产品将被自动列入该清单。 穆勒纳尔众议员在小组委员会通过后发表声明称，该法案旨在实现与中国竞争环境的公平，并保护美国人免受从内部损害美国的危险机器人的侵害。 接下来该法案将送交全体众议院能源与商务委员会审议。

Escudo Digital — Critical flaws in Unitree's G1 robot enable remote takeover and chain‑reaction infections

Security researcher Olivier Laflamme discovered two vulnerabilities in the Unitree G1 humanoid robot, priced at roughly $20,000, that allow remote takeover and chain-reaction infections. The first flaw involves an AI-based file-processing feature that failed to properly verify saved files, allowing an attacker to place a command-executing file in the system and obtain administrator privileges. The second vulnerability enabled remote attacks via Bluetooth by exploiting a decryption service on the manufacturer's servers, which allowed any free account holder to obtain the secret key needed for wireless communication without needing the owner's account or physical access. Laflamme demonstrated that a compromised G1 robot could potentially infect other nearby units via Bluetooth, creating a chain reaction in environments like warehouses and laboratories. Unitree cooperated with the researcher, fixed the server-side verification flaw about two months after receiving the report, and awarded him a $5,000 reward.

总结：安全研究员Olivier Laflamme在售价约20,000美元的宇树G1人形机器人中发现了两个漏洞，可实现远程控制和连锁感染。 第一个漏洞涉及一个基于AI的文件处理功能，该功能未能正确验证保存的文件，从而允许攻击者在系统中放置可执行命令的文件并获得管理员权限。 第二个漏洞通过利用制造商服务器上的解密服务，使得通过蓝牙进行远程攻击成为可能；该服务允许任何免费账户持有人获取无线通信所需的密钥，而无需拥有者的账户或物理访问权限。 Laflamme演示了受感染的G1机器人可以通过蓝牙感染附近的其他单元，在仓库和实验室等环境中产生连锁反应。 宇树科技与该研究员进行了合作，在收到报告约两个月后修复了服务器端验证漏洞，并向他颁发了5,000美元的奖励。
