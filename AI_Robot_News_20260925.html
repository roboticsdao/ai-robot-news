South China Morning Post — China’s humanoid robot IPO slowdown no threat to firms with ‘genuine strength’

Beijing's recent tightening of IPO approvals for humanoid robot makers poses no threat to companies with clear commercialisation strategies, according to Deloitte China. Dick Kay, Deloitte China's capital market services group national leader, stated at a press conference that market funds are shifting from a blind rush to fine screening. Investors and regulators are placing greater weight on whether companies demonstrate sustainable revenue growth, core technological barriers, and real-world application scenarios, ensuring that funding remains available for qualified players with clear strategies.

总结：德勤中国表示，北京最近对寻求首次公开募股的人形机器人制造商收紧上市审批，对具备清晰商业化战略的公司不会构成威胁，因为合格参与者的融资渠道仍然完全畅通。 德勤中国资本市场服务部全国主管合伙人纪文和在新闻发布会上表示，市场资金正在从过去的盲目跟风转向精细筛选。 投资者和监管机构现在更加重视公司是否具备可持续的收入增长、核心技术壁垒和真实世界的应用场景。
