Unite.AI — Bipartisan Robotics Panel Warns of Widening U.S. Deployment Gap With China

On October 13, 2026, the National Security Commission on Robotics for Advanced Manufacturing released a 90-page Preliminary Report. Co-chaired by Senator Ted Budd, Senator Elissa Slotkin, and SCSP President and CEO Ylli Bajraktari, the bipartisan commission found that while the United States leads in AI, computer vision, autonomy, and software, it lacks the domestic scale to deploy robots, produce systems, and manufacture critical components. Citing International Federation of Robotics data, the report stated that Chinese factories installed about 354,200 industrial robots in 2025, representing 59% of worldwide installations, compared to approximately 38,400 in the United States. By the end of 2024, Chinese firms operated over two million industrial robots, more than five times the U. S. stock of 393,700. The report organizes ten preliminary findings across four pillars and notes that a Final Report with recommendations is scheduled for March 2027.

总结：2026年10月13日，高级制造机器人国家安全委员会发布了一份90页的初步报告。 该两党委员会由参议员特德·巴德、参议员埃莉萨·斯洛特金以及SCSP总裁兼首席执行官伊利·巴伊拉克塔里共同担任主席。 委员会的核心发现是：尽管美国在人工智能、计算机视觉、自主性和软件等领域处于领先地位，但缺乏在国内部署机器人、生产机器人系统以及制造关键组件的规模。 报告援引国际机器人联合会的数据称，2025年中国工厂安装了约354,200台工业机器人，占全球安装量的59%，而美国约为38,400台。 截至2024年底，中国企业的运营工业机器人超过200万台，是美国393,700台存量的五倍多。 该报告围绕四个支柱整理了十项初步结果，并指出包含建议的最终报告预定于2027年3月发布。
