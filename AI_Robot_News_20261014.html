Unite.AI — Bipartisan Robotics Panel Warns of Widening U.S. Deployment Gap With China

On October 13, 2026, the National Security Commission on Robotics for Advanced Manufacturing released its preliminary report, warning of a widening U. S. deployment gap with China in industrial robotics. The report states that Chinese factories installed approximately 354,200 industrial robots in 2025, representing 59% of worldwide installations, compared to approximately 38,400 in the United States. By the end of 2024, Chinese firms operated over two million industrial robots, more than five times the U. S. stock of 393,700. The bipartisan commission, co-chaired by Senators Ted Budd and Elissa Slotkin along with SCSP President Ylli Bajraktari, notes that while the U. S. leads in underlying artificial intelligence and software, it lacks domestic scale and manufacturing capacity for robotic systems.

总结：先进制造机器人国家安全委员会于2026年10月13日发布了初步报告，警告美国在工业机器人部署方面与中国的差距正在扩大。 报告指出，中国工厂在2025年安装了约354,200台工业机器人，占全球安装量的59%，而美国约为38,400台。 截至2024年底，中国企业的运营库存超过两万台工业机器人，是美国393,700台库存的五倍多。 由参议员Ted Budd、Elissa Slotkin以及SCSP总裁Ylli Bajraktari共同担任联合主席的该跨党派委员会指出，尽管美国在底层人工智能和软件方面处于领先地位，但其在机器人系统的国内规模和制造能力方面仍显不足。

Mashable — Authorities investigate public harassment of teen girl by humanoid robot

German authorities in Cologne are investigating two teen boys aged 16 and 19 following allegations that they used a walking humanoid robot to harass a 16-year-old girl in public. Police confiscated the robot on October 13 after receiving reports and dispatching emergency services. Witnesses stated the teens controlled the four-foot-tall, jewelry-decorated robot through busy streets while live-streaming and intentionally bumping into women. Investigators are evaluating whether the robot was programmed or maneuvered in real time, noting it is an unprecedented case of public harassment involving a humanoid robot.

总结：德国科隆当局正在调查两名分别为16岁和19岁的少男，他们被指控利用一台步行机器人当众骚扰一名16岁女孩。 警方在接到举报并出动紧急服务后，于10月13日没收了该机器人。 目击者称，两名少年在繁华街道上通过直播操控这台装饰有人类服饰和珠宝、高4英尺的机器人，故意撞击女性并将此作为笑料。 调查人员正在评估该机器人是被编程执行此类行为还是被实时操纵，并指出这是前所未有的人形机器人公共骚扰案件。
