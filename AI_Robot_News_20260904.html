Japanese startup Enactic develops physical AI by combining robotic hardware with artificial intelligence, creating robots that learn everyday tasks through human demonstrations with a mechanical arm. The company utilizes an unusual open-development strategy, publicly releasing its technology without securing patents to improve its systems using public feedback. Enactic has earned recognition from Nvidia as Japan works to advance its capabilities in the emerging physical AI sector.

总结：日本初创企业Enactic正在开发将人工智能与机器人硬件相结合的物理AI技术，其机器人通过记录人类使用机械臂操作的动作来学习日常任务，例如给枕头套上枕套和将勺子放入容器中。 与许多传统科技公司不同，Enactic采取开放式开发策略，公开公开发布其技术而不申请专利，并通过收集反馈来进一步改进系统。 这一创新模式获得了美国半导体公司英伟达的认可，正值日本寻求推进其在这一新兴领域能力的时期。

Humanoid robots are increasingly entering workplaces to support manufacturing and warehouse operations.
