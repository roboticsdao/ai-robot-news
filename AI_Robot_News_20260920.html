UkrMedia News — Tesla will start selling Optimus robots in 2027

Tesla is planning to launch external sales of its Optimus humanoid robot in the second half of 2027, according to discussions with JPMorgan analysts at its Fremont factory. The timeline aligns with statements made by CEO Elon Musk at the World Economic Forum in Davos in January 2026, where he emphasized that reliability, safety, and functionality must be ensured before mass sales. Designed for monotonous, dangerous, and physically demanding work, Optimus is a key component of Tesla's strategy to move artificial intelligence from the digital realm into the physical world. Tesla's factories are currently serving as the initial testing ground where units perform basic tasks, paving the way for the Gen 3 model, which is engineered specifically for mass production. Tesla aims to eventually establish a production capacity of up to 1 million robots annually, though Musk acknowledges that scaling manufacturing will be exceptionally challenging given that virtually all components are new.

总结：特斯拉计划在2027年下半年开始外部销售其Optimus人形机器人，这一时间表与特斯拉代表在Fremont工厂与摩根大通分析师会面时的通报一致，也符合特斯拉首席执行官埃隆·马斯克于2026年1月在达沃斯世界经济论坛上的表态。 马斯克强调，在启动大规模销售之前，必须确保机器人高度可靠、安全且具备足够广泛的功能。 Optimus旨在执行单调、危险和体力要求高的工作，是特斯拉将人工智能从数字领域带入物理世界战略的一部分。 特斯拉自身的工厂目前是首要测试场，相关单元正在执行简单任务，为专注于量产的第三代产品Gen 3做准备。 特斯拉的长远目标是建立高达每年100万台机器人的生产能力，但马斯克承认，由于几乎所有组件都是崭新的，扩大生产规模将极具挑战性。

tbsnews.net — IPO for humanoid robot maker Boston Dynamics unlikely in 2027: Executive

Boston Dynamics, the humanoid robot unit of Hyundai Motor Group, is unlikely to pursue an initial public offering in 2027 because it has yet to deploy its flagship Atlas robots at scale and remains unprofitable. A senior executive at the parent company stated that market conditions must be evaluated and noted that an IPO may still be years away. Analysts suggest a public debut is more likely between 2029 and 2030, after Hyundai gathers sufficient operational data and improves robot capabilities. Hyundai acquired a controlling stake in Boston Dynamics in 2021 and announced plans in July to make it a wholly owned subsidiary by acquiring SoftBank's approximately 10% stake in a transaction reportedly valued at about 500 billion won ($371. 51 million). Boston Dynamics recorded a 2025 loss of 528. 4 billion won, bringing total losses from 2021 through 2025 to nearly 1. 7 trillion won, according to a filing from Hyundai Glovis. Meanwhile, Hyundai plans to build a factory capable of producing 30,000 robots a year by 2028 and begin deploying humanoid robots at its US plant in Georgia that year.

总结：现代汽车集团旗下的人型机器人企业波士顿动力公司（Boston Dynamics）由于尚未大规模部署其旗舰Atlas机器人且处于亏损状态，预计不太可能在2027年进行首次公开募股。 母公司的一位高级高管表示，需要观察市场条件，上市可能还需要数年时间。 分析师认为，IPO更有可能在2029年至2030年之间进行，届时现代汽车已收集到大量运营数据并提升了机器人的能力。 现代汽车于2021年收购了波士顿动力公司的控股权，并于7月宣布通过收购软银约10%的股份将其全资控股，据报道该交易价值约为500亿韩元（$371. 51 million）。 根据现代现代格罗唯视（Hyundai Glovis）提交的文件，波士顿动力2025年的亏损为528. 4亿韩元，2021年至2025年的总亏损接近1. 7万亿韩元。 与此同时，现代汽车计划到2028年建立一座年产3万台机器人的工厂，并计划于同年在其佐治亚州工厂开始部署人形机器人。

Hoodline — ICE Plans to Buy Boston Dynamics Robot Dogs, Burlington Protesters Push Back

Federal immigration authorities are considering purchasing up to four Spot four-legged robots from Massachusetts-based Boston Dynamics for as much as $2 million, according to a Department of Homeland Security procurement plan. The proposed acquisition has sparked weekly protests outside Immigration and Customs Enforcement's processing center in Burlington, where demonstrators argue the technology escalates operations and increases the distance between decision-makers and affected individuals. Officials describe the robots as tools for dangerous situations rather than weapons or replacements for human officers, and state they will be reviewed for compliance with law-enforcement policies. Boston Dynamics prohibits weaponizing its general-purpose mobile robots, a policy established in October 2022. The purchase follows a separate $16. 7 million contract awarded by ICE to Compliant Technologies LLC for 6,000 G. L. O. V. E. electric shock devices.

总结：根据国土安全部的采购计划，联邦移民当局正考虑购买多达四个来自马萨诸塞州波士顿动力公司的Spot四足机器人，金额高达$2 million。 这一拟议的采购引发了伯灵顿移民和海关执法局处理中心外的每周抗议活动，抗议者认为该技术使行动升级，并拉大了决策者与受影响人员之间的距离。 官员们将这些机器人描述为应对危险情况的工具，并非武器或人类警官的替代品，并表示将对其进行审查以确保符合执法政策。 波士顿动力公司在2022年10月确立了禁止将其通用移动机器人武器化的政策。 在此次采购之前，移民和海关执法局向肯塔基州的Compliant Technologies LLC授予了一份价值$16. 7 million的独立合同，用于采购6,000个G. Interesting Engineering — Figure introduces new humanoid robot neural network

Figure has released Helix 2. 5, a neural network tested in 30 unfamiliar Bay Area homes with no prior data collection, fine-tuning, or exposure to the objects it handled. The humanoid successfully completed 56 percent of tasks such as tidying toys, folding towels, and making beds. By contrast, the same network trained from scratch without Figure's Index human behavior dataset achieved a success rate of only 9 percent. The Index dataset processes roughly 35 minutes of new human experience every second to support the model's performance.

总结：Figure发布了Helix 2. 5神经网络，该网络在湾区30个陌生家庭中进行了测试，事先未收集任何数据、未进行微调，也未接触过被要求处理的物品。 该人形机器人成功完成了56%的任务，如收拾玩具、叠毛巾和铺床。 相比之下，在没有Figure的Index人类行为数据集支持下从头训练的相同网络，成功率仅为9%。 Index数据集每秒处理大约35分钟的新人类经验，以支持该模型的表现。

Both companies must continue to scale generalization so their robots can reliably handle completely novel environments.

总结：Figure AI在湾区30个未曾见过的陌生家庭中对其Helix 2. 5神经网络进行了测试，在没有进行微调或预先收集数据的情况下，要求人形机器人收拾客厅、叠毛巾和铺床。 经过Index预训练的版本在56%的试验中获得成功，而从头训练的相同模型成功率仅为9%，这表明人类行为数据可以成功迁移到新环境中。 这与利用庞大车队数据和AI基础设施来训练Optimus的特斯拉形成了竞争对比。 虽然特斯拉拥有显著的现实世界AI数据优势，但Figure通过与Nscale合作获得3. 5 billion US dollars的算力支持，直接收集人类导航和物理操作数据。 两家公司都必须继续扩大泛用性，以确保其机器人能够可靠地处理完全陌生的环境。
