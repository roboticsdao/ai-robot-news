The Business Standard — IPO for humanoid robot maker Boston Dynamics unlikely in 2027: Executive

Boston Dynamics, the humanoid robot unit of Hyundai Motor Group, is unlikely to pursue an initial public offering in 2027, according to a senior executive at the parent company. The executive stated that an IPO won't be easy as the company has yet to deploy flagship Atlas robots at scale and remains unprofitable. Hyundai acquired a controlling stake in Boston Dynamics in 2021 and plans to make it a wholly owned subsidiary by acquiring SoftBank's roughly 10% stake. Hyundai aims to build a factory producing 30,000 robots a year by 2028 and deploy humanoid robots at its Georgia plant that year. Analysts and firms have estimated valuations ranging from 50 trillion won to 100 trillion won, with some projecting an IPO in 2029 or 2030, while IBK Securities suggested a valuation of 141 trillion won by 2030. Filings from Hyundai Glovis indicate Boston Dynamics recorded a 2025 loss of 528. 4 billion won, with total losses from 2021 through 2025 reaching nearly 1. 7 trillion won.

总结：现代汽车集团旗下的人形机器人企业波士顿动力公司的一位高管表示，该公司不太可能在2027年进行首次公开募股。 这位高管指出，由于旗舰Atlas机器人尚未实现大规模部署且处于亏损状态，进行IPO并非易事。 现代汽车于2021年收购了波士顿动力公司的控股权，并计划通过收购软银约10%的股份将其变为全资子公司。 现代汽车计划到2028年建立一家年产3万台机器人的工厂，并于当年开始在其佐治亚州工厂部署人形机器人。 分析师和机构估计其估值在50万亿韩元至100万亿韩元之间，部分分析师预计IPO可能在2029年或2030年进行，IBK证券则预测到2030年其估值可达141万亿韩元。 现代 글로비스（Hyundai Glovis）提交的文件显示，波士顿动力的2025年亏损额为5284亿韩元，2021年至2025年的累计亏损接近1. 7万亿韩元。

Interesting Engineering — Figure introduces new humanoid robot neural network

Figure has released Helix 2. 5, a neural network tested in 30 Bay Area homes with zero prior data collection, no fine-tuning, and no exposure to the objects it handled. The robot performed household tasks such as putting toys in baskets, folding towels, and making beds, completing 56 percent of those attempts. When trained from scratch without Figure's Index human behavior dataset—which ingests roughly 35 minutes of new human experience every second—the same network managed only a 9 percent success rate.

总结：Figure发布了Helix 2. 5神经网络，该网络被部署到湾区的30个家庭中进行了测试，事先未收集任何当地数据，也未进行微调或接触其被要求处理的物品。 机器人执行了将玩具放入篮子、叠毛巾和铺床等家务，完成了56%的尝试。 如果在没有Figure的Index人类行为数据集（该数据集每秒摄入约35分钟的新人类经验）支持的情况下从头训练，相同的网络成功率仅为9%。
