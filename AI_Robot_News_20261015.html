MVPro Media — What NVIDIA’s own assembly challenges reveal about physical AI

NVIDIA’s Seattle Robotics Lab and Isaac engineering team worked on GB300 tester tray assembly, focusing on busbar assembly and cable connector insertion with input from Foxconn and NVIDIA’s operations team. Published on October 7, the project reported busbar assembly success above 95 per cent against a 99.5 per cent target, and a 160-second cycle exceeding the 124-second target, with screwdriving as the main bottleneck. For connector insertion, the team achieved 90 to 95 per cent success, averaging 40 seconds per cable against a 72-second target for all four cables. The project utilized a specialist pose estimation framework named DOPER combining synthetic and real data, custom gripper geometry, and simulation learning followed by physical interaction.

总结：英伟达的西雅图机器人实验室和Isaac工程团队致力于GB300测试仪托盘组装，在富士康及英伟达运营团队的参与下重点开展母线组装和电缆连接器插装。 该项目于10月7日公布，报告显示母线组装成功率超过95%，目标为99.5%；周期为160秒，超过了124秒的目标，拧螺丝是主要的耗时瓶颈。 在连接器插装方面，团队实现了90%至95%的成功率，每根电缆平均耗时40秒，而四根电缆的总目标为不超过72秒。 该项目采用了结合合成数据与真实数据的专业姿态估计框架DOPER、定制抓手几何形状，以及仿真学习加物理交互求精的方法。

supercarblondie.com — Hyundai is officially training humanoid robots to take on factory jobs at Metaplant America

Hyundai Motor Group is training humanoid robots for potential factory jobs at its Metaplant America facility in Georgia to improve manufacturing safety and efficiency. Unlike fixed traditional industrial robots, humanoid robots are built in a human-like shape to operate in spaces designed for people and handle repetitive or physically demanding tasks. The initiative follows Hyundai's 2021 acquisition of a controlling interest in Boston Dynamics, whose technologies include the four-legged robot Spot and the humanoid robot Atlas. Hyundai is using the Georgia facility to explore real-world practical applications, deployment costs, operating speeds, and safe human-robot interaction.

总结：现代汽车集团正在其位于佐治亚州的Metaplant America工厂为人形机器人开展潜在工厂岗位的培训，以提高制造安全性和效率。 与固定式传统工业专有机器人不同，人形机器人采用人形外观设计，以便在专为人类设计的空间中工作并处理重复性或体力要求高的任务。 此前，现代在2021年收购了波士顿动力的控股权，后者的技术包括四足机器人Spot和人形机器人Atlas。 现代正利用佐治亚州的工厂探索实际应用、部署成本、运行速度以及人机安全交互。

Mjengo Hub — UBTECH and FAW Volkswagen partner to deploy humanoid robots in automotive logistics

Gadget Review — Unitree G1 Robot Can Pull a Rickshaw, Stop, Turn and Keep Its Balance

Researchers at the Shanghai Innovation Institute used a Unitree G1 humanoid robot to haul a custom unpowered rickshaw through a sequence starting from rest, sustaining motion, navigating turns, and stopping while maintaining its balance. The G1, a research platform standing about 1.32 meters tall and weighing around 35 kilograms with 29 actuated joints and two Dex1-1 grippers, handled a loaded rickshaw mass of up to 115 kilograms during testing, with an unloaded custom rickshaw weight of 22.8 kilograms. According to an arXiv paper, a single control framework handles the entire sequence without retuning for different payloads. The demonstration illustrates the challenge of managing a coupled passive vehicle introducing continuous unpredictable forces and inertia. The research paper is dated 2026, though the public demonstration date requires independent verification. Unconfirmed details include autonomy level, operating surface conditions, and successful trial counts, while real-world deployment requires further safety validation and terrain testing.

总结：上海创新研究所的研究人员使用宇树科技G1人形机器人，牵引一辆定制的无动力人力车完成了从静止启动、保持运动、转向到停止的全套动作，并在整个过程中自行保持平衡。 作为一款身高约1.32米、重约35公斤、拥有29个驱动关节和两个Dex1-1夹爪的研究平台，该机器人在测试中处理了高达115公斤的载重人力车质量，而卸载后的定制人力车重22.8公斤。 根据arXiv论文显示，单一的控制框架能够处理整个序列，且无需针对不同负载重新调校。 此次演示说明了管理耦合被动车辆的挑战，该车辆会持续引入不可预测的力与惯性。 该研究论文标注日期为2026年，但公开演示的日期需要独立核实。 未确认的细节包括自主程度、运行地面条件以及成功试验次数，而实际部署仍需进一步的安全验证和地形测试。
