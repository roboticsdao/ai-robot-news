Interesting Engineering — Boston Dynamics unveils 13-DOF robotic hand for its Atlas humanoid

Boston Dynamics has unveiled a new 13-degree-of-freedom hand for its Atlas humanoid robot, moving away from a conventional five-fingered design to use four fingers and an opposable thumb. The hand is designed for dexterity, strength, durability, sensing, and simulation training, allowing it to manipulate tools, reorient objects, and recover from slips. Weighing similarly to a large human hand, it can carry a loaded mini-fridge weighing over 100 pounds, matching the Atlas platform's industrial payload rating of an instantaneous 110 pounds and a sustained 66 pounds. The configuration eliminates a pinky finger to reduce failure points and cost while using direct actuation and encapsulated joints. Furthermore, the hand is optimized for high-fidelity simulation and reinforcement learning, featuring backdrivable transmissions and dense pressure-sensitive tactile sensors across the fingertips and palm.

总结：波士顿动力为其Atlas人形机器人推出了一款全新的13自由度机械手，摒弃了传统的五指设计，采用四指和一个可对握的拇指。 该机械手旨在实现灵活性、强度、耐用性、传感和仿真训练，能够操作工具、重新定向物体并从滑落中恢复。 它的重量与人类的大手相当，能够搬运重达100多磅的装满物品的小型冰箱，符合Atlas平台110磅瞬时负载和66磅持续负载的工业负载额定值。 该配置去除了小指，以减少故障点和成本，同时采用直接驱动和密封关节。 此外，该机械手针对高保真仿真和强化学习进行了优化，在指尖和手掌上配备了可逆传动装置和密集的压敏触觉传感器。

PR TIMES — JQA、生活支援ロボット分野で国内初となるJIS B 8445認証書を発行

The Japan Quality Assurance Organization (JQA) issued Japan's first JIS mark certification in the field of service robots on October 1, 2026. The certification was granted to Mitsubishi Electric Mobility Corporation's hospital transport robot, 'MELDY,' following an assessment of its conformity to safety requirements and the manufacturer's quality control system under JIS B 8445 ('Robots and robotic devices — Safety requirements for personal care robots'). JIS B 8445 specifies requirements for safety design, risk reduction measures, and information for use, aligning with the international standard ISO 13482 for co-existing robots. JQA has historically contributed to safety evaluations for service robots, including early certifications under ISO 13482, aiming to support the societal implementation of secure and reliable robotic systems.

总结：一般社团法人日本品质保証机构（JQA）于2026年10月1日，针对三菱電機モビリティ株式会社面向医院的搬送机器人“MELDY”，依据JIS B 8445（“机器人及机器人设备—生活支援机器人的安全要求事项”）颁发了国内首个JIS标志认证。 该认证是生活支援机器人领域的国内首个JIS标志认证，JQA对其安全要求的符合性及制造企业的质量管理体制进行了审查。 JIS B 8445规定了生活支援机器人的安全设计、风险降低措施及使用说明等要求，与国际标准ISO 13482相协调。 JQA长期致力于生活支援机器人的安全评价，包括实施全球领先的ISO 13482认证，以推动安全可靠机器人社会的实现。
