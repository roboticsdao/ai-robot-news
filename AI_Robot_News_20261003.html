Interesting Engineering — Boston Dynamics unveils 13-DOF robotic hand for its Atlas humanoid

Boston Dynamics unveiled its latest Atlas humanoid hand, featuring four fingers and 13 degrees of freedom (DOF) instead of a conventional five-fingered design. The hand balances dexterity, strength, durability, sensing, and sim-to-real reinforcement learning. It uses three DOFs on each of the three fingers and four on the opposable thumb, matching the size of a large human hand. The configuration allows it to carry a loaded mini-fridge weighing over 100 pounds, while the broader Atlas platform is rated for an instantaneous payload of 110 pounds and a sustained payload of 66 pounds. The design excludes a pinky after testing showed four fingers suffice for object reorientation, slipping recovery, and tool manipulation. The hand features backdrivable transmissions, proprioception, and dense tactile sensors on the fingertips and palm to support high-fidelity simulation training for handling industrial tools like drills and welding torches.

总结：波士顿动力公司为其Atlas人形机器人发布了全新的13自由度（DOF）四指灵巧手，摒弃了传统的五指设计。 该手部配置了包含可对指大拇指在内的四个手指，其余三指各有3个自由度，大拇指有4个自由度。 其尺寸与成年人的大手相仿，能够搬运超过100磅的满载迷你冰箱，而Atlas平台本身的瞬时负载额定值为110磅，持续负载为66磅。 测试表明四指足以完成物体重定向、防滑恢复和工具操作，因此去掉了小指以减少故障点和成本。 该手部集成了可逆传动装置、本体感觉及密集的触感传感器，支持高保真模拟训练，能够熟练操作钻头、砂轮机和焊枪等工业工具。

Spot, the robot dog created by Boston Dynamics in 2020, is being utilized by ingredients company Cargill at its Amsterdam factory to perform inspections, data collection, and spot checks. PR TIMES — JQA、生活支援ロボット分野で国内初となるJIS B 8445認証書を発行

The Japan Quality Assurance Organization (JQA) issued Japan's first JIS mark certification in the service robot category on October 1, 2026, to Mitsubishi Electric Mobility's hospital delivery robot, 'MELDY'. The certification was based on JIS B 8445, which specifies safety requirements, design, and risk reduction for service robots, aligning with the international standard ISO 13482. The evaluation covered both safety compliance and the manufacturer's quality control system. JQA, an independent conformity assessment body established in 1957, has long contributed to safety standardization for service robots, including mobile servant and physical assistant robots, to enhance social implementation and user trust.

总结：一般財団法人日本品质保证机构（JQA）于2026年10月1日，对三菱电机モビリティ株式会社的医院搬送机器人“MELDY”颁发了生活支援机器人领域的日本国内首个JIS标志认证书。 该认证依据规定了生活支援机器人安全要求、设计及风险减小措施的JIS B 8445标准进行，并与国际标准ISO 13482相衔接。 认证审查内容涵盖了安全要求的符合性及制造企业的质量管理体制。 成立于1957年的JQA作为公正中立的第三方合格评定机构，长期致力于生活支援机器人的安全性评估与国际标准制定，此次认证旨在推进机器人的社会应用并提升用户的安心感与信任度。
