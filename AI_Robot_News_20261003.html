Interesting Engineering — Boston Dynamics unveils 13-DOF robotic hand for its Atlas humanoid

Boston Dynamics unveiled a new 13-degree-of-freedom hand for its Atlas humanoid robot, using four fingers instead of five to balance dexterity, strength, and durability. The hand increases articulation from the previous seven-DOF design, features an opposable thumb, and uses encapsulated direct-actuation joints. It can carry loads over 100 pounds, matching the industrial material-handling focus of the Atlas platform. The design is optimized for high-fidelity simulation, sim-to-real reinforcement learning, and includes tactile sensors and proprioception to manipulate tools and recover from slips.

总结：波士顿动力为其Atlas人形机器人推出了一款全新的13自由度机械手，采用四根手指而非五指设计，以平衡灵活性、强度和耐用性。 该机械手将关节活动度从先前的7自由度提升至13自由度，配备对指拇指，并采用封装式直接驱动关节。 其负载能力超过100磅，符合Atlas平台专注工业物料搬运的定位。 该设计针对高保真仿真与仿真到现实的强化学习进行了优化，并配备了触星觉传感器与本体感觉，能够操作工具并从滑落中恢复。

This milestone aims to objectively prove safety and reliability for human-coexisting robots as they advance toward broader social implementation.

总结：日本质量保证机构（JQA）于2026年10月1日依据JIS B 8445标准，向三菱電機モビリティ株式会社的医院搬送机器人“MELDY”颁发了日本生活支援机器人领域的首个JIS标志认证。 该认证依据生活支援机器人安全要求及质量管理体系进行审查，与国际安全标准ISO 13482相协调。 这一里程碑旨在客观证明人机共存机器人的安全性和可靠性，推动其社会普及。
