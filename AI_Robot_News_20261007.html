eeNews Europe — Figure melts retired F.02 humanoid robot fleet ...

Humanoid robotics company Figure retired its F. 02 robot fleet by training the machines to autonomously jump into a 75-tonne electric arc furnace filled with molten steel at a foundry in Imatra, Finland. The decommissioning followed Figure's transition to the newer F. 03 robot. Actor Arnold Schwarzenegger suggested melting them after Figure founder and CEO Brett Adcock asked online for send-off ideas. Before shipping, Figure trained the robots to jump using AI simulations based on stunt performer movements. The F. 02 robots continued running their AI policies despite extreme heat and electromagnetic fields. Most of the fleet was destroyed, while a few remain in storage and some recovered steel was turned into commemorative objects.

总结：人形机器人公司Figure通过训练其F. 02机器人舰队自主跳入位于芬兰伊马特拉的一家铸造厂内装满铁水的75吨电弧炉中，以一种极为直观的方式退役了该舰队。 此次退役项目是在Figure从第二代F. 02人形机器人转向更新的F. 03之后进行的。 在Figure创始人兼首席执行官布雷特·阿德考克在线征集送别创意后，演员阿诺德·施瓦辛格建议将其熔化。 在运送之前，Figure使用基于特技演员动作的AI模拟训练机器人进行跳跃。 尽管面临极高热量和电磁场，F. 02机器人在炉边运行时仍持续运行其AI策略。 该舰队的大部分已被销毁，少数保留在仓库中，部分回收钢材被制成纪念品。

Startup Fortune — FCC's ban on Chinese humanoid robots may starve the US firms it aims to help

On July 28, the FCC added foreign-produced advanced robotic devices, including humanoid and quadruped robots, to its Covered List to secure supply chains, affecting equipment authorizations for new foreign models unless granted Department of War conditional approval. FCC Chair Brendan Carr framed the action around national security, while a 2025 disclosure had found hardcoded Bluetooth keys in Unitree models like the G1, H1, Go2, and B2 allowing nearby takeover. However, US executives such as Apptronik CEO Jeff Cardenas stated in September that domestic companies face severe shortages of basic components like gears and actuators, which depend heavily on rare-earth magnets processed primarily in China. Chinese firms accounted for an estimated 97% of global humanoid shipments in the first half of 2026. Unitree, priced at a roughly $9 billion valuation during its August IPO where shares jumped 460% on August 19, reported that US sales constituted 13.3% of its revenue last year, with models like the G1 starting at about $13,500 in China and $21,600 in the US. While supporters argue the ban forces domestic production and mitigates cybersecurity risks, critics contend it restricts access to essential parts before alternative supply chains are established.

总结：美国联邦通信委员会于7月28日将外国生产的高级机器人设备（包括人形和四足机器人）列入其“受管制清单”，旨在维护供应链安全，此举限制了未来外国新机器人型号的设备授权，除非获得国防部有条件批准。 联邦通信委员会主席布伦丹·卡尔将其归因于国家安全，此前2025年的一项披露曾发现宇树科技的G1、H1人形机器人及Go2、B2四足机器人中存在硬编码蓝牙密钥，可使附近攻击者接管设备。 然而，美国奥斯汀Apptronik公司首席执行官杰夫·卡德纳斯在9月表示，本土企业正面临齿轮和执行器等基础零部件的严重短缺，而这些部件极度依赖中国加工的稀土磁体。 2026年上半年，中国公司约占全球人形机器人出货量的97%。 宇树科技在8月份的首次公开募股中估值约达$9 billion，其股票在8月19日交易首日收盘上涨460%，该公司去年美国销售额占其收入的13.3%，其中G1等型号在中国的起售价约为13500美元，进入美国市场后约为21600美元。 尽管支持者认为该禁令能推动本土生产并降低网络安全风险，但批评者认为在建立替代供应链之前，它切断了企业获取必要零件的途径。
