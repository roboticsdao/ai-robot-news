eeNews Europe — Figure melts retired F.02 humanoid robot fleet ...

Humanoid robotics company Figure has retired its F. 02 robot fleet by training the machines to autonomously jump into a 75-tonne electric arc furnace filled with molten steel at a foundry in Imatra, Finland. The decommissioning followed Figure's transition to the newer F. 03 model and its decision to avoid internal dismantling which would consume engineering time and expose proprietary hardware. Actor Arnold Schwarzenegger suggested melting them after Figure founder and CEO Brett Adcock asked online for ideas. Before shipping, Figure set up airbags in San Jose and trained the F. 02 robots to jump using movements captured from stunt performers to train an AI model. During the melts, the F. 02 robots continued running their AI policies despite extreme heat and electromagnetic fields. Most of the fleet was destroyed, though a small number remain in storage and recovered steel is being machined into commemorative objects in the US.

总结：人形机器人公司Figure通过训练其F. 02机器人跳入芬兰伊马特拉一家充满钢水的75吨电弧炉中，对其旧款机器人进行了退役处理。 此次报废是因为公司转向了更新的F. 03型号，且内部拆解会耗费工程时间并暴露专有硬件。 在Figure创始人兼首席执行官布雷特·阿德科克在线征求建议后，演员阿诺德·施瓦辛格建议将其熔化。 在运往芬兰之前，Figure在圣何塞园区设置了安全气囊，并利用特技演员捕捉的动作来训练人工智能模型，使机器人能够准确跳入炉中。 在熔化过程中，尽管面临极端热量和电磁场，F. 02机器人仍持续运行其人工智能策略。 大部分F. 02机器人已被销毁，仅少量保留在仓库中，从芬兰回收的钢材被运回美国加工成限量纪念品。

Interesting Engineering — New humanoid robot built in five months remotely handles bombs, hazardous energy jobs

A San Francisco-based robotics startup, Minerva Humanoids, has built a walking humanoid named Roger in five months to let specialists remotely handle explosives and hazardous energy equipment. The semi-autonomous robot combines remote human control via a VR headset with onboard software managing balance, navigation, and fall recovery. Minerva secured approximately $10 million in pre-seed funding led by General Catalyst, with Long Journey Ventures and Credo Ventures as co-leads, to support further development and paid pilots planned for this fall.
