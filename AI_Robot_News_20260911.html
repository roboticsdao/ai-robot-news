Tech Xplore — Humanoid robot learns to sprint and perform spin kicks using AI trained on human motion data

Researchers at UC Berkeley and Stanford University developed BeyondMimic, an artificial intelligence framework that broadens the movement repertoire of humanoid robots using reinforcement learning and a unified latent diffusion model. Trained on approximately 2. 5 hours of human motion data adapted to the G1 humanoid robot by Unitree Robotics, the framework enables the robot to master agile movements such as sprinting, spin kicks, flip kicks, and aerial cartwheels. The team utilized a variational autoencoder to compress action data into a lower-dimensional latent space and a diffusion model to learn sequences of predicted physical states and movements. The framework allows the robot to solve unseen downstream tasks, including motion inpainting, joystick teleoperation, and obstacle avoidance, transferred zero-shot to real hardware.

总结：加州大学伯克利分校和斯坦福大学的研究人员开发了BeyondMimic人工智能框架，通过强化学习和一个统一的潜空间扩散模型，拓宽了人形机器人的动作范围。 该框架使用适应宇树科技G1人形机器人的约2. 5小时人类动作数据进行训练，使机器人能够掌握冲刺、旋转踢、空翻踢和空中侧手翻等敏捷动作。 研究团队利用变分自编码器将动作数据压缩为低维潜空间，并通过扩散模型学习预测的物理状态和动作序列。 该框架使机器人能够零样本迁移至真实硬件，解决诸如动作修补、操纵杆远程操作和避障等未见过的下游任务。

Agility Robotics provided a glimpse into the economics of its two-legged robot, Digit, in a securities filing. Under one business model for the next-generation Digit v5, a customer would pay about $200,000 for the robot, a roughly $20,000 onetime deployment fee, and about $36,000 a year for software and maintenance, totaling around $400,000 over an assumed five-year useful life. Agility estimates customer breakeven in approximately 1. 1 years based on productivity, uptime, and labor availability. The Digit v5 is designed to operate for about 20 hours within a 24-hour period and lift up to 50 pounds. Agility's previous Digit v4 has been deployed at nine customer facilities, including Schaeffler, GXO, Toyota Motor Manufacturing Canada, and Amazon, with the v5 expected to be released later this year.

总结：Agility Robotics在提交的一份证券文件中披露了其双足机器人Digit的经济账。 根据下一代Digit v5的一种商业模式，客户购买机器人的费用约为20,000美元（原文如此，应为200,000美元），加上约20,000美元的一次性部署费，以及每年约36,000美元的软件和维护费用，在假设5年使用寿命期间总计约为400,000美元。 Agility根据生产力、正常运行时间和劳动力可用性估计，客户大约在1. 1年内即可实现收支平衡。 Digit v5设计为在24小时内运行约20小时，并可举起重达50磅的物品。 Agility先前的Digit v4已部署在Schaeffler、GXO、丰田加拿大汽车制造公司和亚马逊等9个客户设施中，v5预计将于今年晚些时候发布。
