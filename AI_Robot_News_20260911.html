Tech Xplore — Humanoid robot learns to sprint and perform spin kicks using AI trained on human motion data

Researchers at UC Berkeley and Stanford University developed BeyondMimic, an AI framework published in Science Robotics that trains humanoid robots to perform diverse agile movements using approximately 2. 5 hours of human motion data adapted to the Unitree G1 robot. Using reinforcement learning combined with a variational autoencoder and a latent diffusion model, the framework allows the robot to master behaviors like sprinting, spin kicks, and cartwheels from a single setup with shared hyperparameters. The researchers validated the framework in simulation and successfully deployed representative motion clips on physical hardware, enabling the robot to tackle unseen downstream tasks zero-shot.

总结：加州大学伯克利分校和斯坦福大学的研究人员在《科学机器人》上发表了名为BeyondMimic的新AI框架，该框架利用适应了宇树G1机器人的约2. 5小时人类动作数据，训练人形机器人执行各种敏捷动作。 通过将强化学习与变分自编码器和潜在扩散模型相结合，该框架使机器人能够通过带有共享超参数的单一设置掌握冲刺、旋风踢和侧手翻等行为。 研究人员在仿真中验证了该框架，并成功在实体硬件上部署了代表性动作剪辑，使机器人能够零样本解决未见过的下游任务。
