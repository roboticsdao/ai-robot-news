Azərtac — NVIDIA, Bulgaria’s INSAIT develop platform to evaluate AI Models in robotics

NVIDIA and INSAIT, an institute at Sofia University, developed the LADDER platform to evaluate AI models in controlling robots for sequential actions. The project focuses on Physical AI, where AI systems interact with the real world. Initial tests revealed that leading models, such as GPT-6 Astra, Claude Opus 5.5, and Gemini 4 Argon, still struggle with multi-step tasks, succeeding in only about one-third of attempts in the most challenging tests involving eight to ten consecutive actions. The study also highlighted high evaluation costs, exceeding USD 6 per task and around USD 8,000 for a full evaluation. Additionally, Google made a USD 1 million donation to INSAIT on October 23, bringing its total support to over USD 7 million for the institute, which was founded in partnership with ETH Zurich and EPFL.

总结：英伟达与索菲亚大学下属的INSAIT研究所合作开发了名为LADDER的平台，用于评估人工智能模型在控制机器人执行连续动作方面的能力。 该项目聚焦于物理人工智能。 初步测试显示，GPT-6 Astra、Claude Opus 5.5和Gemini 4 Argon等领先模型在面对包含八到十个连续动作的极具挑战性测试时，成功率仅约为三分之一。 研究还指出使用顶级AI模型的成本高昂，单项任务平均成本超过USD 6，完整评估费用约为USD 8,000。 此外，谷歌于10月23日向INSAIT捐赠了USD 1 million，使其对该研究所的总资助超过USD 7 million。 INSAIT由索菲亚大学与瑞士苏黎世联邦理工学院及洛桑联邦理工学院合作建立。

总结：东京海上日动火灾保险于26日宣布，将于11月推出“AI机器人保险”，以防范搭载AI自律行动机器人的误操作等风险。 这是日本国内财产保险公司首次开展此项业务。 对象为通过传感器和摄像头判断周围环境并控制动作的“物理AI”移动式机器人，不仅限于双足步行的人形，还包括通过四足或车轮自律移动的机体。 保障内容包括机器人因灾害或碰撞产生的修理费、误操作导致的损害赔偿费用以及替代机型的租赁费等。 不过，遭受网络攻击所产生的损害不属于补偿对象。

Gasgoo — Agile Robots and NVIDIA Unveil Agile ONE SN Humanoid Robot for Research

Agile Robots and NVIDIA jointly unveiled the Agile ONE SN humanoid robot for research at the NVIDIA GTC conference in Berlin. Built on the NVIDIA Isaac GR00T development platform, the unit stands 1.4 meters tall, weighs 43 kilograms, and has 31 degrees of freedom. It features an onboard NVIDIA Jetson Thor computing platform, a multi-view sensor system including an RGB-D camera, wrist cameras, 3D LiDAR, and an IMU. Joint torque reaches up to 140 newton-meters, with a maximum single-arm payload of 3 kilograms, while supporting Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth. The software includes the Isaac GR00T system with the GR00T 1.7 open-source vision-language-action model. The Agile ONE S and SN series is scheduled for an early 2027 launch.

总结：Agile Robots与英伟达在柏林英伟达GTC大会上联合发布了专为研究设计的人形机器人Agile ONE SN。 该机器人基于英伟达Isaac GR00T开发平台构建，高1.4米，重43公斤，具有31个自由度。 它配备了机载英伟达Jetson Thor计算平台以及包括RGB-D相机、腕部相机、3D激光雷达和IMU在内的多视角传感器系统。 其关节扭矩高达140牛米，单臂最大负载为3公斤，并支持Wi-Fi 7和蓝牙连接。 软件方面搭载了包含GR00T 1.7开源视觉-语言-动作模型的Isaac GR00T系统。 Agile ONE S和SN系列计划于2027年初发布。
