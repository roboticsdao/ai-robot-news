Interesting Engineering — Amazon draws on fleet of 1 million robots to help machines learn and act in real world

Amazon Web Services has introduced an open-source development stack called the Physical AI Toolchain on AWS to help companies build and deploy robots that can perceive surroundings, make decisions, and perform physical tasks. The platform combines AWS infrastructure with NVIDIA's physical AI software ecosystem to reduce engineering effort in moving AI models into real-world machines. The toolchain covers five stages: synthetic data generation, model training, simulation and validation, edge deployment, and continuous improvement. It integrates AWS services like Amazon SageMaker, Amazon EC2, AWS IoT Greengrass, and Amazon Bedrock AgentCore with NVIDIA tools including Isaac Sim, Isaac Lab, Isaac GR00T, and Cosmos. Amazon states the toolchain draws on its experience across more than one million robots, and highlights developers such as NEURA Robotics, RLWRLD, and Config working on physical AI applications.

总结：亚马逊网络服务推出了一款名为Physical AI Toolchain on AWS的开源开发套件，旨在帮助企业构建和部署能够感知环境、做出决策并执行物理任务的机器人。 该平台将亚马逊的云基础设施与英伟达的物理AI软件生态系统相结合，以减少将AI模型投入现实世界机器所需的工程工作。 该工具链涵盖五个阶段：合成数据生成、模型训练、仿真与验证、边缘部署以及持续改进。 它整合了亚马逊的各项服务以及英伟达的工具。 亚马逊表示，该工具链借鉴了其在超过100万台机器人网络运营中获得的经验，并重点提到了正在从事物理机器人开发的合作伙伴。

WRIF Rocks Detroit — Detroit Debuts Boston Dynamics’ Robot Dogs for Urban Innovation Testing

Bedrock Detroit and Boston Dynamics are testing Spot robot dogs in downtown Detroit to turn the city into a technology proving ground. Kevin Mull, Bedrock Detroit's senior director for the Office of Urban Strategy, discussed the plan and introduced a robot named Astro, noting potential tasks such as picking up small litter like cigarette butts, inspecting buildings, mapping tricky areas, and handling packages. The initiative has been in development for six to eight months. The litter pickup solution is not yet commercially available, making Detroit one of the first testing locations. Officials will monitor the trial for a few months before potentially moving to a live setting.

总结：Bedrock Detroit与波士顿动力公司正在底特律市中心测试Spot机器狗，旨在将该市打造成未来技术的试验场。 Bedrock Detroit城市战略办公室高级主任凯文·穆尔在采访中讨论了这一计划，并介绍了一台名为Astro的机器人，指出其潜在任务包括捡拾烟头等小件垃圾、检查建筑物、测绘复杂区域以及处理包裹。 该项目已经在过去六到八个月中低调推进。 捡拾垃圾的方案尚未在市场上推出，使得底特律成为首批测试地点之一。 相关官员将在未来几个月密切关注测试模式，随后再决定是否进入实际运营环境。

The Robot Report — Boston Dynamics gives more insight into its redesigned humanoid hand

Boston Dynamics revealed a new four-fingered humanoid robot hand with 13 degrees of freedom and direct actuation, designed for mass manufacturing, high-fidelity simulation, and sim-to-real reinforcement learning. The company dropped the pinky finger and reduced the gripper size. Alberto Rodriguez, director of robot behavior for Atlas, explained that the design prioritizes dexterity, strength, ruggedness, cost, reparability, and sensing, utilizing 13 identical encapsulated actuators with no fragile cables crossing joints. The hand was developed in-house to integrate with the product roadmap. Boston Dynamics tested the new hand on common industrial tasks such as using handheld power tools, handling cables, and fishing for screws, alongside exploratory tasks.

总结：波士顿动力公司公布了一款全新的四指人形机器人手，该手部拥有13个自由度并采用直接驱动，专为大规模制造、高保真仿真以及仿真到现实的强化学习而设计。 该公司去除了小指并减小了夹持器的尺寸。 Atlas机器人行为总监阿尔贝托·罗德里格斯解释称，该设计优先考虑了灵巧性、强度、坚固性、成本、可修复性和传感，并使用了13个相同的完全封装执行器，没有脆弱的电缆穿过关节。 这款手部为内部研发，以与产品路线图相契合。 波士顿动力公司在诸如使用手持电动工具、处理电缆和寻找螺丝等常见的工业任务以及探索性任务上对这只新手进行了测试。

Chinese automaker BYD has unveiled the design of its first self-developed humanoid robot in a patent published by China’s National Intellectual Property Administration, describing a robot capable of walking, maintaining balance, and interacting in service, education, and industrial environments. BYD's robotics project began with a dedicated team formed in 2022, and the company has previously invested in Agibot in August 2023 and Percibot in April 2025, alongside hosting Ubtech's Walker S1 robot at its Changsha factory. BYD has not announced a production timeline or confirmed if the published patent is the final version.
