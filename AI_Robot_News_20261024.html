Fortune — Washington's robot curbs could slash the U.S.'s humanoid robotics revenue by 58%, as companies lose access to China

According to market research firm International Data Corporation (IDC), U. S. trade restrictions could reduce the U. S. humanoid robotics market by 58% by 2030 compared to pre-restriction baselines, causing over $6 billion in lost revenue and lowering the sector's annual growth rate to about 9% from 13%. On July 28, the FCC added foreign-produced advanced robotic devices to its Covered List, restricting new model imports while permitting previously authorized models and specific updates through at least January 1, 2029. U. S. startups face supply chain cost pressures for parts like motors and batteries. On October 23, the FCC granted Intel a one-year waiver for automated guided vehicles. Globally, IDC projects over 750,000 humanoid robot shipments by 2030, with Chinese vendors like Unitree and AGIBOT accounting for about 95% of global shipments, following 25,000 shipments in the first half of 2026.

总结：根据市场研究公司国际数据公司（IDC）的报告，美国的贸易限制可能使美国人形机器人市场在2030年之前比没有限制时的基准缩小58%，导致超过$6 billion的收入损失，并将该行业的年增长率从13%降至约9%。 7月28日，美国联邦通信委员会（FCC）将“外国生产的高级机器人设备”加入其受管制清单，限制新机型进口，但允许已获授权的机型以及部分软件和固件更新至少持续至2029年1月1日。 10月23日，FCC向英特尔公司授予了一项为期一年的豁免。 IDC预测，到2030年全球人形机器人出货量将超过75万台，而以宇树科技和agiBot等为代表的中国供应商占全球人形机器人出货量的95%左右。
