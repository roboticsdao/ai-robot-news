Globally, vendors shipped an estimated 25,000 humanoid robots in the first six months of 2026, up 26% from 18,500 in all of 2025, with Chinese vendors like Unitree and AGIBOT accounting for roughly 95% of global shipments, while China and the U. S. made up 21% and 18% of global consumption.
