Robotics & Automation News — Boston Dynamics launches next-generation Stretch robot with major reliability upgrades

Boston Dynamics introduced Stretch Gen 2, the next generation of its autonomous mobile trailer unloading robot designed to maximize uptime and streamline maintenance in high-volume logistics. Drawing on operational data from over 100,000 operating hours and tens of millions of handled boxes, Stretch Gen 2 features structural and hardware enhancements including heavy-duty automotive-grade connectors, an upgraded gripper with precise sensing, continuous rotation joints beyond 360°, and an 81 percent reduction in internal cabling. Maintenance procedures are accelerated through standardized fasteners and onboard diagnostics, while a redesigned computing platform enables continuous software updates for case detection and motion planning without hardware modifications. The robot unloads up to 1000 cases per hour, operates for two shifts on a single battery charge, and utilizes integrated vision and lighting systems to function inside dark trailers.

总结：波士顿动力推出了新一代自主移动挂车卸货机器人Stretch Gen 2，旨在最大化高吞吐量物流环境中的正常运行时间并简化维护。 基于超过10万小时的运行数据和处理数千万个纸箱的经验，Stretch Gen 2进行了多项硬件和结构升级，包括采用车规级重型连接器、配备精确传感的升级版抓取器、突破360度的连续旋转关节，并减少了81%的内部电缆。 通过标准化紧固件和内置诊断工具加快了维护速度，同时重新设计的计算平台支持在无需修改硬件的情况下持续更新软件以提升目标检测和运动规划能力。 该机器人每小时可卸载多达1000个箱子，单次充电可运行两个班次，并借助集成视觉和照明系统在黑暗的挂车内部作业。

사이언스모니터 — Nvidia Isaac and GR00T: The Race for Robot AI Platform Dominance - Science Monitor

Nvidia provides a comprehensive robotics ecosystem encompassing simulation tools, foundation models, and hardware chips, competing with Google DeepMind’s VLA approach. The core of Nvidia's robotics business includes the Isaac GR00T N1.7 humanoid VLA model with 3 billion parameters and the Cosmos world model, which generates synthetic training data. These models are tested and trained in virtual environments using Isaac Sim, Isaac Lab 3.0, and the Newton physics engine—jointly developed by Nvidia, Disney Research, and Google DeepMind. Nvidia's Isaac GR00T N1.7 model, unveiled on April 17, was trained on 20,854 hours of first-person human video, which Nvidia stated more than doubled the average task completion rate compared to smaller datasets. The model has been released under a commercial-use license and validated on hardware such as Unitree's G1 and AgiBot's Genie 1.

总结：Nvidia提供涵盖模拟工具、基础模型和硬件芯片的完整机器人生态系统，与Google DeepMind的VLA方法展开竞争。 Nvidia机器人业务的核心包括拥有30 billion参数的人形VLA模型Isaac GR00T N1.7以及能够生成合成训练数据的Cosmos世界模型。 这些模型通过Isaac Sim、Isaac Lab 3.0以及由Nvidia、Disney Research和Google DeepMind联合开发的Newton物理引擎在虚拟环境中进行测试和训练。 Nvidia于4月17日发布的Isaac GR00T N1.7模型接受了20,854小时的第一人称人类视频训练，Nvidia表示，与较小的数据集相比，这使机器人的平均任务完成率提高了一倍以上。 该模型已在商业使用许可下发布，并在宇树科技的G1和智元机器人的Genie 1等硬件上进行了验证。
