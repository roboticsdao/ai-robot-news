The Robot Report — AGIBOT to explain how to scale humanoids from the lab to the real world at RoboBusiness

Yinghao Song, head of North America and product lead at AGIBOT, will speak at RoboBusiness 2026 on October 20 and 21 in Santa Clara, California. In his session titled “Scaling Humanoids from Lab to Real-World Deployment,” Song will discuss the investment logic, supply chain dynamics, and ROI frameworks required to mass-produce humanoid and quadruped robots. He will also address the software-hardware gap, explaining how modular software stacks and open SDKs enable partners to deploy full-size humanoids and dexterous hands across various industries.

总结：AGIBOT北美负责人兼产品主管Yinghao Song将于10月20日至21在加利福尼亚州圣克拉拉举行的RoboBusiness 2026上发表演讲。 在他的题为“将人形机器人从实验室扩展到现实世界部署”的会议中，Song将讨论大规模生产人形和四分体机器人所需的投资逻辑、供应链动态和投资回报率框架。 他还将探讨软硬件差距，解释模块化软件栈和开放SDK如何使合作伙伴能够在各个行业部署全尺寸人形机器人和灵巧手。
