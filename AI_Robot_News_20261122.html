Robotics & Automation News — Boston Dynamics launches next-generation Stretch robot with major reliability upgrades

Boston Dynamics has unveiled Stretch Gen 2, the next generation of its autonomous mobile trailer unloading robot, featuring major hardware and structural upgrades designed to maximize uptime and streamline maintenance based on insights from over 100,000 operating hours. Upgrades include automotive-grade connectors, an improved gripper with precise sensing, continuous rotation joints, and an 81 percent reduction in internal cabling.
