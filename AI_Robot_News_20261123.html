Interesting Engineering — US team builds 23-inch flexible robot that links up for bigger tasks

Colorado State University Adaptive Robotics Lab researchers developed the TeCoBot, a flexible, lightweight 23-inch (59 cm) robot that can bend, grip, and connect with other units for tasks. Operating individually or as part of larger constructions, the robot uses task-adaptive morphology based on tensegrity principles. Carbon-fiber rods and three motor-driven cables allow continuous shape change without conventional joints. While demonstrating crawling, rolling, climbing, and autonomous docking, most movements and cooperative tasks still require human control, and limitations remain in load capacity, power endurance, and long-term durability.

总结：科罗拉多州立大学自适应机器人实验室的研究人员开发了TeCoBot，这是一种灵活且轻便的23英寸（59厘米）机器人，能够弯曲、抓取并与其他单元连接以执行更大的任务。 该机器人可独立运行或作为更大结构的模块，利用基于张拉整体原理的任务自适应形态。 碳纤维杆和三根电机驱动的缆绳使其无需传统关节即可连续改变形状。 尽管展示了爬行、滚动、攀爬和自主对接能力，但其大部分运动和协同任务仍需人类控制，且在负载能力、电力续航和长期耐用性方面仍存在局限性。
