- **[2026.10.07] Interesting Engineering — Watch: Chinese robot hand takes on delicate Suzhou embroidery with human-like dexterity**
  English: Chinese robotics startup AGILINK, a spin-off of AgiBot, demonstrated its OmniHand 3 Ultra dexterous robot hand performing traditional Suzhou embroidery techniques. In the demonstration, guided by embroidery master Fu Xianghong, the robot split a silk thread into 16 finer strands, threaded a needle, stretched fabric across a hoop, twisted thread, and made stitches, adapting its movements because the thread landed in a different position each time. The OmniHand 3 Ultra is sized similarly to a human hand, weighs 1.9 pounds (630 grams), uses a direct-drive architecture, holds 6.6 pounds (3 kilograms) steadily, lifts up to 17.6 pounds (8 kilograms), opens and closes in about 0.3 seconds, and has a positional repeatability of about 0.008 inches (0.2 millimeters). For sensing, each fingertip features a vision-based sensor that measures force in three dimensions and detects surface deformations as small as 0.08 millimeters, while the palm incorporates a 300-point contact sensor. AGILINK combined teleoperation data with reinforcement learning for the training process. The article also notes other robotic hands such as LinkerBot's Linker Hand L30 Pro and Boston Dynamics' latest Atlas hand.
  中文：总结：中国机器人初创公司AGILINK（AgiBot的衍生公司）展示了其OmniHand 3 Ultra灵巧手执行中国传统苏州刺绣技艺的过程。 在苏绣大师傅向红的指导下，该机器人将一根丝线分拆为16根更细的细丝，进行了穿针、将织物绷在绣绷上、捻线以及缝针等操作，由于每次落线位置不同，机器人需要调整其实际动作。 OmniHand 3 Ultra尺寸接近人手，重1.9磅（630克），采用直驱架构，可稳定抓取6.6磅（3公斤）物品并举起最高17.6磅（8公斤）的重量，开合时间约为0.3秒，位置重复精度约为0.008英寸（0.2毫米）。 在感知方面，每个指尖均采用基于视觉的传感器，可测量三维力并检测小至0.08毫米的表面形变，手掌则集成了一个300点接触传感器。 AGILINK在训练过程中结合了遥操作数据与强化学习。 文章还提及了LinkerBot的Linker Hand L30 Pro以及波士顿动力最新Atlas手等其他公司的灵巧手设计。
