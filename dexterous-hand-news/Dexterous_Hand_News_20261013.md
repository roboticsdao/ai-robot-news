- **[2026.10.12] Interesting Engineering — BYD’s humanoid robot has 31 degrees of freedom and a dexterous hand**
  English: Chinese automaker BYD has unveiled the design of its first self-developed humanoid robot in a patent published by China’s National Intellectual Property Administration, describing a robot designed to walk, maintain balance, and interact in service, education, and industrial environments.
