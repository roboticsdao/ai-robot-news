- **[2026.11.19] Interesting Engineering — GENTO’s 21-DoF robot hand takes on backlash, fragility and weak grip in a single design**
  English: GENTO, an independent humanoid robot brand incubated by Tianji Intelligence, introduced its 21-degree-of-freedom dexterous-hand prototype at the Conference on Robot Learning in Austin. The hand weighs 750 g excluding cables, measures 200 × 96 × 45 mm, features a maximum grasp diameter of 100 mm, a rated fingertip force of 10 N, an opening and closing time of 0.5 seconds, and a repeat positioning accuracy of ±0.05 mm. The design addresses backlash, fragility, backdrivability, and grip strength using a high-precision linkage transmission and MIT force-position hybrid control. At the event, GENTO demonstrated the hand on the GENTO Luna wheeled humanoid and the TIANJI Marvin Pro dual-arm platform, which was also featured in NVIDIA's GR00T 2 demonstrations introduced by Jim Fan on Nov. 9.
  中文：总结：天机智能孵化的独立人形机器人品牌GENTO在奥斯汀的机器人学习会议上首次展示了其21自由度灵巧手原型。 该手不含电缆重750 g，尺寸为200 × 96 × 45 mm，最大抓取直径100 mm，额定指尖力10 N，最小开合时间0.5秒，重复定位精度±0.05 mm。 该设计通过高精度低间隙连杆传动和MIT力位混合控制，解决了反向间隙、抗冲击、可反向驱动及抓握力等问题。 在展会上，GENTO将其展示在GENTO Luna轮式人形机器人和TIANJI Marvin Pro双臂平台上，该平台亦在11月9日由Jim Fan介绍的NVIDIA GR00T 2演示中亮相。

- **[2026.11.16] Unite.AI — ROBOTERA Opens Orders for XHAND 1 Pro-U Dexterous Robot Hand**
  English: ROBOTERA announced the commercial launch of its XHAND 1 Pro-U five-finger dexterous robot hand on November 16, 2026, opening orders for an initial 40-unit release. The hand is designed around NVIDIA GEAR Lab's DexUMI exoskeleton to allow direct mapping of human demonstrations without requiring a robot. The DexUMI hardware records human movements, wrist-camera video, and fingertip forces, which are used to post-train GR00T 2 for a dual-arm Tianji robot fitted with two XHAND1 Pro-U hands. In NVIDIA's evaluations across banana-yogurt preparation, granola scooping, and fire chemistry, GR00T 2 achieved success rates of 60%, 80%, and 75% respectively, while GR00T 1.7 and pi0.5 completed none of the tasks.
  中文：总结：ROBOTERA于2026年11月16日宣布推出XHAND 1 Pro-U五指灵巧机器人手，并为其首批40台发布开启订购。 该产品围绕英伟达GEAR实验室的DexUMI外骨骼进行设计，使人类示范动作能够直接映射到机器人手关节上。 DexUMI硬件可记录人类手部动作、腕部相机视频及指尖受力，用于对搭载两只XHAND1 Pro-U手的双臂Tianji机器人上的GR00T 2进行后训练。 在英伟达进行的香蕉酸奶制作、麦片舀取和消防化学三项评估中，GR00T 2的总体成功率分别为60%、80%和75%，而GR00T 1.7和pi0.5未完成任何任务。
