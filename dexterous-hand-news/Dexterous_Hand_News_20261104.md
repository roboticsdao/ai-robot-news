- **[2026.10.30] Tech Briefs — Color-Changing Tactile Sensor Allows Robots to “See” and Touch in Real-Time**
  English: Engineers at Queen Mary University of London, including postdoctoral researcher Giacomo Sasso and Professor James Busfield alongside collaborators from Italian universities, have invented a new color-changing tactile sensor that allows robots to see and touch in real-time. The sensor works by transforming invisible forces into dynamic color patterns, enabling high-resolution maps of contact, strain, and pressure to emerge instantly without complex reconstruction algorithms. When pressure is applied to the soft sensing surface, the material produces spatially varying structural colors captured immediately by a standard low-cost USB camera. This enables robotic grippers to assemble micro-scale components with precision, helps external prosthetics gain a richer sense of touch, and allows surgical systems to distinguish healthy from abnormal tissue by reading fine pressure signatures. The technology integrates sensing directly into the material, addressing the traditional trade-off between high-resolution systems requiring heavy computation and faster systems sacrificing spatial detail.
  中文：总结：伦敦玛丽女王大学的工程师（包括博士后研究员Giacomo Sasso与詹姆斯·巴斯菲尔德教授，以及来自意大利多所大学的合作者）发明了一种新型变色触觉传感器，使机器人能够实时感知触觉与视觉。 该传感器通过将无形的作用力转化为动态的色彩图案，无需复杂的重建算法即可即时生成接触、应变和压力的的高分辨率图。 当压力施加于柔软的传感表面时，材料会产生空间变化的结构色，并通过标准低成本USB摄像头立即捕捉。 这使得机器人抓手能够以高精度装配微型组件，帮助外部假肢获得更丰富的触觉，并使手术系统能够通过读取材料色彩响应中的精细压力特征来区分健康组织与异常组织。 该技术将传感直接嵌入材料本身，解决了传统高分辨率系统需要繁重计算与快速系统牺牲空间细节之间的权衡问题。

- **[2026.10.30] BigGo Finance — China's AGILINK dexterous hand runs 72-step production line at Wolong's Shaoxing plant, 8.5 minutes per cycle nears skilled-worker pace**
  English: On October 29 at the dexterous-manipulation forum of Zhiyuan Innovation Day, Chinese embodied-AI company AGILINK disclosed validation results for its OmniHand dexterous hand at a proof-of-concept workstation on Wolong Electric's Shaoxing production line. The hand completed 72 consecutive process steps autonomously, taking 8.5 minutes per cycle with a 98% task success rate and the ability to run continuously for 8 hours, which is 30 seconds slower than a skilled worker's roughly 8-minute pace. The process was handled by a single robotic arm and dexterous hand without real-time human intervention, including flexible assembly, barcode scanning, screw fastening using a screw gun, testing-equipment button pressing, and part unloading. Screw fastening presented a major challenge requiring sub-millimeter alignment precision to handle anti-loosening screw wobble and tool recoil. AGILINK chose a five-fingered dexterous hand over grippers to manage multiple operations consecutively. The setup used real-machine reinforcement learning and the Omni-Maestro industrial deployment agent framework for process verification and failure rollback. CEO Qiao Tianjie noted this marks a shift from development mode to deployment mode, while chief embodied scientist Huang Bidan highlighted the data shortfall in current models relying on pure-vision datasets.
  中文：总结：10月29日，在智元创新日灵巧操作论坛上，中国具身智能公司临界点（AGILINK）披露了其OmniHand灵巧手在卧龙电气绍兴生产线概念验证工位上的验证结果。 该灵巧手自主完成了72个连续工序步骤，单次循环耗时8.5分钟，任务成功率达98%，能够连续运行8小时，该循环周期仅比熟练工人的约8分钟慢30秒。 整个过程由单台机械臂搭配单只灵巧手在无实时人工干预下完成，涵盖柔性装配、条形码扫描、使用螺丝枪紧固螺丝、按压测试设备按钮及零件卸载。 拧螺丝面临重大挑战，需要亚毫米级的对齐精度以应对防松螺丝晃动与工具反作用力。 临界点最终选择五指灵巧手而非夹爪来连续执行多类操作。 该系统借助真机强化学习及Omni-Maestro工业部署智能体框架来进行工序验证和故障回滚。 CEO乔天杰指出这标志着从开发模式向部署模式的转变，首席具身科学家黄碧丹则指出了当前依赖纯视觉数据集的模型中存在的数据短缺问题。
