- **[2026.09.26] Quality Magazine — How Tactile Sensing Fills the Gap Vision Can’t Measure**
  English: Robotic tactile sensing measures physical signals such as contact forces, slip, pressure, vibration, and temperature that machine vision cannot directly capture. Different tactile sensor types offer specific trade-offs: piezoresistive sensors provide simple construction and high sensitivity but may lose stability over time; capacitive sensors draw low power and respond quickly with limited spatial resolution; piezoelectric and triboelectric sensors are self-powered and fast but cannot measure static or sustained forces; magnetic sensors decouple three-dimensional forces cleanly in controlled magnetic environments; and optical or vision-based tactile sensors deliver the highest spatial resolution at the cost of complex optical systems and restricted form factor flexibility. While robots have advanced significantly in mobility, vision alone falls short in providing direct information about contact interactions like grip force, slip, compliance, and shear forces required for physical tasks such as grasping, insertion, press-fitting, sanding, and polishing. Artificial tactile systems replicate aspects of human mechanoreceptors to convert signals through signal processing or machine learning into useful outputs including grasp stability, slip detection, in-hand manipulation, object and texture recognition, and orientation estimation.
  中文：总结：机器人触觉传感可测量接触力、滑移、压力、振动和温度等机器视觉无法直接捕捉的物理信号。 不同类型的触觉传感器各有权衡：压阻传感器结构简单、灵敏度高但随时间可能失去稳定性；电容传感器功耗低、响应快但空间分辨率有限；压电和摩擦电传感器自供电且速度快，但无法测量静态和持续力；磁性传感器能在受控磁环境中清晰解耦三维力；光学或基于视觉的触觉传感器空间分辨率最高，但代价是光学系统更复杂且外形尺寸灵活性受限。 尽管机器人在移动能力上取得了显着进步，但单纯依靠视觉无法直接提供抓握力、滑移、柔顺度以及抓取、插入、压装、打磨和抛光等物理任务所需的剪切力等接触交互信息。 人工触觉系统能够复制人类机械感受器的部分功能，通过信号处理或机器学习将信号转化为有用的输出，包括抓握稳定性、滑移检测、手内操作、物体和纹理识别以及方向估计。
