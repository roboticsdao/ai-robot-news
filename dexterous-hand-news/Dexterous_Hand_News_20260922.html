MarkTechPost — Reward AI Releases OM-1: A Robot Policy Trained on Human Demonstrations Only, With No Teleoperation or On-Robot Data

Robotics startup Reward AI has released OM-1 (Omnibody Model 1), a general-purpose manipulation policy trained exclusively on human demonstrations without using any teleoperation data or on-robot data. The system follows the principle of 'One Model, One Data Interface, Any Body' and currently operates as an in-house policy with no weights, code, dataset, or API released. The hardware stack begins with the Omnibody Hand, a 7-DoF wearable that captures essential functions such as choosing contact points and reorienting objects. The One Data Interface captures wearer motion using high-frequency tactile sensing, proximity sensing, and global-shutter in-hand cameras. Reward AI utilizes visual-inertial tracking augmented with electromagnetic sensing and disturbance compensation, which reduced mean overshoot error by 60% at the highest speed of 67 cm/s in tests. OM-1 processes multimodal streams at their native sampling rates to generate robot actions directly. Below the policy, a high-frequency control layer trained via reinforcement learning handles velocity- and acceleration-dependent dynamics and system delays, operating on its own clock.

总结：机器人初创公司 Reward AI 发布了 OM-1（Omnibody Model 1），这是一个完全通过人类示范训练而成的通用操作策略，未使用任何远程操作数据或机器人端数据。 该系统遵循“一个模型、一个数据接口、任意机体”的原则，目前作为内部策略，未公开权重、代码、数据集或 API。 其硬件堆栈始于 Omnibody Hand，这是一个 7 自由度的可穿戴设备，能够捕捉选择接触点和重新定向物体等核心功能。 One Data Interface 通过高频率触手觉感知、接近感知以及全局快门手内相机来捕捉佩戴者的运动。 Reward AI 采用了结合电磁传感与干扰补偿的视觉惯性追踪，在最高 67 厘米/秒的速度测试中，平均超调误差降低了 60%。 OM-1 以传感器原生采样率处理多模态数据流，直接生成机器人动作。 在策略下方，一个通过强化学习训练的高频控制层负责处理速度与加速度相关的动力学以及系统延迟，并按自己的时钟独立运行。

PR Newswire — ShengShu Technology Unveils Motus2, a Self-Evolving General World Model for Dexterous Manipulation

ShengShu Technology Co-founder and CEO Yihang Luo unveiled Motus2, a self-evolving general world model for robotic dexterous manipulation, at the 2026 Inclusion Conference on the Bund on September 10. Motus2 unifies action generation, consequence prediction, and outcome evaluation within a single video-action model with shared parameters. It achieved an average success rate of 84% across five primary real-robot tasks. Combining model-based reinforcement learning with inference-time planning increased the average success rate from 65% to 75% in policy-optimization studies. The model has been evaluated on dual-arm robot platforms equipped with WUJI, WUJI Hand 2, and Sharpa Wave dexterous hands. Motus2 creates a closed loop where a policy interface generates candidate actions, a simulator interface predicts future visual states, and an evaluator interface assesses outcomes. The model leverages approximately 130,000 hours of monocular and stereo human video data combined with over 100 hours of robot-domain trajectories and supplementary alignment data to provide manipulation priors and observation adaptation.

总结：生数科技联合创始人兼CEO罗一航于9月10日在2026外滩大会上发布了 Motus2，这是一个用于机器人灵巧操作的自进化通用世界模型。 Motus2 在一个共享参数的视频动作模型中统一了动作生成、后果预测和结果评估。 它在五个主要的真实机器人任务中取得了 84% 的平均成功率。 在策略优化研究中，将基于模型的强化学习与推理时规划相结合，使平均成功率从 65% 提高到 75%。 该模型已在配备 WUJI、WUJI Hand 2 和 Sharpa Wave 灵巧手的双臂机器人平台上进行了评估。 Motus2 构建了一个闭环，其中策略接口生成候选动作，模拟器接口预测未来视觉状态，评估接口评估结果。 该模型利用约 13,0000 小时的单目和立体人类视频数据，结合 100 多个小时的机器人领域轨迹及补充对齐数据，提供操作先验和观测适应。

Interesting Engineering — Why robot hands are one of the hardest problems in humanoid robotics

Humanoid robots can easily perform athletic movements like backflips because the environment and movements are predictable, but household chores involving manipulation, such as folding shirts or picking up wet sponges, present high uncertainty due to objects bending, slipping, or deforming. A robotic hand requires sophisticated mechanics, tactile sensors, and software to constantly adjust grip without conscious thought, as vision alone cannot determine crushing force. Recent studies, including a 2026 Science Robotics paper from Zhejiang University, combine visual and tactile information with reinforcement learning, achieving an 85% success rate across five complex tasks involving 25 objects. Unlike industrial environments where trajectories and lighting are strictly controlled, homes introduce unpredictable object geometries. Research identifies bimanual coordination, stable navigation, and reachability as key requirements for real-world whole-body manipulation. Chinese robotics companies are increasingly targeting household chores, but a major missing ingredient is a large-scale dataset of people manipulating thousands of objects with synchronized sensor information.

总结：人形机器人可以轻松完成空翻等运动，因为环境和动作是可预测的，但涉及操作的家务活（如叠衬衫或捡起湿海绵）由于物体弯曲、滑动或变形而具有高度的不确定性。 机器人手部需要复杂的机械结构、触觉传感器和软件来在没有意识的情况下不断调整抓握，因为仅靠视觉无法确定挤压力量。 最近的研究（包括浙江大学 2026 年发表在 Science Robotics 上的论文）将视觉和触觉信息与强化学习结合，在涉及 25 个对象的五个复杂任务中实现了 85% 的成功率。 与轨迹和光照受到严格控制的工业环境不同，家庭环境引入了不可预测的物体几何形状。 研究将双臂协调、稳定导航和可达性确定为真实世界全身操作的关键要求。 中国机器人公司正越来越多地将目标瞄准家务活，但缺少的一个主要要素是包含同步传感器信息、人类操作数千种物体的宏大规模数据集。
