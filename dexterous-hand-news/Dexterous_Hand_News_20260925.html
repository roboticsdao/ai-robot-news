AZoSensors — Giving Robots a Sense of Touch: The Next Generation of Tactile Sensors

Researchers are developing tactile sensors that combine pressure, vibration, proximity, temperature, and texture into a flexible layer to give robots a working sense of touch. Single-signal sensors often mislead robots when properties overlap, but multimodal data significantly improves recognition accuracy, such as identifying 26 textiles with 95.49% accuracy by combining pressure and triboelectric data. Proximity sensing using capacitive twin helical electrodes detects objects over 5 cm away, allowing grippers to slow down before contact, which is especially useful for transparent items like glass. Advanced pressure sensing resolves normal and shear forces using microstructures like domes and pyramids, while arrays track 3D force and load distribution. Additionally, piezoelectric films like a 28 μm PVDF film on silicone layers convert mechanical oscillations into voltage to catch slips early.

总结：研究人员正在开发将压力、振动、接近度、温度和纹理结合到一个柔层中的触觉传感器，以赋予机器人实用的触觉。 当物理特性重合时，单一信号传感器常常会误导机器人，而多模态数据显著提高了识别准确率，例如通过结合压力和摩擦电数据，以95.49%的准确率识别了26种纺织品。 使用电容式双螺旋电极的接近传感可以检测5厘米以外的物体，使抓取器能够在接触前减速，这对于玻璃等透明物品特别有用。 先进的压力传感利用微结构（如圆顶和金字塔）来解析法向力和剪切力，而阵列则可以跟踪3D力和载荷分布。 此外，硅胶层上厚度为28微米的PVDF薄膜等压电薄膜可将机械振动转换为电压，以便早期捕捉滑动。

The Manila Times — Xynova Dexterous Hands: Programmable Dexterity for the Physical World

Xynova, a Hangzhou-based robotics startup founded in 2024, has achieved unicorn status while addressing the industry's need for high-performance dexterous hands. Led by founder and CEO Alex Xia, the company utilizes vertically integrated in-house R&D and manufacturing for motors, electronic controls, reducers, and sensors. Xynova pursues two parallel technology approaches: hybrid-drive dexterous hands for industrial and consumer applications, and direct-drive dexterous hands for AI research. Its products include the 23-DoF Flex 2 hybrid-drive hand unveiled at ICRA in June 2026, weighing 400 grams, and the 22-DoF Prima 1 direct-drive hand introduced in August at WRC in Beijing. The company has raised RMB 1.5 billion (approx. USD 220 million) from investors including CATL, Xiaomi, JD. com, and Meituan, and has commissioned a 5,400-square-meter manufacturing facility in Hangzhou to fulfill global orders.

总结：成立于2024年的杭州机器人初创公司曦诺未来（Xynova）已跻身独角兽行列，致力于满足行业对高性能灵巧手的需求。 在创始人兼CEO Alex Xia的领导下，该公司对电机、电子控制、减速器和传感器进行垂直整合的内部研发与制造。 曦诺未来并行推进两条技术路线：面向工业和消费级应用的混驱灵巧手，以及专为AI研究定制的直驱灵巧手。 其产品包括2026年6月在ICRA上亮相的23自由度Flex 2混驱手（重400克），以及8月在北京WRC上推出的22自由度Prima 1直驱手。 该公司已从宁德时代、小米、京东和美团等投资者处筹集了15亿元人民币（约合2.2亿美元），并在杭州投运了一座5,400平方米的制造工厂以满足全球订单。

finance.biggo.com — Unitree Robotics unveils Dex5-S dexterous hand: 22 degrees of freedom starting at 39,900 yuan, humanoid robot "hand" price war heats up

Unitree Robotics has launched its Dex5-S five-finger dexterous hand featuring 22 degrees of freedom at a starting price of 39,900 yuan (approximately $6,000), cutting the previous cost threshold of 100,000 yuan (approximately $15,000) for high-performance options. The hand matches a human hand's 1:1 scale with dimensions of 102x187x26 millimeters and weighs about 620 grams in an aluminum alloy shell, nearly halving its predecessor's 1.1-kilogram weight. All 22 motors support direct drive, back-drive, and dual-encoder control, achieving independent joint control alongside a peak impact torque protection structure. It supports a maximum single-hand load of 2 kilograms and a continuous load of 1 kilogram. The Dex5-S offers Standard and Pro editions, with the Pro edition adding tactile sensing, and is compatible with Unitree's H1 and G1 humanoid robots.

总结：宇树科技（Unitree Robotics）推出了Dex5-S五指灵巧手，具备22个自由度，起售价为3.99万元人民币（约合6,000美元），将以往高性能产品10万元人民币（约合15,000美元）以上的高价门槛缩减了一半以上。 该产品采用1:1真手比例设计，尺寸为102×187×26毫米，铝合金外壳整手重量约620克，较上一代的1.1公斤重量几乎减半。 全部22个电机支持直驱、反驱和双编码器控制，实现关节独立可控并配有峰值冲击扭矩保护结构。 其单手最大负载为2公斤，持续作业负载为1公斤。 Dex5-S提供标准版和Pro版，其中Pro版增加触觉传感功能，并兼容宇树H1和G1人形机器人平台。
