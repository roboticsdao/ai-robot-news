- **[2026.09.24] AZoSensors — Giving Robots a Sense of Touch: The Next Generation of Tactile Sensors**
  English: Researchers are developing tactile sensors that combine pressure, vibration, proximity, temperature, and texture into a flexible layer to give robots a working sense of touch. Single-signal sensors often mislead robots when properties overlap, but multimodal data significantly improves recognition accuracy, such as identifying 26 textiles with 95.49% accuracy by combining pressure and triboelectric data. Proximity sensing using capacitive twin helical electrodes detects objects over 5 cm away, allowing grippers to slow down before contact, which is especially useful for transparent items like glass. Advanced pressure sensing resolves normal and shear forces using microstructures like domes and pyramids, while arrays track 3D force and load distribution. Additionally, piezoelectric films like a 28 μm PVDF film on silicone layers convert mechanical oscillations into voltage to catch slips early.
  中文：总结：研究人员正在开发将压力、振动、接近度、温度和纹理结合到一个柔层中的触觉传感器，以赋予机器人实用的触觉。 当物理特性重合时，单一信号传感器常常会误导机器人，而多模态数据显著提高了识别准确率，例如通过结合压力和摩擦电数据，以95.49%的准确率识别了26种纺织品。 使用电容式双螺旋电极的接近传感可以检测5厘米以外的物体，使抓取器能够在接触前减速，这对于玻璃等透明物品特别有用。 先进的压力传感利用微结构（如圆顶和金字塔）来解析法向力和剪切力，而阵列则可以跟踪3D力和载荷分布。 此外，硅胶层上厚度为28微米的PVDF薄膜等压电薄膜可将机械振动转换为电压，以便早期捕捉滑动。
