Tech Briefs — This Robot Hand Doesn’t Just Grab—It Walks

Researchers at ETH Zurich have developed an untethered, 818-gram robotic hand that can walk on its five fingers across various surfaces, recover from falls, and interact with objects and a keyboard. By combining an anthropomorphic hand with reinforcement learning, the project demonstrates a design approach that enables existing hardware to serve as both a mobile platform and a manipulator.

总结：苏黎世联邦理工学院的研究人员开发了一款重818克的无绳机器人手，它可以用五根手指在不同表面上行走、从跌落中恢复，并与物体和键盘进行交互。 通过将拟人手与强化学习相结合，该项目展示了一种设计方法，使现有硬件能够同时充当移动平台和操作器。

Tech Briefs — This $500 Robot Hand Takes a Simpler Approach to Dexterity

The Cartisian Hand is a $500, seven degree of freedom robot hand built from 3D-printed parts that uses seven linear actuators and two simple grippers to perform inhand manipulation. Developed to match mechanical mechanisms like threads and pivots, it features two independently actuated parallel grippers, four sliding fingertips, and one adjustable separation between grippers. Utilizing five reusable motion primitives, the hand can manipulate 35 objects across laboratory, manufacturing, and household settings without hardware changes or external visual feedback.

总结：Cartisian手是一款售价低于$500、拥有七个自由度的机器人手，由3D打印部件制成，使用七个线性执行器和两个简单的夹爪来执行手内操作。 为了匹配螺纹和枢轴等机械机构而开发，它具有两个独立驱动的平行夹爪、四个滑动指尖以及夹爪之间可调节的间距。 利用五个可重复使用的运动基元，该手无需硬件更改或外部视觉反馈即可在实验室、制造和家庭环境中操作35种物体。

Trend Hunter — Skill-Capturing Robotics Gloves: Tacta Systems Records Human Tasks to Train Robotic Hands

Tacta Systems is scaling production of skill-capturing robotics gloves that record force, motion, and temperature data from skilled workers. Combined with video through the company's Skill Capture system, this data helps train TactaBot, which integrates the Tacta Hand with a Dexterous Intelligence AI engine for precise manufacturing tasks. AZoSensors — Giving Robots a Sense of Touch: The Next Generation of Tactile Sensors

Researchers are developing advanced tactile sensors that combine pressure, vibration, proximity, temperature, and texture cues into a flexible layer to give robots a working sense of touch. A recent Advanced Science study showed that combining pressure and triboelectric data significantly improved textile recognition accuracy compared to single signals. Twin helical electrodes in capacitive sensors enabled proximity detection over 5 cm away, while microstructures tracked normal and oblique forces for slip detection. Additionally, a 28 μm polyvinylidene fluoride film placed on a silicone finger layer successfully detected slip-induced mechanical oscillations.

总结：研究人员正在开发先进的触觉传感器，将压力、振动、接近度、温度和纹理提示组合到一个柔性层中，赋予机器人工作触觉。 Advanced Science最近的一项研究表明，与单一信号相比，结合压力和摩擦电数据显著提高了纺织品识别准确率。 电容传感器中的双螺旋电极能够实现5厘米以上的接近检测，同时微结构可以追踪法向力和斜向力以进行滑移检测。 此外，放置在硅胶手指层上的28微米聚偏氟乙烯薄膜成功检测到了滑移引起的机械振动。
