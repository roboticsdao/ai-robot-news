- **[2026.11.12] Electronics Media — ATFCD52 Tactile Sensor for Advanced Robotic Touch Sensing**
  English: Microchip Technology introduced the ATFCD52 high-speed capacitance digitizer to convert signals from fine-grid, two-dimensional capacitive sensor arrays into high-sensitivity digital data for robotic touch sensing. Supporting up to 52 channels with up to 32 TX by 20 RX lines, the device enables dense sensor arrays with frame rates of up to 800 Hz to detect slip and adjust grip. The ATFCD52 can resolve capacitance changes below 1 femtofarad and includes frequency hopping and oversampling to mitigate interference from servo motors and power electronics in noisy environments. It is supported by the EV-ATFCD52 development board and ATFCD52 Configurator software.
  中文：总结：Microchip Technology推出了ATFCD52高速电容数字化仪，用于将细网格二维电容传感器阵列的信号转换为高灵敏度数字数据，以支持机器人触觉传感。 该器件支持多达52个通道以及最高32个发射线和20个接收线，使密集的传感器阵列能够实现高达800 Hz的帧率，从而检测滑移并调整抓握。 ATFCD52能够分辨低于1飞法拉的电容变化，并采用跳频和过采样技术来减轻嘈杂环境中伺服电机和电力电子设备的干扰。 该产品由EV-ATFCD52开发板和ATFCD52 Configurator软件提供支持。
