- **[2026.11.22] Superpower Daily — Sundae Robotics 11: Beyond the Gripper — Teaching Robots Dexterity Through Touch, Vision, and Play**
  English: Amazon Frontier AI and Robotics Research Scientist Akash Sharma presented a talk titled 'Beyond the Gripper — Teaching Robots Dexterity Through Touch, Vision, and Play' at Sundae Robotics 11. The talk addressed limitations in conventional parallel-jaw grippers and standard human demonstrations or simulations. Sharma introduced four approaches: PTLD for learning tactile policies without tactile simulation by distilling privileged simulation-trained teachers on real hardware; INVAR for learning visual invariance from simulation pairs for zero-shot transfer; CARD for retargeting human demonstrations based on touch rather than movement; and DSP for turning human play into goal-conditioned dexterity through autonomous play and world models. Akash Sharma is a Research Scientist at Amazon Frontier AI and Robotics, working with Jitendra Malik, and holds a PhD from the Carnegie Mellon University Robotics Institute.
  中文：总结：Amazon Frontier AI & Robotics研究科学家、卡耐基梅隆大学机器人研究所博士Akash Sharma在Sundae Robotics 11活动中发表了题为《Beyond the Gripper — Teaching Robots Dexterity Through Touch, Vision, and Play》的主题演讲。 演讲探讨了超越传统平行夹爪的灵巧操作方法，并指出传统人类示范和模拟的局限性。 Sharma介绍了四种方法：PTLD利用真实硬件上的特权仿真训练教师来学习触觉策略；INVAR通过仿真对学习视觉不变性以实现零样本迁移；CARD根据接触点而非运动方式来重定向人类示范；DSP通过自主玩耍和世界模型将人类玩耍转化为目标条件的灵巧性。 Akash Sharma目前在Amazon Frontier AI & Robotics担任研究科学家并拥有卡耐基梅隆大学机器人研究所博士学位。
