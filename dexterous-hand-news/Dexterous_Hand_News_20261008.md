- **[2026.10.07] Interesting Engineering — Watch: Chinese robot hand takes on delicate Suzhou embroidery with human-like dexterity**
  English: Chinese robotics startup AGILINK, a spin-off of AgiBot, demonstrated its OmniHand 3 Ultra robot hand performing traditional Suzhou embroidery techniques, including separating a silk thread into 16 finer strands, threading a needle, stretching fabric across a hoop, twisting thread, and making stitches. Embroidery master Fu Xianghong taught the robot how to manipulate the silk, which can bend, slip, and change shape under force. The OmniHand 3 Ultra features 21 active degrees of freedom in a direct-drive architecture, a vision-based tactile sensor in every fingertip measuring three-dimensional force and 0.08-millimeter deformations, and a 300-point contact sensor in the palm. Weighing 1.9 pounds (630 grams), the hand can hold 6.6 pounds (3 kilograms) steadily, lift up to 17.6 pounds (8 kilograms), open and close in about 0.3 seconds, and achieve a positional repeatability of about 0.008 inches (0.2 millimeters). AGILINK combined teleoperation data with reinforcement learning to let the robot adapt its movements. The article also notes other systems, such as LinkerBot’s Linker Hand L30 Pro and Boston Dynamics' four-digit Atlas hand.
  中文：总结：中国机器人初创企业AGILINK（AgiBot的衍生公司）展示了其OmniHand 3 Ultra机器人灵巧手执行传统苏州刺绣技术的全过程，包括将一根丝线分出16根细丝、穿针、在绣绷上拉紧织物、捻线以及缝针。 刺绣大师傅香红指导机器人如何操作在受力时会弯曲、滑动和变形的丝绸。 OmniHand 3 Ultra采用直驱架构，拥有21个主动自由度，每个指尖均配有基于视觉的触觉传感器以测量三维力和0.08毫米的形变，手掌则集成了一个300点接触传感器。 该手重1.9磅（630克），可稳定抓取6.6磅（3公斤）重物并举起最高17.6磅（8公斤）的物体，开合时间约为0.3秒，位置重复精度约为0.008英寸（0.2毫米）。 AGILINK将遥操作数据与强化学习相结合，使机器人能够适应不同的动作。 文章还提及了LinkerBot的Linker Hand L30 Pro以及波士顿动力的四指Atlas手等其他系统。
