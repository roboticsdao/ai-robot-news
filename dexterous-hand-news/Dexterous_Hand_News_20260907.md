- **[2026.09.02] Tech Briefs — Nature vs. Nurture with Regards to Robotic Hands**
  English: Researchers in the ValeroLab at the USC Viterbi School of Engineering explored whether tactile sensation is strictly necessary for learning to control robotic fingers. Led by Professor Francisco Valero-Cuevas, alongside Romina Mir, Ali Marjaninejad, and Andrew Erwin, the team published a paper in Science Advances addressing the nature versus nurture question using computational modeling and machine learning. Simulating a three-finger robotic hand, the study demonstrated that the sequence of learning, or the curriculum, is critical. The findings reveal that if the curriculum follows a specific sequence, a simulated robotic hand can successfully learn to manipulate objects even with incomplete or absent tactile sensation, highlighting the crucial role of rewards in guiding system development.
  中文：总结：南加州大学维特比工程学院Valero实验室的研究人员探讨了触觉感知对于学习控制机器人手指是否绝对必要。 由弗朗西斯科·瓦莱罗-奎瓦斯教授以及罗米娜·米尔、阿里·马尔加尼内贾德和安德鲁·欧文组成的团队在《科学进展》杂志上发表了一篇论文，利用计算建模和机器学习探讨了“先天与后天”的问题。 通过模拟三指机器人手，该研究表明学习顺序即“课程”至关重要。 研究结果揭示，如果课程遵循特定顺序，模拟机器人手即使在触觉感知不完整或缺失的情况下也能成功学会操控物体，这凸显了奖励在引导系统发展中的关键作用。

- **[2026.09.02] AI Insider — X Square Robot Launches TwinDEX for Robot-Free Dexterous Manipulation Training**
  English: X Square Robot has introduced TwinDEX, a paired wearable and robotic manipulation system designed to collect robot-free training data and transfer demonstrations to real-world dexterous manipulation. The system features a matched three-finger, nine-degree-of-freedom design with seven active and two passive degrees of freedom for both the wearable device and the robotic end effector. TwinDEX integrates multi-view RGB cameras, six-degree-of-freedom wrist poses, finger joint states, and fingertip tactile signals. X Square Robot reported up to 5.3 times the effective data-collection throughput of on-robot teleoperation and tested the system on tasks including tool use, object manipulation, and a 24-step autonomous chemistry experiment.
