- **[2026.09.02] Tech Briefs — Nature vs. Nurture with Regards to Robotic Hands**
  English: Researchers in the ValeroLab in the Viterbi School of Engineering at USC, including Romina Mir, Ali Marjaninejad, Andrew Erwin, and Professor Francisco Valero-Cuevas, explored whether tactile sensation is always necessary for learning to control robotic and prosthetic fingers. Publishing their findings in Science Advances in a paper titled 'Curriculum Is More Influential Than Haptic Information During Reinforcement Learning of Object Manipulation Against Gravity', the team used computational modeling and machine learning to simulate a three-finger robotic hand. The study demonstrates that the sequence of learning, or the curriculum, is critical, and that a simulated robotic hand can learn to manipulate with incomplete or even absent tactile sensation if the curriculum occurs in a particular sequence. Co-first author Mir noted the importance of the sequence of rewards for training, while Valero-Cuevas stated that reward guides the development of the system, connecting machine learning and biology to advance artificial intelligence systems.
  中文：总结：南加州大学维特比工程学院ValeroLab的研究人员Romina Mir、Ali Marjaninejad、Andrew Erwin和Francisco Valero-Cuevas教授探讨了触觉是否在学习控制机器人和假肢手指时总是必不可少的。 该团队在《科学进展》期刊上发表了题为《在对抗重力的物体操作强化学习中，课程比触觉信息更有影响力》的论文，通过计算建模和机器学习模拟了三指机器人手。 研究表明，学习顺序（即课程）至关重要，如果课程按特定顺序进行，模拟机器人手即使在触觉不完整甚至缺失的情况下也能学会操作。 共同第一作者Mir指出奖励序列对训练的重要性，而Valero-Cuevas教授则表示奖励指导系统发展，将机器学习与生物学联系起来，以推进人工智能系统。

- **[2026.09.02] AI Insider — X Square Robot Launches TwinDEX for Robot-Free Dexterous Manipulation Training**
  English: X Square Robot introduced TwinDEX, a paired wearable and robotic manipulation system designed to collect robot-free training data and transfer demonstrations to real-world dexterous manipulation. The system combines a wearable data-collection device with a closely matched robotic end effector, both utilizing a three-finger, nine-degree-of-freedom design consisting of seven active and two passive degrees of freedom. TwinDEX matches the wearable and robotic systems across kinematic chains, joint axes, link proportions, contact geometry, surface materials, visual appearance, and sensor placement, allowing finger states to map directly to the robot's joint space without complex retargeting. Equipped with multi-view RGB cameras, six-degree-of-freedom wrist poses, finger joint states, and fingertip tactile signals, the system achieves up to 5.3 times the effective data-collection throughput of on-robot teleoperation. X Square Robot evaluated TwinDEX on tasks such as cap twisting, sweeping, opening books, releasing latches, operating syringes, and a 24-step autonomous chemistry experiment.
  中文：总结：X Square Robot推出了TwinDEX，这是一个成对的穿戴式与机器人操作系统，旨在收集免机器人训练数据并将示范转移到现实世界的灵巧操作中。 该系统将穿戴式数据采集设备与紧密匹配的机器人末端执行器相结合，两者均采用三指、九自由度设计，包含七个主动自由度和两个被动自由度。 TwinDEX在运动链、关节轴、连杆比例、接触几何形状、表面材料、外观和传感器布置等方面使穿戴式和机器人系统相匹配，使手指状态能够直接映射到机器人的关节空间，无需复杂的重新定位。 该系统配备了多视角RGB摄像头、六自由度手腕位姿、手指关节状态和指尖触觉信号，实现了高达机器人端遥操作5.3倍的有效数据采集吞吐量。 X Square Robot对TwinDEX进行了评估，任务包括拧瓶盖、扫地、打开书籍、释放工具箱闩锁、操作注射器以及一项24步自主化学实验。
