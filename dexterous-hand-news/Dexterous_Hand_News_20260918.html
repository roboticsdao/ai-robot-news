Vietnam.vn — Why can't robotic hands be as dexterous as human hands?

Humanoid robots excel at running and backflips because these movements are predictable and precisely controlled, but household tasks like folding clothes or picking up wet sponges remain extremely difficult due to unpredictable environments, complex object physics, and a lack of training data. Unlike factory robots that operate in controlled settings with simple grippers, human hands feature over 30 muscles, 27 joints, and 17,000 sensory receptors, giving them high dexterity. According to LinkerBot founder Alex Zhou Yong, building a robotic arm is 100 times harder than producing a humanoid robot, with dexterity demands 10 times higher than other body parts. Researchers are increasingly focusing on tactile sensing and combining visual and tactile data with reinforcement and imitation learning, as highlighted by a 2026 Zhejiang University study published in Science Robotics that achieved an 85% success rate in 5 complex tasks involving 25 subjects.

总结：人形机器人虽然能够跑步、跳跃甚至做后空翻，但折衣服、捡起湿海绵或转动钥匙等简单家务对它们而言却极具挑战，主要由于环境不可预测、物体物理形态复杂以及缺乏训练数据。 与环境可控、使用简单夹爪的工厂机器人不同，人手拥有30多块肌肉、27个关节以及17000多个感觉感受器，具备极高的灵巧度。 LinkerBot创始人Alex Zhou Yong向《卫报》表示，制造机械臂的难度是生产人形机器人的100倍，其灵巧度要求比其他身体部位高出10倍。 当前研究正日益聚焦于触觉传感，例如浙江大学2026年在《Science Robotics》上发表的一项研究，将视觉与触觉信息同在线强化学习和模仿学习相结合，在涉及25个对象的5个复杂任务中取得了85%的成功率。

MarkTechPost — Reward AI Releases OM-1: A Robot Policy Trained on Human Demonstrations Only, With No Teleoperation or On-Robot Data

Reward AI has released OM-1 (Omnibody Model 1), a general-purpose manipulation policy trained exclusively on human demonstrations using a sensorized glove, avoiding teleoperation data and on-robot data entirely. The system follows the principle 'One Model, One Data Interface, Any Body' and runs on industrial arms and humanoids at human speed. The input stack begins with the Omnibody Hand, a 7-degree-of-freedom wearable designed around pinch, flexion, and coupled finger movements without requiring per-user adjustments. The Data Interface captures contact using high-frequency tactile sensing, proximity sensing, and global-shutter in-hand cameras, augmented by electromagnetic sensing with disturbance compensation to reduce overshoot error by 60% at high speeds. Below the policy, a reinforcement learning control layer handles dynamics, disturbances, and system delays on its own clock.

总结：Reward AI发布了通用操作策略OM-1（Omnibody Model 1），该模型完全通过人类佩戴传感器手套的示范进行训练，未使用任何遥操作数据或真机数据，并遵循“一个模型、一个数据接口、任意本体”的原则在工业机械臂和人形机器人上以人类速度运行。 输入端采用7自由度的Omnibody Hand可穿戴设备，支持捏合、屈曲及手指耦合运动且无需逐个用户调整。 数据接口结合了高频率触觉传感、接近传感和全局快门手内相机，并辅以具备抗扰动补偿的电磁传感技术，在高速下将超调误差降低了60%。 在策略下方，则运行着一个通过强化学习训练的独立时钟控制层，用于处理动力学、外部干扰和系统延迟。

Interesting Engineering — Why robot hands are one of the hardest problems in humanoid robotics

Humanoid robots can perform complex athletic movements like backflips because they represent predictable, precisely controlled sequences, whereas household chores like folding laundry, picking up wet sponges, or turning keys involve high uncertainty because objects bend, slip, and deform. While factory arms operate in controlled environments with specialized tools and fixed trajectories, household robots face unpredictable physical contact and must coordinate bimanual actions, stable navigation, and reachability. The human hand features over 30 muscles, 27 joints, and 17,000 sensory receptors, offering high dexterity that vision alone cannot replicate without tactile sensing. A 2026 Science Robotics study from Zhejiang University combined visual and tactile data with reinforcement and online imitation learning to achieve an 85% success rate across 5 complex tasks involving 25 objects, highlighting the critical role of touch in overcoming the data and control bottlenecks of household robotics.

总结：人形机器人能够完成如后空翻等复杂的运动，因为这些动作属于可预测且能精确控制的序列；而折衣服、捡起湿海绵或转动钥匙等家务活却充满不确定性，物体往往会弯曲、滑动和变形。 工厂机械臂在有固定轨迹、可预测光照和专用工具的可控环境中运行，而家庭环境则截然相反，机器人需要应对复杂的物理接触并协调双手操作、稳定导航与触及范围。 人手拥有30多块肌肉、27个关节和17000多个感觉感受器，具备视觉单凭自身无法复制的高灵巧度。 浙江大学2026年发表在《Science Robotics》上的一项研究将视觉与触觉信息同强化学习及在线模仿学习相结合，在涉及25个对象的5个复杂任务中取得了85%的成功率，凸显了触觉在克服家庭机器人数据与控制瓶颈中的关键作用。
