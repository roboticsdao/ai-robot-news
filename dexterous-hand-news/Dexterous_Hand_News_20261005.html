Interesting Engineering — Boston Dynamics unveils 13-DOF robotic hand for its Atlas humanoid

Boston Dynamics unveiled a new four-finger robotic hand with 13 degrees of freedom for its Atlas humanoid, moving away from a conventional five-fingered design to prioritize dexterity, strength, and manufacturability. The hand features direct actuation, backdrivable transmissions, and dense tactile sensors on the fingertips and palm. According to the company, the design allows the robot to handle heavy loads, manipulate tools, and recover from slips while using simulation-based AI training to transfer control policies to physical hardware.

总结：波士顿动力公司为其阿特拉斯人形机器人推出了一款拥有四根手指和13个自由度的新型机器人手，放弃了传统的五指设计，以优先考虑灵巧性、强度和可制造性。 该手部采用直接驱动、可反向驱动的传动装置，并在指尖和手掌上配备了密集的触觉传感器。 该公司表示，这种设计使机器人能够搬运重物、操作工具并从滑落中恢复，同时利用基于模拟的AI训练将控制策略转移到物理硬件上。

Unite.AI — Honda, Redwire Pair Robotic Hand and Arm for Space Station Research

Honda announced on October 1, 2026, that its U. S. space development team is partnering with Redwire to pair its multi-fingered robotic hand with Redwire's STAARK robotic arm for future commercial space stations, aiming to increase research capacity and reduce astronaut crew time. The robotic hand features 16 actuated joints, finger-thumb opposability, a maximum fingertip force of 50 newtons, six-axis force sensors on each fingertip, and 288 tactile sensor channels, having completed over 450,000 durability test cycles. Representatives from both companies will discuss the integrated solution at the International Astronautical Congress in Antalya, Turkey, from October 5 to 9, 2026.

总结：本田于2026年10月1日宣布，其美国太空开发团队正与Redwire合作，将本田的多指机器人手与Redwire的STAARK机器人机械臂配对，用于未来的商业空间站，旨在提高研究能力并减少宇航员的乘组时间。 该机器人手具有16个驱动关节、手指与拇指的对向性、最大50牛顿的指尖力、每个指尖上的六维力传感器以及288个触觉传感器通道，并已完成超过45万次的耐久性测试。 两家公司的代表将于2026年10月5日至9日在土耳其安塔利亚举行的International Astronautical Congress上讨论这一集成解决方案。

Unite.AI — DH-Robotics Unveils ADH-5-13 Five-Finger Dexterous Hand at IROS 2026

DH-Robotics debuted the ADH-5-13 five-finger dexterous hand on September 30, 2026, at the IROS 2026 conference in Pittsburgh, alongside its UDH-3-7 three-finger direct-drive hand and wider end-effector portfolio. IROS 2026 runs September 27–October 1, 2026. The ADH-5-13 features 13 independently controllable active degrees of freedom in an anthropomorphic layout, weighs 735 g, and supports a 20 kg payload. It uses a fully direct-drive, backdrivable architecture with dual magnetic encoders per joint, fingertip tactile sensing, and a serviceable modular design that DH-Robotics states increases maintenance efficiency by 300%. The UDH-3-7 carries seven active degrees of freedom across a three-finger layout, offering a 15 kg maximum payload, 50 N maximum fingertip force, and a one-million-cycle design life.

总结：DH-Robotics于2026年9月30日在匹兹堡举行的IROS 2026会议上首次推出了ADH-5-13五指灵巧手，该手拥有13个独立可控的主动自由度，并展示了UDH-3-7三指直接驱动灵巧手及其末端执行器产品组合。 IROS 2026于2026年9月27日至10月1日举行。 ADH-5-13采用类人五指配置，重735 g，额定抓取负载达20 kg，每个关节带有双磁编码器，并集成指尖触觉传感及可独立更换的模块化设计，可将维护效率提升300%。 UDH-3-7采用三指布局和7个主动自由度，额定负载15 kg，最大指尖力50 N，设计寿命为一百万次周期。
