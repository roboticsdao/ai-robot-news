- **[2026.10.30] Tech Briefs — Color-Changing Tactile Sensor Allows Robots to “See” and Touch in Real-Time**
  English: Engineers at Queen Mary University of London, including postdoctoral researcher Giacomo Sasso, have developed a color-changing tactile sensor that allows robots to see and touch in real-time. By transforming invisible forces into dynamic color patterns on a soft sensing surface, the material captures high-resolution maps of contact, strain, and pressure using a standard USB camera without needing complex reconstruction algorithms. This technology can enable robotic grippers to assemble micro-scale components, provide artificial limbs with a richer sense of touch, and help surgical systems distinguish healthy from abnormal tissue. The project combines the research fields of soft robotics and material science through collaboration with institutions such as the University of Florence, University of Trieste, and University of Trento.
  中文：总结：伦敦玛丽女王大学的工程师（包括博士后研究员 Giacomo Sasso）开发了一种变色触觉传感器，使机器人能够实时“看到”并触摸。 通过将无形力转化为软传感表面上的动态色彩图案，该材料无需复杂的重建算法，即可使用标准 USB 摄像头立即捕获接触、应变和压力的的高分辨率图。 这项技术能够使机器人抓手组装微型组件，为假肢提供更丰富的触感，并帮助手术系统通过材料的颜色反应直接读取精细压力特征来区分健康与异常组织。 该项目通过与佛罗伦萨大学、的里雅斯特大学和特伦托大学等机构的合作，将软体机器人与材料科学的研究领域结合在了一起。
