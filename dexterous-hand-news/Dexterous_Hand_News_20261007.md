- **[2026.10.02] Interesting Engineering — Boston Dynamics unveils 13-DOF robotic hand for its Atlas humanoid**
  English: Boston Dynamics has unveiled a new 13-degree-of-freedom, four-finger robotic hand designed for its Atlas humanoid, replacing conventional five-fingered designs to improve industrial dexterity and tool operation. The hand features an opposable thumb, direct actuation, a size comparable to a large human hand, and the ability to carry a loaded mini-fridge weighing over 100 pounds. The company omitted a pinky finger after tests showed four fingers could successfully handle in-house reorientation, slip recovery, and trigger pressing while reducing costs and failure points. Built with AI training in mind, the design uses backdrivable transmissions for proprioception, dense pressure-sensitive tactile sensors, and high-fidelity simulation compatibility to enable reinforcement learning transfer to physical hardware.
  中文：总结：波士顿动力为其 Atlas 人形机器人发布了一款采用四根手指和 13 个自由度的新型机器人手，旨在替代传统的五指设计以提升工业灵活性与工具操作能力。 该手部配备可对指的拇指、直接驱动、与大型人手相近的尺寸，并能搬运重量超过 100 磅的已装载迷离冰箱。 公司在测试表明四指已能处理重定向、防滑恢复和扳机按压同时降低成本和故障点后，去除了小指。 该设计以人工智能训练为核心，采用可逆传动系统以实现本体感觉、密集的压力敏感触觉传感器，以及高保真仿真兼容性，从而将强化学习策略传输至物理硬件。
