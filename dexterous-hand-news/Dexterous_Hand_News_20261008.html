Interesting Engineering — Watch: Chinese robot hand takes on delicate Suzhou embroidery with human-like dexterity

Chinese robotics startup AGILINK, a spin-off of AgiBot, demonstrated its OmniHand 3 Ultra robot hand performing traditional Suzhou embroidery techniques, including separating a silk thread into 16 finer strands, threading a needle, stretching fabric across a hoop, twisting thread, and making stitches. Embroidery master Fu Xianghong taught the robot how to manipulate the silk, which can bend, slip, and change shape under force. The OmniHand 3 Ultra features 21 active degrees of freedom in a direct-drive architecture, a vision-based tactile sensor in every fingertip measuring three-dimensional force and 0.08-millimeter deformations, and a 300-point contact sensor in the palm. Weighing 1.9 pounds (630 grams), the hand can hold 6.6 pounds (3 kilograms) steadily, lift up to 17.6 pounds (8 kilograms), open and close in about 0.3 seconds, and achieve a positional repeatability of about 0.008 inches (0.2 millimeters). AGILINK combined teleoperation data with reinforcement learning to let the robot adapt its movements. The article also notes other systems, such as LinkerBot’s Linker Hand L30 Pro and Boston Dynamics' four-digit Atlas hand.

总结：中国机器人初创企业AGILINK（AgiBot的衍生公司）展示了其OmniHand 3 Ultra机器人灵巧手执行传统苏州刺绣技术的全过程，包括将一根丝线分出16根细丝、穿针、在绣绷上拉紧织物、捻线以及缝针。 刺绣大师傅香红指导机器人如何操作在受力时会弯曲、滑动和变形的丝绸。 OmniHand 3 Ultra采用直驱架构，拥有21个主动自由度，每个指尖均配有基于视觉的触觉传感器以测量三维力和0.08毫米的形变，手掌则集成了一个300点接触传感器。 该手重1.9磅（630克），可稳定抓取6.6磅（3公斤）重物并举起最高17.6磅（8公斤）的物体，开合时间约为0.3秒，位置重复精度约为0.008英寸（0.2毫米）。 AGILINK将遥操作数据与强化学习相结合，使机器人能够适应不同的动作。 文章还提及了LinkerBot的Linker Hand L30 Pro以及波士顿动力的四指Atlas手等其他系统。

Hyundai Motor Group — Boston Dynamics Atlas Next-Generation Four-Finger Robot Hand

Hyundai Motor Group detailed the design of the next-generation four-finger robot hand for its Atlas robot. The hand features 13 degrees of freedom, with 4 degrees of freedom in the opposable thumb and 3 in each of the other fingers, enabling tool use, precision assembly, and in-hand reorientation. Dense pressure tactile sensors across the fingertips and palm detect subtle contact. Sim-to-real reinforcement learning supports robust manipulation across diverse conditions by transferring behaviors trained in high-fidelity simulation to real hardware. Designed with human-hand size, simplicity, ruggedness, and repairability, the hand targets real-world industrial tasks within existing workspaces.

总结：现代汽车集团详细介绍了其Atlas机器人新一代四指机器人手的设计。 该机械手拥有13个自由度，其中对立拇指有4个自由度，其余每根手指有3个自由度，能够支持工具使用、精密装配和手中重新定向。 指尖和手掌上的密集压力触感传感器可检测微小接触。 仿真到真实的强化学习通过将仿真训练的行为转移到真实硬件上，支持在各种条件下的强大操作。 该机械手采用人手尺寸、简单性、坚固性和可修复性设计，旨在现有工作空间内完成真实的工业任务。

Interesting Engineering — Watch: Chinese robot hand uses 20 joints to pull off magic tricks

Chinese robotics startup LinkerBot demonstrated the dexterity of its LinkerHand O30 through magic tricks including a card change, pulling a toy car out of a card, and a coin disappearance. Founded in 2023, LinkerBot designed the O30 with 20 active degrees of freedom and a direct-drive architecture combining brushless motors and a precision transmission system. The hand weighs 1.6 pounds (730 grams), can communicate via CAN and CAN FD connections receiving commands up to 500 times per second, achieves a positioning accuracy of +-0.20 mm, opens or closes in about 0.8 seconds, and runs on a 24-48 V DC power supply. It provides a maximum load of up to 66 pounds (30 kilograms), with a thumb fingertip force of up to 24 N, a four-finger lateral pinch force of 30 N, and a five-finger grasping force of 70 N. The hand is rated for a maximum power consumption of 150 W and static current as low as 0.4 A at 24 V, making it suitable for high-precision manufacturing applications in electronics, automotive, and aerospace production.

总结：中国机器人初创公司灵动科技通过扑克牌变牌、从牌中拉出玩具车和硬币消失等魔术表演展示了其LinkerHand O30的灵活性。 灵动科技成立于2023年，其设计的O30拥有20个主动自由度，并采用结合了无刷电机与精密传动系统的直驱架构。 该手重1.6 pounds (730 grams)，可通过CAN和CAN FD连接进行通信，接收指令频率高达每秒500次，定位精度达+-0.20 mm，开合时间约0.8秒，采用24-48 V DC电源供电。 它提供高达66 pounds (30 kilograms)的最大负载，拇指指尖力高达24 N，四指侧捏力达30 N，五指抓握力达70 N。 该手的最大功耗为150 W，24 V下的静态电流低至0.4 A，适用于电子、汽车和航空航天生产中的高精度制造应用。
