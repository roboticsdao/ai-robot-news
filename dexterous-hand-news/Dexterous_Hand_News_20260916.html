MarkTechPost — Reward AI Releases OM-1: A Robot Policy Trained on Human Demonstrations Only, With No Teleoperation or On-Robot Data

Reward AI has released OM-1 (Omnibody Model 1), a general-purpose manipulation policy that learns exclusively from human demonstrations without using any teleoperation data or on-robot data. The system follows the principle 'One Model, One Data Interface, Any Body. ' The training stack begins with the Omnibody Hand, a 7-DoF wearable that captures human hand movements and tactile cues using high-frequency tactile sensors, proximity sensors, and global-shutter in-house cameras, augmented by electromagnetic sensing. OM-1 processes multimodal streams at their native sampling rates and employs a single-stage training approach. Below the policy, a reinforcement learning control layer runs on its own clock to handle velocity- and acceleration-dependent dynamics and external disturbances. OM-1 is currently an in-house policy with no released weights, code, dataset, or API.

总结：Reward AI发布了通用操作策略OM-1（Omnibody Model 1），该策略完全通过人类示范进行学习，不使用任何遥操作数据或机器人端数据，遵循“一个模型、一个数据接口、任意机体”的原则。 训练技术栈始于Omnibody Hand，这是一款7自由度可穿戴设备，通过高频率触觉传感、接近传感、全局快门机载相机以及电磁传感来捕捉人类手部动作与触觉。 OM-1以传感器的原生采样率处理多模态数据流，并采用单阶段训练方法。 在策略下方，一个通过强化学习训练的控制层以其自身的时钟运行，以处理速度和加速度相关的动力学以及外部干扰。 OM-1目前是Reward AI的内部策略，尚未发布权重、代码、数据集或API。

PR Newswire — ShengShu Technology Unveils Motus2, a Self-Evolving General World Model for Dexterous Manipulation

ShengShu Technology Co-founder and CEO Yihang Luo unveiled Motus2, a self-evolving general world model for robotic dexterous manipulation, at the 2026 Inclusion Conference on the Bund on September 10. Motus2 integrates action generation, consequence prediction, and outcome evaluation within a single video-action model with shared parameters. In tests across five primary real-robot tasks, Motus2 achieved an average success rate of 84%. Combining model-based reinforcement learning with inference-time planning increased the average success rate from 65% to 75%. The model has been evaluated on dual-arm robot platforms equipped with WUJI, WUJI Hand 2, and Sharpa Wave dexterous hands. Motus2 is supported by approximately 130,000 hours of raw egocentric human data and over 100 hours of robot trajectories, with robot-domain mid-training raising performance from 51% to 84%.

总结：生数科技联合创始人兼CEO罗一航于9月10日在2026外滩大会上发布了用于机器人灵巧操作的自进化通用世界模型Motus2。 Motus2在一个具有共享参数的单一视频动作模型中统一了动作生成、结果预测与效果评估。 在五个主要的真实机器人任务测试中，Motus2取得了84%的平均成功率。 将基于模型的强化学习与推理时规划相结合，使平均成功率从65%提升至75%。 该模型已在配备WUJI、WUJI Hand 2和Sharpa Wave灵巧手的双臂机器人平台上进行了评估。 Motus2依托约13万小时的原始第一视角人类数据以及超过100小时的机器人轨迹，其中机器人领域的中期训练将性能从51%提升至84%。

Interesting Engineering — Why robot hands are one of the hardest problems in humanoid robotics

Humanoid robots can easily perform athletic movements like backflips because they rely on predictable, tightly controlled sequences, whereas everyday household tasks such as folding shirts or picking up wet sponge involve high uncertainty and difficult-to-model physical contacts. Building a dexterous robotic hand requires combining mechanics, sensing, and control, as vision alone cannot determine grip force or slippage. Recent research emphasizes tactile sensing, with a 2026 Science Robotics study from Zhejiang University achieving an 85% success rate across five complex tasks using visual and tactile information combined with reinforcement learning and online imitation learning. Chinese robotics companies, including X Square Robot, are actively testing humanoids on household chores, but a major bottleneck remains the lack of internet-scale datasets containing synchronized multi-sensor manipulation data.

总结：人形机器人可以轻松执行后空翻等运动，因为这些动作依赖于可预测且受严格控制的序列，而折叠衬衫或捡起湿海绵等日常家务则涉及高度的不确定性以及难以建模的物理接触。 制造灵巧的机器人手需要结合机械、传感和控制，因为仅靠视觉无法确定抓握力或滑移情况。 近期研究强调了触觉传感的重要性，浙江大学2026年发表在《Science Robotics》上的一项研究结合了视觉与触觉信息以及强化学习与在线模仿学习，在五个复杂任务中取得了85%的成功率。 包括X Square Robot在内的中国机器人公司正在积极测试人形机器人的家务能力，但主要的瓶颈依然是缺乏包含同步多传感器操作数据的互联网规模数据集。
