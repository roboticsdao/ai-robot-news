ARC Advisory Group — China Humanoid Robot Dexterous Hands: Localization, Technology Routes, and Leading Suppliers

Dexterous hands serve as the final-mile execution system and critical bottleneck for humanoid robots, with 2026 marking their transition into scalable commercial markets. Three mature technical routes currently dominate the industry. The coreless motor + micro reducer + tendon-driven structure offers fast dynamic response and ultra-compact size, with Linker Hand as a representative manufacturer, though limited continuous torque and high costs for imported micro coreless motors remain challenges. The micro servo electric cylinder solution uses iron-core micro motors and screw structures inside the palm, delivered by InTimes Robotics, offering higher grasping force and durability for mass production with slight compromises in bionic slimness. The hybrid tendon-driven solution places motors in the palm or forearm with tensioned tendon ropes, represented by DH-Robotics, though tendons require real-time tension compensation. Domestically, Chinese enterprises have achieved independent R&D and large-scale mass production, expanding applications beyond scientific research to industrial teleoperation and batch supporting applications.

总结：灵巧手被视为人形机器人的“最后一公里执行系统”和具身智能的关键瓶颈，2026年标志着其向规模化商业市场过渡。 当前行业主导三种成熟技术路线：无心电机+微型减速器+肌腱驱动结构具备快速动态响应与超紧凑尺寸，代表厂商为Linker Hand，但面临持续转矩有限及进口高档微型无心电机成本高的问题；微型伺服电缸方案采用铁芯微电机与丝杠结构，由InTimes Robotics代表，提供更高抓握力和耐用性以利于量产，但牺牲了部分仿生纤细度；混合肌腱驱动方案将电机置于手掌或前臂并通过张紧的肌腱绳驱动，代表厂商为DH-Robotics，需进行实时张力补偿。 在国内，中国企业已实现自主研发与大规模量产，应用场景从纯科研扩展至工业遥操作和批量配套应用。

Quality Magazine — How Tactile Sensing Fills the Gap Vision Can’t Measure

While modern humanoid robots have achieved remarkable capabilities in walking, balancing, and moving, they still struggle with tasks requiring reliable physical interaction such as grasping, insertion, press-fitting, sanding, and polishing. Machine vision remains the primary perception modality for navigation and obstacle avoidance, but it cannot directly measure physical quantities beneath the surface like grip force, slip, compliance, and subtle shear forces. Artificial tactile sensing addresses this limitation by enabling robots to perceive contact directly. Various sensing mechanisms—including piezoresistive, capacitive, piezoelectric, triboelectric, magnetic, and optical sensors—offer distinct trade-offs in sensitivity, spatial resolution, response speed, durability, and force measurement. Processing these tactile signals through signal processing or machine learning translates them into outputs like grasp stability, slip detection, in-hand manipulation, object and texture recognition, and orientation estimation, which are crucial for successful robotic manipulation.

总结：尽管现代人形机器人在行走、平衡和移动方面取得了显著能力，但在需要可靠物理交互（如抓取、插拔、压合、打磨和抛光）的任务中依然存在困难。 机器视觉仍然是导航和避障的主要感知模态，但它无法直接测量表面之下的物理量，如抓握力、滑移、顺应性和微小剪切力。 人工触觉感知通过使机器人能够直接感知接触来解决这一局限。 压阻式、电容式、压电式、摩擦电式、磁性式和光学式等各种传感机制在灵敏度、空间分辨率、响应速度、耐久性和力测量方面各有不同的权衡。 通过信号处理或机器学习处理这些触觉信号，可将其转化为抓握稳定性、滑移检测、手内操作、物体与纹理识别以及方向估计等输出，这些对于成功的机器人操作至关重要。

Tech Briefs — This $500 Robot Hand Takes a Simpler Approach to Dexterity

The Cartisian Hand is a seven-degree-of-freedom robot hand that performs in-hand manipulation using only linear joints, built from 3D-printed parts at a cost of less than $500. It features two independently actuated parallel grippers, four sliding fingertips, and one adjustable separation between the grippers, requiring about two hours to assemble. The system identifies five reusable motion primitives, including grasping, twisting, and lifting, which compose procedures that allow the hand to manipulate 35 objects across laboratory, manufacturing, and household settings without hardware changes. The hand detects contact from position feedback, enabling tasks such as opening threaded caps of varying diameters by setting cap height, operating pipettes, using scissors, and solving a Rubik's cube while the arm stays stationary.

总结：Cartisian手是一款仅使用线性关节执行手内操作的7自由度机器人手，由3D打印零件制成，成本低于500美元。 它拥有两个独立驱动的平行夹持器、四个滑动指尖以及一个可调节的夹持器间距，组装大约需要两小时。 该系统确定了五个可重复使用的运动基元（包括抓取、扭转和提升），这些基元组成的程序使机械手无需更换硬件即可在实验室、制造和家庭环境中操作35种物体。 该手通过位置反馈检测接触，能够执行诸如通过设定瓶盖高度来打开不同直径的螺纹瓶盖、操作移液器、使用剪刀以及在手臂保持静止的情况下解魔方等任务。
