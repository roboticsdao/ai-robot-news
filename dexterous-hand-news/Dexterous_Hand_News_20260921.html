MarkTechPost — Reward AI Releases OM-1: A Robot Policy Trained on Human Demonstrations Only, With No Teleoperation or On-Robot Data

Reward AI has released OM-1, a general-purpose manipulation policy trained exclusively on human demonstrations without using any teleoperation data or on-robot data. The system uses a 7-DoF wearable called Omnibody Hand, featuring a distal flexion mechanism to bypass per-user adjustments. Its One Data Interface captures wearer motion alongside high-frequency tactile sensing, proximity sensing, and global-shutter in-hand cameras, with electromagnetic sensing reducing visual-inertial overshoot errors by 60% at high speeds. OM-1 processes multimodal streams at native sampling rates within a single-stage training architecture. A reinforcement learning control layer operates below the policy on its own clock to manage dynamics, disturbances, and system delays. Currently, OM-1 is an in-house model with no released weights, code, dataset, or API.

总结：Reward AI已发布OM-1（全称Omnibody Model 1），这是一种通用的操控策略，完全通过人类示范训练，未使用任何遥操作数据或机器人本体数据。 该系统采用名为Omnibody Hand的7自由度可穿戴设备，配有远端屈曲机构以省去用户调节。 其“单一数据接口”将佩戴者动作与高频触觉传感、近距离传感及全局快门随手相机相结合，其中电磁传感在高速下的视觉惯性超调误差降低了60%。 OM-1在单阶段训练架构中按原生采样率处理多模态数据流。 策略下方运行着一个在自身时钟下进行强化学习的控制层，用于处理动力学、干扰和系统延迟。 目前，OM-1为内部模型，未发布权重、代码、数据集或API。

PR Newswire — ShengShu Technology Unveils Motus2, a Self-Evolving General World Model for Dexterous Manipulation

ShengShu Technology Co-founder and CEO Yihang Luo unveiled Motus2, a self-evolving general world model for robotic dexterous manipulation, at the 2026 Inclusion Conference on the Bund on September 10. Motus2 unifies action generation, consequence prediction, and outcome evaluation within a single video-action model with shared parameters, and uses model-based reinforcement learning to improve policies. In evaluations across five primary real-robot tasks, it achieved an average success rate of 84%, while combining reinforcement learning with inference-time planning increased the success rate from 65% to 75%. The model is supported by approximately 130,000 hours of egocentric human video data and over 100 hours of robot-domain trajectories, and has been evaluated on dual-arm platforms equipped with WUJI, WUJI Hand 2, and Sharpa Wave dexterous hands.

总结：盛数科技联合创始人兼CEO罗一航于9月10日在2026年外滩大会上发布了用于机器人灵巧操作的自进化通用世界模型Motus2。 Motus2在一个具有共享参数的单一视频-动作模型中统一了动作生成、结果预测和成果评估，并利用基于模型的强化学习来改进策略。 在五项主要的真实机器人任务评估中，它取得了84%的平均成功率，而将强化学习与推理时规划结合则使成功率从65%提升至75%。 该模型依托约130,000小时的第一人称人类视频数据和100多小时的机器人领域轨迹，并在配备WUJI、WUJI Hand 2和Sharpa Wave灵巧手的双臂平台上进行了评估。

Interesting Engineering — Why robot hands are one of the hardest problems in humanoid robotics

Humanoid robots can easily perform predictable, highly controlled athletic movements like backflips, but struggle with unpredictable household chores such as folding laundry, picking up wet sponges, or turning keys, due to objects bending, slipping, and varying in placement. Building a functional robotic hand requires combining mechanics, sensing, and control to manage multiple motors, degrees of freedom, force, and tactile feedback, as vision alone cannot prevent crushing fragile items. Recent research, including a 2026 Science Robotics study from Zhejiang University, combines visual and tactile information with reinforcement learning and online imitation learning to achieve an 85% success rate across five complex tasks. Additionally, Chinese robotics firms like X Square Robot are testing humanoids on household tasks, while researchers emphasize that data collection combining joint positions, cameras, force, torque, and tactile sensors remains a major challenge.

总结：人形机器人可以轻易完成诸如后空翻这样可预测且高度受控的运动，但由于物体弯曲、打滑以及位置多变，在折衣物、捡起湿海绵或转动钥匙等不可预测的家务上却面临困难。 制造一个功能性的机器人手需要结合机械、传感和控制来管理多个电机、自由度、力和触觉反馈，因为仅靠视觉无法防止压碎易碎物品。 浙江大学2026年的一项《科学机器人》研究等近期成果将视觉与触觉信息同强化学习和在线模仿学习相结合，在五项复杂任务中取得了85%的成功率。 此外，像X Square Robot这样的中国机器人公司正在测试人形机器人的家务能力，而研究人员强调，结合关节位置、相机、力、扭矩和触觉传感器的的数据收集仍然是一项重大挑战。
