- **[2026.09.17] vietnam.vn — Why can't robotic hands be as dexterous as human hands?**
  English: Humanoid robots face significant challenges in tasks like folding clothes or picking up a wet sponge due to uncertainty, object deformation, and the complexity of the human hand, which features 27 joints and over 30 muscles. While factory robots operate in controlled environments with simple grippers, household robots require bimanual coordination and tactile sensing. A 2026 Zhejiang University study published in Science Robotics achieved an 85% success rate in 5 complex tasks by combining visual and tactile information with learning methods.
  中文：总结：人形机器人在折衣服或捡起湿海绵等任务中面临巨大挑战，原因在于环境的不确定性、物体变形以及人手结构的复杂性（人手拥有27个关节和30多块肌肉）。 尽管工厂机器人可在受控环境中使用简单夹爪，但家庭机器人需要双臂协调和触觉感知。 浙江大学2026年发表在Science Robotics上的一项研究通过将视觉和触觉信息与学习方法相结合，在5个复杂任务中取得了85%的成功率。
