- **[2026.10.23] خبرگزاری آنا — Chinese Scientists Develop a Robotic Arm with Human-Like Tactile Sensing**
  English: A research team at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) led by Shen Yajing has developed an integrated sensorimotor robotic arm named EmArm, featuring full-arm tactile sensing and adaptive manipulation capabilities. The system embeds more than 71,000 square millimeters of soft optical tactile skin onto the upper arm and forearm of a rigid mechanical skeleton, using internal submillimeter-scale cameras to analyze applied force distributions. EmArm achieves a spatial resolution of 0.085 millimeters and a two-point discrimination threshold of 18.2 millimeters. The optical sensing approach resists electromagnetic, optical, thermal, humidity-related, and acoustic interference, maintaining a sensing accuracy of up to 97.9 percent when exposed to five types of environmental interference. The research team proposed a perception-action loop that integrates joint-angle data and tactile information in real time to enable autonomous decision-making by extracting features such as contact position, contact area, force, and the number of contact points.
  中文：总结：香港科技大学由沈亚京领导的研究团队开发了一种集成式感觉运动机械臂EmArm，具备全臂触觉感知和自适应操作能力。 该系统将超过71000平方毫米的柔性光学触觉皮肤嵌入刚性机械骨架的上臂和前臂，并利用内部亚毫米级相机分析施加在机械臂上的力分布。 EmArm的空间分辨率达到0.085毫米，两点辨别阈值可达18.2毫米。 光学传感方法可抵御电磁、光学、热、湿度和声学干扰，在暴露于五种环境干扰时仍能保持高达97.9%的传感精度。 研究团队提出了一种感知-动作循环，将关节角度数据与触觉信息实时整合，通过提取接触位置、接触面积、力和接触点数量等特征来实现自主决策。
