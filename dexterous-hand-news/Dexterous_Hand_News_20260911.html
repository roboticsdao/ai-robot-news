AI Insider — Japan's Algomatic Dynamics Laucnhes with $32.5M in Funding to Develop Physical AI, Robotic Hand

Algomatic Dynamics has raised $32.5 million from DMM. com in its first funding round and officially began operations on Sept. 9. Led by CEO Yuki Nanri, the Tokyo-based robotics startup prepares to launch an AI-powered multi-fingered robotic hand platform in Japan by the end of 2026. The company focuses on three main business areas: motion data and robot learning by analyzing video of skilled human movements, AI robotic hands sold as integrated hardware and software systems with task-specific tuning, and AI entertainment robots using animatronics technology. Its technical projects include multi-fingered robotic hands combining hardware, tactile sensing, and an AI learning platform; bipedal stability control systems combining model-based control and reinforcement learning; and video-based tacit knowledge capture. Algomatic Dynamics plans to use the funding for hardware and computing resources, research expansion, and collaborative demonstrations in industrial and everyday settings to address labor shortages in Japan.

finance.biggo.com — Eric Li: The Robot Tactile Sensing Breakthrough Arrives by End of This Year — Optical Sensor Resolution Already 200x Human Fingertips

Embodied intelligence researcher Chen Tianyi and Yimu Technology founder Eric Li discussed the critical role and current breakthroughs of tactile sensing in robotics. The robotics industry faces a core dilemma: robots can see well, but lack tactile understanding to regulate force, determine stopping points, or distinguish materials, often resulting in stripped screws when relying solely on vision. As of September 2026, the robot tactile sensing industry is approaching a breakout, with Eric Li predicting a milestone by the end of this year or early next year based on humanoid robot mass-production schedules. Tactile information encompasses "force" (vertical pressure, lateral friction, rotational torque) and "morphology" (surface texture, shape, hardness, elasticity). Current sensors use five technology routes, but a hybrid deployment is necessary since whole-body coverage with high-end sensors is cost-prohibitive. Following human anatomy, robots should use high-precision optical sensors on fingertips and lower-cost piezoresistive or capacitive sensors on palms and arms. Yimu Technology's optical sensors achieve a resolution of 240,000 sensing points (about 20 times human fingertips), with procurement costs around 1,000 yuan (approximately $149) that are expected to drop to a few hundred yuan as volume scales. Meanwhile, capacitive solutions cost one-quarter to one-half of optical options and have seen batch deployment in factory sorting and inspection. Additionally, the industry currently suffers from a severe scarcity of tactile data.

总结：具身智能研究员陈毅顿时天一与一目科技创始人Eric Li探讨了触觉传感在机器人中的关键作用与当前突破。 机器人行业面临核心困境：机器人视觉良好，但缺乏触觉理解来调节力、确定停止点或区分材料，仅依视觉常导致螺丝滑丝。 截至2026年9月，机器人触觉传感行业正接近爆发，Eric Li根据人形机器人量产时间表预测，下一个里程碑将在今年底或明年初出现。 触觉信息包含“力”（垂直压力、侧向摩擦、旋转扭矩）和“形态”（表面纹理、形状、硬度、弹性）。 当前传感器采用五种技术路线，但由于全身上最高端传感器成本难以控制，必须采用混合部署。 遵循人体解剖学，机器人应在指尖使用高精度光学传感器，并在手掌和手臂等较大面积上部署成本较低的压阻或电容传感器。 一目科技的光学传感器分辨率达到了240,000个感应点（约为人类指尖神经元数量的20倍），单个指尖传感器采购价约为1000元（约合149美元），随着规模扩大有望降至几百元。 同时，电容式解决方案的采购价仅为光学选项的四分之一到二分之一，已在工厂分拣和检测中实现批量部署。 此外，该行业目前严重缺乏触觉数据。
