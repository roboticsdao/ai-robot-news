- **[2026.10.08] alphaXiv — Dex-One2Many: Learning Dexterous Manipulation from a Single Human Demonstration**
  English: Dex-One2Many is a real-to-sim-to-real framework designed to learn generalizable dexterous manipulation policies from a single human video without costly robot demonstrations or strict motion matching. Developed by researchers, the system abstracts videos into sequential scene graphs where nodes represent hands, objects, and parts, and edges encode relations such as grasp and contact. A vision-language model extracts entities and orders relations into stages. These graphs generate constrained simulation resets and provide dense rewards for reinforcement learning across tasks like sweeping, hammer use, doll manipulation, can handling, and stamping. Tested in simulation and zero-shot on a UR3 arm with a 20-DoF Wuji 1 hand, the method achieved 75% full-task success on unseen initial and goal poses, outperforming baseline approaches significantly.
  中文：总结：Dex-One2Many是一个由研究人员开发的仿真到现实框架，旨在通过单个人类视频学习具备通用性的灵巧操作策略。 该系统将视频抽象为顺序场景图，利用节点表示手、物体和功能部件，边编码抓取和接触等关系。 视觉语言模型提取实体并将关系排序为不同阶段。 这些场景图为强化学习生成受约束的重置状态并提供密集奖励，应用于扫地、用锤子、操作玩偶、罐头和盖章等任务。 在仿真训练并零样本部署到配备20自由度Wuji 1手的UR3机械臂上后，该方法在未见过的初始与目标位姿测试中取得了75%的全任务成功率，显著优于基线方法。
