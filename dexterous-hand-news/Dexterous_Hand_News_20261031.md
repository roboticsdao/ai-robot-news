- **[2026.10.28] Tech Times — Xiaomi Engineers Robotic Hand From 1,827 Factory SOPs: China Spy Law Travels With It**
  English: Xiaomi has developed a new robotic hand for automotive manufacturing, engineered directly from 1,827 standard operating procedures used in its automotive test center. The device features 26 electric motors, 21 degrees of freedom, and approximately 1,500 tactile sensing points within a 324-gram palm assembly.
