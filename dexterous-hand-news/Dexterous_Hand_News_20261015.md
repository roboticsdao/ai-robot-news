  English: On October 8 at the Wave by Vento technology conference in Turin, Italy, Agile Robots CEO Zhaopeng Chen announced that the company's Munich Robot Academy is generating robot training data directly from worker videos using hand-tracking software, simulation, and physical transfer without specialized teleoperation rigs.
