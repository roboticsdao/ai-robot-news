- **[2026.09.28] ARC Advisory Group — China Humanoid Robot Dexterous Hands: Localization, Technology Routes, and Leading Suppliers**
  English: Dexterous hands serve as the final-mile execution system and critical bottleneck for humanoid robots, with 2026 marking their transition into scalable commercial markets. Three mature technical routes currently dominate the industry. The coreless motor + micro reducer + tendon-driven structure offers fast dynamic response and ultra-compact size, with Linker Hand as a representative manufacturer, though limited continuous torque and high costs for imported micro coreless motors remain challenges. The micro servo electric cylinder solution uses iron-core micro motors and screw structures inside the palm, delivered by InTimes Robotics, offering higher grasping force and durability for mass production with slight compromises in bionic slimness. The hybrid tendon-driven solution places motors in the palm or forearm with tensioned tendon ropes, represented by DH-Robotics, though tendons require real-time tension compensation. - **[2026.09.26] Quality Magazine — How Tactile Sensing Fills the Gap Vision Can’t Measure**
  English: While modern humanoid robots have achieved remarkable capabilities in walking, balancing, and moving, they still struggle with tasks requiring reliable physical interaction such as grasping, insertion, press-fitting, sanding, and polishing. Machine vision remains the primary perception modality for navigation and obstacle avoidance, but it cannot directly measure physical quantities beneath the surface like grip force, slip, compliance, and subtle shear forces. Artificial tactile sensing addresses this limitation by enabling robots to perceive contact directly. Various sensing mechanisms—including piezoresistive, capacitive, piezoelectric, triboelectric, magnetic, and optical sensors—offer distinct trade-offs in sensitivity, spatial resolution, response speed, durability, and force measurement. Processing these tactile signals through signal processing or machine learning translates them into outputs like grasp stability, slip detection, in-hand manipulation, object and texture recognition, and orientation estimation, which are crucial for successful robotic manipulation.
  中文：总结：尽管现代人形机器人在行走、平衡和移动方面取得了显著能力，但在需要可靠物理交互（如抓取、插拔、压合、打磨和抛光）的任务中依然存在困难。 机器视觉仍然是导航和避障的主要感知模态，但它无法直接测量表面之下的物理量，如抓握力、滑移、顺应性和微小剪切力。 人工触觉感知通过使机器人能够直接感知接触来解决这一局限。 压阻式、电容式、压电式、摩擦电式、磁性式和光学式等各种传感机制在灵敏度、空间分辨率、响应速度、耐久性和力测量方面各有不同的权衡。 通过信号处理或机器学习处理这些触觉信号，可将其转化为抓握稳定性、滑移检测、手内操作、物体与纹理识别以及方向估计等输出，这些对于成功的机器人操作至关重要。
