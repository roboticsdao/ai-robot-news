  English: Boston Dynamics unveiled a dexterous hand purpose-built for its humanoid robot Atlas with 4 fingers and 13 degrees of freedom (DoF), nearly doubling its 7-DoF predecessor. Unitree Robotics' Dex5-S starts from 39,900 yuan, whereas higher-DoF hands cost over 100,000 yuan or millions due to more motors and sensors. Zhongke Siliconji CEO Zhang Tianyi stated that three-finger dexterous hands balance opposition and general gripping capabilities at a lower cost, earning customer recognition. High-DoF hands rely on full motor drive, with Unitree equipping the Dex5-S with 22 motors, creating challenges in heat dissipation, energy consumption, and system integration. Crucially, existing VLA models can only output about 10 motion control vectors at the same timestamp, meaning software capability restricts hardware and lacks a brain to drive 20 to 30 DoF hands.

- **[2026.10.13] Tech Times — Agile Robots Unveils Robot Academy: Worker Video Generates Training Data Without Teleoperation**
  English: On October 8, at the Wave by Vento technology conference in Turin, Italy, Agile Robots CEO Zhaopeng Chen announced that his company's Munich-based Robot Academy is generating robot training data directly from video footage of human workers. The facility uses hand-tracking software to extract motion trajectories and moves those trajectories through simulation before physical transfer, bypassing the specialized teleoperation rigs that have defined the field. Agile Robots has more than 20,000 robotic systems already deployed across customer sites worldwide.
  中文：总结：在意大利都灵举行的Wave by Vento技术会议上，Agile Robots首席执行官Zhaopeng Chen宣布，该公司位于慕尼黑的Robot Academy正直接从人类工人的视频画面中生成机器人训练数据。 该设施使用手部追踪软件提取运动轨迹，并在物理传输前通过模拟运行这些轨迹，从而绕过了该领域过去一直依赖的专用遥操作装置。 Agile Robots已在全球客户现场部署了20,000多个机器人系统。
