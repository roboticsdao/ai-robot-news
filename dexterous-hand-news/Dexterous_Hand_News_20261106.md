- **[2026.10.28] Tech Times — Xiaomi Engineers Robotic Hand From 1,827 Factory SOPs: China Spy Law Travels With It**
  English: Xiaomi engineered a new bionic robotic hand for automotive manufacturing, designed directly from 1,827 standard operating procedures used in its automotive test center rather than abstract academic benchmarks. The 324-gram palm assembly packs 26 electric motors, 21 degrees of freedom, and approximately 1,500 tactile sensing points. By analyzing its SOP corpus, Xiaomi found that moving from simpler manipulators to a 21-degrees-of-freedom design raises stable grasp success rates by roughly 15 to 38 percent. To accommodate touch sensing and keep fingers light, 16 of the 21 degrees of freedom use cable-driven tendons routed from motors housed in the forearm, while tendon tension sensors track fingertip pressure in real time to modulate grip. A single hand is rated at a maximum power draw of 100W.
  中文：总结：小米为汽车制造工程设计了一种全新的仿生机器人手，该手直接根据其汽车测试中心的1,827项标准作业程序进行设计，而不是依据抽象的学术基准。 这个重324克的掌部组件包含26个电动机、21个自由度以及大约1,500个触觉传感点。 通过分析标准作业程序语料库，小米发现从更简单的操作器转向21自由度设计，可将稳定抓握成功率提高约15%到38%。 为了适应触觉传感并保持手指轻便，21个自由度中有16个采用电缆驱动的肌腱，由收纳在前臂中的电动机引导，同时肌腱张力传感器实时追踪指尖压力以调节握力。 单手最大功耗额定为100W。
