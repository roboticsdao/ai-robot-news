- **[2026.10.26] 36Kr — Tsinghua Research Team Builds HandsOn Dexterous Manipulation Benchmark: Comprehensive 66-Task Evaluation of Robot Bimanual Capabilities**
  English: A research team led by Ning Ding from Tsinghua University, in collaboration with researchers from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications, Peking University, and Nature Will, has built the composable simulation platform and evaluation benchmark HandsOn for cross-embodiment dexterous manipulation. HandsOn contains 66 human-validated tasks and 18,000 human teleoperation demonstration records in simulation, focusing evaluations on tool use, fine contact, bimanual collaboration, and task transfer across 5 types of robotic arms, 6 types of dexterous hands, and about 200 assets. Evaluations of different technical routes showed that the best-performing trained multi-task policy achieved a success rate of 29.1% on Core-50 tasks with provided training demonstrations, while the average success rate on Transfer-16 tasks without target task training demonstrations was lower than 1%. Furthermore, GPT-6 achieved success rates of 5.2% and 1.9% on Core-50 and Transfer-16 respectively across 660 tests without fine-tuning.
  中文：总结：清华大学丁宁领导的团队联合北京邮电大学、北京大学和Nature Will的研究人员，共同构建了用于跨具身灵巧操作的可组合仿真平台与评估基准HandsOn。 HandsOn包含66个经人类验证的任务以及18000条仿真中的人类遥操作示范记录，评估聚焦于工具使用、精细接触、双臂协作和任务迁移，支持5种机械臂、6种灵巧手以及约200种资产。 不同技术路线的评估结果显示，在提供训练示范的Core-50任务中，表现最好的多任务策略成功率为29.1%；而在无目标任务训练示范的Transfer-16任务中，所有8个多任务策略的平均成功率低于1%。 此外，GPT-6在未经基准数据微调的情况下，在660次测试中对Core-50和Transfer-16的成功率分别为5.2%和1.9%。

- **[2026.10.23] Vietnam.vn — Why don't robotic hands look like human hands?**
  English: Robotic hands remain one of the most difficult parts to develop for humanoid robots due to the complex interplay of mechanics, tactile sensation, and control required to handle unpredictable real-world tasks like folding shirts. Researchers must simultaneously address three major challenges: mechanical design (fitting actuators and sensors into limited spaces while balancing power, precision, and friction), tactile sensing (real-time slip and pressure detection to adjust grip instantly), and data/learning (collecting complex manipulation data rather than simple images). Various robotics companies are tackling these challenges differently: Unittree developed the G1 with force-controlled hands and tactile sensors, Apptronik created Apollo combining mobility and manipulation, Tesla developed Optimus as a versatile humanoid, and 1X focuses on household chore robots. Despite advanced mobility capabilities, versatile humanoid robots still require significant time to master basic tasks like picking up a towel.
  中文：总结：机器人手部由于需要复杂的机械结构、触感和现实世界适应能力，成为人形机器人开发中最困难的部分之一。 研究人员必须同时应对三大挑战：机械问题（在狭小空间内平衡致动器与传感器的功率、精度和摩擦力）、触觉问题（实现实时滑移和压力检测以动态调整抓握力）以及数据和学习挑战（收集复杂的操控数据）。 多家机器人公司正从多角度切入：宇树科技开发了带有力控手和触觉传感器的G1机器人，Apptronik开发了Apollo，特斯拉开发了Optimus，1X专注于家务机器人。 尽管机器人在跳跃和奔跑方面表现出色，但要学会拿起一条毛巾仍需很长时间。
