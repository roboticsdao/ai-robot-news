- **[2026.11.10] Robotics & Automation News — Nature Will launches 50-billion-parameter AI model for dexterous robot hands**
  English: Nature Will, a physical AI lab, has announced the launch of Natura-1, a 50-billion-parameter physical general foundation model designed for dexterous hand control and zero-shot instruction following in advanced robotics applications.
