Interesting Engineering — Watch: Chinese robot hand takes on delicate Suzhou embroidery with human-like dexterity

Chinese robotics startup AGILINK, a spin-off of AgiBot, demonstrated its OmniHand 3 Ultra robot hand performing traditional Suzhou embroidery techniques. In the demonstration with master Fu Xianghong, the robot separated a main silk thread into 16 finer strands, threaded a needle, stretched fabric across a hoop, and made initial stitches. The OmniHand 3 Ultra is about the size of a human hand, weighs 1.9 pounds (630 grams), uses a direct-drive architecture, and can hold 3 kilograms while lifting up to 8 kilograms. It features a vision-based sensor in each fingertip measuring three-dimensional force and deformations down to 0.08 millimeters, alongside a 300-point contact sensor in the palm. AGILINK combined teleoperation data with reinforcement learning to train the hand. The article also mentions alternative designs from LinkerBot and Boston Dynamics.

总结：中国机器人初创公司AGILINK是AgiBot的衍生企业，该公司展示了其OmniHand 3 Ultra机器人手执行传统苏州刺绣技术的全过程。 在刺绣大师傅湘红的指导下，机器人完成了将一根主丝线拆分为16根更细的丝线、穿针、在绣绷上拉紧织物以及进行基础针法缝制的操作。 OmniHand 3 Ultra大小与人手相近，重1.9 pounds (630 grams)，采用直驱架构，可稳定握持3 kilograms并提起最多8 kilograms。 其每个指尖均配有基于视觉的传感器，可测量三维力和0.08 millimeters的微小形变，手掌则集成了一个300点接触传感器。 AGILINK将遥操作数据与强化学习相结合来训练该手部。 文章还提到了LinkerBot和Boston Dynamics的不同设计方案。

Hyundai Motor Group — Boston Dynamics Atlas Next-Generation Four-Finger Robot Hand

Hyundai Motor Group detailed the features of the next-generation four-finger robot hand for the Atlas robot. The hand incorporates tactile sensing to detect subtle contact and uses sim-to-real reinforcement learning to support precision manipulation for industrial tasks. Its capabilities include tool use, precision assembly, and in-hand reorientation such as manipulating two golf balls simultaneously. It features 13 degrees of freedom with 4 DOFs in the opposable thumb and 3 DOFs in each of the other fingers, enabled by dense pressure tactile sensors across the fingertips and palm. The hand is designed with human-hand size for industrial work environments, focusing on simplicity, ruggedness, and repairability.

总结：现代汽车集团详细介绍了Atlas机器人下一代四指机器人手的功能特性。 该手部结合了触控感知以检测细微接触，并利用仿真到现实的强化学习来支持现实世界工业任务的精确操作。 其功能涵盖工具使用、精密装配以及手内重新定向（例如同时操作两个高尔夫球）。 该手部拥有13个自由度，包括对立拇指上的4个DOF和其他各手指上的3个DOF，指尖和手掌各处均布有密集压力触控传感器。 其尺寸按人手设计以适应工业工作空间，并秉持简单、坚固和易修的设计理念。
