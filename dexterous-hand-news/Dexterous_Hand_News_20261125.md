- **[2026.11.23] Superpower Daily — Sundae Robotics 11: Beyond the Gripper — Teaching Robots Dexterity Through Touch, Vision, and Play**
  English: At Sundae Robotics 11, Akash Sharma, an Amazon Frontier AI & Robotics Research Scientist and CMU Robotics Institute PhD, presented four approaches to advance robot dexterity through touch, vision, human demonstrations, and autonomous play. The presentation covered PTLD for learning tactile policies without tactile simulation, INVAR for visual invariance transfer from simulation, CARD for touch-based demonstration retargeting, and DSP for turning human play into goal-conditioned dexterity using world models. Akash works at Amazon Frontier AI & Robotics with Jitendra Malik, received his PhD from Carnegie Mellon University advised by Prof. Michael Kaess, and previously was a visiting researcher with Meta FAIR Robotics.
  中文：总结：在 Sundae Robotics 11 活动中，Amazon Frontier AI & Robotics 研究科学家兼卡耐基梅隆大学机器人研究所博士 Akash Sharma 介绍了通过触觉、视觉、人类示范和自主玩耍推进机器人灵巧操作的四种方法。 演讲涵盖了无需触觉模拟即可学习触觉策略的 PTLD、从模拟中进行视觉不变性迁移的 INVAR、基于接触部位而非纯粹运动轨迹重定向人类示范的 CARD，以及利用世界模型将人类玩耍转化为目标条件化灵巧性的 DSP。 Akash 目前在 Amazon Frontier AI & Robotics 与 Jitendra Malik 共事，其博士学位由卡耐基梅隆大学授予，导师为 Michael Kaess 教授，此前他曾担任 Meta FAIR Robotics 的访问研究员。
