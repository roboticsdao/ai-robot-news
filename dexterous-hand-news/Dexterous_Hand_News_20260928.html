Tech Briefs — This Robot Hand Doesn’t Just Grab—It Walks

Researchers at ETH Zurich have developed an untethered, 818-gram robotic hand that can walk on its five fingers like legs. The device can crawl across various surfaces, recover from falls, and interact with objects and a keyboard. By combining an anthropomorphic hand design with reinforcement learning, the project turns a standard robotic component into both a mobile platform and a manipulator, demonstrating an approach that makes existing hardware perform multiple functions.

总结：苏黎世联邦理工学院的研究人员开发了一种重818克的独立式机器人手，能够像腿一样用五根手指行走。 该设备可以在不同表面上爬行、从跌倒中恢复，并与物体和键盘进行交互。 通过将拟人手部设计与强化学习相结合，该项目将标准的机器人组件转变为移动平台和操作器，展示了一种使现有硬件执行多种功能的方法。

Tech Briefs — This $500 Robot Hand Takes a Simpler Approach to Dexterity

The Cartisian Hand is a 3D-printed, $500 robot hand featuring seven degrees of freedom using only linear joints and two simple grippers. Developed to avoid the complexity of multi-fingered hands, it uses seven servos and can perform 35 tasks across laboratory, manufacturing, and household settings without hardware changes. The hand relies on five reusable motion primitives, such as grasping, twisting, and lifting, to operate tools like pipettes, scissors, and threaded caps while keeping its arm stationary.

总结：Cartisian手是一款采用3D打印、售价低于500美元的机器人手，仅使用线性关节和两个简单夹爪便具备七个自由度。 为避免多指手的复杂性，它使用七个伺服电机，无需更换硬件即可在实验室、制造和家庭环境中执行35项任务。 该手依靠抓取、扭转和提升等五个可重复使用的运动基元来操作移液器、剪刀和螺纹瓶盖等工具，同时保持机械臂静止。

TechTarget — China leads dexterous hand supply for humanoids

A report by Smart Analytics Global (SAG) found that Chinese companies accounted for 96% of shipments of dexterous hands with six or more active degrees of freedom (DOF) in the first half of 2026, while the Western share stood at 4%. Chinese firms prioritize selling hands as standalone components at high volume and low cost, backed by a localized manufacturing supply chain including motors, reducers, sensors, and actuators. Western producers, led by companies like Figure AI, tend to focus on customized or in-house designs to protect intellectual property for commercial solutions. Meanwhile, the adoption rate of hands with six or more active DOF declined in early 2026 as initial industrial tasks required less dexterity, with component development shifting toward application-driven reliability.

总结：Smart Analytics Global（SAG）的一项报告显示，2026年上半年，中国公司占有六个或更多活动自由度（DOF）灵巧手出货量的96%，而西方份额为4%。 在中国本土电机、减速器、传感器和执行器等制造供应链的支持下，中国企业优先以高产量和低成本将灵巧手作为独立组件销售。 以Figure AI等公司为代表的西方生产商则倾向于专注于定制或内部设计，以保护商业解决方案的知识产权。 同时，由于初期工业任务所需的灵巧度较低，2026年上半年六个或更多活动自由度手部的采用率有所下降，组件开发正转向以应用为导向的可靠性。

AZoSensors — Giving Robots a Sense of Touch: The Next Generation of Tactile Sensors

Researchers are developing advanced tactile sensors that combine pressure detection with vibration, proximity, temperature, and texture cues into a flexible layer. Single-signal sensors often mislead robots when properties overlap, but multimodal systems show significantly higher recognition accuracy, such as identifying 26 textiles with 95.49% accuracy by combining pressure and triboelectric data. Proximity sensors using capacitive twin helical electrodes detect objects over 5 cm away, aiding in transparent item handling. Microstructures in pressure sensors distinguish normal force from shear force to detect slips, while piezoelectric films like a 28 μm PVDF film on silicone layers convert tiny mechanical oscillations into voltage to catch slips early.

总结：研究人员正在开发先进的触觉传感器，将压力检测与振动、接近度、温度和纹理线索组合到一个柔层中。 当属性重叠时，单一信号传感器经常误导机器人，但多模态系统显示出更高的识别精度，例如通过组合压力和摩擦电数据以95.49%的准确率识别26种纺织品。 使用电容式双螺旋电极的接近传感器可检测5厘米以外的物体，有助于处理透明物品。 压力传感器中的微结构可区分法向力和剪切力以检测滑移，而硅胶层上厚度为28微米的PVDF薄膜等压电薄膜则将微小的机械振动转化为电压，以便及早发现滑移。
