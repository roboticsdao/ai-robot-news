  English: Xiaomi has developed a new robotic hand for automotive manufacturing, engineered from 1,827 standard operating procedures derived from Xiaomi's automotive test center.   English: A research team led by Ning Ding from Tsinghua University, in collaboration with researchers from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications, Peking University, and Nature Will, developed a composable simulation platform and evaluation benchmark called HandsOn for cross-embodiment dexterous manipulation. - **[2026.10.26] A3 Association for Advancing Automation — Industry Insights: This Disembodied Robot Hand Moves on its Own**
  English: Robot engineers from ETH Zurich developed a robotic hand that moves independently by crawling across 14 different surfaces while balancing and interacting with its environment. Led by Amirhossein Kazemipour, the researchers mounted motion sensors, a battery, and a miniaturized computer on the back of a commercial anthropomorphic hand, retaining its finger kinematics and position controller. The untethered setup weighs 818g (1.8 lbs) or around 800 grams, features 20 powered joints, and uses reinforcement learning inside computer simulations to train locomotion and balance. The approach accounted for the hand's unequal fingers and offset thumb using a reward formulation and a penalty pulling each fingertip toward its nominal stance position. The robot was tested inside and outside on surfaces including carpet, gravel, grass, and metal grating, demonstrating capabilities to crawl, steer, recover from falls, press operator-requested keyboard keys, and push objects using an overhead camera.
  中文：总结：苏黎世联邦理工学院的机器人工程师开发了一款能够独立移动的机器人手，它通过在14种不同的表面上爬行来保持平衡并与环境交互。 在首席作者Amirhossein Kazemipour的带领下，研究人员在一个商用仿生手的背面安装了运动传感器、电池和微型计算机，保留了其手指运动学和位置控制器。 这个无线缆的装置重818g（1.8磅）或约800克，拥有20个动力关节，并利用计算机仿真内部的强化学习来训练运动和平衡。 该方法通过奖励公式以及将每个指尖拉向其名义站姿位置的惩罚项，解决了该手不均等手指和偏置拇指的问题。 该机器人在室内和室外的地毯、碎石、草地以及金属格栅等14种表面上进行了测试，展示了爬行、转向、从跌落中恢复、按下操作员要求的键盘按键以及使用头顶摄像机推动物体的能力。
