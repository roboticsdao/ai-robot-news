Tech Briefs — This Robot Hand Doesn’t Just Grab—It Walks

Researchers at ETH Zurich have built an untethered, 818-gram robotic hand that can walk on its five fingers across different surfaces, recover from falls, and interact with objects and a keyboard. The project demonstrates an alternative approach to robot design by making existing hardware do more rather than adding more components. By combining an anthropomorphic hand with reinforcement learning, the team transforms a standard robotic part into both a mobile platform and a manipulator.

总结：苏黎世联邦理工学院的研究人员制造了一款重818克的无线缆机器人手，能够用五根手指在不同表面上爬行、从跌倒中恢复，并与物体和键盘进行交互。 该项目展示了一种不同的机器人设计方法，即通过让现有硬件发挥更多作用，而不是增加更多硬件。 通过将拟人手与强化学习相结合，研究人员将一个熟悉的机器人部件同时转变为移动平台和操作器。

Tech Briefs — This $500 Robot Hand Takes a Simpler Approach to Dexterity

The Cartisian Hand is a 7-degree-of-freedom robot hand built from 3D-printed parts and seven linear actuators, costing less than $500 and taking about two hours to assemble. It uses two independently actuated parallel grippers, four sliding fingertips, and one adjustable separation between the grippers to perform inhand manipulation. The system relies on five reusable motion primitives including grasping, twisting, and lifting, detected through position feedback. Without changing hardware or using visual feedback, the hand can manipulate 35 objects across laboratory, manufacturing, and household settings, performing tasks such as opening threaded caps, operating pipettes, using scissors, and solving a Rubik's cube.

总结：Cartisian手是一款由3D打印部件和七个线性执行器构成的7自由度机器人手，造价低于500美元，组装耗时约两小时。 它使用两个独立驱动的平行夹爪、四个滑动指尖以及一个可调节的夹爪间距来执行手内操作。 该系统依赖抓取、扭转和提升等五个可重用的运动基元，并通过位置反馈检测接触。 在无需更换硬件或使用视觉反馈的情况下，该手能够跨实验室、制造和家庭环境操作35种物件，执行打开螺纹盖、操作移液器、使用剪刀以及解魔方等任务。

TechTarget — China leads dexterous hand supply for humanoids

A new report from Smart Analytics Global found that Chinese companies accounted for 96% of shipments of dexterous hands with six or more active degrees of freedom in the first hlaf of 2026, while the Western share stood at 4%. Chinese firms prioritize selling hands as standalone components at high volumes and low costs, backed by a mature domestic supply chain of motors, reducers, sensors, and actuators. Western producers tend to favor customized or in-house designs to protect their competitive advantage. Meanwhile, adoption rates of hands with six or more active degrees of freedom fell as industrial tasks often do not require that level of complexity, and analysts note that robot brains and foundation models will ultimately determine commercial viability.

总结：Smart Analytics Global的一项新报告发现，中国公司在2026年上半年占具有6个或以上主动自由度的灵巧手出货量的96%，而西方份额为4%。 在电机、减速器、传感器和执行器等成熟国内供应链的支持下，中国企业优先将灵巧手作为独立组件以大批量和低成本进行销售。 西方生产商则倾向于采用定制或内部设计以维护其竞争优势。 同时，由于许多工业任务并不需要那样级别的复杂性，6个或以上主动自由度手部的采用率有所下降，分析师指出机器人的大脑和基础模型将最终决定商业可行性。

AZoSensors — Giving Robots a Sense of Touch: The Next Generation of Tactile Sensors

Researchers are developing next-generation tactile sensors that combine multiple signals into a single flexible layer to give robots a working sense of touch. Traditional sensors measuring a single quantity often reduce recognition accuracy because softness, texture, and temperature can produce similar pressure changes. A recent Advanced Science study showed that combining pressure and triboelectric data increased textile identification accuracy to 95.49%. Proximity sensing using capacitive twin helical electrodes detects objects from over 5 cm away, providing early warning for transparent items. Microstructures help sensors distinguish normal force from shear force to detect slips, while piezoelectric PVDF films convert tiny mechanical oscillations into voltage to catch slips early, though the thickness of the soft layer affects signal strength.

总结：研究人员正在开发下一代触觉传感器，将多种信号组合到一个柔性层中，赋予机器人工作触感。 传统传感器仅测量单一物理量，由于柔软度、质地和温度可能产生相似的压力变化，往往会降低识别准确率。 Advanced Science最近的一项研究表明，将压力和摩擦电数据结合，使纺织品识别准确率提升至95.49%。 使用电容式双螺旋电极的接近传感可在5厘米以外检测物体，为透明物品提供早期预警。 微结构帮助传感器区分正向力和剪切力以检测滑移，而压电PVDF薄膜则将微小机械振荡转换为电压以便尽早捕获滑移，尽管软层的厚度会影响信号强度。
