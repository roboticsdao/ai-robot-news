虎嗅网 — 机器人触觉传感器商业化路径分叉：硬件先行，数据与模型商业化尚需验证

总结：2026年，机器人触觉传感器与具身数据商业化路径出现分化。 Sharpa、戴盟、帕西尼等厂商在IROS 2026上展示了触觉传感器、数采工具、全模态数据集及世界模型。 在硬件方面，福莱新材与灵心巧手在今年2月签下10万套触觉传感器订单。 在数采工具方面，上海大学拟以22万元采购相关系统，吉林大学则以20万元购入采集平台。 在数据方面，戴盟上线Daimon-Infinity公开1000小时数据，卓世科技与戴盟签署协议获取1亿条模型训练数据，帕西尼将全模态具身数据按任务标价5元、7元和9元。 然而，触觉数据面临跨传感器复用的“换肤成本”难题，不同硬件采集的信号难以直接通用。 在模型方面，戴盟发布了触觉锚定世界模型Daimon-TWM，在四类测试任务中的平均成功率为64.0%，抗干扰下为53.5%。 整体而言，机器人“皮肤”从技术样品走向产品化，但数据和模型仍缺乏可独立验证的稳定收入模式。

Interesting Engineering — Watch: Chinese robot hand takes on delicate Suzhou embroidery with human-like dexterity

Chinese robotics startup AGILINK, a spin-off of AgiBot, demonstrated its OmniHand 3 Ultra dexterous robot hand performing traditional Suzhou embroidery techniques. In the demonstration, guided by embroidery master Fu Xianghong, the robot split a silk thread into 16 finer strands, threaded a needle, stretched fabric across a hoop, twisted thread, and made stitches, adapting its movements because the thread landed in a different position each time. The OmniHand 3 Ultra is sized similarly to a human hand, weighs 1.9 pounds (630 grams), uses a direct-drive architecture, holds 6.6 pounds (3 kilograms) steadily, lifts up to 17.6 pounds (8 kilograms), opens and closes in about 0.3 seconds, and has a positional repeatability of about 0.008 inches (0.2 millimeters). For sensing, each fingertip features a vision-based sensor that measures force in three dimensions and detects surface deformations as small as 0.08 millimeters, while the palm incorporates a 300-point contact sensor. AGILINK combined teleoperation data with reinforcement learning for the training process. The article also notes other robotic hands such as LinkerBot's Linker Hand L30 Pro and Boston Dynamics' latest Atlas hand.

总结：中国机器人初创公司AGILINK（AgiBot的衍生公司）展示了其OmniHand 3 Ultra灵巧手执行中国传统苏州刺绣技艺的过程。 在苏绣大师傅向红的指导下，该机器人将一根丝线分拆为16根更细的细丝，进行了穿针、将织物绷在绣绷上、捻线以及缝针等操作，由于每次落线位置不同，机器人需要调整其实际动作。 OmniHand 3 Ultra尺寸接近人手，重1.9磅（630克），采用直驱架构，可稳定抓取6.6磅（3公斤）物品并举起最高17.6磅（8公斤）的重量，开合时间约为0.3秒，位置重复精度约为0.008英寸（0.2毫米）。 在感知方面，每个指尖均采用基于视觉的传感器，可测量三维力并检测小至0.08毫米的表面形变，手掌则集成了一个300点接触传感器。 AGILINK在训练过程中结合了遥操作数据与强化学习。 文章还提及了LinkerBot的Linker Hand L30 Pro以及波士顿动力最新Atlas手等其他公司的灵巧手设计。

Hyundai Motor Group — Boston Dynamics Atlas Next-Generation Four-Finger Robot Hand

Hyundai Motor Group detailed the next-generation four-finger robot hand for its Atlas robot. The hand features 13 degrees of freedom, including four in the opposable thumb and three in each other finger, and is human-hand sized for industrial workspaces. It uses dense pressure tactile sensors across fingertips and palms to detect subtle contact, alongside sim-to-real reinforcement learning to enable precise manipulation tasks such as tool use, precision assembly, and in-hand reorientation.

总结：现代汽车集团详细介绍了为其Atlas机器人开发的下一代四指机器人手。 该机械手拥有13个自由度，其中对立拇指有4个自由度，其余每根手指有3个自由度，采用与人手大小相仿的设计以适应工业工作空间。 它在指尖和手掌各处配备了密集压力触控传感器以检测微小接触，并结合从仿真到真实的强化学习来实现精准抓握、工具使用、精密装配和手中物体重新定向等真实世界的工业任务。

Interesting Engineering — Video: Robot hand performs magic tricks with precision using 20 independent joints

Chinese robotics startup LinkerBot demonstrated the dexterity of its LinkerHand O30 through magic tricks including a card change, pulling a toy car from a card, and coin disappearance. Founded in 2023, the company designed the O30 with 20 active degrees of freedom using a direct-drive architecture and brushless motors paired with precision transmission. The hand weighs 730 grams, communicates via CAN and CAN FD at up to 500 commands per second, offers a ±0.20 mm repeatability, and runs on a 24-48 V DC power supply. It provides a maximum load up to 30 kilograms, with up to 24 N of fingertip force, 30 N four-finger lateral pinch force, and 70 N five-finger grasping force for precision manufacturing applications.

总结：中国机器人初创企业LinkerBot通过变牌、从卡片中拉出玩具车和硬币消失等魔术表演，展示了其LinkerHand O30机器手的灵活性。 该公司成立于2023年，O30采用直驱架构和无刷电机配以精密传动系统，拥有20个主动自由度。 该机械手重730克，通过CAN和CAN FD通信，每秒可接收高达500条控制指令，重复定位精度达±0.20毫米，使用24-48伏直流电源。 它提供高达30公斤的最大负载，指尖力可达24牛，四指侧捏力达30牛，五指抓握力达70牛，适用于精密制造应用。
