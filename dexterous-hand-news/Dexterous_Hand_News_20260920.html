Vietnam.vn — Why can't robotic hands be as dexterous as human hands?

Humanoid robots excel at running and jumping, but household tasks like folding clothes or picking up wet sponges remain difficult because household objects bend, slip, and deform unpredictably. Human hands combine mechanics, feel, and control, whereas robotic hands require complex sensors and software. While factory robots operate in controlled environments with simple grippers, household robots must handle irregular items and whole-body coordination. Collecting manipulation data involving joint positions, force, and tactile sensors remains a major bottleneck.

总结：人形机器人擅长奔跑和跳跃，但折叠衣服或拿起湿海绵等家务仍然很困难，因为家居物品会不可预测地弯曲、滑动和变形。 人手结合了力学、触觉和控制，而机器人手则需要复杂的传感器和软件。 虽然工厂机器人使用简单的夹爪在受控环境中运行，但家用机器人必须处理不规则物品和全身协调。 收集涉及关节位置、力和触觉传感器的操作数据仍然是一个主要瓶颈。

MarkTechPost — Reward AI Releases OM-1: A Robot Policy Trained on Human Demonstrations Only, With No Teleoperation or On-Robot Data

Reward AI has released OM-1, a general-purpose manipulation policy trained exclusively on human demonstrations without teleoperation or on-robot data. The system uses the Omnibody Hand, a 7-DoF wearable that captures contact points, reorientation, and grasps using high-frequency tactile sensing, proximity sensing, and in-hand cameras. Electromagnetic tracking is augmented to reduce overshoot error. OM-1 processes multimodal streams at native sampling rates in a single-stage training setup, while a simulation-trained reinforcement learning control layer handles physical dynamics.

总结：Reward AI 发布了 OM-1，这是一种通用的操作策略，仅通过人类示范进行训练，无需远程操作或机载机器人数据。 该系统使用 Omnibody Hand（一款 7 自由度可穿戴设备），利用高频触手觉感知、接近感知和手内摄像头来捕获接触点、重新定向和抓取。 增强了电磁跟踪以减少超调误差。 OM-1 在单阶段训练设置中以原生采样率处理多模态流，而模拟训练的强化学习控制层则处理物理动力学。

PR Newswire — ShengShu Technology Unveils Motus2, a Self-Evolving General World Model for Dexterous Manipulation

ShengShu Technology Co-founder and CEO Yihang Luo unveiled Motus2, a self-evolving general world model for robotic dexterous manipulation, at the 2026 Inclusion Conference on the Bund. Motus2 unifies action generation, consequence prediction, and outcome evaluation in a single video-action model. Evaluated on dual-arm platforms with WUJI hands, it achieved an 84% average success rate across five real-robot tasks after robot-domain mid-training. The model combines egocentric human video datasets with robot trajectories to achieve policy improvement through model-based reinforcement learning.

总结：盛数科技联合创始人兼CEO罗一航在外滩大会上发布了 Motus2，这是一个用于机器人灵巧操作的自 evolving 通用世界模型。 Motus2 将动作生成、结果预测和结果评估统一在一个视频动作模型中。 在配备 WUJI 手的双臂平台上进行评估后，经过机器人领域的中期训练，它在五个真实机器人任务中取得了 84% 的平均成功率。 该模型将以自我为中心的人类视频数据集与机器人轨迹相结合，通过基于模型的强化学习来实现策略改进。
