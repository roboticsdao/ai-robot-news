Tech Times — Xiaomi Engineers Robotic Hand From 1,827 Factory SOPs: China Spy Law Travels With It

Xiaomi designed its latest robotic hand for automotive manufacturing by starting with 1,827 standard operating procedures from the company's automotive test center, rather than using general benchmarks. Announced by Xiaomi, the device features 26 electric motors, 21 degrees of freedom, and approximately 1,500 tactile sensing points within a 324-gram palm assembly. By analyzing its SOP corpus, Xiaomi found that moving from simpler 6-to-15 DoF manipulators to a 21-DoF design raises stable grasp success rates by roughly 15 to 38 percent. To accommodate touch sensing while keeping fingers light, 16 of the 21 DoF are cable-driven with motors housed in the forearm, while the remaining 5 DoF use direct drive. Maximum power draw for a single hand is rated at 100W, and control accuracy is maintained using tendon tension sensors that track fingertip pressure in real time.

总结：小米在设计用于汽车制造的最新仿生手时，并未采用通用基准，而是从汽车测试中心的1,827条标准作业程序出发。 小米宣布该设备拥有26台电机、21个自由度以及约1,500个触觉传感点，手掌组件重量为324克。 通过分析SOP语料库，小米发现将自由度从较简单的6至15个提升至21个，可使稳定抓取成功率提升约15%至38%。 为了在保持手指轻便的同时容纳触觉感知，21个自由度中有16个采用腱绳驱动，电机容纳在前臂中，其余5个自由度则使用直接驱动。 单只手的最大功耗额定为100W，并通过实时追踪指尖压力的腱绳张力传感器来维持控制精度。

eu.36kr.com — Tsinghua Research Team Builds HandsOn Dexterous Manipulation Benchmark: Comprehensive 66-Task Evaluation of Robot Bimanual Capabilities

A research team led by Ning Ding from Tsinghua University, in collaboration with researchers from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications, Peking University and Nature Will, developed a composable simulation platform and evaluation benchmark named HandsOn for cross-embodiment dexterous manipulation. The platform addresses the lack of systematic evaluation systems for dexterous hands, featuring 66 human-validated tasks and 18,000 human teleoperation demonstration records in simulation. HandsOn supports 5 types of robotic arms, 6 types of dexterous hands, and about 200 assets of rigid bodies, articulated objects and deformable objects, divided into Core-50 and Transfer-16 tasks. Evaluations of different technical routes showed that the best-performing trained multi-task policy on Core-50 achieved a success rate of 29.1%, while the average task success rate of all 8 trained multi-task policies on Transfer-16 was lower than 1%. GPT-6 achieved success rates of 5.2% and 1.9% on Core-50 and Transfer-16 respectively across 660 tests without fine-tuning. The team released relevant papers, codes, and data resources.

总结：清华大学丁宁领导的团队联合北京邮电大学、北京大学和Nature Will的研究人员，共同构建了一个用于跨具身灵巧操作的可组合仿真平台与评估基准HandsOn。 该平台旨在填补灵巧手系统化评估体系的空白，包含66个经人类验证的任务以及18000条仿真中的人类遥操作示范记录。 HandsOn支持5种机械臂、6种灵巧手以及约200种刚体、关节对象和可变形对象资产，任务分为Core-50和Transfer-16。 对不同技术路线的评估显示，在有提供训练示范的Core-50任务中，表现最好的已训练多任务策略取得了29.1%的成功率；而在没有目标任务训练示范的Transfer-16任务中，所有8个已训练多任务策略的平均任务成功率均低于1%。 GPT-6在未经基准数据微调的情况下，在Core-50和Transfer-16上的成功率分别为5.2%和1.9%。 团队已公开相关论文、代码与数据资源。

خبرگزاری آنا — Chinese Scientists Develop a Robotic Arm with Human-Like Tactile Sensing

A research team at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) led by Shen Yajing has developed an integrated sensorimotor robotic arm named EmArm, featuring full-arm tactile sensing and adaptive manipulation capabilities. The system embeds more than 71,000 square millimeters of soft optical tactile skin onto the upper arm and forearm of a rigid mechanical skeleton, using internal submillimeter-scale cameras to analyze applied force distributions. EmArm achieves a spatial resolution of 0.085 millimeters and a two-point discrimination threshold of 18.2 millimeters. The optical sensing approach resists electromagnetic, optical, thermal, humidity-related, and acoustic interference, maintaining a sensing accuracy of up to 97.9 percent when exposed to five types of environmental interference. The research team proposed a perception-action loop that integrates joint-angle data and tactile information in real time to enable autonomous decision-making by extracting features such as contact position, contact area, force, and the number of contact points.

总结：香港科技大学由沈亚京领导的研究团队开发了一种集成式感觉运动机械臂EmArm，具备全臂触觉感知和自适应操作能力。 该系统将超过71000平方毫米的柔性光学触觉皮肤嵌入刚性机械骨架的上臂和前臂，并利用内部亚毫米级相机分析施加在机械臂上的力分布。 EmArm的空间分辨率达到0.085毫米，两点辨别阈值可达18.2毫米。 光学传感方法可抵御电磁、光学、热、湿度和声学干扰，在暴露于五种环境干扰时仍能保持高达97.9%的传感精度。 研究团队提出了一种感知-动作循环，将关节角度数据与触觉信息实时整合，通过提取接触位置、接触面积、力和接触点数量等特征来实现自主决策。
