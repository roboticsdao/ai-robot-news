# Dexterous Hand News

Daily multilingual global coverage of robotic dexterous hands, tactile sensing, end effectors, teleoperation, grasping and manipulation research. Articles are presented in one worldwide timeline with at least five full-text items per issue.

The GitHub Actions workflow runs every day at 08:00 JST. It gathers recent Google News RSS results, resolves the original article links, extracts article bodies, and uses Gemini to produce factual local-language and Simplified Chinese summaries.

Public page: https://roboticsdao.github.io/ai-robot-news/dexterous-hand-news/latest.html
