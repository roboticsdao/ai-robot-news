openPR.com — Robot Teleoperation Platform Market Outlook 2026-2032: AI

The global market for Robot Teleoperation Platform was estimated to be worth US$ 87.60 million in 2025 and is projected to reach US$ 444 million, growing at a CAGR of 25.8% from 2026 to 2032. Central technology trends include the transition from one-operator-one-robot control toward supervised autonomy where remote operators intervene only during exceptions, and the convergence of teleoperation with embodied AI data infrastructure for imitation learning and policy training. Markets are shifting toward reusable platform architectures using ROS and ROS2 connectivity, browser-based interfaces, cloud APIs and edge computing, alongside enterprise requirements for secure deployment and data governance. Business models span software subscriptions, per-robot licenses, enterprise contracts, integrated hardware-software packages and managed services. Growth is driven by the expansion of industrial and service robot installations—such as 542,000 new industrial robot installations and over 102,000 professional service robot sales recorded by IFR in 2024—and embodied AI development. Constraints involve network latency, bandwidth stability, control safety, proprietary robot-control stacks and integration costs for heterogeneous fleets.

总结：全球机器人远程操作平台市场在2025年估计价值87.60 million美元，预计到2032年将达到444 million美元，2026至2032年的复合年增长率为25.8%。 核心技术趋势包括从“一个操作员控制一台机器人”向“监督式自主控制”的转变（即机器人在常规情况下自主执行，远程操作员仅在遇到不确定性或异常时介入），以及远程操作与具身AI数据基础设施的融合，用于模仿学习和策略训练。 市场正转向采用ROS和ROS2连接、基于浏览器的操作界面、云API和边缘计算的可重用平台架构，同时企业客户也对安全部署和数据治理提出了更高要求。 商业模式涵盖软件订阅、按机器人授权、企业合同、软硬件集成包以及托管的远程操作服务。 增长动力来自工业和服务机器人装机量的扩大——例如IFR记录2024年工业机器人新装机量542,000台，专业服务机器人销售超过102,000台——以及具身AI的发展。 市场限制因素包括网络延迟、带宽稳定性、控制安全性、专有机器人控制栈以及异构车队的集成成本。

Tech Times — Agile Robots Unveils Robot Academy: Worker Video Generates Training Data Without Teleoperation

On October 8 at the Wave by Vento technology conference in Turin, Italy, Agile Robots CEO Zhaopeng Chen announced that the company's Munich Robot Academy is generating robot training data directly from worker videos using hand-tracking software, simulation, and physical transfer without specialized teleoperation rigs. Agile Robots operates more than 20,000 robotic systems worldwide and uses the Munich facility to capture recordings of human industrial tasks. Computer vision software extracts finger joint positions to create skeletal trajectory data. The seven-stage pipeline retargets human joint configurations to robot joint configurations and loads the data into simulation environments running on the Industrial AI Cloud, a joint infrastructure operated by Deutsche Telekom and NVIDIA utilizing 10,000 NVIDIA GPUs in German data centers.

总结：10月8日，在意大利都灵举行的Wave by Vento技术展上，Agile Robots首席执行官陈兆鹏宣布，该公司位于慕尼黑的机器人学院正利用手部追踪软件、仿真和物理转换，直接从人类工人的视频中生成机器人训练数据，而无需专门的遥操作设备。 Agile Robots在全球已部署超过20,000套机器人系统，并利用慕尼黑实体设施记录人类工业任务。 计算机视觉软件提取手指关节位置以创建骨骼轨迹数据。 该七阶段技术管道将人类关节配置重新定向为机器人关节配置，并将数据加载到由德国电信和NVIDIA运营的工业AI云仿真环境中，该环境基于德国数据中心内的10,000张NVIDIA GPU构建。

BigGo Finance — Leju Robotics Partners with Huazhong University of Science and Technology to Advance Flexible 3D Tactile Sensing

Hong Kong-listed robotics technology company Leju Robotics (01236. HK) signed a cooperation agreement with Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST) to jointly invest in R&D regarding flexible 3D tactile sensing for embodied robots. The collaboration covers three areas: developing flexible 3D tactile sensors and high-density sensing arrays; developing supporting signal acquisition hardware, 3D force decoupling, and tactile imaging algorithms; and validating deployment capabilities on robotic dexterous hands and robotic arms. Leju Robotics seeks to combine visual perception with tactile sensing, while HUST brings expertise in sensor technology, micro-electromechanical systems, and robotics research to accelerate laboratory-stage technology transition toward industrial validation.

总结：港股上市机器人科技公司乐聚机器人（01236. HK）与华中科技大学正式签署合作协议，双方将共同投资研发具身智能机器人柔性三维触觉感知技术。 合作涵盖三个领域：开发柔性三维触觉传感器和高密度传感阵列；开发配套信号采集硬件、三维力解耦及触觉成像算法；验证其在机器人灵巧手和机械臂上的部署能力。 乐聚机器人旨在将视觉感知与触觉感知相结合，而华中科技大学则凭借在传感器技术、微机电系统及机器人相关研究领域的专长，加速实验室阶段技术的产业化验证。
