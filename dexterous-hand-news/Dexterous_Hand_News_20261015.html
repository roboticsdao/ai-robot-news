Tech Times — Agile Robots Unveils Robot Academy: Worker Video Generates Training Data Without Teleoperation

On October 8 at the Wave by Vento technology conference in Turin, Italy, Agile Robots CEO Zhaopeng Chen announced that the Munich-based Robot Academy generates robot training data directly from human worker video footage. The academy uses hand-tracking software to extract motion trajectories and runs them through simulation before physical transfer, avoiding specialized teleoperation rigs. Agile Robots has deployed over 20,000 robotic systems worldwide and aims to build a compounding training data flywheel. Workers perform tasks at the Munich facility while cameras record. Hand-pose estimation software infers three-dimensional joint positions from two-dimensional images. Retargeting algorithms then map human joint configurations to robot configurations while preserving motion intent. The retargeted data is loaded into simulation environments on the Industrial AI Cloud, operated jointly by Deutsche Telekom and NVIDIA and housed in German data centers using 10,000 NVIDIA GPUs, where robots practice motions thousands of times.

总结：10月8日，在意大利都灵举行的 Wave by Vento 技术会议上，Agile Robots 首席执行官 Zhaopeng Chen 宣布，其位于慕尼黑的 Robot Academy 直接从人类工人的视频画面中生成机器人训练数据。 该学院使用手部追踪软件提取运动轨迹，并在实体转移前通过仿真运行轨迹，无需专门的遥操作设备。 Agile Robots 已在全球客户现场部署了超过 20,000 个机器人系统，并致力于构建训练数据飞轮。 工人在慕尼黑设施现场执行任务，摄像头进行记录。 手部姿态估计软件从二维图像中推断出三维关节位置。 随后，重定向算法将人类关节配置映射到机器人关节配置，同时保留运动意图。 重定向后的运动数据被加载到工业 AI 云上的仿真环境中；该基础设施由德国电信和 NVIDIA 共同运营， housed 在符合欧洲数据保护标准的德国数据中心内，拥有 10,000 个 NVIDIA GPU，机器人在其中练习目标动作数百或数千次。

BigGo Finance — Leju Robotics Partners with Huazhong University of Science and Technology to Advance Flexible 3D Tactile Sensing

Hong Kong-listed robotics technology company Leju Robotics (01236. HK) has signed a cooperation agreement with Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST) to jointly research and develop flexible 3D tactile sensing for embodied robots. The collaboration focuses on three main areas: developing flexible 3D sensors and high-density sensing arrays; creating supporting signal acquisition hardware, 3D force decoupling, and tactile imaging algorithms; and validating the deployment of this system on robotic dexterous hands and robotic arms. Leju Robotics aims to expand from vision-centric perception to tactile sensing, completing a full technology chain from sensors and algorithms to array systems. The resulting technologies are expected to be applied in dexterous hands, robotic arms, humanoid robots, collaborative robots, and intelligent service robots. HUST provides fundamental research expertise in sensor technology, micro-electromechanical systems, and robotics, while Leju Robotics offers industrial manufacturing and deployment capabilities.

总结：港股上市机器人科技企业乐聚机器人（01236. HK）宣布与华中科技大学正式签署合作协议，双方将共同投资研发具身智能机器人柔性3D触觉感知技术。 合作聚焦三大领域：开发柔性3D触觉传感器与高密度传感阵列；研发配套信号采集硬件、3D力解耦及触觉成像算法；验证3D柔性触觉感知系统在机器人灵巧手和机械臂等平台上的部署能力。 乐聚机器人旨在将视觉感知扩展至触觉感知，完善从传感器、算法到阵列系统的全技术链。 相关技术预期应用于灵巧手、机械臂、人形机器人、协作机器人及智能服务机器人。 华中科技大学提供传感器技术、微机电系统及机器人领域的底层科研实力，乐聚机器人则提供工业制造与部署能力。

Interesting Engineering — BYD’s humanoid robot has 31 degrees of freedom and a dexterous hand

Chinese automaker BYD has unveiled a patent published by China's National Intellectual Property Administration detailing its first self-developed humanoid robot, featuring a black-and-white body, rounded head without a display, and articulated joints across the torso, arms, and legs. Previously disclosed specifications for the Xiao Di concept indicate a height of approximately 5 feet 3 inches, a weight of around 129 pounds (58 kg), 31 degrees of freedom, and a dexterous hand with a repeatable positioning accuracy of approximately 0.04 inches. BYD's robotics project began in 2022 with a dedicated team, and the company has previously invested in Agibot in August 2023 and acquired a stake in Percibot in April 2025. Additionally, BYD's Changsha factory has hosted Ubtech's Walker S1 humanoid robot for material handling and identification. BYD executives have indicated potential applications in its dealership network for greeting customers and explaining vehicle features, though BYD has not confirmed a production timeline or whether the patent represents the final production model.

总结：中国汽车制造商比亚迪公布了一项由中国国家知识产权局发布的专利，展示了其首款自主研发的人形机器人设计，该机器人拥有黑白两色躯体、无显示屏的圆润头部，以及分布在躯干、手臂和腿部的关节。 此前披露的Xiao Di概念机规格显示其身高约为5英尺3英寸，体重约129磅（58 kg），拥有31个自由度，其灵巧手的重复定位精度约为0.04英寸。 比亚迪的机器人项目始于2022年成立的专属团队，该公司此前还于2023年8月投资了智元机器人，并在2025年4月收购了柏犀智驾的股份。 此外，比亚迪长沙工厂曾引入优必选Walker S1人形机器人进行物料搬运和识别任务。 比亚迪高管曾表示其人形机器人未来有望部署在经销商网络中用于迎接客户和讲解汽车功能，但比亚迪尚未公布量产时间表，也未确认该专利设计是否代表最终量产版本。
