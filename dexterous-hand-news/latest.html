Robotics & Automation News — Prensilia launches new robotic hand for industrial humanoids and cobots

Italian robotics company Prensilia introduced NH1 Era, a robotic hand designed for humanoid and collaborative robots, unveiled at SPS 2026 in Nuremberg, Germany. The robotic hand combines versatile grasping capabilities with industrial durability and robustness, aiming to help bring physical AI capabilities from research laboratories to factory floors. NH1 Era supports multiple grasping modes, features underactuated fingers with mechanical intelligence, and can be equipped with force sensors. Key technical features include up to 70 newtons of thumb fingertip force, tri-axis fingertip force sensors, an ISO 9409-1-compatible mechanical interface or an interface adapted to humanoid robot wrists, supply voltage of up to 48 volts, RS-485 and Ethernet communication protocols, and Robot Operating System libraries. Founded in 2009 as a spin-off from the Scuola Superiore Sant'Anna in Pisa, Prensilia is based in Pontedera, Italy. Pre-orders for the first production batch are open through the Prensilia website.

总结：意大利机器人公司Prensilia在德国纽伦堡的SPS 2026展会上推出了专为人形机器人和协作机器人设计的NH1 Era机器人手。 该产品结合了多功能抓取能力与工业耐久性，旨在将具身智能能力从实验室带到工厂车间。 NH1 Era支持多种抓取模式，采用具有机械智能的欠驱动手指，并可配备力传感器。 其关键技术特征包括最高70牛顿的拇指指尖力、三轴指尖力传感器、兼容ISO 9409-1或适配人形机器人手腕的机械接口、最高48伏的供电电压、RS-485及以太网通信协议，以及用于控制和仿真的机器人操作系统库。 Prensilia于2009年作为比萨圣安娜高等学校的分支机构创立，总部位于意大利蓬泰代拉。 首批产品的预订已通过Prensilia网站开放。

Robotics & Automation News — Researchers develop 3D-printable humanoid robotic hand costing less than $650

Researchers at the New Jersey Institute of Technology developed the PHD-Hand, a five-fingered dexterous humanoid robotic hand that can be manufactured using an affordable 3D printer and readily available components for less than $650 in parts. Authored by Neel Adwani, Muhaiminul Islam Akash, Saffia Ouali, Ethan Liu, and Cong Wang, the research paper has been accepted at the IEEE-RAS International Conference on Humanoid Robots (Humanoids 2026). The hand features 15 independently actuated degrees of freedom, using 3D-printed flexible hinges made from thermoplastic polyurethane and spring steel drive rods to eliminate miniature moving parts. It enables bidirectional actuation and demonstrates all 33 grasp types in the GRASP taxonomy, alongside capabilities to manipulate objects like a 55 mm cube, jar lid, precision screwdriver, and adjustable wrench. The researchers used Anthropic's Claude AI system to generate the graphical control interface and motion trajectories. They plan to release CAD models, 3D-printing files, control software, motion sequences, and bill of materials through an open-source GitHub repository.

总结：新泽西理工学院的研究人员开发了PHD-Hand，这是一种五指具身灵巧人形机器人手，使用经济实惠的3D打印机和易获取的零部件制造，零件成本低于$650。 该研究论文由Neel Adwani、Muhaiminul Islam Akash、Saffia Ouali、Ethan Liu和Cong Wang撰写，已被IEEE-RAS人形机器人国际会议（Humanoids 2026）接受。 该手具备15个独立驱动的自由度，采用由热塑性聚氨酯制成的3D打印柔性铰链和弹簧钢传动杆，省去了微型活动部件。 它支持双向驱动，展示了GRASP分类法中的全部33种抓取类型，并能操作55毫米立方体、瓶盖、精密螺丝刀和活动扳手等物品。 研究人员使用Anthropic的Claude人工智能系统生成了图形控制界面和运动轨迹。 他们计划通过开源GitHub仓库发布CAD模型、3D打印文件、控制软件、运动序列和物料清单。

Bioengineer.org — NJIT Engineers Develop Inexpensive 3D-Printed Dexterous Robotic Hand with Modular Digits

Researchers at the New Jersey Institute of Technology, including Associate Professor Cong Wang, doctoral student Neel Adwani, research assistant Muhaiminul Akash, undergraduate researcher Saffia Ouali, and high school student Ethan Liu, designed and built the PHD-Hand, a printable humanoid dexterous robotic hand. The project was initiated following Wang's 2024 hospital stay, which inspired him to pursue a simplified, resilient alternative to existing commercial robotic hands that he viewed as expensive and complex. The PHD-Hand avoids traditional tendon-driven architectures and miniature moving parts, relying on an accessible mechanical architecture produced using a budget-grade 3D printer. The design incorporates 3D-printed knuckles, modular digits, a 2-degree-of-freedom flexure hinge, internal springs, and off-the-shelf hobby servos, featuring an inherent safety mechanism where digits passively return to a neutral resting position upon power loss. System operation is handled via a web browser-based software interface supporting mouse inputs and natural language processing.

总结：新泽西理工学院的研究人员（包括副教授Cong Wang、博士生Neel Adwani、研究助理Muhaiminul Akash、本科研究员Saffia Ouali以及高中生Ethan Liu）设计并制造了PHD-Hand，这是一款可打印的人形灵巧机器人手。 该项目始于Wang在2024年的住院经历，这激发他去寻找现有商业机器人手的简化且具韧性的替代方案，他认为商业机器人手过于昂贵和复杂。 PHD-Hand避开了传统的线传动架构和微型活动部件，依赖于使用经济型3D打印机制造的可及机械架构。 该设计结合了3D打印指关节、模块化手指、2个自由度的柔性铰链、内部弹簧以及现成的业余爱好级伺服电机，并具有在断电时手指被动回到中立静止位置的固有安全机制。 系统操作通过支持鼠标输入和自然语言处理的网络浏览器软件界面进行处理。

FabScene — 米ニュージャージー工科大、安価な3Dプリンターで作れるロボットハンドを開発

Researchers at the New Jersey Institute of Technology developed the PHD-Hand, a robotic hand made with low-cost 3D printers and off-the-shelf parts, featuring five fingers driven by three servo motors each. Measuring about 200mm in length, the hand uses a linkage mechanism where motors are placed outside the fingers. The flexible TPU joints bend in two directions and contain a 0.6mm stainless steel spring steel rod. Each finger uses 13 main parts when printed as a single unit. In testing, the prototype performed 33 grasp types and tasks like operating a precision screwdriver, though chopsticks handling was limited due to range and missing webbing. The team tested the prototype for about 200 hours, experiencing two spring steel rod breakages.

总结：米ニュージャージー工科大学的研究团队开发了人型机器人手“PHD-Hand”，由低成本3D打印机和市販零件制成，每根手指由三个伺服电机驱动，长度约200mm。 该手采用将电机置于指外的连杆机构，由TPU材料打印的柔性关节可通过中心厚度0.6mm的不锈钢弹簧钢棒进行双向弯曲。 一体打印的单指主要部件为13点。 在测试中，样机完成了33种抓握分类中的动作及精密螺丝刀操作，但在使用筷子等操作上受到限制。 团队进行了约200小时的测试，期间发生了两次弹簧钢棒折断。
