Gasgoo — SynDexy dexterous manipulation humanoid robot, completes first small-batch delivery

SynDexy announced on October 30 that its in-house dual-arm wheeled humanoid robot, the SynDexy C1, has completed its first small-batch delivery for precision assembly lines at consumer electronics and component factories. The C1 features vision-tactile fusion with micron-level spatial resolution and runs the SynDexy M1 tri-state fusion model. It packs 22 degrees of freedom and 1-millisecond real-time motion control, achieving a success rate above 98% on critical tasks during prior testing. Development began in April 2026, with a prototype ready by July and small-batch deliveries in October. Founded in 2025 and led by Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications Professor Fang Bin, SynDexy is a full-stack embodied AI company focused on high-precision industrial dexterity.

总结：SynDexy于10月30日宣布，其自研双臂轮式人形机器人SynDexy C1已完成首批小批量交付，将用于消费电子和零部件工厂的精密装配线。 C1核心搭载了具备微米级空间分辨率的视触觉融合技术，运行SynDexy M1三态融合模型，拥有22个自由度与1毫秒实时运动控制，此前关键任务成功率超过98%。 该产品自2026年4月启动开发，7月完成首代原型机，10月实现小批量交付。 SynDexy成立于2025年，由北京邮电大学教授方斌带队，是一家专注于高精度工业灵巧操作的全栈具身AI公司。

Electronic Products & Technology — Tactile sensors give humanoid robots a ‘sense of touch’

IDTechEx report reveals that a third of all currently active humanoid robot developers are integrating or developing tactile sensors into their designs. Humanoid robots are transitioning from design to commercial use, driven by advancements in sensor technology. IDTechEx projects the sensor market for humanoid robots will exceed US$6.59 billion by 2037, with a compound annual growth rate of 21.4%. Key sensors include perception, force/torque, encoders, inertial measurement units (IMUs), and tactile sensors, which enhance safety and task performance. About one-third of humanoid robot developers, including Tesla and Unitree, are integrating tactile sensors into hands or grippers to precisely manipulate objects and discern material types. Emerging technologies such as optical or magnetic sensing and e-skins may expand tactile sensor applications beyond hands to other parts of the robot body, enhancing safety in shared environments.

总结：IDTechEx的报告显示，目前活跃的人形机器人开发者中有三分之一正在将触觉传感器整合到设计中或进行相关开发。 在传感器技术进步的推动下，人形机器人正从概念设计向早期商业化过渡。 IDTechEx预测，到2037年，人形机器人传感器市场将超过US$6.59 billion，复合年增长率为21.4%。 关键传感器包括感知传感器、力/扭矩传感器、编码器、惯性测量单元以及触觉传感器，这些传感器能够提升安全性和任务执行性能。 包括Tesla和Unitree在内的约三分之一人形机器人开发者正将触觉传感器部署在手部或夹爪上，使机器人能够精准操纵物体并通过表面纹理辨别材质类型。 光学或磁性传感以及电子皮肤等新兴技术有望将触觉传感器的应用从手部扩展至机器人身体的其他部位，从而增强人机共享环境中的安全性。
