TechTimes Vietnam — GENTO unveils 21‑DoF dexterous robot hand at CoRL 2026

GENTO showcased its proprietary 21-DoF dexterous hand at the CoRL 2026 conference in Austin, Texas, alongside TIANJI force-controlled arms, the Marvin Pro platform, and the Luna mobile robot. The hand uses a single linkage-driven transmission to reduce backlash, allow backdrivability, and boost fingertip force within a compact size. TIANJI Intelligence equips the system with in-house MEMS joint torque sensors resolving torques down to 0.02 N·m with a 160 Hz torque-loop bandwidth. Additionally, NVIDIA introduced GR00T 2 at the event, demonstrating a banana-yogurt preparation task using a TIANJI Marvin Pro platform equipped with two GENTO dexterous hands.

总结：GENTO在德克萨斯州奥斯汀举行的CoRL 2026会议上首次展示了其专有的21自由度灵巧手，并同台展出了TIANJI力控机械臂、Marvin Pro平台和Luna移动机器人。 该灵巧手采用单连杆驱动传动系统，可减少背隙、增强抗冲击能力并提高指尖力。 TIANJI Intelligence为该系统配备了内部MEMS关节扭矩传感器，扭矩分辨率达0.02 N·m，扭矩环带宽为160 Hz。 此外，英伟达在会上推出了GR00T 2，并使用配备两只GENTO灵巧手的TIANJI Marvin Pro平台演示了香蕉酸奶制备任务。

Electronics Media — ATFCD52 Tactile Sensor for Advanced Robotic Touch Sensing

Microchip Technology introduced the ATFCD52 high-speed capacitance digitizer to convert signals from fine-grid, two-dimensional capacitive sensor arrays into high-sensitivity digital data for robotic touch sensing. Supporting up to 52 channels with up to 32 TX by 20 RX lines, the device enables dense sensor arrays with frame rates of up to 800 Hz to detect slip and adjust grip. The ATFCD52 can resolve capacitance changes below 1 femtofarad and includes frequency hopping and oversampling to mitigate interference from servo motors and power electronics in noisy environments. It is supported by the EV-ATFCD52 development board and ATFCD52 Configurator software.

总结：Microchip Technology推出了ATFCD52高速电容数字化仪，用于将细网格二维电容传感器阵列的信号转换为高灵敏度数字数据，以支持机器人触觉传感。 该器件支持多达52个通道以及最高32个发射线和20个接收线，使密集的传感器阵列能够实现高达800 Hz的帧率，从而检测滑移并调整抓握。 ATFCD52能够分辨低于1飞法拉的电容变化，并采用跳频和过采样技术来减轻嘈杂环境中伺服电机和电力电子设备的干扰。 该产品由EV-ATFCD52开发板和ATFCD52 Configurator软件提供支持。

The Manila Times — Tianji Intelligence Debuts 21-DoF Dexterous Hand at CoRL 2026 as NVIDIA Demos GR00T 2 on Custom Marvin Pro

GENTO, the embodied robotics brand of Tianji Intelligence, publicly unveiled its 21-degree-of-freedom dexterous hand at Booth 602 at CoRL 2026 in Austin, Texas. The hand features a linkage-driven design, weighs 750 g excluding cables, measures 200 × 96 × 45 mm, has a rated fingertip force of 10 N, and achieves a repeat positioning accuracy of ±0.05 mm. At the same event, NVIDIA introduced its GR00T 2 world action model and demonstrated two-handed manipulation using a custom Marvin Pro dual-arm platform built by Tianji Intelligence.

总结：天机智能旗下的具身机器人品牌GENTO在德克萨斯州奥斯汀CoRL 2026的602号展位上首次公开展示其21自由度灵巧手。 该灵巧手采用连杆驱动设计，不含电缆重750克，尺寸为200×96×45毫米，额定指尖力为10 N，重复定位精度为±0.05毫米。 在同一活动中，英伟达推出了GR00T 2世界动作模型，并使用由天机智能为其定制的Marvin Pro双臂平台演示了双手操作。

Gasgoo — Inspire Robots Showcases Three New Dexterous Hand Models

On November 4, Beijing Inspire Robots Technology Co. , Ltd. and Beijing Shougang Construction Investment Co. , Ltd. co-hosted a robotics supply chain forum and new product showcase at Shougang Park in Beijing. During the event, Inspire Robots unveiled three new dexterous hands: the RH524J1, featuring a 24-degree-of-freedom tendon-cable hybrid drive architecture; the RH5MK1, utilizing a 22-degree-of-freedom full direct-drive design with fingertip six-dimensional force tactile sensing; and the RH37D1, adopting a three-finger, full direct-drive configuration with 7 active degrees of freedom. Inspire also launched joint laboratories with Shougang Zhixin Electromagnetic Materials (Qian'an) Co. , Ltd. and the Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications.
