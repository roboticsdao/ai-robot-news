EE Times Japan — ロボットの「滑り」を早期検出 高速触覚センサー向けIC：最大800Hzで滑りの兆候を検出

Microchip Technology announced the ATFCD52, a high-speed capacitive digitizer designed to enhance tactile sensing for robot hands. The IC supports up to 52 channels with 32 transmit and 20 receive lines, enabling 2D capacitive sensor arrays across contact surfaces. Featuring 20 parallel analog front-ends, it collects data from all receive lines simultaneously and achieves up to 800Hz frame rates depending on the sensor array size. The device can resolve capacitance changes under 1 femtofarad, providing high sensitivity to detect slight tactile sensor deformations. Targeted primarily at robotics, it is also proposed for AR, gaming accessories, data collection gloves, and wearable sensing. Additionally, Microchip offers the EV-ATFCD52 development board and ATFCD52 configurator software for evaluating sensor design and logging data.

总结：Microchip Technology宣布推出高速静电容量デジタイザー“ATFCD52”，旨在增强机器人手部把持物体的触觉传感。 该集成电路支持最大32条发送线和20条接收线，共计最多52个通道，可实现覆盖整个接触面的二维静电容量式传感器阵列。 它内置20个并行动作的模拟前端，能够同时收集所有接收线的数据，根据传感器阵列的大小实现最大800Hz的帧速率。 该器件具备高灵敏度，能够检测触觉传感器的微小变形，分解小于1飞法拉的静电容量变化。 ATFCD52的主要用途包括机器人技术，同时也被提议用于AR、游戏配件数据收集手套或脚垫以及可穿戴传感等应用。 此外，Microchip还准备了搭载ATFCD52的开发板“EV-ATFCD52”以及用于参数设置和实时数据记录的配置软件。

Unite.AI — ROBOTERA Opens Orders for XHAND 1 Pro-U Dexterous Robot Hand

ROBOTERA commercially launched the XHAND 1 Pro-U on November 16, 2026, opening orders for an initial 40-unit release of its five-finger dexterous robot hand. The hand features 16 degrees of freedom and is designed around NVIDIA GEAR Lab's DexUMI exoskeleton, a wearable device that records human demonstrations without requiring a robot, using direct joint mapping. Each DexUMI unit combines 15 magnetic encoders for finger joints, an OAK wrist camera, and fingertip sensors for normal and shear force. During collection, a wearer manipulates objects while DexUMI records movements, video, and forces, which are used to post-train GR00T 2 for execution on a dual-arm Tianji robot fitted with two XHAND1 Pro-U hands. In NVIDIA evaluations across three bimanual sequences—banana-yogurt preparation, granola scooping, and fire chemistry—GR00T 2 achieved overall success rates of 60%, 80%, and 75% respectively, while GR00T 1.7 and π0.5 completed none of the tasks.

总结：ROBOTERA于2026年11月16日宣布商业化推出XHAND 1 Pro-U，并为首批40只五指灵巧手发布开启订购。 这款拥有16个自由度的灵巧手围绕NVIDIA GEAR Lab的DexUMI外骨骼进行了设计，该可穿戴设备无需机器人即可记录人类示范，并实现关节之间的直接动作映射。 每个DexUMI单元结合了15个用于手指关节追踪的磁性编码器、OAK腕部相机以及用于测量法线力和せん断力的指尖传感器。 在采集过程中，佩戴者直接操作真实物体，DexUMI记录手部动作、腕部相机视频和指尖力，这些示范随后用于对GR00T 2进行后训练，并将习得的技能运行在配有两只XHAND1 Pro-U双臂的天机机器人上。 在NVIDIA对香蕉酸奶准备、麦片舀取和消防化学三项双手任务的评估中，GR00T 2的总体成功率分别达到60%、80%和75%，而GR00T 1.7和π0.5未能完成其中任何任务。
