Market restraints include high initial costs reducing CAGR by 4.0 percentage points and regulatory challenges subtracting 3.0 percentage points. By degrees of freedom, 6 to 12 DoF holds a 35% share while Above 20 DoF is the fastest-growing at a 31.5% CAGR. Electric Motor-Driven actuation leads at 40% with a 29.6% CAGR. THE ELEC, Korea Electronics Industry Media — Infineon Targets Semiconductor Market for Humanoid Robotic Hands

Infineon is targeting the market for robotic hands in humanoid robots using its industrial and automotive semiconductor solutions, such as microcontrollers, power semiconductors, and tactile sensors. At a session held alongside SEDEX 2026 in Seoul on Oct. 16, Sarah Wu introduced Infineon's robotics solutions. Robotic hands require precise motor control and tactile sensing, which are challenging to develop due to the need for independent joint control and variable gripping force. Infineon supports centralized, hybrid, and fully distributed control architectures depending on the hand's degrees of freedom. Tactile sensing relies on magnetic, pressure, and capacitive sensors, adapting smartphone and home appliance technologies, and exploring MEMS microphones to identify objects through sound and vibration. Infineon also demonstrated a board with an MCU, GaN power semiconductors, and sensors capable of driving motors up to 1.2 kilowatts within 66 millimeters, noting that GaN helps reduce the size of motor-drive circuits.

总结：英飞凌正凭借其在工业和汽车半导体领域的专业知识，将目标瞄准人形机器人关键组件机器人手部市场，其核心产品包括微控制器、功率半导体和触界传感器。 10月16日，在首尔COEX举办的SEDEX 2026期间举行的“构建人形机器人的半导体”专题会议上，英飞凌代表Sarah Wu介绍了公司的机器人半导体解决方案。 人形机器人手部必须像人手一样抓取和操控物体，每个手指和关节都需要精确的独立控制，并且要根据处理的物体调整握力，这使其成为最难开发的组件之一。 机器人手部需要电机控制和触觉传感技术，其中电机控制MCU和驱动器用于移动手指关节，传感器用于检测手指位置和抓取力。 英飞凌通过工业和汽车半导体产品支持这些技术。 机器人手部控制架构分为集中式、混合式和完全分布式三种，英飞凌解释称架构取决于手部的自由度，且公司可以支持这三种架构。 触觉传感依赖于磁性、压力和电容传感器，安装在指尖的三维磁传感器和压力传感器用于测量接触压力，电容传感器用于检测手指位置和指尖施加的力。 英飞凌已将此前用于智能手机和家用电器的技术调整用于机器人手部，并在探索使用微机电系统麦克风通过声音和振动来识别接触手部的物体特征。 英飞凌还展示了一块配备MCU、氮化镓功率半导体以及功率和位置传感器的电路板，能够驱动功率高达1.2千瓦的电机，尺寸约为66毫米。 由于氮化镓的开关速度更快且功率损耗比硅更低，英飞凌表示氮化镓技术有助于减小电机驱动电路的尺寸。

Robotics & Automation News — Tesla patent reveals touch-sensitive skin for robot hands

Tesla has filed a US patent application, US 2026/0310299 A1, published on October 8 and brought to light by Patentlyze, for a touch-sensitive skin that can be molded around robot fingers and palms to detect contact, force, and pressure at scale. The manufacturing process involves printing tactile sensors onto flat, flexible materials before heat-forming them into 3D shapes. The proposed sensor uses two flexible substrate layers with conductive regions separated by a compressible layer, forming sensing points where regions overlap. The patent describes capacitive and piezoresistive approaches and lists thermoplastic polyurethane, PET, and polyimide as substrate materials. Emphasizing manufacturability, Tesla describes screen printing, laser cutting, conductive paste deposition, and thermoforming processes. The 3D tactile sensors can conform to curved geometries and measure surface force, touch, and pressure. While the filing does not specify use in the Optimus humanoid robot, it identifies fingertips, palms, and end-effectors as potential applications. Patentlyze noted that the filing lacks figures for sensitivity, resolution, or durability, but the patent indicates Tesla's work on hardware for sophisticated manipulation capabilities.

总结：特斯拉提交了一项美国专利申请，即10月8日公布并经专利情报出版物Patentlyze分析的US 2026/0310299 A1号专利，内容涉及一种可模压在机器人手指和手掌周围的触觉敏感皮肤，为人形机器人提供了一种可扩展的检测接触、力和压力的方法。 该专利描述了一种制造工艺，即将触觉传感器印刷到扁平的柔性材料上，然后热成型为三维形状。 特斯拉提出的传感器使用两个带有导电区域的柔性基板层，中间由可压缩层隔开，在导电区域重叠处形成能够检测压力引起的变化的感应点。 该专利描述了电容式和压阻式方法，并列举了热塑性聚氨酯、PET和聚酰胺作为可能的基板材料。 该申请强调了可制造性，描述了丝网印刷、激光切割、导电浆料沉积和热成型等成熟制造工艺，使得触觉表面能够大规模生产。 该申请称三维触觉传感器可以贴合曲面几何形状，用于测量表面力、触觉和压力。 尽管该文件未明确指出该技术将用于特斯拉的Optimus人形机器人，但它将机器人指尖、手掌和末端执行器列为潜在应用。 Patentlyze指出该文件未提供传感器的灵敏度、分辨率或耐用性数据，因此其实际性能尚无法评估，但该专利表明了特斯拉在赋予机器人更复杂的操控能力所需硬件方面的工作。
