Schunk has renamed its humanoid robotics spin-off to Tactilia Robotics, establishing a Munich-based company focused on developing modular robotic hands for industrial automation. Originally founded in January 2026 as Schunk Humanoid Robotics GmbH, the company designs robotic hands for manufacturing and logistics across humanoid robots, collaborative robots, and mobile manipulators. Dr. Markus Netzer became CEO on November 1, 2026. The development program combines mechanical engineering, sensors, actuators, software, and physical AI. CTO Timo Gessmann emphasizes integration and reliability. Tactilia operates independently while drawing on Schunk's gripping expertise and its network of 15 CoLabs worldwide. Headquartered in Munich's Arabellapark district, the company's immediate priority is developing and validating industrial robotic hands.

Superpower Daily — Sundae Robotics 11: Beyond the Gripper — Teaching Robots Dexterity Through Touch, Vision, and Play

Akash Sharma, an Amazon Frontier AI & Robotics Research Scientist and CMU Robotics Institute PhD, presents four approaches to advancing dexterous robot manipulation through touch, vision, human demonstrations, and autonomous play during Sundae Robotics 11. The talk addresses limitations of standard routes like parallel-jaw grippers, human demonstrations, and simulation. First, PTLD learns tactile policies without tactile simulation by distilling a privileged simulation-trained teacher on real hardware. Second, INVAR learns visual invariance once from clean and randomized simulation pairs for zero-shot transfer. Third, CARD retargets human video, glove, and motion-capture demonstrations based on contact rather than movement. Fourth, DSP turns minutes of human play into goal-conditioned dexterity using autonomous play, world models, and distillation. Akash Sharma works with Jitendra Malik at Amazon FAR, received his PhD from CMU advised by Prof. Michael Kaess, and was a visiting researcher with FAIR Robotics at Meta.

总结：Amazon Frontier AI & Robotics研究科学家兼CMU机器人研究所博士Akash Sharma在Sundae Robotics 11活动中，展示了通过触觉、视觉、人类示范和自主玩耍推进具身机器人灵巧操作的四种方法。 演讲探讨了这些方法如何帮助机器人超越基本夹爪并学习可靠的双手内技能。 首先，PTLD通过在真实硬件上蒸馏特权模拟训练的教师，在不模拟触觉传感器的前提下学习触觉策略。 其次，INVAR从干净且随机化的模拟对中学习一次视觉不变性，实现零样本迁移。 接着，CARD根据手接触的内容而非运动方式来重定向人类视频、手套和动作捕捉示范。 最后，DSP通过自主玩耍、世界模型和蒸馏，将数分钟的人类玩耍转化为目标条件灵巧性。 Akash Sharma是Amazon FAR的研究科学家，与Jitendra Malik合作从事机器人手和灵巧操作研究，其博士学位毕业于卡耐基梅隆大学机器人研究所，师从Michael Kaess教授，并曾担任Meta FAIR机器人团队的访问研究员。
