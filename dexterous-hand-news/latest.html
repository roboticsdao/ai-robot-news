Tech Times — Xiaomi Engineers Robotic Hand From 1,827 Factory SOPs: China Spy Law Travels With It

Xiaomi has developed a new robotic hand for automotive manufacturing, engineered directly from 1,827 standard operating procedures used in its automotive test center. The device features 26 electric motors, 21 degrees of freedom, and approximately 1,500 tactile sensing points within a 324-gram palm assembly. To accommodate touch sensors and keep fingers light, 16 of the 21 degrees of freedom are cable-driven with motors housed in the forearm, drawing a maximum power of 100W. By analyzing SOP data, Xiaomi determined that a 21-degree-of-freedom design optimizes stable grasp success rates while avoiding the mechanical complexity of joints exceeding 23.

总结：小米为汽车制造开发了一种新型机械手，其设计直接源自汽车测试中心使用的1,827项标准操作程序。 该设备在324克的掌部组件中集成了26个电动机、21个自由度以及大约1,500个触觉传感点。 为了容纳触觉传感器并保持手指轻便，21个自由度中有16个采用绳索驱动，电机容纳在前臂中，单手最大功耗为100W。 通过分析标准操作程序数据，小米确定21自由度的设计能够在优化稳定抓握成功率的同时，避免超过23个关节带来的机械复杂性。
