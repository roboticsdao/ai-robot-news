Interesting Engineering — GENTO’s 21-DoF robot hand takes on backlash, fragility and weak grip in a single design

GENTO, an independent humanoid robot brand incubated by Tianji Intelligence, introduced its 21-degree-of-freedom dexterous-hand prototype at the Conference on Robot Learning in Austin. The hand weighs 750 g excluding cables, measures 200 × 96 × 45 mm, features a maximum grasp diameter of 100 mm, a rated fingertip force of 10 N, an opening and closing time of 0.5 seconds, and a repeat positioning accuracy of ±0.05 mm. The design addresses backlash, fragility, backdrivability, and grip strength using a high-precision linkage transmission and MIT force-position hybrid control. At the event, GENTO demonstrated the hand on the GENTO Luna wheeled humanoid and the TIANJI Marvin Pro dual-arm platform, which was also featured in NVIDIA's GR00T 2 demonstrations introduced by Jim Fan on Nov. 9.

总结：天机智能孵化的独立人形机器人品牌GENTO在奥斯汀的机器人学习会议上首次展示了其21自由度灵巧手原型。 该手不含电缆重750 g，尺寸为200 × 96 × 45 mm，最大抓取直径100 mm，额定指尖力10 N，最小开合时间0.5秒，重复定位精度±0.05 mm。 该设计通过高精度低间隙连杆传动和MIT力位混合控制，解决了反向间隙、抗冲击、可反向驱动及抓握力等问题。 在展会上，GENTO将其展示在GENTO Luna轮式人形机器人和TIANJI Marvin Pro双臂平台上，该平台亦在11月9日由Jim Fan介绍的NVIDIA GR00T 2演示中亮相。

에이빙 — CES 2027: Osenze Technology's LightSkin, Optical Tactile Sensing for Robots

Osenze Technology Limited was named a 2027 Honoree in Robotics at the CES 2027 Innovation Awards for LightSkin, a high-resolution optical tactile sensing solution. LightSkin provides robots with a human-like sense of touch by integrating thin, flexible tactile sensor modules with advanced optoelectronic sensing and real-time signal processing to measure contact forces, pressure distribution, and slip precisely. The solution is designed for robotic hands and body surfaces to support dexterous manipulation, safe human-robot interaction, and Physical AI applications. The CES Innovation Awards program recognizes outstanding design and engineering in consumer technology, evaluated by industry experts and global media based on engineering, functionality, aesthetics, design, and innovation.

总结：Osenze Technology Limited凭借高分辨率光学触觉传感解决方案LightSkin荣获CES 2027创新奖机器人领域的2027年度荣誉奖。 LightSkin通过将薄型柔性触觉传感器模块与先进光电传感及实时信号处理相结合，为机器人提供类人的触觉，能够精确测量接触力、压力分布和滑移。 该方案旨在无缝集成到机器人手部和身体表面，以支持灵巧操作、安全的人机交互及具身AI应用。 CES创新奖项目旨在表彰消费技术产品中的杰出设计与工程，由行业专家和全球媒体评审团根据工程与功能、美学与设计以及独创性与创新性对每个产品进行评分。

Unite.AI — ROBOTERA Opens Orders for XHAND 1 Pro-U Dexterous Robot Hand

ROBOTERA announced the commercial launch of its XHAND 1 Pro-U five-finger dexterous robot hand on November 16, 2026, opening orders for an initial 40-unit release. The hand is designed around NVIDIA GEAR Lab's DexUMI exoskeleton to allow direct mapping of human demonstrations without requiring a robot. The DexUMI hardware records human movements, wrist-camera video, and fingertip forces, which are used to post-train GR00T 2 for a dual-arm Tianji robot fitted with two XHAND1 Pro-U hands. In NVIDIA's evaluations across banana-yogurt preparation, granola scooping, and fire chemistry, GR00T 2 achieved success rates of 60%, 80%, and 75% respectively, while GR00T 1.7 and pi0.5 completed none of the tasks.

总结：ROBOTERA于2026年11月16日宣布推出XHAND 1 Pro-U五指灵巧机器人手，并为其首批40台发布开启订购。 该产品围绕英伟达GEAR实验室的DexUMI外骨骼进行设计，使人类示范动作能够直接映射到机器人手关节上。 DexUMI硬件可记录人类手部动作、腕部相机视频及指尖受力，用于对搭载两只XHAND1 Pro-U手的双臂Tianji机器人上的GR00T 2进行后训练。 在英伟达进行的香蕉酸奶制作、麦片舀取和消防化学三项评估中，GR00T 2的总体成功率分别为60%、80%和75%，而GR00T 1.7和pi0.5未完成任何任务。
