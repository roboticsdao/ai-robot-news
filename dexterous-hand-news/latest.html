Superpower Daily — Sundae Robotics 11: Beyond the Gripper — Teaching Robots Dexterity Through Touch, Vision, and Play

At Sundae Robotics 11, Akash Sharma, an Amazon Frontier AI & Robotics Research Scientist and CMU Robotics Institute PhD, presented four approaches to advance robot dexterity through touch, vision, human demonstrations, and autonomous play. The presentation covered PTLD for learning tactile policies without tactile simulation, INVAR for visual invariance transfer from simulation, CARD for touch-based demonstration retargeting, and DSP for turning human play into goal-conditioned dexterity using world models. Akash works at Amazon Frontier AI & Robotics with Jitendra Malik, received his PhD from Carnegie Mellon University advised by Prof. Michael Kaess, and previously was a visiting researcher with Meta FAIR Robotics.

总结：在 Sundae Robotics 11 活动中，Amazon Frontier AI & Robotics 研究科学家兼卡耐基梅隆大学机器人研究所博士 Akash Sharma 介绍了通过触觉、视觉、人类示范和自主玩耍推进机器人灵巧操作的四种方法。 演讲涵盖了无需触觉模拟即可学习触觉策略的 PTLD、从模拟中进行视觉不变性迁移的 INVAR、基于接触部位而非纯粹运动轨迹重定向人类示范的 CARD，以及利用世界模型将人类玩耍转化为目标条件化灵巧性的 DSP。 Akash 目前在 Amazon Frontier AI & Robotics 与 Jitendra Malik 共事，其博士学位由卡耐基梅隆大学授予，导师为 Michael Kaess 教授，此前他曾担任 Meta FAIR Robotics 的访问研究员。

Robotics & Automation News — Schunk spin-off Tactilia Robotics launches to develop dexterous robot hands

Schunk has renamed its humanoid robotics spin-off to Tactilia Robotics, establishing a Munich-based company focused on developing modular robotic hands for industrial automation. Founded in January 2026 as Schunk Humanoid Robotics GmbH, the company designs robotic hands for manufacturing and logistics to handle various objects across humanoid robots, collaborative robots, and mobile manipulators. Dr. Markus Netzer became CEO on November 1, 2026, while Timo Gessmann serves as CTO. Tactilia operates independently in Munich's Arabellapark district, leveraging Schunk's automation expertise and network of 15 CoLabs worldwide.

总结：Schunk 将其人形机器人衍生公司更名为 Tactilia Robotics，在慕尼黑成立了一家专注于为工业自动化应用开发模块化机器人手的公司。 该公司最初于 2026 年 1 月以 Schunk Humanoid Robotics GmbH 的名义成立，致力于设计适用于制造和物流环境的机器人手，以处理人形机器人、协作机器人和移动操作臂上的各种物体和组件。 Dr. Markus Netzer 于 2026 年 11 月 1 日起担任首席执行官，Timo Gessmann 担任首席技术官。 Tactilia 在慕尼黑的 Arabellapark 地区独立运营，并依托 Schunk 在抓取和自动化技术方面的专业知识以及全球 15 个 CoLabs 的网络。
