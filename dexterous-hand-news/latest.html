搜狐网 — 中科大团队用一层梯度网格，撬动触觉传感器的“不可能三角”| Science Advances

总结：中国科学技术大学工程科学学院人形机器人研究院牵头，联合新南威尔士大学、苏州大学、伍伦贡大学、西北工业大学研究团队，在触觉传感器研究上取得了新突破。 针对人形机器人视觉能定位物体但无法感知软硬和分辨轻重的痛点，研究团队跳出了修改凝胶或电极等核心功能材料的传统思路，选择在电极与离子凝胶中间新增一层直写打印的梯度网格夹层。 该梯度网格夹层实现了渐进式接触调控，能够同时兼顾高灵敏度与宽线性量程，相关研究成果已发表于机器人权威期刊《Science Advances》。

Gasgoo — SynDexy dexterous manipulation humanoid robot, completes first small-batch delivery

SynDexy announced on October 30 that its in-house dual-arm wheeled humanoid robot, the SynDexy C1, has completed its first small-batch delivery to precision assembly lines at consumer electronics and component factories. In prior testing, the C1 achieved a success rate above 98% on critical tasks. The C1 is built for dexterous manipulation with vision-tactile fusion at its core, featuring in-house vision-tactile sensors with micron-level spatial resolution and running the SynDexy M1 vision-tactile-force tri-state fusion model. The robot includes 22 degrees of freedom and 1-millisecond real-time motion control. Development of the C1 began in April 2026, with a first-generation prototype ready by July and small-batch deliveries following in October. Founded in 2025 by Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications Professor Fang Bin, SynDexy is a full-stack embodied AI company focused on high-precision industrial dexterity, with technology already deployed in tasks like flexible circuit assembly and cable insertion.

总结：SynDexy于10月30日宣布，其自主研发的双臂轮式人形机器人SynDexy C1已完成首批小批量交付，送往消费电子和零部件工厂的精密组装线。 在先前的测试中，C1在关键任务上的成功率超过98%。 C1是SynDexy首款专为灵巧操作而构建的人形机器人，核心采用视触觉融合技术，硬件上配备具有微米级空间分辨率的自研视触觉传感器，软件上运行被称为全球首个视触觉力三态融合模型的SynDexy M1。 该机器人拥有22个自由度和1毫秒实时运动控制能力。 C1从2026年4月开始研发，7月制成第一代原型机，10月实现小批量交付。 SynDexy成立于2025年，由北京邮电大学教授方斌领导，是一家专注于高精度工业灵巧性的全栈具身智能公司，其技术已部署于柔性电路板组装和线缆插接等任务中。

Tech Briefs — Color-Changing Tactile Sensor Allows Robots to “See” and Touch in Real-Time

Engineers at Queen Mary University of London, led by postdoctoral researcher Giacomo Sasso and collaborating with professors including James Bustfield, Federico Carpi and researchers from Italian universities, have developed a color-changing tactile sensor. The device transforms invisible mechanical forces and pressure into dynamic color patterns that can be read immediately by a standard low-cost USB camera, eliminating the need for complex reconstruction algorithms. This technology allows robotic grippers to handle micro-scale components, aids external prosthetics in healthcare, and helps surgical systems distinguish healthy from abnormal tissue directly through color response.

总结：伦敦玛丽女王大学的工程师 Giacomo Sasso 与詹姆斯·巴斯特菲尔德教授、费德里科·卡尔皮教授及意大利多所大学的研究人员合作，开发了一种变色触觉传感器。 该装置将不可见的机械力和压力转化为动态色彩图案，可通过标准的低成本 USB 摄像头立即读取，从而无需复杂的重建算法。 这项技术使机器人抓手能够处理微型组件，帮助医疗领域的外部假肢获得更丰富的触觉，并协助外科系统直接通过颜色响应来区分健康组织与异常组织。
