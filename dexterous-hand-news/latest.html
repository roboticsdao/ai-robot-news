The Manila Times — Tianji Intelligence Debuts 21-DoF Dexterous Hand at CoRL 2026 as NVIDIA Demos GR00T 2 on Custom Marvin Pro

At Booth 602 during CoRL 2026 in Austin, Texas, Tianji Intelligence's embodied robotics brand GENTO is showcasing its in-house 21-degree-of-freedom dexterous hand for the first time alongside its Luna and Marvin Pro robots. The prototype hand features a linkage-driven design with five active degrees of freedom in the thumb and four in each other finger, weighing 750 g excluding cables, measuring 200 × 96 × 45 mm, delivering a rated fingertip force of 10 N, and operating at DC 36-60 V. Concurrently, NVIDIA introduced its GR00T 2 world action model at the conference, using a custom Marvin Pro dual-arm platform built by Tianji Intelligence to demonstrate two-handed tasks like preparing banana yogurt.

总结：在得克萨斯州奥斯汀举行的CoRL 2026会议上，天机智能旗下的具身机器人品牌GENTO首次公开展示了其自主研发的21自由度灵巧手，并同台展出了Luna和Marvin Pro机器人。 该灵巧手原型采用连杆驱动设计，拇指拥有5个主动自由度，其余每指4个，重量为750克（不含线缆），尺寸为200×96×45毫米，额定指尖力为10牛，工作电压为直流36-60伏。 与此同时，NVIDIA在会上推出了GR00T 2世界动作模型，并使用由天机智能为其定制的Marvin Pro双臂机器人平台演示了制作香蕉酸奶等双手操作任务。

The Manila Times — Xynova's Prima 1 Robotic Hand Named a CES 2027 Innovation Awards® Honoree

Xynova announced that Prima 1, its fully direct-drive dexterous robotic hand with 22 degrees of freedom, has been named a CES 2027 Innovation Awards Honoree in the Artificial Intelligence category. Designed for embodied AI research and scalable deployment, Prima 1 integrates responsive actuation, high force transparency, and five-finger visuo-tactile sensing to support imitation learning, teleoperation, and Sim-to-Real policy transfer. Founder and CEO Alex Xia stated that dexterous manipulation enables humanoid robots to move beyond basic tasks and interact with the physical world. Headquartered in Hangzhou, China, with a 5,400-square-meter manufacturing facility, Xynova is exhibiting at CoRL and Web Summit, and will showcase Prima 1 alongside its hybrid-drive Flex 2 hand at CES 2027.

总结：晓多科技宣布，其拥有22自由度的全直驱灵巧仿生手Prima 1荣获CES 2027创新奖人工智能类别的荣誉奖。 Prima 1专为具身智能研究和可扩展部署而构建，集成了响应式驱动、高力透明度以及五指视触觉传感，支持机器人模仿学习、遥操作和仿真到真实策略迁移。 创始人兼首席执行官夏超表示，灵巧操作将使人形机器人超越基本任务并与物理世界进行有意义的交互。 晓多科技总部位于中国杭州，拥有5400平方米的制造设施，目前正在参加CoRL和Web Summit展会，并计划在CES 2027上展示Prima 1及混动驱动灵巧手Flex 2。

alphaXiv — Learning Heterogeneous Tactile Representation for General Dexterous Manipulation

Tactile sensing is crucial for contact-rich dexterous manipulation in embodied intelligence. To address the challenge of leveraging heterogeneous tactile data, the authors developed LingBot-Touch, a tactile foundation model. It yields a unified representation across piezoresistive, magnetic, and visuotactile sensors, producing plug-and-play tactile tokens that integrate into policy models like diffusion policy and vision-language-action models to enhance dexterity. Experiments in simulation and real-robot deployments show the representation benefits complex manipulation tasks while maintaining computational efficiency. The pretraining corpus pools 4,983 hours, 464,431 episodes, and about 1.33 billion frames from human tactile gloves and teleoperated Sharpa Wave hands across 24 task categories without fine-grained annotations. It uses a shared Haptic Transformer and Tactile-JEPA training with a masking ratio rising from 40% to 80% over the first 300 steps.

总结：触觉感知对于具身智能中接触丰富的灵巧操作至关重要。 为了解决利用异构触觉数据的挑战，作者开发了触觉基础模型LingBot-Touch。 该模型在压阻式、磁触觉和视触觉传感器之间产生统一的表征，生成可插入扩散策略和视觉语言动作模型等策略模型的即插即用触觉token，以增强灵巧度。 仿真和真实机器人部署的实验表明，该表征在保持计算效率的同时有利于复杂的操控任务。 预训练语料库汇集了来自人类触觉手套和远程操作Sharpa Wave手在24个任务类别中的4,983小时、464,431个episode以及约13.3亿帧数据，且没有细粒度注释。 它使用共享的Haptic Transformer和Tactile-JEPA训练，掩码比例在前300步内从40%上升到80%。
