Sohu — 蚂蚁灵波开源触觉模型LingBot-Touch，尝试机器人手感跨硬件复用

总结：蚂蚁灵波于11月10日开源触觉基础模型LingBot-Touch，尝试用统一触觉表征解决机器人手感难以跨硬件复用的问题。 该模型基于近5000小时真实触觉数据预训练，数据来自触觉手套与机器人手，覆盖磁触觉、视触觉和压阻式三类主流传感器，技术报告、模型和代码已在Hugging Face、GitHub和魔搭社区开放下载。 针对不同触觉传感器原始信号形态差异很大的问题，LingBot-Touch按传感原理分别设置编码入口，先将异构信号各自处理，再把不同布局的触点映射到统一的指尖坐标空间，输出统一的触觉Token。

The Manila Times — Tianji Intelligence Debuts 21-DoF Dexterous Hand at CoRL 2026 as NVIDIA Demos GR00T 2 on Custom Marvin Pro

Tianji Intelligence's embodied robotics brand, GENTO, is unveiling its in-house 21-degree-of-freedom dexterous hand at Booth 602 at CoRL 2026. At the same conference, NVIDIA introduced its GR00T 2 world action model and used the Marvin Pro dual-arm robotic platform, custom-built for NVIDIA by Tianji Intelligence, in demonstrations of two-handed manipulation. GENTO is also showcasing Luna and Marvin Pro with teleoperation and voice-directed grasping demonstrations. The 21-DoF hand prototype features five active degrees of freedom in the thumb and four in each other finger, combining linkage-driven design, force and position hybrid control, a weight of 750 g excluding cables, 200 × 96 × 45 mm dimensions, 100 mm maximum grasp diameter, 10 N rated fingertip force, 0.5 seconds minimum opening and closing time, repeat positioning accuracy of ±0.05 mm, and a no-load grasping life of one million cycles. GENTO's core force-controlled arm platforms can detect torque as low as 0.02 N•m and have a torque-loop bandwidth of 160 Hz. Meanwhile, NVIDIA's Jim Fan introduced GR00T 2 in his keynote, running it on the custom Marvin Pro equipped with two dexterous hands for tasks such as preparing banana yogurt.

总结：天机智能旗下的具身机器人品牌GENTO在CoRL 2026的602展位首次公开展示其自主研发的21自由度灵巧手。 在同一会议上，英伟达介绍了其GR00T 2世界动作 model，并使用由天机智能为英伟达定制的Marvin Pro双臂机器人平台进行了双手操作演示。 GENTO还展示了Luna和Marvin Pro，分别进行远程操作和语音抓取演示。 该21自由度灵巧手原型在拇指上有5个主动自由度，其余每根手指上有4个，结合了连杆驱动设计、力位混合控制，不含电缆重750克，尺寸为200×96×45毫米，最大抓握直径100毫米，额定指尖力10牛，最小开合时间0.5秒，重复定位精度为正负0.05毫米，额定无负载抓握寿命为100万次。 天机智能的核心力控手臂平台可检测低至0.02牛·米的扭矩，扭矩环带宽为160赫兹。 同时，英伟达的Jim Fan在主题演讲中介绍了GR00T 2，并将其运行在配备两只灵巧手的定制Marvin Pro上，执行制作香蕉酸奶等双手任务。

The Manila Times — Xynova's Prima 1 Robotic Hand Named a CES 2027 Innovation Awards® Honoree

Xynova announced on November 10, 2026, in Hangzhou, China, that Prima 1, its fully direct-drive dexterous robotic hand with 22 degrees of freedom, has been named a CES 2027 Innovation Awards Honoree in the Artificial Intelligence category. Prima 1 combines responsive actuation, high force transparency, and five-finger visuo-tactile sensing to provide fine-grained control and sensory feedback for embodied AI research and scalable deployment. Its fully direct-drive architecture delivers millisecond-level response and precise force control, supported by advanced thermal management and durability for continuous operation. Designed as an open platform, Prima 1 supports imitation learning, teleoperation, and Sim-to-Real policy transfer with SDK access. Founder and CEO Alex Xia noted that dexterous manipulation enables humanoid robots to interact meaningfully with the physical world. Xynova is exhibiting at CoRL at Booth 604, Web Summit in Lisbon at Booth E109, and will showcase Prima 1 alongside the Flex 2 hybrid-drive dexterous hand at Booth 12918 in the North Hall of the Las Vegas Convention Center during CES 2027 from January 6-9. Headquartered in Hangzhou with a 5,400-square-meter manufacturing facility, Xynova is expanding its relationships with customers and research partners across the United States and Europe.

Robotics & Automation News — Nature Will launches 50-billion-parameter AI model for dexterous robot hands

Nature Will, a physical AI lab, has announced the launch of Natura-1, a 50-billion-parameter physical general foundation model designed for dexterous hand control and zero-shot instruction following in advanced robotics applications. Pre-trained on over 1 million hours of egocentric human data, Natura-1 combines instruction following, manipulation under changing task conditions, and sustained actions across extended interactions. Built as a mixture-of-experts model trained jointly on language, images, video, and action, it operates at a 100-millisecond control loop to connect language-directed behavior with physical control. Ning Ding, CEO of Nature Will, states that general models handling every task must utilize dexterous hands, and that Natura-1 serves as the first step towards scaling human experience into purposeful action while reasoning through spatial constraints, multi-object relationships, and negative conditions.

总结：专注于构建物理通用人工智能的物理AI实验室Nature Will宣布推出Natura-1，这是一个拥有500亿参数的物理通用基础模型，用于先进机器人应用中的灵巧手控制和零样本指令遵循。 该模型在超过100万小时的第一视角人类数据上进行了预训练，将指令遵循与适应多变任务条件及长交互过程中的持续动作能力结合起来。 Natura-1构建于一个混合专家模型之上，联合训练了语言、图像、视频和动作，以100毫秒的控制循环运行，将语言指导的行为与物理控制统一起来。 Nature Will首席执行官Ning Ding表示，能够处理各项任务的通用模型必须利用灵巧手，而Natura-1是迈向将人类经验扩展为有目的动作的第一步，使其能够在空间约束、多物体关系和否定条件下进行推理。
