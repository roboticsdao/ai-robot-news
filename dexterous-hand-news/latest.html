Unite.AI — ROBOTERA Opens Orders for XHAND 1 Pro-U Dexterous Robot Hand

ROBOTERA announced the commercial launch and open orders for its XHAND 1 Pro-U five-finger dexterous robot hand on November 16, 2026, releasing an initial 40 units of the 16-degree-of-freedom hand. The design mirrors NVIDIA's DexUMI exoskeleton, mapped directly to robot joints to allow human demonstrations to train GR00T 2 without additional teleoperation. Evaluated across three bimanual tasks—banana-yogurt preparation, granola scooping, and fire chemistry—GR00T 2 achieved success rates of 60%, 80%, and 75% respectively, whereas GR00T 1.7 and pi0.5 completed none of the tasks. NVIDIA and ROBOTERA's open licensing of capture designs and supply of execution hardware provide research teams a direct method to collect force-aware data.

总结：ROBOTERA于2026年11月16日宣布推出XHAND 1 Pro-U五指灵巧手并开放首批40只的手部订单。 该16自由度灵巧手采用NVIDIA的DexUMI外骨骼设计，实现一对一关节映射，使人类示范动作可直接用于训练GR00T 2。 在香蕉酸奶制作、格兰诺拉麦片舀取和消防化学三项双手任务评估中，GR00T 2的成功率分别为60%、80%和75%，而GR00T 1.7和pi0.5未完成任何任务。 开源的采集硬件设计与商业灵巧手相结合，为研究团队提供了收集力觉感知数据的途径。
