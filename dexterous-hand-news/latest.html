Robotics & Automation News — Tesla patent reveals touch-sensitive skin for robot hands

Tesla has filed a US patent application, US 2026/0310299 A1, published on October 8, for a touch-sensitive skin that can be molded around robot fingers and palms to detect contact, force, and pressure. The sensor is manufactured by printing tactile sensors onto flat, flexible materials before heat-forming them into three-dimensional shapes. The design uses two flexible substrate layers carrying conductive regions, separated by a compressible layer. Tesla’s proposed manufacturing processes include screen printing, laser cutting, conductive paste deposition, and thermoforming. Potential substrate materials include thermoplastic polyurethane, PET, and polyimide. While the application does not state the technology will be used in the Optimus humanoid robot, it identifies robot fingertips, palms, and end-effectors among potential applications.

总结：特斯拉提交了一项美国专利申请，即于10月8日公布的US 2026/0310299 A1，内容涉及一种可围绕机器人手指和手掌模塑的触敏“皮肤”，用于检测接触、力和压力。 该传感器的制造工艺是将触觉传感器印刷在扁平的柔性材料上，然后热成型为三维形状。 该设计使用两个带有导电区域的柔性基板层，中间由可压缩层隔开。 特斯拉提出的制造工艺包括丝网印刷、激光切割、导电浆料沉积和热成型。 可能的基板材料包括热塑性聚氨酯、PET和聚酰亚胺。 虽然该申请并未明确说明该技术将用于特斯拉的Optimus人形机器人，但它将机器人指尖、手掌和末端执行器列为潜在应用场景。

Robotics & Automation News — ETH Zurich scientists turn WUJI robotic hand into self-propelled mobile manipulator

Scientists at ETH Zurich demonstrated an anthropomorphic robotic hand from WUJI that uses its fingers for both manipulation and locomotion, enabling it to crawl, recover from falls, and move without a separate mobility system. The research was detailed in a paper by Amirhossein Kazemipour, Hehui Zheng, and Robert Katzschmann. Using reinforcement learning developed in simulation, the hand supports its own weight and performs tasks such as pressing keyboard keys and pushing objects. The system incorporates onboard power and computation. WUJI also develops the WUJI Hand 2 and WUJI Glove for real-time teleoperation and data collection, and provides native device support for Nvidia Isaac Teleop.

总结：苏黎世联邦理工学院的科学家展示了一种来自WUJI的人形机器人手，该手能够利用其手指进行操作和运动，使其能够在没有独立移动系统的情况下爬行、从跌落中恢复并四处移动。 该研究由Amirhossein Kazemipour、Hehui Zheng和Robert Katzschmann撰写的论文进行了详细介绍。 通过在仿真中开发的强化学习，该手能够支撑自身重量并执行诸如按压键盘按键和推动物体等任务。 该系统集成了车载电源和计算能力。 WUJI还开发了WUJI Hand 2和WUJI Glove用于实时遥操作和数据收集，并为Nvidia Isaac Teleop提供本地设备支持。

36Kr — Embodied Intelligence Paradigm Shift: Why Dexterous Hands Are Moving Beyond Freedom Degree Competition – Brain Lag & Cost Control Challenges

Embodied intelligence is experiencing a critical shift from parameter competition to pragmatism as the humanoid robot industry focuses on scenario-based and large-scale implementation. Enterprises have significantly slowed down the pursuit of ultra-high degrees of freedom. While companies previously developed dexterous hands with massive DoF exceeding the human hand—such as Yuequan Bionics' Y-Hand M2 with 38 DoF, Zhongke Huisi's F-series with 37 DoF, and Lingxin Qiaoshou's LinkerHand with 42 DoF—recent designs have scaled back, with Boston Dynamics introducing a 4-finger, 13-DoF hand for Atlas and Unitree Robotics releasing the Dex5-S with 22 DoF. Industry experts note that when degrees of freedom exceed 25, marginal effects decrease while costs rise exponentially. High-DoF hands require extensive motor and sensor integration, creating heat dissipation, energy consumption, and high manufacturing cost challenges, with prices ranging from tens of thousands of yuan to millions. Furthermore, software limitations, such as existing VLA models outputting only about 10 motion control vectors per timestamp, restrict the capabilities of high-DoF hardware and necessitate remote control for precise movements.

总结：具身智能行业正经历从参数竞争向实用主义的关键转变，人形机器人领域更加关注场景化和规模化落地。 企业已明显放缓对超高自由度的盲目追求。 此前多家企业曾开发远超人手自由度的灵巧手，例如泉源仿生开发的38自由度Y-Hand M2、中科慧思的37自由度F系列以及灵心巧手的42自由度LinkerHand；然而近期设计有所精简，波士顿动力为Atlas推出了4指13自由度灵巧手，宇树科技则发布了具备22自由度的Dex5-S。 行业专家指出，当自由度超过25时，边际效用递减而成本呈指数级上升。 高自由度手需要大量电机与传感器集成，带来了散热、能耗和高昂制造成本等挑战，价格从数万元到数百万元不等。 此外，软件能力受限（例如现有VLA模型在每个时间戳仅能输出约10个运动控制向量）限制了高-DoF硬件的上限，许多精确动作仍需通过遥控来实现。
