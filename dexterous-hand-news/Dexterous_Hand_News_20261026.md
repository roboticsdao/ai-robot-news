- **[2026.10.26] 36Kr — Tsinghua Research Team Builds HandsOn Dexterous Manipulation Benchmark: Comprehensive 66-Task Evaluation of Robot Bimanual Capabilities**
  English: A research team led by Ning Ding from Tsinghua University, alongside researchers from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications, Peking University, and Nature Will, developed a composable simulation platform and evaluation benchmark named HandsOn for cross-embodiment dexterous manipulation. The platform addresses the lack of systematic evaluation systems for dexterous hands, which involve complex contact interactions, bimanual collaboration, and long action sequences. HandsOn features 66 human-validated tasks divided into Core-50 and Transfer-16, along with 18,000 simulation teleoperation demonstrations, supporting 5 robotic arms, 6 dexterous hands, and about 200 assets. Evaluations of different technical routes revealed that the best-performing trained multi-task policy on Core-50 achieved a 29.1% success rate, while the average success rate on Transfer-16 across 8 policies was below 1%. Furthermore, GPT-6 achieved success rates of 5.2% on Core-50 and 1.9% on Transfer-16 in 660 tests. The team found that robots often fail at critical moments in the second half of action sequences, and they have made papers, codes, and data publicly available.
  中文：总结：清华大学丁宁团队联合北京邮电大学、北京大学以及Nature Will的研究人员，共同构建了用于跨具身灵巧操作的可组合仿真平台与评估基准HandsOn。 该平台包含66个经人类验证的任务（分为Core-50和Transfer-16）以及18000条仿真遥操作演示记录，支持5种机械臂、6种灵巧手和约200种资产。 评估结果显示，在提供训练演示的Core-50任务中，表现最好的多任务策略成功率为29.1%；在没有目标任务训练演示的Transfer-16任务中，8个训练有多任务策略的平均成功率低于1%。 此外，GPT-6在Core-50和Transfer-16上的成功率分别为5.2%和1.9%。 相关论文、代码和数据资源已公开。

- **[2026.10.23] Vietnam.vn — Why don't robotic hands look like human hands?**
  English: Robotic hands remain difficult to develop because hands require complex integration of tactile senses, grip strength, and real-world adaptability. While humanoid robots can perform predictable acrobatics like somersaults, unpredictable housework such as folding a shirt requires simultaneous coordination of hands, arms, torso, vision, and the surrounding environment. Developing a near-human robotic hand presents major challenges across mechanics, sensors, and learning capabilities. Mechanically, the human hand possesses 27 bones, 34 muscles, and over 17,000 receptors. Replicating this requires fitting actuators and sensors into limited spaces, balancing power, torque, precision, and response speed while managing friction and heat. Cable-driven systems reduce hand size by placing motors in the forearm, but introduce friction and flexibility challenges. Tactilely, biological receptors provide instant data on pressure, vibration, slippage, and texture, allowing real-time slip detection and gripping adjustments, which is an engineering hurdle for robots. Regarding data and learning, collecting sophisticated manipulation data is much harder than image data. Various companies are approaching these challenges, including Unittree with the G1 robot featuring force-controlled hands and tactile sensors, Apptronik with Apollo, Tesla with Optimus, and 1X focusing on household chores. Despite laboratory success, bridging the gap to reliable real-world performance in unfamiliar situations remains ongoing.
  中文：总结：机器人手难以开发的原因在于手部集成了复杂的触觉、握力以及对真实世界的适应能力。 人形机器人虽然能完成空翻等可预测的杂技动作，但折衬衫等不可预测的家务需要双手、手臂、躯干、视觉系统及周围环境的协调。 开发类人机器人手需要在机械、传感器和学习能力三大挑战上取得平衡。 在机械方面，人手拥有27块骨骼、34块肌肉和17000多个感受器，工程师必须在极小空间内塞入致动器与传感器，权衡动力、扭矩、精度与反应速度。 线驱动系统虽能减小手部尺寸，但带来了摩擦力和灵活性问题。 在触觉方面，生物感受器提供压力、振动、滑移和质地的实时数据以调整抓握，这在机器人工程中是一个复杂难题。 在数据与学习方面，收集复杂操作数据的难度远大于图像数据。 Unittree、Apptronik、特斯拉和1X等公司正从不同角度切入研发，但要在真实家庭环境中可靠地应对不熟悉的情况，机器人仍需克服从测试条件到实际应用之间的巨大差距。

- **[2026.10.22] Robotics & Automation News — Robotiq launches the 2FC physical AI gripper**
  English: Robotiq has launched the 2FC physical AI gripper, a production-ready adaptive gripper featuring tactile sensing and embedded intelligence. Pre-orders are open, with shipping scheduled for Q1 2027. The gripper addresses the industry-wide challenge of turning raw tactile data into useful training data and real-time behavior. Built on Robotiq's 2F adaptive gripper platform, which has more than 30,000 units deployed worldwide, the 2FC integrates tactile sensors, compute at the wrist via the AI Hub, and Contact Core software for physical AI. It captures synchronized pressure, vibration, proprioception, force, position, and current data at the millisecond level. The onboard compute runs grasp skills locally and in real time, including quality assessment, incipient slip detection, object pose estimation, object centering, and grip check. Rated for 2 million cycles, the 2FC is available in 85 mm (2FC-85) and 140 mm (2FC-140) stroke versions, and existing 2F owners can upgrade using TSF tactile sensors and the AI Hub.
  中文：总结：Robotiq发布了2FC物理AI抓手，这是一款具备触觉感知和嵌入式智能的量产型自适应抓手，现已开放预订，并将于2027年第一季度开始发货。 该抓手旨在解决将原始触觉数据转化为有用训练数据和实时行为的行业挑战。 2FC建立在Robotiq的2F自适应抓手平台基础之上，该平台在全球已部署超过3万台，新产品集成了触觉传感器、通过AI Hub实现的腕部计算以及用于物理AI的Contact Core软件。 它能以毫秒级捕获同步的压力、振动、本体感觉、力、位置和电流数据。 其板载计算可本地实时运行抓取技能，包括抓取质量评估、初期滑移检测、物体位姿估计、物体居中和抓握检查。 2FC额定寿命为200万次循环，推出85毫米（2FC-85）和140毫米（2FC-140）行程版本，现有的2F用户可以通过添加TSF触觉传感器和AI Hub进行升级。
