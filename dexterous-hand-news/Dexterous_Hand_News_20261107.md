- **[2026.10.30] Tech Briefs — Color-Changing Tactile Sensor Allows Robots to “See” and Touch in Real-Time**
  English: Engineers at Queen Mary University of London have developed a new color-changing tactile sensor invented by postdoctoral researcher Giacomo Sasso. The sensor transforms invisible forces into dynamic color patterns, allowing robots to see and touch in real-time by capturing spatially varying structural colors on a soft sensing surface with a standard camera. This removes the need for complex reconstruction algorithms. The technology can be applied in precision manufacturing for assembling micro-scale components, in healthcare for artificial limbs to achieve a richer sense of touch, and in surgical systems to distinguish healthy from abnormal tissue. Unlike traditional tactile sensors, this system embeds sensing directly into the material itself, converting mechanical interactions into color fields read by a low-cost USB camera. Researchers from the University of Florence, University of Trieste, and University of Trento also collaborated on the project, addressing the trade-off between high resolution and speed in vision-based tactile sensing.
  中文：总结：伦敦玛丽女王大学的工程师开发了一种新型变色触觉传感器，由该校材料科学与工程学院的博士后研究员Giacomo Sasso发明。 该传感器通过将看不见的力转化为动态颜色图案，使机器人能够实时观察和触摸；当压力施加到柔软的传感表面上时，材料会产生空间变化的结构色，可用标准摄像头立即捕捉，从而消除了复杂重建算法的需求。 这项技术可应用于微型元件组装的精密制造、为假肢提供更丰富触觉的医疗保健领域，以及帮助外科系统区分健康组织与异常组织的手术系统。 与传统触觉传感器不同，该系统将传感直接嵌入材料本身，将机械相互作用转化为可由低成本USB摄像头读取的色彩场。 来自意大利佛罗伦萨大学、的里雅斯特大学和特伦托大学的研究人员也参与了该项目，解决了基于视觉的触觉传感中高分辨率与速度之间的权衡问题。
