- **[2026.10.16] Robotics & Automation News — Tesla patent reveals touch-sensitive skin for robot hands**
  English: Tesla has filed a US patent application, published on October 8 as US 2026/0310299 A1, for a touch-sensitive skin that can be molded around robot fingers and palms to detect contact, force, and pressure. The sensor is manufactured by printing tactile sensors onto flat, flexible materials before heat-forming them into 3D shapes, using processes such as screen printing, laser cutting, conductive paste deposition, and thermoforming. The design features two flexible substrate layers carrying conductive regions separated by a compressible layer, utilizing materials like thermoplastic polyurethane, PET, and polyimide to operate via capacitive and piezoresistive approaches. While the patent does not specify its use in the Optimus humanoid robot, it lists fingertips, palms, and end-effectors as potential applications to provide robots with more sophisticated manipulation capabilities.
  中文：总结：Tesla已提交一项美国专利申请，于10月8日公布，编号为US 2026/0310299 A1，内容涉及一种可塑形环绕在机器人手指和手掌周围的触觉敏感“皮肤”，用于检测接触、力和压力。 该传感器采用制造工艺，将触觉传感器印刷到扁平的柔性材料上，随后热成型为三维形状，涉及丝网印刷、激光切割、导电浆料沉积和热成型等工艺。 其设计包含两个带有导电区域并通过可压缩层分隔的柔性基板层，使用热塑性聚氨酯、PET和聚酰亚胺等基板材料，通过电容和压阻方法运行。 虽然该专利未明确指出该技术将用于Tesla的Optimus人形机器人，但它将机器人指尖、手掌和末端执行器列为潜在应用，旨在为机器人提供更复杂的操控能力。

- **[2026.10.16] Robotics & Automation News — ETH Zurich scientists turn WUJI robotic hand into self-propelled mobile manipulator**
  English: Scientists at ETH Zurich have demonstrated a robotic hand from WUJI that uses its fingers for self-supported locomotion and manipulation without a separate mobility mechanism. Presented in a paper by Amirhossein Kazemipour, Hehui Zheng, and Robert Katzschmann, the system used reinforcement learning developed in simulation to achieve untethered crawling, steering, and fall recovery. The hand incorporated onboard power and computation, successfully performing tasks like pressing successive keyboard keys without vision and pushing objects toward target positions using overhead visual feedback. Additionally, WUJI demonstrated its WUJI Hand 2 and WUJI Glove at IROS 2026 in Pittsburgh, highlighting real-time teleoperation and data collection for training embodied AI models.
  中文：总结：苏黎世联邦理工学院的科学家展示了一种来自WUJI的机器人手，它利用手指进行自我支撑的运动和操作，而无需单独的移动机构。 该系统由Amirhossein Kazemipour、Hehui Zheng和Robert Katzschmann在一篇论文中提出，利用在仿真中开发的强化学习来实现无束缚的爬行、转向和跌倒恢复。 该手集成车载电源和计算能力，成功执行了无需视觉连续按压键盘按键以及利用头顶视觉反馈将物体推向目标位置等任务。 此外，WUJI在匹兹堡举行的IROS 2026上展示了WUJI Hand 2和WUJI Glove，突出了用于训练具身AI模型的实时远程操作和数据采集。
