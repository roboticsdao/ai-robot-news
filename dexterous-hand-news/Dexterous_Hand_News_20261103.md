- **[2026.10.30] Tech Briefs — Color-Changing Tactile Sensor Allows Robots to “See” and Touch in Real-Time**
  English: Engineers at Queen Mary University of London, led by postdoctoral researcher Giacomo Sasso and collaborating with professors including James Bustfield, Federico Carpi and researchers from Italian universities, have developed a color-changing tactile sensor. The device transforms invisible mechanical forces and pressure into dynamic color patterns that can be read immediately by a standard low-cost USB camera, eliminating the need for complex reconstruction algorithms. This technology allows robotic grippers to handle micro-scale components, aids external prosthetics in healthcare, and helps surgical systems distinguish healthy from abnormal tissue directly through color response.
  中文：总结：伦敦玛丽女王大学的工程师 Giacomo Sasso 与詹姆斯·巴斯特菲尔德教授、费德里科·卡尔皮教授及意大利多所大学的研究人员合作，开发了一种变色触觉传感器。 该装置将不可见的机械力和压力转化为动态色彩图案，可通过标准的低成本 USB 摄像头立即读取，从而无需复杂的重建算法。 这项技术使机器人抓手能够处理微型组件，帮助医疗领域的外部假肢获得更丰富的触觉，并协助外科系统直接通过颜色响应来区分健康组织与异常组织。
