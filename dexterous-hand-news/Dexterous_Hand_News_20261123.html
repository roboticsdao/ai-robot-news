아시아경제 — IL Signs Exclusive Korea Supply Deal with XYNOVA, Maker of the "World's First Hybrid Robotic Hand"

IL has signed an exclusive supply agreement with XYNOVA to distribute its entire lineup of robotic hands and humanoid joint actuators in South Korea. The partnership aims to expand the local industrial robotics market and adopt automation in manufacturing and logistics. Founded in late 2024, XYNOVA specializes in core robotics components and has achieved unicorn status with a post-investment valuation exceeding $1 billion as of July 2026. Its flagship product, Flex 2, is a 23-degree-of-freedom hybrid robotic hand combining wire drive and direct-drive systems with tactile, proximity, and vision sensing. IL plans to supply these hands to domestic humanoid manufacturers and system integration firms while collecting task data from industrial sites to train artificial intelligence. IL Chairman Song Seong-geun stated that physical AI leadership relies on field-generated task data to train AI and meet customer demands.

总结：物理AI数据平台公司IL与机器人核心部件制造商XYNOVA签署韩国独家供应协议，将负责XYNOVA全系列机器人灵巧手和人形关节执行器的韩国独家供应。 XYNOVA成立于2024年底，截至2026年7月其估值超过$1 billion并获得多方投资。 其旗舰产品Flex 2是采用线驱与直驱系统的23自由度混合灵巧手，结合了触觉、接近觉和视觉传感技术。 IL计划将这些灵巧手供应给韩国国内的人形机器人制造商、系统集成商和工业自动化企业，同时收集制造与物流基地的任务数据用于人工智能训练。 IL董事长Song Seong-geun表示，物理AI的核心竞争力在于利用现场生成的任务数据来训练AI并满足客户需求。

Robotics & Automation News — Schunk spin-off Tactilia Robotics launches to develop dexterous robot hands

Schunk has renamed its humanoid robotics spin-off Tactilia Robotics, establishing a Munich-based company focused on developing modular robotic hands for industrial automation. Originally founded in January 2026 as Schunk Humanoid Robotics GmbH, the company designs hands to handle various objects in manufacturing and logistics across humanoid robots, collaborative robots, and mobile manipulators. Dr. Markus Netzer became CEO on November 1, 2026, while Timo Gessmann serves as CTO. Tactilia combines mechanical engineering, sensors, actuators, software, and physical AI to enable tasks requiring human dexterity without being restricted to a specific robot manufacturer. The company operates independently while leveraging Schunk's gripping expertise and its global network of 15 CoLabs for testing. Headquartered in Munich's Arabellapark district, Tactilia's immediate priority is developing and validating reliable robotic hands for continuous industrial operation.

总结：Schunk将其人形机器人衍生公司更名为Tactilia Robotics，并在慕尼黑建立了一家专注于为工业自动化应用开发模块化机器人灵巧手的新公司。 该公司最初于2026年1月作为Schunk Humanoid Robotics GmbH创立，其开发的机器人灵巧手旨在处理制造和物流环境中的各种对象，适用于人形机器人、协作机器人和移动操作臂等不同平台。 Dr. Markus Netzer于2026年11月1日出任首席执行官，Timo Gessmann担任首席技术官。 Tactilia将机械工程、传感器、执行器、软件和物理AI相结合，以实现需要人类灵巧度的任务。 该公司总部位于慕尼黑的Arabellapark地区，其近期首要任务是开发并验证能够在工业环境中可靠运行的机器人灵巧手。

Superpower Daily — Sundae Robotics 11: Beyond the Gripper — Teaching Robots Dexterity Through Touch, Vision, and Play

Amazon Frontier AI and Robotics Research Scientist Akash Sharma presented a talk titled 'Beyond the Gripper — Teaching Robots Dexterity Through Touch, Vision, and Play' at Sundae Robotics 11. The talk addressed limitations in conventional parallel-jaw grippers and standard human demonstrations or simulations. Sharma introduced four approaches: PTLD for learning tactile policies without tactile simulation by distilling privileged simulation-trained teachers on real hardware; INVAR for learning visual invariance from simulation pairs for zero-shot transfer; CARD for retargeting human demonstrations based on touch rather than movement; and DSP for turning human play into goal-conditioned dexterity through autonomous play and world models. Akash Sharma is a Research Scientist at Amazon Frontier AI and Robotics, working with Jitendra Malik, and holds a PhD from the Carnegie Mellon University Robotics Institute.

总结：Amazon Frontier AI & Robotics研究科学家、卡耐基梅隆大学机器人研究所博士Akash Sharma在Sundae Robotics 11活动中发表了题为《Beyond the Gripper — Teaching Robots Dexterity Through Touch, Vision, and Play》的主题演讲。 演讲探讨了超越传统平行夹爪的灵巧操作方法，并指出传统人类示范和模拟的局限性。 Sharma介绍了四种方法：PTLD利用真实硬件上的特权仿真训练教师来学习触觉策略；INVAR通过仿真对学习视觉不变性以实现零样本迁移；CARD根据接触点而非运动方式来重定向人类示范；DSP通过自主玩耍和世界模型将人类玩耍转化为目标条件的灵巧性。 Akash Sharma目前在Amazon Frontier AI & Robotics担任研究科学家并拥有卡耐基梅隆大学机器人研究所博士学位。

The Guardian — ‘The doom of humanity’: will the quest to make robotic hands render us obsolete? | AI (artificial intelligence)

Amazon operates a large fulfilment centre near Luton featuring digital shelving systems and automated sorting to handle products before human workers stack them at workstations. Amazon uses guided public tours to showcase how humans and technology collaborate, where computers make judgment calls on item placement and box sizes while human workers provide the physical labor. Replicating the dexterity of the human hand remains a major challenge in robotics, as producing hands capable of tasks like packing or picking fruit is difficult. Investors and governments are heavily investing in humanoid robots, with the Chinese government pledging over £100bn in robotics over the next two decades, and Tesla shifting Model X and Model S production to focus on Optimus humanoid robots. Elon Musk noted that solving the hands problem is essential for creating a generalized humanoid. Alistair Hunter, a hand and wrist surgeon at University College London hospitals, notes that human hands contain 27 bones alongside complex nerves and muscles controlled by the brain.

总结：Amazon在Luton附近运营着一个大型物流中心，通过数字化货架系统和自动化分流来处理商品，随后由人类员工在工作站进行堆叠。 Amazon通过受控的公开参观展示人机协作，计算机负责决定商品存放位置和包装箱尺寸，而人类员工则提供双手进行操作。 在机器人领域，复制人手的高灵巧性仍然是一项重大挑战，因为制造能够执行打包或采摘等任务的机器人手极其困难。 投资者和政府正在大量投资人形机器人，中国政府已承诺在未来二十年内向机器人和高科技产业投资超过£100bn，Tesla也暂停了Model X和Model S车型的生产以专注于Optimus人形机器人。 Elon Musk指出，解决手部问题对于创造通用人形机器人至关重要。 伦敦大学学院医院的手部和腕部外科医生Alistair Hunter指出，人手包含27块骨头以及由大脑控制的复杂神经和肌肉。
