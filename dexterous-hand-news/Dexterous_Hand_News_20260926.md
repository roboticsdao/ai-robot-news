- **[2026.09.24] AZoSensors — Giving Robots a Sense of Touch: The Next Generation of Tactile Sensors**
  English: Researchers are developing advanced tactile sensors that combine pressure, vibration, proximity, temperature, and texture cues into a flexible layer to give robots a working sense of touch.
