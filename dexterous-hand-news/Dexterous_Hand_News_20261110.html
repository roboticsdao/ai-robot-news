Robotics & Automation News — Nature Will launches 50-billion-parameter AI model for dexterous robot hands

Nature Will has announced the launch of Natura-1, a 50-billion-parameter physical general foundation model designed for dexterous hand control and zero-shot instruction following in advanced robotics. Pre-trained on over 1 million hours of egocentric human data, Natura-1 combines language, vision, and high-degree-of-freedom action into a 100-millisecond control loop. The model uses a mixture-of-experts architecture, trained jointly on language, images, video, and action, enabling it to execute continuous workflows and reason through spatial constraints and multi-object relationships. CEO Ning Ding stated that the model marks a first step toward scaling human experience into purposeful physical action using real hardware.

总结：物理AI实验室Nature Will宣布推出Natura-1，这是一个拥有500亿参数的物理通用基础模型，用于先进机器人应用中的灵巧手控制和零样本指令遵循。 该模型预训练于超过100万小时的人类第一视角数据，将语言、视觉和高自由度动作结合在一个100毫秒的控制循环中。 Natura-1采用混合专家模型架构，联合训练语言、图像、视频和动作，能够自主执行持续的工作流并推理空间约束与多物体关系。 Nature Will首席执行官宁丁表示，该模型是迈向利用真实硬件将人类体验规模化为有目的物理动作的第一步。

Wowtale — Physical AI Startup RLWRLD Releases DexBench, a Benchmark for Humanoid Dexterous Manipulation

Physical AI company RLWRLD released DexBench, a benchmark evaluating dexterous manipulation by humanoid robots, built on NVIDIA's Isaac Lab-Arena framework and the Newton physics engine. Presented at NVIDIA's AI Day Seoul 2026, DexBench implements 15 tasks across retail, logistics, assembly, and hotel food and beverage settings, requiring five-finger dexterity and testing generalization across object, physics, and spatial conditions. Development partners including NdotLight, SPACE AI, and PhysicsSimLab supplied 3D assets and environments. RLWRLD plans to release code, simulation assets, training data, and baseline models measured with its RLDX 1.1 foundation model, and confirmed an investment plan announced by NVIDIA.

总结：物理AI公司RLWRLD于11月9日表示，已推出基于NVIDIA的Isaac Lab-Arena框架和Newton物理引擎构建的人形机器人灵巧操作基准测试DexBench。 DexBench在首尔COEX举行的NVIDIA AI Day Seoul 2026上发布，涵盖零售、物流、装配和酒店餐饮四个工业环境中的15项任务，要求五指灵活性并测试物体、物理和空间条件下的泛化能力。 NdotLight、SPACE AI和PhysicsSimLab等开发伙伴提供了3D资产与仿真环境。 RLWRLD计划发布代码、仿真资产、训练数据及基准模型，其初始基准结果是通过其机器人基础模型的下一次更新RLDX 1.1测量的，并确认了NVIDIA宣布的投资计划。
