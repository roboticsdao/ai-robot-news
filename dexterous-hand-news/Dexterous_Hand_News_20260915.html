PR Newswire — ShengShu Technology Unveils Motus2, a Self-Evolving General World Model for Dexterous Manipulation

ShengShu Technology Co-founder and CEO Yihang Luo unveiled Motus2, a self-evolving general world model for robotic dexterous manipulation, on September 10, 2026 at the Inclusion Conference on the Bund. Motus2 unifies action generation, consequence prediction, and outcome evaluation within a single video-action model with shared parameters. According to the team's research paper, Motus2 achieved an average success rate of 84% across five primary real-robot tasks: placing a ball, multi-finger manipulation, attaching an eraser, screwing in a light bulb, and placing a phone. Combining model-based reinforcement learning with inference-time planning increased the average success rate from 65% to 75% in a separate policy-optimization study, representing a gain of 10 percentage points based on 20 trials per task. The model is evaluated on dual-arm robot platforms equipped with WUJI, WUJI Hand 2, and Sharpa Wave dexterous hands. Motus2 uses Best-of-N planning during execution and updates action-related parameters using value scores during training. The model relies on an egocentric human dataset of approximately 130,000 hours of raw video recordings and more than 100 hours of robot-domain mid-training data.

总结：ShengShu Technology联合创始人兼CEO Yihang Luo于2026年9月10日在外滩大会上发布了Motus2，这是一个用于机器人灵巧操作的自进化通用世界模型。 Motus2将动作生成、后果预测和结果评估统一在共享参数的单一视频-动作模型中。 研究论文显示，Motus2在放置球体、多指操作、安装橡皮擦、拧入灯泡和放置手机这五个主要的真实机器人任务中，平均成功率为84%。 在另一项结合模型强化学习与推理时规划的策略优化研究中，平均成功率从65%提高到75%，提升了10个百分点，每项任务使用20次试验。 该模型在配备WUJI、WUJI Hand 2和Sharpa Wave灵巧手的双臂机器人平台上进行了评估。 Motus2在执行时使用Best-of-N规划，在训练时通过价值分数更新动作相关参数。 该模型依赖约13万小时的原始第一人称人类数据集以及超过100小时的机器人领域中级训练数据。

Interesting Engineering — Why robot hands are one of the hardest problems in humanoid robotics

Humanoid robots can perform athletic movements like backflips because the environment and movement sequences can be tightly controlled, whereas household tasks such as folding laundry, picking up a wet sponge, or turning a key involve high uncertainty as objects bend, slip, and deform. A robotic hand requires sophisticated mechanics, sensors, and control software to manage finger positions, object location, touch force, slippage, and safety limits. Vision alone cannot provide all necessary feedback, making tactile sensing increasingly important. A 2026 Science Robotics study from Zhejiang University combined visual and tactile information with reinforcement learning and online imitation learning, achieving an 85% success rate across five complex tasks involving 25 objects. Unlike factory settings with fixed trajectories and predictable lighting, real-world home environments present irregular, soft, and fragile objects, requiring bimanual coordination, stable navigation, and reachability. Chinese robotics companies, including X Square Robot, are actively testing humanoids on household chores such as picking up litter and arranging flowers, though collecting synchronized multimodal manipulation data remains a significant hurdle.

总结：人形机器人可以执行后空翻等运动，因为环境和动作序列可以被严格控制，而折叠衣物、拿起湿海绵或转动钥匙等家务活涉及高度的不确定性，因为物体会弯曲、滑动和变形。 机器人手需要复杂的机械装置、传感器和控制软件来管理手指位置、物体位置、触摸力、滑动和安全极限。 仅靠视觉无法提供所有必要的反馈，这使得触觉传感变得日益重要。 浙江大学2026年的一项《Science Robotics》研究将视觉和触觉信息与强化学习及在线模仿学习相结合，在涉及25个物体的五项复杂任务中取得了85%的成功率。 与具有固定轨迹和可预测光照的工厂环境不同，真实世界的家庭环境呈现出不规则、柔软和易碎的物体，需要双臂协调、稳定导航和足够的到达范围。 包括X Square Robot在内的中国机器人公司正在积极测试人形机器人从事捡垃圾和插花等家务，尽管收集同步的多模态操作数据仍然是一个重大障碍。
