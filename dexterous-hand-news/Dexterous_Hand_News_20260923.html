finance.biggo.com — Unitree Robotics unveils Dex5-S dexterous hand: 22 degrees of freedom starting at 39,900 yuan, humanoid robot "hand" price war heats up

Unitree Robotics unveiled its next-generation five-finger dexterous hand, the Dex5-S, on September 21. The device features 22 degrees of freedom distributed as 5 in the thumb, 4 each in the index, middle, and ring fingers, and 5 in the pinky, achieving a one-to-one correspondence between 22 motors and 22 DoF. The Dex5-S measures 102×187×26 millimeters, weighs approximately 620 grams with an aluminum alloy shell, and matches a 1:1 scale human hand. It offers a starting price of 39,900 yuan (approximately $6,000) for the Standard edition, while the Pro edition adds tactile sensing. The product supports a maximum single-load capacity of 2 kilograms and continuous operation load of 1 kilogram, and is compatible with Unitree's H1 and G1 humanoid platforms.

总结：宇树科技于9月21日正式发布新一代五指灵巧手Dex5-S，单手自由度提升至22个，标准版起售价为39,900元（约合$6,000美元）。 该产品采用1:1人手比例设计，尺寸为102×187×26毫米，铝合金外壳总重约620克。 Dex5-S实现22个电机与22个自由度的一对一对应，每个关节均可独立控制，大拇指5个、食指中指无名指各4个、小指5个。 其支持直接驱动、反驱动和双encoder控制，单手最大负载能力为2公斤，连续工作负载为1公斤。 产品分为标准版和Pro版，Pro版增加触觉感知功能，兼容宇树H1和G1人形机器人平台。

PR Newswire — ShengShu Technology Unveils Motus2, a Self-Evolving General World Model for Dexterous Manipulation

ShengShu Technology Co-founder and CEO Yihang Luo unveiled Motus2, a self-evolving general world model for robotic dexterous manipulation, at the 2026 Inclusion Conference on the Bund on September 10. Motus2 integrates action generation, consequence prediction, and outcome evaluation within a single video-action model with shared parameters. According to the team's research paper, Motus2 achieved an average success rate of 84% across five primary real-robot tasks including placing a ball, multi-finger manipulation, attaching an eraser, screwing in a light bulb, and placing a phone. Combining model-based reinforcement learning with inference-time planning increased the average success rate from 65% to 75% in a policy-optimization study.

总结：生数科技联合创始人兼CEO罗一航于9月10日在2026外滩大会上发布了机器人具身操作自进化通用世界模型Motus2。 Motus2将动作生成、后果预测和结果评估统一在共享参数的单个视频动作模型中。 研究论文显示，Motus2在放置球体、多指操作、贴橡皮擦、拧灯泡和放置手机这五项核心真实机器人任务中取得了84%的平均成功率。 在涉及手机放置和多指操作的策略优化研究中，将基于模型的强化学习与推理时规划相结合，使平均成功率从65%提升至75%，增幅达10个百分点。
