- **[2026.09.15] MarkTechPost — Reward AI Releases OM-1: A Robot Policy Trained on Human Demonstrations Only, With No Teleoperation or On-Robot Data**
  English: Reward AI has released OM-1, a general-purpose manipulation policy trained exclusively on human demonstrations without teleoperation or on-robot data. The system uses the Omnibody Hand, a 7-DoF wearable that captures contact points, reorientation, and grasps using high-frequency tactile sensing, proximity sensing, and in-hand cameras. Electromagnetic tracking is augmented to reduce overshoot error. OM-1 processes multimodal streams at native sampling rates in a single-stage training setup, while a simulation-trained reinforcement learning control layer handles physical dynamics.
  中文：总结：Reward AI 发布了 OM-1，这是一种通用的操作策略，仅通过人类示范进行训练，无需远程操作或机载机器人数据。 该系统使用 Omnibody Hand（一款 7 自由度可穿戴设备），利用高频触手觉感知、接近感知和手内摄像头来捕获接触点、重新定向和抓取。 增强了电磁跟踪以减少超调误差。 OM-1 在单阶段训练设置中以原生采样率处理多模态流，而模拟训练的强化学习控制层则处理物理动力学。
