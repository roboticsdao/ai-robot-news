Interesting Engineering — BYD’s humanoid robot has 31 degrees of freedom and a dexterous hand

BYD has unveiled the design of its first self-developed humanoid robot in a patent published by China’s National Intellectual Property Administration. The robot features a black-and-white body, a rounded head without a display, a height of approximately 5 feet 3 inches, a weight of around 129 pounds (58 kg), 31 degrees of freedom, and a dexterous hand with repeatable positioning accuracy of approximately 0.04 inches. Its potential applications span service, education, and industrial environments, with executives exploring dealership uses such as greeting customers and demonstrating infotainment systems. The project originated from a research effort started in 2022, alongside investments in Agibot and Percibot, and testing of Ubtech’s Walker S1 at its Changsha factory. BYD has not confirmed a production timeline or whether the patent represents the final version of the Xiao Di concept.

总结：比亚迪在国家知识产权局发布的一项专利中公布了其首款自主研发的人形机器人设计。 该机器人拥有黑白相间的外观、无显示屏的圆润头部、约5英尺3英寸的身高、约129磅（58公斤）的体重、31个自由度以及重复定位精度约为0.04英寸的灵巧手。 其潜在应用涵盖服务、教育和工业环境，高管曾探讨将其用于经销商网络以迎接客户和演示信息娱乐系统。 该项目源于2022年启动的研究努力，期间比亚迪还投资了智元机器人并收购了帕西博的股份，且在长沙工厂测试了优必选的Walker S1机器人。 比亚迪尚未公布生产时间表，也未确认该专利是否代表“小迪”概念的最终版本。

The 1st GMO Robot Hand Contest has been announced, hosted by GMO Internet Group and the Singularity Society. The competition challenges participants aged 18 and older, whether individuals or corporations, to program a 5-fingered robot hand using tweezers or chopsticks to pick up roasted beans one by one and transfer them from plate to plate within a 5-minute time limit. Document screening results will be announced in early December 2026, followed by a 3-month development period, video submissions and reviews in February 2027, and the final competition in late March 2027.

总结：第一届GMO机器人手部大赛已经宣布举办，由GMO互联网集团和一般社团法人奇点协会共同主办。 该比赛面向18岁及以上的个人或法人参赛者，不限人数，要求使用5指机器人手配合镊子或筷子，在5分钟的限时内将炒豆子逐一从盘子夹到另一盘子中。 报名截止日期为2026年11月30日。 奖品包括最高300万日元的奖金，以及使用涩谷人形机器人实验室、G1-D实机、GPU与云存储、数据收集装备和技术支持。 文件筛选结果将于2026年12月初公布，随后进入为期3个月的开发期，2027年2月底提交视频并进行评审，2027年3月底举行本赛。

alphaXiv — Dex-One2Many: Learning Dexterous Manipulation from a Single Human Demonstration

Dex-One2Many is a real-to-sim-to-real framework designed to learn generalizable dexterous manipulation policies from a single human video by abstracting the video into sequential scene graphs to guide reinforcement learning. Using a vision-language model, the system extracts entities and relations like grasp, contact, or inside, and converts the video into stages that constrain simulation resets and provide dense rewards. Trained entirely in simulation, the method transfers zero-shot to a UR3 arm with a 20-DoF Wuji 1 hand. Evaluated across five tool-use and manipulation tasks including Doll, Can, Stamp, Hammer, and Sweep with 30 real trials each, Dex-One2Many achieved an average 75% full-task success rate on unseen initial and goal poses, compared to 4% for the best baseline.

总结：Dex-One2Many是一个“现实-仿真-现实”框架，旨在通过将单个人类视频抽象为顺序场景图来引导强化学习，从而从单个视频中学习可泛化的灵巧操作策略。 利用视觉语言模型，系统提取实体以及抓取、接触或内部等关系，并将视频转化为约束仿真重置并提供密集奖励的阶段。 该方法完全在仿真中进行训练，并零样本迁移至配备20自由度乌稽1号手的UR3机械臂上。 在包含娃娃、罐头、印章、锤子和清扫等五个工具使用和操作任务中，每个任务进行30次真实试验，Dex-One2Many在未见过的初始和目标姿态下平均取得了75%的全任务成功率，而最佳基线方法的成功率为4%。
