Interesting Engineering — Why robot hands are one of the hardest problems in humanoid robotics

A humanoid robot can run, jump, dance, and even perform a backflip, yet ask it to fold a shirt, pick up a wet sponge, or turn a key, and the problem suddenly becomes much harder. That sounds counterintuitive. A backflip looks vastly more complicated than picking up a shirt. But for a robot, the backflip is a largely predictable sequence of precisely controlled movements. Household work is full of uncertainty.
