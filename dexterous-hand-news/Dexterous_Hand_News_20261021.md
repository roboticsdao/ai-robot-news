- **[2026.10.16] Robotics & Automation News — Tesla patent reveals touch-sensitive skin for robot hands**
  English: Tesla has filed a US patent application, US 2026/0310299 A1, published on October 8, describing a touch-sensitive skin that can be molded around robot fingers and palms. The application, highlighted by patent intelligence publication Patentlyze, outlines a manufacturing process in which tactile sensors are printed onto flat, flexible materials before being heat-formed into three-dimensional shapes. The proposed sensor uses two flexible substrate layers carrying conductive regions, separated by a compressible layer, utilizing capacitive and piezoresistive approaches with materials such as thermoplastic polyurethane, PET, and polyimide. Emphasizing manufacturability, Tesla details processes including screen printing, laser cutting, conductive paste deposition, and thermoforming to produce 3D tactile sensors that conform to curved geometries for measuring surface force, touch, and pressure. While the filing does not specify its use in the Optimus humanoid robot, it identifies fingertips, palms, and end-effectors as potential applications. Patentlyze notes that the application lacks figures for sensitivity, resolution, or durability, but the patent indicates Tesla's ongoing hardware development for sophisticated robotic manipulation.
  中文：总结：Tesla已提交美国专利申请US 2026/0310299 A1并于10月8日公开，内容涉及一种可塑形于机器人手指和手掌的触控感应“皮肤”。 根据专利情报出版物Patentlyze的分析，该专利描述了一种制造工艺，即将触觉传感器印刷到扁平的柔性材料上，随后热成型为三维形状。 该传感器采用两个带有导电区域的柔性基板层，中间由可压缩层隔开，运用电容和压阻方法，并列举了热塑性聚氨酯、PET和聚酰胺等基板材料。 该专利强调可制造性，描述了丝网印刷、激光切割、导电浆料沉积和热成型等工艺，以生产能够贴合曲面几何形状并用于测量表面力、触觉和压力的3D触觉传感器。 虽然该申请并未明确指出该技术将用于Tesla的Optimus人形机器人，但它将机器人指尖、手掌和末端执行器列为潜在应用。 Patentlyze指出，该申请未提供有关传感器灵敏度、分辨率或耐久性的数据，但该专利表明Tesla仍在继续开发使机器人具备更复杂操纵能力所需的硬件。
