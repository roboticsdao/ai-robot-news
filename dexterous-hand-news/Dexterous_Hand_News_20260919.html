vietnam.vn — Why can't robotic hands be as dexterous as human hands?

Humanoid robots face significant challenges in tasks like folding clothes or picking up a wet sponge due to uncertainty, object deformation, and the complexity of the human hand, which features 27 joints and over 30 muscles. While factory robots operate in controlled environments with simple grippers, household robots require bimanual coordination and tactile sensing. A 2026 Zhejiang University study published in Science Robotics achieved an 85% success rate in 5 complex tasks by combining visual and tactile information with learning methods.

总结：人形机器人在折衣服或捡起湿海绵等任务中面临巨大挑战，原因在于环境的不确定性、物体变形以及人手结构的复杂性（人手拥有27个关节和30多块肌肉）。 尽管工厂机器人可在受控环境中使用简单夹爪，但家庭机器人需要双臂协调和触觉感知。 浙江大学2026年发表在Science Robotics上的一项研究通过将视觉和触觉信息与学习方法相结合，在5个复杂任务中取得了85%的成功率。

marktechpost.com — Reward AI Releases OM-1: A Robot Policy Trained on Human Demonstrations Only, With No Teleoperation or On-Robot Data

Reward AI has released OM-1 (Omnibody Model 1), a general-purpose manipulation policy that learns from humans wearing a sensorized glove called Omnibody Hand, a 7-DoF wearable designed for contact points and grasps. OM-1 is trained exclusively on human demonstrations with no teleoperation or on-robot data. The system combines multimodal streams including images, tactile signals, proximity, and hand pose trajectories. A high-frequency reinforcement learning control layer handles velocity, acceleration, external disturbances, and system delays.

总结：Reward AI发布了OM-1（Omnibody Model 1），这是一种通用的操控策略，通过佩戴名为Omnibody Hand（一款专为接触点和抓取设计的7自由度可穿戴设备）传感器手套的人类示范进行学习。 OM-1完全通过人类示范进行训练，不使用远程操作或机器人机载数据。 该系统结合了图像、触觉信号、接近度以及手部姿态轨迹等多模态数据流。 一个高频的强化学习控制层负责处理速度、加速度、外部干扰以及系统延迟。
