TechNode — When robots dive underwater, HeymanDex sees new possibilities for dexterous hands

As robotic dexterous hands face growing competition in degrees of freedom, gripping force, and response speed, HeymanDex has adopted a differentiated approach by focusing on underwater and harsh environment protection capabilities. Drawing on the core team's experience with 8,000-meter-class deep-sea landers and the Jiaolong manned submersible, the company aims to make protection a fundamental feature to support tasks ranging from household chores to deep-sea engineering. To maintain finger flexibility and dynamic sealing underwater without compromising transmission efficiency, HeymanDex has developed an independent underwater integrated dynamic sealing technology system. For depths from 0 to 1,000 meters, its solutions include high-linear-speed axial dynamic sealing, high-reliability radial static sealing, and magnetic-coupling dynamic sealing. For ultra-deep-sea environments from 1,000 to 10,000 meters, it utilizes hydraulic dynamic pressure compensation, oil-filled magnetic-coupling dynamic pressure balancing, and dual-stage static sealing. The company has launched its first commercial product, the WA100 underwater dexterous hand, featuring a standard configuration supporting depths up to 300 meters, a five-finger structure with six active degrees of freedom and 11 linked joints, a weight of no more than 1,250 grams, a minimum output force of 10N per finger, and a total pulling load of at least 5 kilograms.

总结：随着机器人灵巧手在自由度、抓取力和响应速度方面的竞争日益加剧，HeymanDex采取了差异化路线，重点关注水下及严苛环境的防护能力。 凭借核心团队参与8000米级深海着岸器及蛟龙号载人潜水器项目的经验，该公司旨在将防护能力打造为基础特性，以支持从日常家务到深海工程的任务。 为了在水下保持手指的灵活性和动态密封而不损耗传动效率，HeymanDex开发了独立的水下集成动态密封技术系统。 针对0至1000米水深，其方案涵盖高速线性轴向动态密封、高可靠径向静态密封以及磁力耦合动态密封；针对1000至10000米的超深海环境，则采用液压动压补偿、充油磁力耦合动压平衡及双级静态密封。 该公司推出了首款商业产品WA100水深灵巧手，标准配置支持高达300米水深操作，采用五指结构、6个主动自由度和11个联动关节，重量不超过1250克，每指最小输出力为10N，总拉力负载至少为5公斤。

The Manila Times — Xynova's Prima 1 Robotic Hand Named a CES 2027 Innovation Awards® Honoree

Xynova's Prima 1 fully direct-drive dexterous robotic hand with 22 degrees of freedom has been named a CES 2027 Innovation Awards Honoree in the Artificial Intelligence category by the Consumer Technology Association. Designed for embodied AI research and scalable deployment, Prima 1 integrates responsive actuation, high force transparency, and five-finger visuo-tactile sensing to support robot imitation learning, teleoperation, and Sim-to-Real policy transfer. Its fully direct-drive architecture delivers millisecond-level response and precise force control with low backdrive force. Additionally, advanced thermal management and engineering-grade durability sustain continuous, long-duration operation, while SDK access allows researchers to develop and evaluate manipulation models. Founder and CEO Alex Xia noted that dexterous manipulation enables humanoid robots to interact meaningfully with the physical world. Headquartered in Hangzhou, China, with a 5,400-square-meter manufacturing facility, Xynova is expanding its relationships with customers and university research teams across the United States and Europe. The company is exhibiting at CoRL and Web Summit, and will showcase Prima 1 alongside its hybrid-drive Flex 2 hand at CES 2027 from January 6-9 in Las Vegas.

总结：由Consumer Technology Association评选，Xynova旗下拥有22个自由度的全直驱灵巧机械手Prima 1被评为CES 2027创新奖人工智能类别荣誉奖得主。 Prima 1专为具身AI研究和可扩展部署而设计，集成了响应式驱动、高力透明度以及五指视触觉传感，支持机器人模仿学习、远程操作和仿真到真实策略迁移。 其全直驱架构可实现毫秒级响应和低反驱动力的精确力控。 此外，先进的热管理和工程级耐用性支持持续长时间运行，而SDK访问权限则允许研究人员开发和评估操作模型。 创始人兼首席执行官Alex Xia表示，灵巧操作使人形机器人能够与物理世界进行有意义的交互。 Xynova总部位于中国杭州，拥有5400平方米的制造设施，正积极拓展美国和欧洲的客户及高校研究团队合作。 该公司正在参加CoRL和Web Summit，并将于1月6日至9日在拉斯维加斯举行的CES 2027上展示Prima 1及其混合驱动的Flex 2灵巧手。

Gasgoo — Inspire Robots Showcases Three New Dexterous Hand Models

Beijing Inspire Robots Technology Co. , Ltd. and Beijing Shougang Construction Investment Co. , Ltd. co-hosted a robotics supply chain forum and new product showcase at Shougang Park in Beijing on November 4. During the event, Inspire unveiled three new dexterous hands: the RH524J1, RH5MK1, and RH37D1. The RH524J1 features a 24-degree-of-freedom tendon-cable hybrid drive architecture for anthropomorphic hand flexibility and fine manipulation. The RH5MK1 utilizes a 22-degree-of-freedom full direct-drive design with high-speed dynamic response and fingertip six-dimensional force tactile sensing. The RH37D1 adopts a three-finger, full direct-drive configuration with 7 active degrees of freedom, balancing gripping force, compliant control, and operational flexibility. Inspire also established joint laboratories with Shougang Zhixin Electromagnetic Materials (Qian'an) Co. , Ltd. for soft magnetic material application technology, and with the Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications for embodied intelligence robot anthropomorphic dexterous manipulation. Founder and CEO Cai Yingpeng stated that dexterous manipulation is crucial for embodied intelligence to advance toward practical application following a decade of R&D.

总结：北京灵巧智能科技有限公司与北京首钢建设投资有限公司于11月4日在北京首钢园共同举办了机器人供应链论坛暨新品发布会。 活动期间， Inspire发布了三款全新灵巧手：RH524J1、RH5MK1和RH37D1。 RH524J1采用24自由度肌腱混合驱动架构，主打拟人手运动灵活性与精细操作能力；RH5MK1采用22自由度全直驱设计，具备高速动态响应和指尖六维力触觉传感；RH37D1采用三指全直驱配置及7个主动自由度，兼具强力抓取、柔顺控制与操作灵活性。 Inspire还分别与首钢智新电磁材料（迁安）有限公司成立了“软磁材料应用技术联合实验室”，以及与北京邮电大学成立了“具身智能机器人人机协同灵巧操作联合实验室”。 创始人兼首席执行官蔡颖鹏表示，经过十年的研发积累，灵巧操作是具身智能走向实际应用的关键支撑。

Wowtale — Physical AI Startup RLWRLD Releases DexBench, a Benchmark for Humanoid Dexterous Manipulation

Physical AI company RLWRLD announced on Nov. 9 that it has released DexBench, a benchmark for evaluating humanoid robot dexterous manipulation, built on NVIDIA's Isaac Lab-Arena framework and the Newton physics engine. DexBench implements 15 tasks across retail, logistics, assembly, and hotel food and beverage settings, measuring task success and generalization across object, physics, and spatial conditions. Development partners NdotLight, SPACE AI, and PhysicsSimLab supplied 3D assets and environments. RLWRLD plans to release code, evaluation environments, simulation assets, training data, baseline models, and a leaderboard, with initial baseline results measured using its robotics foundation model update, RLDX 1.1. The company confirmed NVIDIA's investment plan announced at AI Day Seoul 2026 to refine its robotics foundation model and expand on-site deployment.

总结：具身智能公司RLWRLD于11月9日宣布推出DexBench，这是一个评估人形机器人灵巧操作的基准，构建于NVIDIA的Isaac Lab-Arena框架与Newton物理引擎之上。 DexBench在零售、物流、组装和酒店餐饮四种工业环境中实现了15项任务，测试任务成功率及物体、物理和空间条件下的泛化能力。 开发伙伴NdotLight、SPACE AI和PhysicsSimLab提供了3D资产与环境。 RLWRLD计划发布代码、评估环境、仿真资产、训练数据、基准模型及排行榜，初始基准结果通过其机器人基础模型更新版本RLDX 1.1进行测量。 该公司确认了NVIDIA在首尔AI日宣布的投资计划，并将利用资金完善机器人基础模型并扩大现场部署。
