Vietnam.vn — Why don't robotic hands look like human hands?

Robot hands are exceptionally difficult to develop because housework requires unpredictable, highly coordinated interactions involving hands, arms, torso, vision, and the environment, unlike calculated acrobatic routines. To approach human-like hand dexterity, researchers must simultaneously overcome three primary challenges: hardware, tactile sensing, and learning capabilities. First, the mechanical challenge involves fitting actuators, motors, or cable-driven mechanisms and sensors into a restricted space while balancing force, precision, friction, and heat. Second, the tactile challenge requires integrating real-time pressure, vibration, slip detection, and texture feedback to dynamically adjust grip strength before an object falls. Third, the data and learning challenge stems from the difficulty of collecting complex manipulation data compared to images. Companies including Unittree, Apptronik, Tesla, and 1X are developing various humanoid robots with mobility and manipulation features, though bridging the gap between test conditions and real-world domestic adaptability remains ongoing.

总结：机器人手部极难开发，因为家务劳动涉及不可预测、高度协调的互动，需要手部、手臂、躯干、视觉系统和周围环境的共同配合，这与经过计算的杂技动作不同。 为了接近人类手部的灵巧度，研究人员必须同时克服硬件、触觉感知和学习能力这三大主要挑战。 首先，机械挑战涉及在有限空间内配置执行器、马达或拉线驱动机构以及传感器，同时平衡力量、精度、摩擦力和热量。 其次，触觉挑战需要集成实时的压力、振动、防滑检测和纹理反馈，以便在物体掉落前动态调整抓握力度。 最后，数据与学习挑战源于收集复杂操作数据比图像数据更为困难。 Unittree、Apptronik、Tesla和1X等公司都在开发具备移动与操作功能的人形机器人，不过要弥合测试条件与真实家庭适应能力之间的差距仍在进行中。

Robotics & Automation News — Robotiq launches the 2FC physical AI gripper

Robotiq has launched the 2FC physical AI gripper, a production-ready adaptive gripper featuring tactile sensing and embedded intelligence designed to help robots learn from physical interaction. Pre-orders are open now, with shipping scheduled for Q1 2027. The 2FC builds on the 2F adaptive gripper platform by integrating tactile sensing, an AI Hub for wrist compute, and Contact Core software. It captures synchronized and timestamped contact-rich data including pressure, vibration, force, position, and current at a millisecond level. Running compute locally on the gripper enables real-time grasp skills such as grasp quality assessment, incipient slip detection, object pose estimation, object centering, and advanced grip check. Rated for 2 million cycles, the 2FC is available in 85 mm and 140 mm stroke versions, and existing 2F owners can upgrade using TSF tactile sensors and the AI Hub. Robotiq CEO Samuel Bouchard noted that tactile intelligence is finally catching up to make use of touch data. Robotiq will present the gripper in a launch webinar on October 28, 2026.

总结：Robotiq推出了2FC物理AI抓手，这是一款具备触觉感知和嵌入式智能的量产型自适应抓手，旨在帮助机器人通过物理交互进行学习。 该产品即日起接受预订，预计于2027年第一季度发货。 2FC在Robotiq的2F自适应抓手基础之上，增加了触觉传感、腕部计算模块AI Hub以及用于物理AI的Contact Core软件。 它能够以毫秒级同步捕获压力、振动、本体感觉、力、位置和电流等丰富的数据。 通过在抓手上直接运行计算，2FC可实时执行抓取质量评估、初期滑移检测、物体位姿估计、物体定心和高级抓取检查等抓取技能。 2FC额定寿命为200万次循环，提供85毫米和140毫米行程版本，现有2F抓手用户可以通过添加TSF触觉传感器和AI Hub进行升级。 Robotiq首席执行官Samuel Bouchard表示，触觉智能的发展终于能够充分利用触觉数据。 公司将于2026年10月28日上午11点举办线上发布会展示该产品。

TrendForce — Dexterous Hands: A US$6 Billion Market

Elon Musk describes building human-like robot hands as shockingly difficult, and TrendForce research indicates that Tesla's pursuit of near-human dexterity makes manufacturing the toughest bottleneck for Optimus, with hands relying on manual assembly and tactile sensor reliability still improving. Tesla plans a modular, swappable sensing glove for its 2027 model. Dexterous hands determine whether humanoid robots can transition from demos to real-world work by enabling grasping, pinching, and tool use, and are expected to account for 20% to 30% of total hardware cost, ranking second behind body actuation. TrendForce projects global humanoid robot sales will exceed 2 million units by 2030, driving the dexterous hand market to around US$6 billion. Capital is flowing into key components; as of 1H26, China had nearly 510 dexterous hand-related companies, and three core module makers—LinkerBot, AGILINK, and GeniTech—raised a combined RMB 5.3 billion (about US$790 million) in 1H26.

总结：马斯克称制造类人机器人手“极其困难”，TrendForce的最新研究显示，特斯拉对接近人类灵巧度的追求使得制造成为Optimus最大的瓶颈，其手部高度依赖手工装配，触觉传感器可靠性仍在改进，特斯拉计划在2027年型号中采用模块化、可更换的感应手套。 灵巧手决定了人形机器人能否从演示走向现实工作，作为末端执行器直接决定机器人能否抓取、捏取、使用工具和执行精细操作。 长期来看，灵巧手预计占人形机器人总硬件成本的20%至30%，仅次于身体驱动系统。 TrendForce预计到2030年全球人形机器人销量将超过200万台，推动灵巧手市场达到约US$6 billion。 资本正在涌入，截至2026年上半年中国有近510家与灵巧手相关的公司，三大核心灵巧手模块制造商LinkerBot、AGILINK和GeniTech在2026年上半年共筹集了53亿元人民币（约合US$790 million）。
