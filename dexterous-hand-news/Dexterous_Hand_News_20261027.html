eu.36kr.com — Tsinghua Research Team Builds HandsOn Dexterous Manipulation Benchmark: Comprehensive 66-Task Evaluation of Robot Bimanual Capabilities

A research team led by Ning Ding from Tsinghua University, alongside researchers from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications, Peking University, and Nature Will, developed HandsOn, a composable simulation platform and evaluation benchmark for cross-embodiment dexterous manipulation. The benchmark addresses the lack of systematic evaluation systems for dexterous hands, which involve many active joints, contact interactions, and bimanual collaboration. HandsOn features 66 human-validated tasks divided into Core-50 and Transfer-16, along with 18,000 human teleoperation demonstration records in simulation. The platform supports 5 types of robotic arms, 6 types of dexterous hands, and approximately 200 assets. Evaluations of technical routes including diffusion policies, vision-language-action models, and world-action models showed that the best-performing multi-task policy on Core-50 achieved a 29.1% success rate, while the average task success rate on Transfer-16 across 8 trained multi-task policies was below 1%. Furthermore, GPT-6 achieved success rates of 5.2% and 1.9% on Core-50 and Transfer-16 respectively across 660 tests without fine-tuning.

总结：清华大学丁宁团队联合北京邮电大学、北京大学及Nature Will的研究人员，共同构建了跨具身灵巧操作的可组合仿真平台与评估基准HandsOn。 该基准旨在填补灵巧手系统化评估体系的空白，包含66个人工验证的任务（分为Core-50与Transfer-16）以及18,000条仿真中的人类遥操作演示记录。 平台支持5种机械臂、6种灵巧手以及约200个资产。 团队评估了扩散策略、视觉语言动作模型和世界动作模型等技术路线，结果显示在提供训练演示的Core-50任务中，表现最好的多任务策略取得了29.1%的成功率；而在无目标任务训练演示的Transfer-16任务中，全部8个经训练的多任务策略平均任务成功率低于1%。 此外，未在基准数据上进行微调的GPT-6在660次测试中，在Core-50和Transfer-16上的成功率分别为5.2%和1.9%。

Ubergizmo JAPAN — ムラタとMELT、ロボットハンド訓練向け受賞グローブ「ContactGlove3 Pro」を披露

Murata Manufacturing re-exhibited at CEATEC 2026, highlighting the ContactGlove3 Pro advanced hand-tracking glove developed jointly with MELT Interface Technologies. The glove won the CEATEC AWARD 2026 Excellence Award for capturing precise human finger movements to generate data for robotics and physical AI applications. During a demonstration, an operator's glove movements were replicated in real time on a digital hand and a physical robot arm. Unlike camera-based systems, the glove uses electromagnetic field (EMF) sensing with ultra-small 3-axial low-frequency antennas provided by Murata, achieving approximately three times the power efficiency of existing EMF gloves with the same battery capacity. MELT's zero-drift technology measures absolute finger position to prevent tracking error accumulation, with the Pro version featuring sensors moved closer to the fingertips than the standard ContactGlove3. The Pro model tracks 5 fingers with a median position error of 0.5mm and maximum error of 1.5mm under specified conditions, operating at 100Hz with less than 30 milliseconds of latency. The system measures position and orientation at 30 degrees of freedom and outputs 25 degrees of freedom through a biomechanical model. Compatible with ROS 2, C++, Python, Unity, Unreal Engine, and MotionBuilder, the Pro version offers up to 10 hours of battery life and is planned for US release in November 2026 priced at $3,999 by MELT.

总结：村田制作所再次亮相CEATEC 2026，其重头戏是由MELT Interface Technologies与村田制作所共同开发的先进手部追踪手套“ContactGlove3 Pro”。 该技术因捕捉人类手指精密运动以生成机器人及物理AI应用宝贵数据的能力，荣获“CEATEC AWARD 2026”优秀奖。 在演示中，操作者的手部动作在数字手套和物理机械臂上实时重现。 与基于摄像头的系统不同，该手套采用电磁场（EMF）传感，内置村田制作所提供的超小型3轴低频率天线，在同等电池容量下实现了现有EMF手套约3倍的电力效率。 MELT的“零漂移技术”测量手指绝对位置以防止追踪误差累积，Pro版相比标准版“ContactGlove3”将追踪传感器移至更靠近指尖的位置。 Pro版可追踪5根手指，在指定条件下的位置误差中位数为0.5mm，最大误差为1.5mm，工作频率为100Hz，延迟小于30毫秒。 系统以30自由度测量指尖位置和方向，并通过生力学模型输出25自由度。 该手套兼容ROS 2、C++、Python、Unity、Unreal Engine和MotionBuilder，电池续航时间长达10小时，MELT计划于2026年11月在美国以3,999美元的价格发售Pro版。

alphaXiv — DexPort: A Hand-Held Skill Interface for Dexterous Hand Data Collection

DexPort is a portable data-collection interface introduced to gather high-quality demonstrations for learning dexterous manipulation without requiring a robot arm, motion-capture rig, or independent control of numerous finger joints. Developed as a transferable module, it rigidly mounts a five-fingered, 20-degree-of-freedom Wuji hand and a fisheye camera together, attaching either to a Quest 2 controller for collection or to a robot wrist for deployment to preserve embodiment and camera-to-hand geometry. During collection, the Quest controller specifies wrist trajectories, while the hand controller records commanded joint targets and measured joint states, acknowledging discrepancies during contact. To maintain manageable control, operators use a small skill set instead of individual joints, including independent thumb positioning, an incremental power grasp, a pinch, a two-finger grasp, and a learned closed-loop skill for finger gaiting during in-hand tool rotation. Visuomotor policies trained via behavior cloning from camera images, wrist motion, and hand data predict wrist poses and raw targets for all 20 hand joints without skill labels. Evaluated across 4 tasks (pick-and-place, screwing, drawer stowage, and bimanual scanning) over 15 episodes per task, command-supervised policies achieved 64% final-task success compared to 11% for the state-as-action baseline averaged over success-rate scored tasks, while in-the-wild data improved task success from 36% to 78%.

总结：DexPort是一个便携式数据采集接口，旨在收集用于学习灵巧操作的高质量演示，而无需机械臂、动作捕捉设备或对众多手指关节进行独立控制。 作为一个可转移模块，它将一个五指、20自由度的无极手和一个鱼眼相机刚性安装在一起，既可连接Quest 2控制器进行采集，也可安装在机器人手腕上进行部署，从而保持具身性和相机到手部的几何关系。 在采集过程中，Quest控制器指定手腕轨迹，而手控器则记录指令关节目标和测量的关节状态，以应对接触时的差异。 为了保持可控性，操作员使用一小套技能而非单独控制关节，包括独立的拇指定位、增量式强力抓握、捏握、双指抓握以及用于手内工具旋转的闭环手指步态技能。 通过模仿学习利用相机图像、手腕运动和手部数据训练的视觉运动策略可以直接预测手腕姿势和所有20个手部关节的原始目标，且无需技能标签。 在涵盖4个任务（抓取放置、拧螺丝、抽屉收纳和双臂扫描）的评估中（每个任务15个回合），以指令监督的策略在按成功率评分的任务中平均最终任务成功率为64%（状态即动作基线为11%），而使用野外数据则将任务成功率从36%提升至78%。

Vietnam.vn — Why don't robotic hands look like human hands?

Developing robotic hands for humanoid robots is exceptionally difficult because hands require complex coordination of mechanics, sensors, and control. Unlike acrobatic movements that follow precise pre-calculated sequences, household chores are unpredictable and require whole-body balance, vision, and tactile feedback. Researchers face three main hurdles: hardware mechanics such as fitting actuators and sensors into tight spaces while balancing force and precision; tactile sensing including real-time slip detection to adjust grip; and the data and learning challenge of handling complex manipulation tasks rather than simple images. Companies like Unittree, Apptronik, Tesla, and 1X are developing robotic hands and robots, but bridging the gap between test conditions and real homes remains a challenge.

总结：人形机器人的灵巧手开发极具挑战，因为手部需要复杂的机械、感觉和控制系统协同。 与动作可预测的杂技不同，家务劳动充满不确定性，需要手、臂、躯干、视觉系统和环境的配合。 研究人员面临三大挑战：硬件机械方面，需在极小空间内塞入致动器与传感器并平衡力量和精度；触觉传感方面，需实现实时防滑检测以动态调整握力；数据与学习方面，复杂抓取的数据远比图像数据难以收集。 Unittree、Apptronik、Tesla和1X等公司都在开发相关机器人和灵巧手，但实验室测试条件与真实家庭环境之间仍存在差距。
