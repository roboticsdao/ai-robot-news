- **[2026.09.24] AZoSensors — Giving Robots a Sense of Touch: The Next Generation of Tactile Sensors**
  English: Researchers are developing advanced tactile sensors that combine pressure detection with vibration, proximity, temperature, and texture cues into a flexible layer. Single-signal sensors often mislead robots when properties overlap, but multimodal systems show significantly higher recognition accuracy, such as identifying 26 textiles with 95.49% accuracy by combining pressure and triboelectric data. Proximity sensors using capacitive twin helical electrodes detect objects over 5 cm away, aiding in transparent item handling. Microstructures in pressure sensors distinguish normal force from shear force to detect slips, while piezoelectric films like a 28 μm PVDF film on silicone layers convert tiny mechanical oscillations into voltage to catch slips early.
  中文：总结：研究人员正在开发先进的触觉传感器，将压力检测与振动、接近度、温度和纹理线索组合到一个柔层中。 当属性重叠时，单一信号传感器经常误导机器人，但多模态系统显示出更高的识别精度，例如通过组合压力和摩擦电数据以95.49%的准确率识别26种纺织品。 使用电容式双螺旋电极的接近传感器可检测5厘米以外的物体，有助于处理透明物品。 压力传感器中的微结构可区分法向力和剪切力以检测滑移，而硅胶层上厚度为28微米的PVDF薄膜等压电薄膜则将微小的机械振动转化为电压，以便及早发现滑移。
