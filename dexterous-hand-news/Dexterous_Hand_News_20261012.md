- **[2026.10.08] alphaXiv — Dex-One2Many: Learning Dexterous Manipulation from a Single Human Demonstration**
  English: Dex-One2Many is a real-to-sim-to-real framework designed to learn generalizable dexterous manipulation policies from a single human video by abstracting the video into sequential scene graphs to guide reinforcement learning. Using a vision-language model, the system extracts entities and relations like grasp, contact, or inside, and converts the video into stages that constrain simulation resets and provide dense rewards. Trained entirely in simulation, the method transfers zero-shot to a UR3 arm with a 20-DoF Wuji 1 hand. Evaluated across five tool-use and manipulation tasks including Doll, Can, Stamp, Hammer, and Sweep with 30 real trials each, Dex-One2Many achieved an average 75% full-task success rate on unseen initial and goal poses, compared to 4% for the best baseline.
  中文：总结：Dex-One2Many是一个“现实-仿真-现实”框架，旨在通过将单个人类视频抽象为顺序场景图来引导强化学习，从而从单个视频中学习可泛化的灵巧操作策略。 利用视觉语言模型，系统提取实体以及抓取、接触或内部等关系，并将视频转化为约束仿真重置并提供密集奖励的阶段。 该方法完全在仿真中进行训练，并零样本迁移至配备20自由度乌稽1号手的UR3机械臂上。 在包含娃娃、罐头、印章、锤子和清扫等五个工具使用和操作任务中，每个任务进行30次真实试验，Dex-One2Many在未见过的初始和目标姿态下平均取得了75%的全任务成功率，而最佳基线方法的成功率为4%。
