- **[2026.09.12] Interesting Engineering — Why robot hands are one of the hardest problems in humanoid robotics**
  English: Humanoid robots can easily perform athletic movements like backflips because they rely on predictable, tightly controlled sequences, whereas everyday household tasks such as folding shirts or picking up wet sponge involve high uncertainty and difficult-to-model physical contacts. Building a dexterous robotic hand requires combining mechanics, sensing, and control, as vision alone cannot determine grip force or slippage. Recent research emphasizes tactile sensing, with a 2026 Science Robotics study from Zhejiang University achieving an 85% success rate across five complex tasks using visual and tactile information combined with reinforcement learning and online imitation learning. Chinese robotics companies, including X Square Robot, are actively testing humanoids on household chores, but a major bottleneck remains the lack of internet-scale datasets containing synchronized multi-sensor manipulation data.
  中文：总结：人形机器人可以轻松执行后空翻等运动，因为这些动作依赖于可预测且受严格控制的序列，而折叠衬衫或捡起湿海绵等日常家务则涉及高度的不确定性以及难以建模的物理接触。 制造灵巧的机器人手需要结合机械、传感和控制，因为仅靠视觉无法确定抓握力或滑移情况。 近期研究强调了触觉传感的重要性，浙江大学2026年发表在《Science Robotics》上的一项研究结合了视觉与触觉信息以及强化学习与在线模仿学习，在五个复杂任务中取得了85%的成功率。 包括X Square Robot在内的中国机器人公司正在积极测试人形机器人的家务能力，但主要的瓶颈依然是缺乏包含同步多传感器操作数据的互联网规模数据集。
