- **[2026.09.12] Interesting Engineering — Why robot hands are one of the hardest problems in humanoid robotics**
  English: Humanoid robots can easily perform predictable, highly controlled athletic movements like backflips, but struggle with unpredictable household chores such as folding laundry, picking up wet sponges, or turning keys, due to objects bending, slipping, and varying in placement. Building a functional robotic hand requires combining mechanics, sensing, and control to manage multiple motors, degrees of freedom, force, and tactile feedback, as vision alone cannot prevent crushing fragile items. Recent research, including a 2026 Science Robotics study from Zhejiang University, combines visual and tactile information with reinforcement learning and online imitation learning to achieve an 85% success rate across five complex tasks. Additionally, Chinese robotics firms like X Square Robot are testing humanoids on household tasks, while researchers emphasize that data collection combining joint positions, cameras, force, torque, and tactile sensors remains a major challenge.
  中文：总结：人形机器人可以轻易完成诸如后空翻这样可预测且高度受控的运动，但由于物体弯曲、打滑以及位置多变，在折衣物、捡起湿海绵或转动钥匙等不可预测的家务上却面临困难。 制造一个功能性的机器人手需要结合机械、传感和控制来管理多个电机、自由度、力和触觉反馈，因为仅靠视觉无法防止压碎易碎物品。 浙江大学2026年的一项《科学机器人》研究等近期成果将视觉与触觉信息同强化学习和在线模仿学习相结合，在五项复杂任务中取得了85%的成功率。 此外，像X Square Robot这样的中国机器人公司正在测试人形机器人的家务能力，而研究人员强调，结合关节位置、相机、力、扭矩和触觉传感器的的数据收集仍然是一项重大挑战。
