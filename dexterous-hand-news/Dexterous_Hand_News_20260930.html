ARC Advisory Group — China Humanoid Robot Dexterous Hands: Localization, Technology Routes, and Leading Suppliers

The article discusses the localization, technology routes, and leading suppliers of China's humanoid robot dexterous hands, noting that 2026 marks a watershed year for the industry's transition from research-driven to a scalable and commercially viable market. Three mature technical routes dominate the industry: 1) Coreless Motor + Micro Reducer + Tendon-Driven Structure, featuring low inertia and fast dynamic response, using coreless motors with micro planetary or harmonic reducers and steel tendon ropes, with Linker Hand as a representative manufacturer, though constrained by continuous torque limits and high costs; 2) Micro Servo Electric Cylinder Solution (Coreless-Free), adopting iron-core micro motors and screw structures inside the palm to drive fingers through connecting rods, offering higher grasping force and durability for mass production with slightly reduced bionic slimness, with InTimes Robotics as a representative; 3) Hybrid Tendon-Driven Solution, placing motors in the palm or forearm to drive fingers via tensioned tendon ropes, with DH-Robotics as a representative. China's anthropomorphic multi-fingered dexterous hand industry has achieved rapid growth, transitioning from costly imported research products to independent R&D and large-scale mass production, becoming a critical pillar for the humanoid robot industry.

总结：文章探讨了中国人形机器人灵巧手的本土化、技术路线及领先供应商，指出2026年是灵巧手从研究驱动向规模化商业市场过渡的分水岭。 当前行业主导三种成熟的技术路线：1）无心电机+微型减速器+肌腱驱动结构，具备低惯量和快速动态响应，采用无心电机配合微型行星或谐波减速器及钢肌腱绳，代表厂商为Linker Hand，但存在持续扭矩有限及成本较高的局限；2）微型伺服电缸方案（无无心电机），采用铁芯微型电机和螺丝结构在手掌内形成集成微型电缸并通过连杆机构驱动手指，提供更高的抓握力和耐久性以利于量产，代表厂商为InTimes Robotics；3）混合肌腱驱动方案，将电机置于手掌或前臂通过张紧的肌腱绳驱动手指，代表厂商为DH-Robotics。 中国仿生多指灵巧手行业近年来实现快速增长，从高成本的进口科研产品转变为独立研发和大规模量产，成为人形机器人产业的关键支柱。

Quality Magazine — How Tactile Sensing Fills the Gap Vision Can’t Measure

Robotic tactile sensing measures physical signals such as contact forces, slip, pressure, vibration, and temperature that machine vision cannot directly capture. Different tactile sensor types offer specific trade-offs: piezoresistive sensors provide simple construction and high sensitivity but may lose stability over time; capacitive sensors draw low power and respond quickly with limited spatial resolution; piezoelectric and triboelectric sensors are self-powered and fast but cannot measure static or sustained forces; magnetic sensors decouple three-dimensional forces cleanly in controlled magnetic environments; and optical or vision-based tactile sensors deliver the highest spatial resolution at the cost of complex optical systems and restricted form factor flexibility. While robots have advanced significantly in mobility, vision alone falls short in providing direct information about contact interactions like grip force, slip, compliance, and shear forces required for physical tasks such as grasping, insertion, press-fitting, sanding, and polishing. Artificial tactile systems replicate aspects of human mechanoreceptors to convert signals through signal processing or machine learning into useful outputs including grasp stability, slip detection, in-hand manipulation, object and texture recognition, and orientation estimation.

总结：机器人触觉传感可测量接触力、滑移、压力、振动和温度等机器视觉无法直接捕捉的物理信号。 不同类型的触觉传感器各有权衡：压阻传感器结构简单、灵敏度高但随时间可能失去稳定性；电容传感器功耗低、响应快但空间分辨率有限；压电和摩擦电传感器自供电且速度快，但无法测量静态和持续力；磁性传感器能在受控磁环境中清晰解耦三维力；光学或基于视觉的触觉传感器空间分辨率最高，但代价是光学系统更复杂且外形尺寸灵活性受限。 尽管机器人在移动能力上取得了显着进步，但单纯依靠视觉无法直接提供抓握力、滑移、柔顺度以及抓取、插入、压装、打磨和抛光等物理任务所需的剪切力等接触交互信息。 人工触觉系统能够复制人类机械感受器的部分功能，通过信号处理或机器学习将信号转化为有用的输出，包括抓握稳定性、滑移检测、手内操作、物体和纹理识别以及方向估计。
