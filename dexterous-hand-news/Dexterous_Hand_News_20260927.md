- **[2026.09.24] AZoSensors — Giving Robots a Sense of Touch: The Next Generation of Tactile Sensors**
  English: Researchers are developing next-generation tactile sensors that combine multiple signals into a single flexible layer to give robots a working sense of touch. Traditional sensors measuring a single quantity often reduce recognition accuracy because softness, texture, and temperature can produce similar pressure changes. A recent Advanced Science study showed that combining pressure and triboelectric data increased textile identification accuracy to 95.49%. Proximity sensing using capacitive twin helical electrodes detects objects from over 5 cm away, providing early warning for transparent items. Microstructures help sensors distinguish normal force from shear force to detect slips, while piezoelectric PVDF films convert tiny mechanical oscillations into voltage to catch slips early, though the thickness of the soft layer affects signal strength.
  中文：总结：研究人员正在开发下一代触觉传感器，将多种信号组合到一个柔性层中，赋予机器人工作触感。 传统传感器仅测量单一物理量，由于柔软度、质地和温度可能产生相似的压力变化，往往会降低识别准确率。 Advanced Science最近的一项研究表明，将压力和摩擦电数据结合，使纺织品识别准确率提升至95.49%。 使用电容式双螺旋电极的接近传感可在5厘米以外检测物体，为透明物品提供早期预警。 微结构帮助传感器区分正向力和剪切力以检测滑移，而压电PVDF薄膜则将微小机械振荡转换为电压以便尽早捕获滑移，尽管软层的厚度会影响信号强度。
