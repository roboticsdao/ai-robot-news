- **[2026.09.15] MarkTechPost — Reward AI Releases OM-1: A Robot Policy Trained on Human Demonstrations Only, With No Teleoperation or On-Robot Data**
  English: Robotics startup Reward AI has released OM-1 (Omnibody Model 1), a general-purpose manipulation policy trained exclusively on human demonstrations without using any teleoperation data or on-robot data. The system follows the principle of 'One Model, One Data Interface, Any Body' and currently operates as an in-house policy with no weights, code, dataset, or API released. The hardware stack begins with the Omnibody Hand, a 7-DoF wearable that captures essential functions such as choosing contact points and reorienting objects. The One Data Interface captures wearer motion using high-frequency tactile sensing, proximity sensing, and global-shutter in-hand cameras. Reward AI utilizes visual-inertial tracking augmented with electromagnetic sensing and disturbance compensation, which reduced mean overshoot error by 60% at the highest speed of 67 cm/s in tests. OM-1 processes multimodal streams at their native sampling rates to generate robot actions directly. Below the policy, a high-frequency control layer trained via reinforcement learning handles velocity- and acceleration-dependent dynamics and system delays, operating on its own clock.

  English: ShengShu Technology Co-founder and CEO Yihang Luo unveiled Motus2, a self-evolving general world model for robotic dexterous manipulation, at the 2026 Inclusion Conference on the Bund on September 10. - **[2026.09.12] Interesting Engineering — Why robot hands are one of the hardest problems in humanoid robotics**
  English: Humanoid robots can easily perform athletic movements like backflips because the environment and movements are predictable, but household chores involving manipulation, such as folding shirts or picking up wet sponges, present high uncertainty due to objects bending, slipping, or deforming. A robotic hand requires sophisticated mechanics, tactile sensors, and software to constantly adjust grip without conscious thought, as vision alone cannot determine crushing force. Recent studies, including a 2026 Science Robotics paper from Zhejiang University, combine visual and tactile information with reinforcement learning, achieving an 85% success rate across five complex tasks involving 25 objects. Unlike industrial environments where trajectories and lighting are strictly controlled, homes introduce unpredictable object geometries. Research identifies bimanual coordination, stable navigation, and reachability as key requirements for real-world whole-body manipulation. Chinese robotics companies are increasingly targeting household chores, but a major missing ingredient is a large-scale dataset of people manipulating thousands of objects with synchronized sensor information.
  中文：总结：人形机器人可以轻松完成空翻等运动，因为环境和动作是可预测的，但涉及操作的家务活（如叠衬衫或捡起湿海绵）由于物体弯曲、滑动或变形而具有高度的不确定性。 机器人手部需要复杂的机械结构、触觉传感器和软件来在没有意识的情况下不断调整抓握，因为仅靠视觉无法确定挤压力量。 最近的研究（包括浙江大学 2026 年发表在 Science Robotics 上的论文）将视觉和触觉信息与强化学习结合，在涉及 25 个对象的五个复杂任务中实现了 85% 的成功率。 与轨迹和光照受到严格控制的工业环境不同，家庭环境引入了不可预测的物体几何形状。 研究将双臂协调、稳定导航和可达性确定为真实世界全身操作的关键要求。 中国机器人公司正越来越多地将目标瞄准家务活，但缺少的一个主要要素是包含同步传感器信息、人类操作数千种物体的宏大规模数据集。
