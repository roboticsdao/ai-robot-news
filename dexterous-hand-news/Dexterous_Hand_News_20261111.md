- **[2026.11.09] alphaXiv — Learning Heterogeneous Tactile Representation for General Dexterous Manipulation**
  English: Tactile sensing is crucial for contact-rich dexterous manipulation in embodied intelligence. To address the challenge of leveraging heterogeneous tactile data, the authors developed LingBot-Touch, a tactile foundation model. It yields a unified representation across piezoresistive, magnetic, and visuotactile sensors, producing plug-and-play tactile tokens that integrate into policy models like diffusion policy and vision-language-action models to enhance dexterity. Experiments in simulation and real-robot deployments show the representation benefits complex manipulation tasks while maintaining computational efficiency. The pretraining corpus pools 4,983 hours, 464,431 episodes, and about 1.33 billion frames from human tactile gloves and teleoperated Sharpa Wave hands across 24 task categories without fine-grained annotations. It uses a shared Haptic Transformer and Tactile-JEPA training with a masking ratio rising from 40% to 80% over the first 300 steps.
  中文：总结：触觉感知对于具身智能中接触丰富的灵巧操作至关重要。 为了解决利用异构触觉数据的挑战，作者开发了触觉基础模型LingBot-Touch。 该模型在压阻式、磁触觉和视触觉传感器之间产生统一的表征，生成可插入扩散策略和视觉语言动作模型等策略模型的即插即用触觉token，以增强灵巧度。 仿真和真实机器人部署的实验表明，该表征在保持计算效率的同时有利于复杂的操控任务。 预训练语料库汇集了来自人类触觉手套和远程操作Sharpa Wave手在24个任务类别中的4,983小时、464,431个episode以及约13.3亿帧数据，且没有细粒度注释。 它使用共享的Haptic Transformer和Tactile-JEPA训练，掩码比例在前300步内从40%上升到80%。
