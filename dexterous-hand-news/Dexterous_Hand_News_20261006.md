- **[2026.10.02] Interesting Engineering — Boston Dynamics unveils 13-DOF robotic hand for its Atlas humanoid**
  English: Boston Dynamics has unveiled a new hand for its Atlas humanoid robot that features four fingers and 13 degrees of freedom, moving away from a conventional five-fingered design. The configuration includes a 4-DOF opposable thumb and 3 DOFs on each of the other three fingers, using direct actuation with a single actuator type and encapsulated joints. The hand is approximately the size of a large human hand and can carry a loaded mini-fridge weighing over 100 pounds, matching the production Atlas platform's instantaneous payload of 110 pounds and sustained payload of 66 pounds. Boston Dynamics concluded after testing that four fingers provide sufficient in-hand object reorientation, slip recovery, and tool manipulation while avoiding the extra cost, size, and failure points of a pinky. The hand's design supports AI training through high-fidelity simulation and sim-to-real reinforcement learning, utilizing backdrivable transmissions, proprioception, and dense pressure-sensitive tactile sensors on the fingertips and palm. It is designed to perform pinch grasps, tripod grasps, and operate tools such as drills, grinders, nail guns, torque drivers, and welding torches.
  中文：总结：波士顿动力为其阿特拉斯人形机器人推出了一款新机械手，采用四根手指和13个自由度，没有使用传统的五指设计。 该配置包括一个4自由度的可对立拇指以及其余三根手指各3个自由度，使用单一执行器类型的直接驱动以及封装关节。 该手的大致尺寸与人类大手相当，能够搬运重达100 pounds以上的已加载迷你冰箱，符合量产阿特拉斯平台110 pounds的瞬时负载和66 pounds的持续负载。 波士顿动力经测试后得出结论，四根手指已足够提供手中物体重新定向、防滑恢复以及工具操作能力，同时避免了小指带来的额外成本、尺寸和故障点。 该机械手的设计支持通过高仿真模拟和模拟到真实的强化学习进行AI训练，利用可反向驱动的传动装置、本体感觉以及指尖和手掌上密集的压敏触觉传感器。 它被设计用于执行捏握、三脚架抓握以及操作钻头、磨床、气钉枪、扭矩起子和焊枪等工具。
