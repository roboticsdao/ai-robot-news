Oak Ridge and Idaho National Laboratories are developing wire arc additive manufacturing for industrial pressure vessels to address U. S. manufacturing bottlenecks. finance.biggo.com — UBTECH Partners with BASiC Semiconductor to Position Silicon Carbide Ahead of the Humanoid Robot High-Voltage Curve

UBTECH and BASiC Semiconductor exchanged signed strategic cooperation documents at the World Robot Conference in Beijing, focusing on silicon carbide power devices in embodied intelligence humanoid robots. Current volume-shipped humanoid robots rely on 48V safety extra-low voltage architectures using silicon MOSFETs and GaN HEMTs, while silicon carbide is virtually unused due to voltage headroom and pricing. The partnership is a bet on future higher-voltage industrial platforms operating at 96V and 400V.

总结：优必选与基本半导体在北京世界机器人大会上交换了签署的战略合作文件，重点关注碳化硅功率器件在具身智能人形机器人领域的应用。 目前大批量出货的人形机器人依赖于使用硅基MOSFET和氮化镓HEMT的48V安全特低电压架构，而碳化硅由于电压裕度和定价原因几乎未被使用。 此次合作是对未来运行在96V和400V的更高电压工业平台的布局。
