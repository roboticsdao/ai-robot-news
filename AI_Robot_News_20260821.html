Dailyhunt — China's robot dogs draw on US military-funded research

Research and development funded by the US military between 2010 and 2020 contributed to the creation of robot dogs by China's Unitree Robotics, according to program researchers and a former defense official. The US Army's DEVCOM Army Research Laboratory funded this work through the Robotics Collaborative Technology Alliance, involving institutions such as the University of Pennsylvania, MIT, Boston Dynamics, and NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory. Former researchers noted that Unitree's popular Go series closely resembles or matches dimensions with the Mini Cheetah and other technologies developed in that US program. Unitree launched its Go2 model in 2023 at approximately $1,600, helping expand its global presence ahead of a valuation around $9 billion and a stock market debut. While US researchers pointed to a challenge in converting publicly funded breakthroughs into commercial products due to limitations in US industrial capacity and supply chains, China leveraged its large manufacturing ecosystem to move rapidly to mass production. The technology has also drawn military attention, with Chinese state media showing Unitree robots alongside PLA troops and carrying rifles. In June, the Pentagon added Unitree to a list of Chinese military companies, while the company maintains its robots are for civilian use amid US restrictions on foreign-made robots.

总结：根据参与项目的研究人员和前美国国防科技官员透露，中国宇树科技的一些成功机器人狗借助了美国军方资助的研究成果。 美国陆军DEVCOM陆军研究实验室在2010年至2020年期间通过机器人协同技术联盟资助了相关机器人研究，参与机构包括宾夕法尼亚大学、麻省理工学院、波士顿动力公司以及美国宇航局喷气推进实验室。 研究人员表示，宇树的Go系列与该项目开发的technologies密切相关，麻省理工学院研究员本·卡茨称Go系列的尺寸与他协助开发的Mini Cheetah几乎完全相同。 宇树于2023年推出了售价约1,600美元的Go2，推动其在全球四足机器人市场迅速扩张，目前估值约为$9 billion并准备上市。 研究人员指出，美国面临的挑战在于如何将公共资金资助的技术突破转化为商业产品，而中国凭借强大的制造生态系统迅速实现了量产。 此外，该技术也引发了军事关注，中国官媒曾展示宇树机器人伴随解放军士兵并搭载步枪，五角大楼于6月将宇树列入中国军事企业名单，而宇树则坚称其机器人用于民用。

Memeburn — LG and NVIDIA Team Up to Build a Humanoid Robot

LG Electronics and NVIDIA signed a memorandum of understanding on August 14 to co-develop a bipedal humanoid robot targeting an early 2027 release. The robot will utilize NVIDIA's Isaac GR00T foundation model for reasoning and interaction, Jetson Thor computing platform for onboard processing and motor control, and Halos for safety.
