Dailyhunt — China's robot dogs draw on US military-funded research

Researchers and a former defense official state that China's Unitree Robotics built some of its most successful robot dogs using innovations from US military-funded research, specifically work funded between 2010 and 2020 by the US Army's DEVCOM Army Research Laboratory. Unitree's Go series closely resembles these US-funded technologies, which researchers say provided the foundation for the company's mass-produced robots. China's manufacturing ecosystem has helped Unitree scale production rapidly, while US companies have struggled with similar scale and prices. 总结：据研究人员及前美国国防技术官员透露，中国宇树科技利用源自美国军方资助研究的技术创新，制造出了部分最成功的机器人狗。 美国陆军DEVCOM陆军研究实验室在2010年至2020年期间通过机器人协同技术联盟资助了相关机器人研究。 研究人员指出，宇树的Go系列与该计划开发的的技术高度相似，并为宇树的大规模量产奠定了基础。 中国庞大的制造生态系统帮助宇树快速实现了从研究到商业化产品的转变，而美国公司在同等规模和价格上举步维艰。 五角大楼已于6月将宇树列入中国军事企业名单，但宇树坚持其机器人仅供民用。
