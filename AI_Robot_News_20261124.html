Daily Sabah — Türkiye launches call for domestic humanoid robot development | Daily Sabah

The Industry and Technology Ministry on Monday launched a call for proposals to develop industrial humanoid robots designed to outperform global rivals in manufacturing assembly, logistics, and precision machining. The program funds the entire process from design to real-world deployment, requiring homegrown development of software, sensors, and motion systems. The call outlines strict technical standards including a minimum height of 1.6 meters (5.25 feet), payload capacities of at least 15 kg (33 pounds) per arm and 35 kg (77 lbs. ) with both arms, walking and running speeds, and battery specifications. Projects can receive a 50% grant for durations up to 48 months with applications open through Dec. 31.

总结：土耳其工业和技术部长于周一发布了一项提案征集，旨在开发用于工业应用的双足人形机器人，使其在制造装配、物流和精密机加工领域超越全球竞争对手。 该项目资助从设计、原型制作、测试到实际部署的全过程，重点推进软件架构、传感器、控制系统和伺服电机的本土化研发。 技术标准要求机器人身高至少1.6米（5.25 feet），单臂负重至少15公斤（33 pounds），双臂腰部高度可提起至少35公斤（77 lbs. ），并具备特定的步行、奔跑速度及电池性能。 项目提供符合条件成本50%的资助，项目期限最长可达48个月，申请截止日期为12月31日。
