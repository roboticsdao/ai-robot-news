Marine Insight — U.S. Navy Quadruples AI Robotics Deal To $300 Million To Boost Fleet Readiness

The U. S. Navy expanded its contract with Gecko Robotics, increasing the agreement ceiling from $71 million to $300 million to advance robotics and AI systems against Indo-Pacific threats such as China. The expanded deal aims to speed up naval ship maintenance and boost fleet readiness toward an 80 percent target to address shipyard delays and labor shortages. Gecko Robotics uses wall-climbing robots to inspect hulls, flight decks, and ballast tanks 50 times faster than manual inspections, feeding data into its Cantilever AI software platform. The company states this technology can save up to 8 million dollars per ship in repair costs. Alongside the deal, Gecko revealed a new specialized robot named Komodo designed to scan steel decks beneath non-skid coatings for hidden corrosion without stripping surfaces. CEO Jake Loosararian emphasized that AI-driven maintenance systems help keep existing vessels ready for combat despite manufacturing volume challenges.

总结：美国海军扩大了与Gecko Robotics公司的合同，将协议上限从$71 million增加到$300 million，以推进机器人和人工智能系统，应对印度洋-太平洋地区来自中国等的新兴海军威胁。 这项扩大的协议旨在加快海军舰艇维护，并将舰队战备状态提升至80%的目标，以解决造船厂延误和劳动力短缺问题。 Gecko Robotics使用爬墙机器人检查船体、飞行甲板和压载舱，速度比人工检查快50倍，并将数据输入其Cantilever人工智能软件平台。 该公司表示，这项技术每艘船可为美国海军节省高达8 million dollars的修理费。 在此次合作之外，Gecko还推出了一款名为Komodo的新型专用机器人，用于扫描防滑涂层下的钢制甲板以寻找隐藏腐蚀，且无需剥离表面。 首席执行官杰克·鲁萨拉里安强调，人工智能驱动的维护系统有助于在制造数量面临挑战的情况下，保持现有舰艇随时准备作战。

Yahoo Finance — Nvidia debuts platform for keeping AI agents from going rogue and humanoid robot design at GTC Berlin

Nvidia has debuted its OpenShell Reference Architecture at the GTC Berlin event in Germany, designed to help enterprises build, contain, and observe AI agents while preventing them from going rogue. The platform runs AI models on Nvidia's Vera Rubin system, utilizes Vera CPUs for agent software, and employs BlueField-4 data processing units as security barriers. Nvidia stated it is collaborating with companies such as CoreWeave, Crusoe, Lambda, Nebius, and Oracle Cloud Infrastructure to roll out the architecture, which can quarantine rogue agents in milliseconds. CEO Jensen Huang emphasized that safety and control must be built into computing architecture as enterprises prepare to deploy billions of AI agents. In addition, Nvidia partner Agile Robots has developed the Agile One SN, a 4.59-foot-tall humanoid robot running on the Nvidia Isaac GR00T software platform and Nvidia Jetson Thor computer to help developers build general-purpose robot brains.

总结：英伟达在德国举行的GTC柏林大会上推出了OpenShell参考架构，旨在帮助企业构建、隔离和观察人工智能代理，并防止其失控。 该平台在英伟达的Vera Rubin系统上运行AI模型，使用Vera CPU运行代理软件，并采用BlueField-4数据处理单元作为安全屏障，以阻止未经授权的操作。 英伟达表示，正与CoreWeave、Crusoe、Lambda、Nebius和Oracle Cloud Infrastructure等公司合作推出该参考架构，该架构可在几毫秒内隔离试图失控的代理。 首席执行官黄仁勋强调，随着企业准备部署数十亿个AI代理，安全性和控制必须内置于计算架构中。 此外，英伟达的合作伙伴Agile Robots开发了一款名为Agile One SN的4.59英尺高的人形机器人，该机器人运行在英伟达Isaac GR00T软件平台和Nvidia Jetson Thor计算机上，旨在帮助开发者构建通用机器人“大脑”。

PR Newswire — Government of Ras Al Khaimah and UBTECH Robotics Sign Strategic MoU to Advance Humanoid Robotics Research and Climate Testing

The Government of Ras Al Khaimah and UBTECH Robotics signed a strategic Memorandum of Understanding witnessed by UAE Supreme Council Member and Ruler of Ras Al Khaimah, Sheikh Saud bin Saqr Al Qasimi. The agreement establishes a framework to test, demonstrate, and deploy humanoid robotic systems across industrial, commercial service, elderly care, and cultural tourism scenarios in Ras Al Khaimah. The partnership will explore launching the Ras Al Khaimah Humanoid Robotics Climate Research Initiative to validate robotic performance in the Emirate's distinct environmental conditions for the UAE and the wider Gulf region. UBTECH will deploy humanoid robots and specialized engineering teams for live demonstrations to generate performance data and advance high-tech research and development. Ras Al Khaimah will provide a live testing sandbox and regulatory frameworks connecting research institutions with industry.

Business Insider — Tesla's Optimus robots are finally rolling off the production line

Tesla has begun producing its Optimus humanoid robot at its Fremont factory, according to its quarterly earnings report. CEO Elon Musk stated during the Q3 earnings call that the current manufacturing process is suitable for a commercial product, though design tweaks are needed to ensure safety, reliability, and long duration. Musk estimated it will take at least a year to scale production to 20,000 units a week in Fremont and expects to begin customer deliveries at the end of next year. He noted early production will involve challenges, including building robot hands, and mentioned plans to establish an 'Optimus Academy' for robots to practice tasks and generate AI training data. Tesla also plans to install its next-generation AI5 chip in Optimus in April to increase computing power. The Fremont plant stopped producing the Model S and Model X earlier this year to make room for an Optimus line aiming for a million robots a year, alongside a second line under construction in Austin planned for an annual capacity of 10 million robots.

总结：特斯拉在季度财报中宣布，已在其弗里蒙特工厂开始生产备受期待的Optimus人形机器人。 首席执行官埃隆·马斯克在第三季度财报电话会议上表示，目前的制造流程适用于商业产品，但仍需对设计进行微调，以确保机器人安全、可靠且具备长寿命。 马斯克预计，将弗里蒙特工厂的Optimus产量扩大到每周20,000台至少需要一年时间，并预计在明年年底开始向客户交付。 他指出，早期生产将面临挑战，包括制造机器人双手，并提到计划建立一个“Optimus学院”，供机器人练习任务并生成AI训练数据。 特斯拉还计划于4月份在Optimus中安装其下一代AI5芯片以提升计算能力。 弗里蒙特工厂于今年早些时候停止生产Model S和Model X，以为年产百万台机器人的Optimus产线腾出空间，此外奥斯汀也在建设第二条产线，计划年产能达到1000万台机器人。
