While Georgia Chalvatzaki of Technische Universität Darmstadt was impressed by the engineering achievements of the prototype in a year compared to Honda’s Asimo from 20 years ago, she noted that the behaviors demonstrated were less impressive, though she expressed excitement over the potential of cheap and accessible hardware for academic research.

Live Science — Faster than Usain Bolt? Here's all the records robot 'athletes' broke at this year's World Humanoid Robot Games in China.

Humanoid robots broke four human athletics world records at the 2026 World Humanoid Robot Games in Beijing, which featured 2,056 robots from 666 teams across 16 countries. The Tiangong Ultra robot, developed by the Beijing Humanoid Robot Innovation Center, won the 100-meter sprint in 8. 64 seconds, the 400-meter run in 38. 15 seconds, and the 1,500-meter race in 2 minutes, 21. 6 seconds, beating human records held by Usain Bolt, Wayde van Niekerk, and Hicham El Guerrouj respectively. Additionally, an X-Humanoid robot cleared 2. 88 meters in a standing high jump, breaking previous robot records though differing in mechanics from Javier Sotomayor's running high jump record. Experts noted that these achievements relied heavily on mechanical optimization, simplicity, and reduced degrees of freedom.

总结：人形机器人在北京举行的2026年世界人形机器人运动会上打破了四项人类田径世界纪录，该运动会有来自16个国家的666支队伍的2,056个机器人参加。 由北京具身智能机器人创新中心开发的天工超人机器人以8. 64秒赢得100米短跑，以38. 15秒赢得400米赛跑，以2分21. 6秒赢得1500米比赛，分别打破了尤塞恩·博尔特、韦德·范尼凯克和希查姆·埃尔·盖鲁伊保持的人类纪录。 此外，X-Humanoid制造的机器人在立定跳高中清除了2. 88米，打破了先前的机器人纪录，尽管其力学原理与哈维尔·索托马约尔的背越式跳高纪录不同。 专家指出，这些成就主要依赖于机械优化、简洁性以及减少自由度。

Tech Times — AgiBot A3 Writes Calligraphy at IFA Berlin: Force-Sensing Hands That Also Map Facilities

At IFA Berlin 2026, AgiBot attracted large crowds by demonstrating its A3 humanoid robot writing Chinese calligraphy, a task requiring precise force transparency and control of downward force, lateral angle, and movement speed. This capability is made possible by AgiBot's OmniHand 3 Ultra-M dexterous hand system, which uses 20 active degrees of freedom driven by fully direct-drive actuation without gearboxes, achieving positional repeatability of ±0. 2 mm. Visuotactile sensors at all five fingertips and a palm tactile array provide data on contact location and force magnitude, while the A3 platform—standing 173 cm tall and weighing 55 kg—incorporates a touch-sensing layer across its entire body, a 360-degree multi-array microphone system, and up to 12 kW of instant peak power.

总结：在2026年柏林IFA展会上，AgiBot通过展示其A3人形机器人书写中国书法吸引了大量人群，这是一项需要精确的力透明度以及控制下压力、横向角度和移动速度的任务。 这一能力得益于AgiBot的OmniHand 3 Ultra-M灵巧手系统，该系统采用完全直接驱动的20个主动自由度，没有齿轮箱，位置重复精度达到±0. 2毫米。 所有五个指尖上的视觉触觉传感器和手掌触觉阵列提供关于接触位置和力大小的数据，而身高173厘米、体重55公斤的A3平台则在其全身整合了触觉感知层、360度多阵列麦克风系统以及高达12千瓦的瞬时峰值功率。
